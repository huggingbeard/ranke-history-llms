Normal, broken, or unthinkable — judged by what the frame writes

Ranke-4B 1900 · 1913 · 1929 (base). 13 ideas × 4 phrasings; free continuations classified by Gemini-3.5-flash (EXTENDS / REVERTS / DISTORTS / STALLS). Honest scope: the era's written-corpus frame, not minds.

Headline. Recognition is not the test — production is. Told a fact, the model can parse it; the question is whether it can run forward. EXTENDS develops the fact. REVERTS the old default creeps back (a woman leader becomes a man). DISTORTS the fact is rewritten into something familiar (a city-killing bomb becomes a house fire). STALLS it leaves no trace. Result: controls run forward in every era; boundary ideas climb revert→extend from 1900 to 1929; genuinely-future ideas distort or stall in all three, never assimilating.

5Why the obvious probe failed (the forced choice)

The first plan was a forced choice: offered the fact-consistent vs the default completion, which does the model prefer? It does not discriminate. Once a fact is stated, the model holds it at the probe almost regardless of whether the idea is normal or absurd — it is reading comprehension of the planted sentence, not assimilation. Lift over the no-fact baseline (registration = pref_near−pref0, retention = pref_far−pref0): the genuinely-future ideas register and retain as strongly as the controls — yet their production (§2) distorts or stalls. 6Method & honest limits

Base models only (Instruct = modern contamination). 4 phrasings/idea; greedy free continuations judged by Gemini-3.5-flash (batch), blind to class/era, forced to name each fact's key novelty and check its survival (flash-lite was too lenient and was rejected). Primary metric is the use-it continuation; the hold-it probe is diagnostic only for authority ideas (a non-sequitur for railway/atom, where it inflates stalls). What this measures: what the era's written-corpus frame can DO with an idea — extend, retain, or only mangle it — not anyone's mind. When the 1900 frame turns a city-killing bomb into a bucket brigade, it shows the 1900 record gave no way to carry that idea: a claim about the discursive horizon of the surviving text, stated as such. Limits: small n (2 use-it continuations/cell), a single judge, greedy decoding, control baseline noisier than ideal.
