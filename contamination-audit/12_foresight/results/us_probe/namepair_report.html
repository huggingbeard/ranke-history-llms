Does the 1929 reader carry post-1929 name-pairs it shouldn’t?

“Dwight → Eisenhower” and “Ronald → Reagan” are learnable only from text written after those men became famous — after 1929. So we strip everything except the bare first name and measure how strongly the model expects the surname next. Pre-1913 pairs (Theodore Roosevelt, Woodrow Wilson) are controls both models must have.
