Two time-locked base models, one contamination set: Talkie-1930 vs Ranke-1929

Talkie-1930-13B base (RunPod) vs Ranke-4B-1929 base (local) · identical date-spike set (src/events_dates.py) · P(true year) metric · shared threshold 10% (95th pct of 30 pooled fictional events) · 2026-07-08

Both are base models frozen around 1929–30, from different labs. On events inside both windows (1914–1929) they should — and do — know the dates, which validates the test: known-fact median is 80% (Ranke) and 87% (Talkie), fictional events sit at chance. The real question is the events after both cutoffs.

Figure 3. Each point is one dated event: Ranke-1929 P(true) on the x-axis, Talkie-1930 P(true) on the y. Squares are 1914–29 events (inside both windows, validation); circles are events after both cutoffs (the test). The grey corner is “both clean” (both at chance); the top-right is “both leak”. A point on the dashed diagonal means the two models leak it equally; off-diagonal means one leaks more.
