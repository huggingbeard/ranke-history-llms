Ranke 1913 has read nothing printed after 1913; Ranke 1929 nothing after 1929. In 1913 Adolf Hitler was a twenty-four-year-old unknown in Vienna, rejected from art school. The honest caveat: neither model knows 1933

Where the 1929 model says “chancellor”, it is guessing, not remembering: it has him “made chancellor by King Ludwig II” (who died in 1886) or “called to that office by Emperor William”. No continuation from either model states the real January-1933 Chancellorship, the Reichstag fire, or anything of the war. The 1929 model knows the rising agitator of the 1920s; it does not know how the story ends — which is exactly the boundary the models were built to hold.
