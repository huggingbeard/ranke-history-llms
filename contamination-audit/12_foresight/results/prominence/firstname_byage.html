First-name knowledge by the year the person turned 25

Each panel is one model. x-axis: birth year + 25. Green = the model’s training window; grey = after its cutoff (dashed vertical). Solid dots are above chance (real signal); hollow dots are below chance, where the true first name is simply a rarer token than its distractors (name-rarity, not extra ignorance) — their depth is not meaningful. The smooth curve is a Gaussian-weighted trend. Read-off: both models hold the first names of people who came of age well within their window and fall to chance for those after; the 1929 model’s green shelf reaches further right, capturing Lenin, Mussolini, Stravinsky, Curie that the 1913 model does not yet firmly hold.
