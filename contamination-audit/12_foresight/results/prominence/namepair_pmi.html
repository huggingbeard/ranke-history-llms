Does the model bind a person’s first name to their last? (across domains, 1900/1913/1929)

The clean form of the combo idea: no generation, no judge. For each person we take log P(surname | first name) − log P(surname | “Mr.”) — how much the first name lifts the surname above a generic person context. This removes the first-name-commonness confound that makes the raw probability near-zero for “Leo” or “Paul”. Positive means the model carries the full name as a bound unit; near zero means it does not.

Rows by rise-to-fame year. Pink rows are post-1929 — a clean model should show ~0 there for every column, 1929 included.
