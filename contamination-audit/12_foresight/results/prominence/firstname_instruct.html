Do the models know the first name? — base vs instruct

Surname + role pin the person; the model picks the first name from four distractors matched on language and token length (chance = 20%). Base models complete “… first name was ___”; instruct models are asked directly through their chat template. Ranke 1913 & 1929 (1900 is the weak base, no instruct variant).

Four columns: base vs instruct, at each cutoff. Instruct (columns 2, 4) is far brighter for people the model knows — it surfaces first-name knowledge the base completion cannot. The pink post-1929 rows stay near chance in every column, instruct included: the chat tuning added confidence, not post-cutoff facts.
