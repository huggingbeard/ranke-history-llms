Verdict

No coherent post-cutoff leak survives the dig. Every apparent positive dissolves under at least one of three independent tests:

1. Coherence. Of four topics, 0 show the cluster-wide coherence a leaked source would produce. The one cluster that clears the binomial bar (WWII West, 6/6) is the one stuffed with places that carry strong pre-cutoff identities (Pearl Harbor the naval base, Normandy the duchy) — and it is stronger in the 1900 model than the 1929 model, which is impossible for a real leak.

2. Decoy specificity. For 12 of 20 facets the pre-1929 decoy lifts more than the post-cutoff event (negative specificity). The model knows Leningrad as ‘the city renamed after Lenin’ (+6.5) not its WWII siege (−0.8); Sevastopol as the Crimean-War fortress (+3.0) not the WWII one (−1.4); Vietnam as a French colony (+5.3) not the American war (+2.9). The lift is generic place-salience, not the event.

3. Cross-model proof. The 1900 model cannot contain a word of WWII or Vietnam text, yet it shows the same or stronger ‘associations’ than 1929 in every cluster. An uncontaminatable model exhibiting the signal equally is the proof the signal is not contamination.
What the dig did surface is the model's real pre-cutoff knowledge bleeding through the controls: French Indochina, the Lenin renaming, the Crimean fortress, the imperial free cities. That is exactly the era it should know — and the wall holds.
