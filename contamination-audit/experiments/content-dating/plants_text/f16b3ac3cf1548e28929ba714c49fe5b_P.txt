Fourth, the 164 retirees who did provide widow survivorship annuities at the time of retirement would be permitted to elect anew, in the maximum amounts in each case, at the low cost ($300 for a $2,400 annuity) now available to annuitants retired or retiring on and after October 16, 1960.
