He took off his hat and raised his head and looked up at the hotel. On the top floor he saw a window opened wide, the curtains blowing out in the wind.
"Mon dieu," he said, "she must have jumped from there!" Together they picked her up and carried her into the hotel lobby and placed her on a lounge. The house-clerk called a doctor. He was a long time arriving. When he finally got there he had to drag Robin away from Joan so he could examine her. He looked at her for a second and then turned to Robin and said: "It was useless and stupid to call me, monsieur. She is already dead." Robin threw himself on Joan, his face against her breast, and cried out loud. His cries rang out strangely in the dim hotel lobby.
