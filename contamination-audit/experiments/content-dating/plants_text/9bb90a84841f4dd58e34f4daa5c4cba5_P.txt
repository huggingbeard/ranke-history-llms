In each house, Mr. Heller believes that a majority and a minority committee would be preferable to a two-party policy committee because a committee formed from the two parties would make it impossible to focus party responsibility and accountability in the policy committees.

Making Appropriations Bills Reasonably Broad How general should appropriation bills be?
Mr. Heller replied that it was difficult to answer the question in generalities. A lump sum appropriation of the blank check type is just as bad as an overly detailed appropriation bill. The point is that appropriations should be made for individual departments or bureaus to accomplish certain stipulated objectives.
Congress should then evaluate the performance of the department or bureau in terms of the extent to which the objectives were accomplished with the money appropriated therefor.
