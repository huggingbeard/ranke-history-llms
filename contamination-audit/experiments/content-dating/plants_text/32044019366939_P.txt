A great part of the iron in a healthy adult's body is in the coloring matter of the red blood cells or hemoglobin, and it functions in the transportation of oxygen to all the arteries and capillaries to the innermost cells of every organism in the body. Most of the remainder is stored in the liver, bone marrow, and 113

spleen, where it is ready for conversion of the hemoglobin when it is needed by the body.

Not all the iron consumed in foodstuffs can be converted into the form necessary for absorption. 

Experiments have shown that the iron that is available to the system very seldom is more than sixty per cent of the total iron content of the diet, so that it is necessary to consume nearly twice as much as the iron content of the various foods would indicate. 

Nutritional or iron deficiency anemia results when the iron intake is low, or when iron is not properly assimilated by the body so that it does not reach the bone marrow. The bone marrow tries to make up for this lack of iron by reducing the red cells and making them particularly low in hemoglobin. Less hemoglobin is formed, and therefore there is less to be broken down, so the iron to be set free to be used again is of a much smaller quantity. 

Therefore, we must supply the deficiency by providing an adequate supply of available iron. 

Many children do not seem to be actually anemic, but they are listless and lacking in vitality. Sometimes they have a very poor appetite. These symptoms indicate definitely that the reserve strength of iron is low, and evidently it is being replaced by enough available iron to overcome the deficiency. If the child suddenly has a spurt of growth such as often occurs during the adolescent period, or has some setback such as an infectious disease or a bad cold where the resistance is lowered, these may so 114

deplete the body of its reserve iron that anemia will not be far off. The child's increasing need for iron cannot be overlooked. The wise mother must plan every meal to contain available iron, and if she cannot accomplish this result she should see that the child gets the proper iron from supplementary feeding. 

Adults, too, need a sufficient quantity of available iron to protect them from iron deficiency diseases such as anemia and other ailments.

Every time you have an altercation with a person close to you, or an emotional upset, there is always a period of depression afterward. These outbursts are the things that age you, tear you to pieces.

It is possible to develop the attitude that there is a band playing in your heart all the time. Try to keep it going all the time, regardless of what your circumstances are. Rise above your environment. Be positive, and don't allow yourself to be dragged through emotional outbursts with others. When you go to a mirror smile at yourself. Try to carry a smile to sleep with you every night of your life. Bed is no place to take your worries. They cannot be solved there. Upon arising, the first thing on the program 271

is to get a smile on your face. If you make smiling a life habit then your whole disposition is going to be a joyous one. You are going to attract people to you. There is nothing so magnetic as a smile; there is nothing so infectious as the sparkle of the eye from a smiling face. 

Your emotions can make or break you, and if you are going to live a long time you want to live in a state of contentment. You must develop tranquillity within the nervous system. 

There is no situation above which you cannot rise. No matter how you have been hurt or humiliated, or what your status may be, you can rise above it and be happy in spite of it. Just remember that as long as you are living and breathing you have the greatest treasure on earth, and that is life. I have gone through a great deal in my life, and I always console myself by saying that at least I am a living, breathing person.

Life will always present its adventures, so let's accept them as they come along. Let's be poised and balanced, and the light of happiness and joyousness will show in our eyes.

Love is like a mirror. It is a reflected light. Many people ask for love and never give it. It isn't how much somebody loves you, but it is how much you love him. If you expect loyalty from people you must give loyalty. If you expect consideration you must give consideration. If you expect to receive you must give. Life follows out the law of compen- 272

sation. What we put into it we get out of it. So learn to be the giver. There is no joy in the world like giving. There is no person who receives the joys the giver receives. It is the acme of all human happiness. That is the reason we have so many miserable people today who are always trying to grasp. 

You can't receive anything with a closed hand. You can't receive anything with a closed ear or eye. It is the open hand, the open eye, ear, or mind that receives. You are here to enjoy life, and it is my desire for you to build a magnificent temple of the human body, so that it will house a beautiful soulthat is happiness, contentment and peace with the world.
