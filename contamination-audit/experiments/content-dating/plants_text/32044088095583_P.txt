World War 1. was supposed to be a war "to make the world safe for democracy." It was to be a "war to end all wars." 

The Jewish people were told then, as they are being told now, that they must pray and fight for a victory of the Allies. 

They were told that once the Allies had won the war a new day would dawn for the Jewish people. They would have freedom and equal rights everywhere. Their own homeland in Palestine. The end of pogroms and persecution. 

Many Jewish leaders led the chorus. Dr. Stephen S. Wise, 3.

in a speech delivered upon the entry of the United States, declared: "What are we fighting for? My answer to mothers and fathers is: Enviable, even glorious is your lot if you give your sons or bless their self-dedication to the highest and holiest of causes in which a people was ever engaged. . . . Remember; mothers and fathers, this is not a war. (Italics mine-M.M.) 

Thousands upon thousands of Jews, believing that this war was really being fought for democracy, gave their lives for the cause of the Allies. 

The Allies won the war. The peace treaties were signed. 

Clauses dealing with the rights of minorities were included. 

Poland, Hungary, Roumania and Austria all agreed "to assure free and complete protection of life and liberty to all inhabitants . . . without distinction of birth, nationality, language, race or religion."

On the basis of these minority rights clauses, Cyrus Adler, Louis Marshall and Nahum Sokolow wrote to Boris Bogen, the Administrator of Jewish relief in Poland, that they were certain that the minority rights clauses would at last relieve European Jewry "from the serious disabilities from which they had so long suffered and will forever end the grave abuses of the past. They will enable the Jews as well as all other minorities to live their own lives and to develop their culture." 

That was in 1919.

Twenty-one years have passed and during that time catastrophic upheavals which have followed in the wake of one another have aroused the people to the realization that such a war and such a peace could only lead to more horrible and more devastating wars.

Twenty-one years have passed and the plight of the Jews

has increased a thousandfold and has become an agonizing tale of woe which defies description.

The promises made at the peace conference regarding the rights of minorities were torn up like so many scraps of paper. 

Poland, Hungary, Roumania and finally Germany, with the rise of Hitler, carried through a ruthless program of Jewish extermination. Anti-Semitism was given free rein. Pogroms, economic boycotts, political and social discrimination became common occurrences condoned and in many cases executed by these governments.

Italy, the country which was always pointed to with pride as the classic example that fascism and anti-Semitism need not necessarily go hand in hand, soon followed suit with the result that the 60,000 Jews of Italy have been barred from all economic, social and political life. 

On January 16, 1939, the Administrative Committee of the World Jewish Congress met in Paris and adopted the following resolution: 

"The Administrative Committee of the World Jewish Con gress records with deep anxiety the rapid and catastrophic deterioration of the status of Jewish communities in Central and Eastern European countries as well as in Italy. One government after another has violated the principle of the equality of rights of the Jews, recognized and confirmed internationally through the Peace and Minority Treaties; by means of anti- 

Jewish legislation and arbitrary administrative measures whole Jewish populations have been deprived of elementary political rights and the possibility of maintaining their material and religious traditions and subjected to a relentless economic pressure designed to compel the Jews to emigrate."

Look back, and try to recapture the events of the past few years.

Japan. All they had to do was to place an embargo on Japan and she would quickly have come to her senses. Why did the democracies fail to act?

In 1938 Austria was swept away by the Nazis without their even having lifted a finger to defend themselves. Hardly had Austria been seized when Hitler began his campaign against Czechoslovakia. The world waited with bated breath.

Has Greenberg forgotten so soon the heritage of the first

How, in God's name, can anyone be fighting to save the

Jews when by their actions they doom a million Jews to death by slow starvation?

The American Jewish Congress Bulletin of April 12th stated that when the war will come to an end there will remain only one Jewish community, the Jewish community of America. 

In other words they admit openly and without equivocation that if the European war continues European Jewry is doomed.

And yet they would have us support one side which can only mean the continuation of the war. 

Hitlerism must be destroyed. Fascism must be destroyed. 

That is the prayer and hope of every Jew, of every peaceloving person.

Were England and France really fighting to achieve this end, there would be no person who has a stake in democracy who would refuse to aid them.

The unfortunate and tragic situation is, however, that the rulers of Britain and France are not fighting to destroy 

Nazism and fascism.

War, they would be the first to prevent the establishment of a democratic Germany. 25

This war is a piece of unfinished business which was interrupted for 21 years. It is being carried on for the same purposes and the same aims as was the World War 1. Both sides would like to complete irrevocably and finally, to their own advantage, what was left unfinished then-namely, the question of who is to dominate the world. 

To support either side in this war, therefore, is to defend further anti-Semitism and further pogroms.

The people of the world, particularly the Jewish people, must unite in order to see that the war is brought to an immediate halt, so that no further lives may be lost. 

It is not the rulers of Britain and France who will destroy Nazism. That is the task of the German people. They and they alone can solve the problem. 

Harold Nicolson, National Labor Member of the English 

Parliament, in his book Why Britain Is at War admits this to be the case when he states: "We are not, however, at war with Hitler because of his contempt for the virtues of the German people, or the masses in general. We may regret that he should have seen fit to crush all independent thought in his own country and have employed the magnificent machine of German State education for the production of a generation believing only in the harsh majesty of force. We may regret that in the twentieth century any government can have imposed upon its fellow citizens such cruelties as Hitler has imposed upon the Jews and Socialists. 

We may deplore that by his actions he has warped the soul of one of the finest races which humanity has ever produced. 

Yet we are not concerned in this particular quarrel; it is a quarrel beteen his countrymen and himself; it is to them that he will one day have to render account." (Italics mine-M.M.) 26

There is no lesser evil. A victory for either side would be equally disastrous.

There is only one evil-millions of human beings are losing their lives in a war which is being carried on by both contestants to further their own ends. "STOP THE WAR!" must be the united cry which should come forth from the throats of all those who are suffering today and who will continue to suffer if the war goes on.

KEEP AMERICA AT PEACE 

Here, in America, forces are at work to drag us into the war. 

Amongst those who are leading in the war cry is President 

Roosevelt and his Administration. 

The actions of the Administration since the outbreak of the war, under the guise of the war emergency, despite the fact that it has at all times loudly protested that it desires to keep America at peace, have proven most alarming to the American people.

Since the outbreak of the war the Administration has begun a series of attacks upon the economic, social and political rights of the American people. President Roosevelt has called for huge slashes in W.P.A. appropriations at a time when there are 12 million unemployed. The Administration under the guise of enforcement of the anti-trust laws has begun a systematic offensive against the trade unions. The Administration has instituted a series of attacks upon the civil liberties of the American people.

In conjunction with this it is important to note that the spread of the war in Europe has spurred the pro-war elements in the United States Congress to devote special attention to the possibilities of passing anti-alien legislation. 

These un-American, anti-alien bills may be enacted before the adjournment of Congress behind the screen of "sabotage," "espionage" or "Trojan horse." 27

The President's reorganization bill, the Reynolds bill, the Dies bill, the Smith bill, and the host of other such vicious legislation, if enacted, will play havoc with thousands of Jewish people who are citizens. Such laws will be used as a smoke-screen behind which to destroy American democracy. 

The war hysteria is spreading rapidly. The war propagandists, skilled masters of their art, are attempting to alarm the American people by telling them that Kansas, Florida and 

Tennessee are in immediate danger of attack. Under the guise of attacking "fifth columnists" every peace-loving and democratic American citizen is in danger.

These acts are an attack upon democracy. They menace the fundamental rights of the American people. It is obvious that those who are in such a hurry to involve us in the war cannot be interested in doing so merely because they desire to save the world for democracy. There are no double standards. One cannot be a friend of democracy abroad while one continues to undermine and destroy democracy at home.

This advance of reaction which has become a menace to all Americans is doubly menacing to the Jews. For the fate of the Jew is bound up with the fate of democracy. Where democracy is strengthened there anti-Semitism is weakened. 

Where democracy is weakened there anti-Semitism is strengthened. That is how it works. One is indissolubly bound up with the other.

It is therefore important to notice that there has been a major increase of anti-Semitic activities in America. While the respectable Jewish organizations have been attempting to hide this fact, yet even they are forced to admit it once in a while. In a report which the American Jewish Congress put out a very short time ago they stated that: "Organized anti-Semitism has been making progress in the United States through the past seven years, such progress that 28

it reminds one of the years in Germany when Hitler was rising to power."

Particularly has the situation become more aggravated since the advent of the war and the increase of warmongering in America. The whole country is being deluged with leaflets and stickers which read as follows: "I will not go to war to save the British or the Jews." While another reads: "Jews with the help of Roosevelt expect every American to do his duty."

George Seldes in his news letter called In Fact points out this grave danger. 

"WARNING: Senator Reynolds of North Carolina, Wil liam Dudley Pelley of the Silver Shirts, and Congressman Dies of Texas can be expected soon to proclaim openly, or at least to hint in no uncertain terms, that the Jews are responsible for the present European war. "Propaganda entitled 'Jewish War-Guilt' was exported by the Nazis to Mexico, Detroit, New York, and elsewhere, as soon as war broke out. In Detroit, the slander was spread by the National Workers League, closely tied to the Ford Motor Company, and a strong advocate of company unionism. Father 

Coughlin immediately added his voice to the anti-Semites, as did the Christian Front, whose followers swear allegiance to Coughlin. Their slogan is 'Jewish influences started the war and are now trying to bring America into it. . . .' "The pogromists expect great things from Dies, Pelley, and 

Reynolds. They bear in mind the statement of Joseph Goebbles, Nazi Minister of Propaganda and Public Enlightenment, in April, 1939, 'If ever a new war should break out in Europe, then this cry, "The Jews are the culprits," shall resound through the world." (May 20, 1940.)

These are days when a war hysteria is being whipped up throughout the country. These are days when the right to 29

think and believe what you please is being challenged-in the attempt to involve us in the war. 

And out of this there is developing a vicious and widespread anti-Semitism, one of the ways by which the forces of reaction hope to break the unity of the American people against the war. 

Tragically, many Jewish leaders are adding fuel to the cries of these anti-Semites who proclaim that the Jews want to drag America into the war.

The Wises, the Greenbergs, the Abe Cahans, are proclaiming day and night that we must join the Allies in order to defend Jewish rights.

The American people do not want to get involved in this war. They were fooled once before by nice phrases. And it was a costly mistake. Thousands of America's finest youth were killed in that war. The American people want no more of such bloodshed.

The Jewish people of America likewise are against this war. The Jewish people do not want one single American young man to lose his life over there. The Jewish people know that War No. 1. did not solve the Jewish problem but created a Versailles Treaty, created a Hitler, and led to 

War No. 2. which can only create an even worse Versailles, more Jewish suffering, and can only lead to War No. 3. 

The Jews of America therefore join with all of the American people in demanding that America stay out of this war. 

We have a war to carry on, but that war is right here and not on Flanders Field. We have a war to carry on against the Coughlins, the Thorkelsons, the Winrods, and the Pelleys. We have a war to carry on against the selfappointed saviour of American democracy-Martin Dieswho is recommended for the Presidency of the United States by all the leading anti-Semites of America. We have a war to carry on against those who refuse to pass the Anti-Lynching Bill. We have a war to carry on 30

This is the battlefield upon which the Jews of America must join with all liberty-loving Americans. 

An America dragged into the war will be a victory for the reactionary forces of America.

An America kept out of the war will be a victory for the democratic forces of America. 

Such an America, whose destiny is decided by those who have a stake in democracy, will be a country which will not only put an end to race hatred and bigotry at home but will also be a great factor in the establishment of democracy throughout the world.
