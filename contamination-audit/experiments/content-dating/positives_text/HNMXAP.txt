LITERARY COLONY OF WINTER PARK By WILLIAM F. YUST 

HIS list was first presented at the annual dinner of the Florida Library Association in Orlando, April 11, 1935, at which most of the authors were the guests of the Association. 

The second edition was presented to the librarians of seven states at a regional library conference at Bolton Landing on Lake George, New 

York, September 9-14, 1935.

The third edition was a part of the Rollins College publication "The town that became a university". This fourth edition includes 109 authors and 605 titles. It is necessarily incomplete. To include magazine articles and their writers would fill a book. Periodical literature and pamphlets are therefore omitted. 

Effort has been made to include especially those authors who have homes here or who sojourn among us more or less regularly. Suggestions for inclusion in future editions will be gratefully received.
