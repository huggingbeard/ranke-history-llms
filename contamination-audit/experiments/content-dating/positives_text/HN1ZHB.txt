ANONYMOUS GIFT

BORN UNWANTED 

A child comes unwanted, into a loveless marriage.

It is a girl, and the mother loves her with fierce possessiveness, and, after getting a divorce, takes her away with her, to travel around the world. For her daughter's sake she marries again only so that through a second divorce she may secure a large alimony, to provide comforts for her daughter. The child is spoiled and grows up wild, wilful, and beautiful. She has learned only to take from her mother, and the taking extends even to her mother's lovers. The tragic experience to which this leads, and the regeneration that comes to her through this tragedy are among the most remarkable passages offered in recent fiction. No reader will fail to be entranced by this impelling story. 1

Pullman berth, Mona Logan yawned from sheer boredom. By the shaded light of the electric wall-lamp she skimmed through the pages of Treasure Island. Just why she had chosen the Stevenson masterpiece in lieu of a popular novel as a train companion, Mona did not know. 

Perhaps because its setting was different; refreshingly unlike her own unvaried existence. That would be it! She smiled faintly as she grew vaguely conscious of the rush of the train through the night.

Muffled sounds of the swift steel monster's progress filtered through the thick glass windows of the stateroom. 

Yet it seemed to Mona that this unexpected journey from New York to Detroit was no less fantastic than the stirring fiction which failed to hold her interest. There in the dim-lit drawing-room, with its lacquered metal walls, even the neat pile of expensive luggage in the corner seemed unreal. But was her whole life real? Would anything calm and gratify the restless discontent which grew and surged within her? 

Certainly not Don, her husband! Not the handsome and successfully ambitious youth who had wooed and won the romantic fancy of a small-town New England girl brief ten months ago. To be the guest of some shrewd executive's fat wife, while Don talked a fatter contract out of her hopeful husband.
