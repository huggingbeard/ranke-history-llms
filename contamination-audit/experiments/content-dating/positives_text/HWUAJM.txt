Note on Pronunciation 

The column indicating how to say the Portuguese expression is an approximation. Nevertheless, a person who pays close attention to the pronunciation here should have no trouble in being understood. In some areas where Portuguese is spoken, the final vowel "o" is pronounced by giving it a quality approximating the sound of the English "oo"; and the final "s" is pronounced “sh,” softer than in the English word "ship."
