NOVEMBER 1968 EDITION PREFACE 

This instruction booklet has been prepared by the Law Department of the Office of the Administrator of National Banks for use by National Bank Officers in preparing for special or annual meetings of shareholders. This edition reflects the most recent revisions of our regulations on corporate practices and disclosure. Sample documents which comply with the regulations have been made up in connection with various agenda items which frequently arise on the occasion of shareholders' meetings. In addition, instructions and forms are included with respect to regulations permitting the submission to stockholders of proposals for employees' stock option plans, preferred stock, capital debentures and authorized but unissued stock, as well as amendments to the Articles of Association which may eliminate the need for certain shareholder action. 

The forms (where provided) and instructions contained in this booklet are applicable to any meeting of shareholders at which directors are to be elected or any of the agenda items listed below are to be considered by shareholders. All banks are urged to retain this booklet intact for future reference in connection with special and annual meetings of shareholders. Appropriate revisions will be made from time to time by this Office to accord with any changes in policy or the practice and disclosure regulations which may be made in the future.

In the event of conflict between the following instructions, forms or illustrations and the regulations, the regulations shall be controlling. 1172845

I. Instructions Applicable to Banks with at least 500 Shareholders of Record

Pursuant to Part 11 of the Comptroller's Regulations, banks having at least 500 shareholders of record must supply their shareholders with a notice of meeting, proxy statement and form of proxy which contain information sufficient to meet the requirements of Section 11. 3 of the regulations. In addition, an annual report meeting the requirements of Part 10 and Part 18 must reach the shareholders prior to the annual meeting, and the requisite number of copies must be filed with this Office (See Section 

10. 4 of the Regulations).

The bank's bylaws and Articles of Association should be checked carefully for the date of the annual meeting and special provisions affecting the conduct of shareholders' meetings.

These items have been chosen for illustrative purposes only and, of course, each bank's papers must be drawn to fit the actual agenda for its annual meeting. We wish to emphasize that the model documents contain the minimum information necessary to comply with Part 11 and that any bank is free and indeed is encouraged to supply its shareholders with whatever further information is deemed appropriate in the opinion of the management, to enable the shareholders to cast an intelligent and informed vote on each item of the agenda. 

A. Notice of Meeting (Exhibit 1) 

The notice of meeting should contain the time and place of the meeting and a list of the matters to be voted upon. The notice (and other proxy material) should be filed with the Administrator's Office pursuant to Section 11. 4 at least two weeks before final printing for this Office to review and comment on the material so that it may be mailed to shareholders at least 10 days prior to the scheduled date of the meeting. 

Prior to the mailing of the notice of meeting, management should nominate a slate of directors in accordance with the number specified in the Articles of Association. If the Articles of Association specify a variable number, management should nominate the number which the shareholders will be asked to fix by resolution at the meeting. If it is desired to elect a number of directors other than that fixed or within the range permitted by the Articles, it will be necessary for an appropriate amendment to the Articles to be on the agenda of the meeting. In such case, management should nominate a slate of directors in accordance with the new number the shareholders will be requested to approve.

Section 7la, U.S. C., Title 12, does not permit the Articles to specify a number of directors less than 5 nor more than 25. 

In addition, when deemed to be in the interest of the bank, 2

management may propose an amendment to the Articles permitting the appointment of additional directors between annual meetings by the vote of a majority of the full Board of Directors in accordance with Section 7.5 of the regulations. 

B. Proxy Statement (Exhibit 2) 

The proxy statement should contain sufficient information as to each agenda item to permit the casting of an intelligent and informed vote by the shareholder. Schedules 

A and B found in Section 11. 6 of the regulations should be consulted as to the minimum disclosure requirements for particular agenda items. The model proxy statement (Exhibit 2) illustrates how the information required may be presented. Variations from the precise form of the model proxy statement are permissible provided that the miniinum information required by Part 11 is provided. 

Proxy statements for meetings at which the only order of business is the election of directors may consist of page 1 of Exhibit 2 plus paragraph 1 on page 17. 

Section 11. 3 of the regulations should be consulted in connection with shareholder proposals. 

C. Form of Proxy (Exhibit 3) 

The attached model proxy is in standard legal form. 

The following special requirements of law should be noted: 

1. No officer or employee of the bank may be named as a proxy. Attorneys and directors who are not also officers or employees may be so named. Shareholders should be advised of this restriction of law on the proxy form. 

2. The proxy should provide appropriate boxes to permit shareholders to vote for or against each proposal on the agenda. With respect to the election of directors, the signing of a management proxy indicates approval of the management slate narned in the proxy statement. 

Shareholders who do not wish to vote for the management slate should withhold their proxy and attend the meeting or give their proxy to someone planning to attend the meeting. Any solicitation by a person opposing the management slate should comply with Section 11. 5. 

3. Management may wish to consider the adoption of an

amendment to the Articles or Bylaws pursuant to Part 17 of the regulations, dealing with required notification to nominate directors. 

4. Section 11. 3 of the regulations should be consulted in connection with shareholder proposals.

Guidelines for the Preparation of Stock Purchase and Stock Option Plans 

The  will consider for approval stock purchase plans and stock option plans (whether or not such plans qualify for special treatment under the Federal income tax laws) which are reasonably designed to attract and hold valuable personnel, provided: 

1. The plan is administered by a committee, none of whose members may participate in the plan; 

2. The number of shares allocable to any person under the plan is reasonable in relation to the purpose of the plan and the needs of the bank; and 

3. In the case of a stock option plan, the number of shares subject to the plan is not unreasonable in relation to the bank's capital structure and anticipated growth.

Part 13 of the regulations describes the procedure for obtaining Office approval of such plans. 11 1

Dividend Rights 

Holders of Preferred Stock will be entitled to cumulative preferential dividends at the annual rate of $4.75 per share payable quarterly on the first days of February, May, August and November of each year. If dividends at the full quarterly rate are not paid or declared and set apart for the Preferred Stock, the deficiency must be fully paid or declared and set apart for payment before any dividend or other distribution may be declared or paid on any stock ranking junior ("junior stock") to the Preferred Stock or before any sums may be utilized for the retirement of any Preferred Stock. 19

Redemption Provisions 

The Preferred Stock will be non-callable for any reason for ten years.

It will thereafter be callable in whole or in part at the option of the Bank, with the consent of the Administrator of National Banks, at $100 per share plus accrued dividends. 

Shares purchased or redeemed must be cancelled.

Purchase Fund 

A Purchase Fund of $10, 000 per year will be set aside out of earnings in each year beginning on January 2, 1974 and applied to the purchase of Preferred Stock in the open market or on calls for tenders at the lowest prices available, but in no event in excess of $99.99 per share plus accrued dividends. 

The Purchase Fund will be non-cumulative so that if it is not provided or used in any year due to the absence of earnings or the inability to buy Preferred Stock below $100 per share plus accrued dividends any such Purchase Fund deficiency or unused balance will not be carried forward. 

Pre-emptive Rights 

The holders of the Preferred Stock will not have any preemptive rights to purchase or subscribe for any shares of stock of any class of the Bank. The holders of the Common 

Stock will not have any pre-emptive rights to purchase or subscribe for any shares of Preferred Stock or any shares of stock of any other class of the Bank having a preference over the Common Stock as to dividends or assets. In the event of any increase in the Common Stock of the Bank by the sale of additional shares thereof, each holder of Common Stock will be entitled to subscribe for such additional shares of Common Stock in proportion to the number of shares of Common Stock owned by him.

Voting Rights The Preferred Stock will not have voting rights in the election of directors unless four quarterly dividends are in arrears at the time of any annual meeting of shareholders. 

In such case, the holders of Preferred Stock will be entitled, voting as a class, to elect three directors to hold office until the annual meeting of shareholders next following the date on which all such arrearages of dividends are cured. 20

The consent of the holders of 66-2/3% of the Preferred Stock will be required to (a) change adversely the provisions of the Preferred Stock or authorize and issue stock ranking prior thereto, or (b) authorize and issue any additional shares of Preferred Stock or stock ranking on a parity therewith.

The Preferred Stock will not be entitled to any other voting rights. 

Liquidation Value 

The liquidation value of the Preferred Stock will be $100 per share plus accrued dividends. Assessability

The holders of Preferred Stock will not be individually responsible for any debts, contracts, or engagements of the Bank, and will not be liable for assessments to restore impairments to the capital of the Bank.

The Capital Debentures will be non-callable for ten years through the application of funds obtained at an interest rate lower than that of the Capital Debentures.
