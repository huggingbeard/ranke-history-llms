TO THOSE WHO HAVE HELPED ME

FOREWORD 

THE writer wishes to mention, by way of explanation, that the following prayers were first prepared for use during small public retreats.

They were read aloud each night from the rear of the chapel immediately after the Blessed Sacrament had been exposed for Benediction; and the reading was punctuated with many brief and several long pauses. After the first five minutes the retreatants were requested to sit down. 

At the urging of persons who found these prayers helpful, they have now been made available in permanent form.

The selection of a title caused some hesitation. 

The book might have been called "Informal 

Meditations." But the word "meditation" seems to imply formality; moreover, it is a word likely to dismay some readers. Other familiar names, such as "Acts," "Affections," "Aspirations,” also seem inappropriate for a variety of reasons. The title finally chosen will, perhaps, come as near as any to conveying an idea of the nature and the aim of the book.

This book is intended to be a series of object lessons for the use of those who are learning the art of communing with God. Each prayer bevii

viii 

gins with reflections on the topic under consideration and then passes on to the expression of acts of the will. The break between these two parts is marked in each instance by an asterisk. 

The prayers are suggestive only; yet in dull moments and cold moods, they may also help to enlighten the beclouded mind and to stimulate the sluggish will. With this purpose in view, the writer offers them to the reader, being himself more acutely aware of the book's defects -he hopes than most of its readers will be. 

In particular he wishes to acknowledge that certain pages are developments of Meditations already printed in A Primer of Prayer. It is not unlikely, therefore, that similar passages and even identical phrases may be found in the two books.

Dear Lord, my power of freedom,

So great in itself, seems greater still, When I contrast myself with other creatures.

The end is predetermined in the beginning. 

Like the rise and fall of the tides, The succession of day and night, The swinging of heavenly bodies 

Along their chartered course, All else too is fixed and determined.

All is subject to the law of mechanical necessity, A law which can be suspended 

By no one but You. 

But Your creature, man, made in Your image, Is gifted with the Godlike power of choice. 

He can decide many an issue;

Often he can interrupt the course of nature, And inaugurate a new series of events. 

Only in You, the Author of nature, And in the soul of man, Is there freedom, liberty of choice,

The faculty of self-determination. 

I see then, dear Lord, That I have more reason to be grateful 

Than any other creature

In the visible world. 

Give me, I beg, a lasting sense 

Of the greatness of Your gift. 

Help me to see clearly 

What it is You have done for me. 

Teach me to reverence my own freedom.

Make me ever mindful 

Of the divine dignity of my soul, Created in Your image, Destined to grow daily

To be like You, To be able to grow steadily more like You, How blessed a privilege! 

How divine a destiny!

It is my privilege, my destiny, And it is You, dear Lord, Who have made it mine.

3. POWER

I see now 

The richness of the gift, The greatness of the trust reposed in me,

The actual truth that here and now, For the time being 

I take the place of God, I possess the power of God.

Your gift, dear Lord, I accept. 

Because it is unique, I am uniquely grateful. 

Because it elevates me, I am humble. 

Because it imposes responsibility, I am afraid. 

Because it makes me resemble You, I respond eagerly, hungrily. 

As a subject from the King, A servant from the Master, A follower from the Leader, A friend from a Friend, A child from a Father, A lover from the Beloved, I accept Your gift. 

I accept this privilege, Which makes me the superior 

Of all other beings in the world, Which makes me little less than the angels, Which makes me resemble You,

The Sovereign of the Universe. 

As ruler of the kingdom of my soul, I acknowledge my allegiance to You.

I resolve to think, to speak, to act, to suffer, As You did, when, here on earth, You pictured for me 

Your idea of human perfection.

4. RESPONSIBILITY 

I am truly made in Your image, dear Lord.

In all this world of created beings, I alone am free. 

It is my privilege

The natural current of events; 

To be responsible for the well being,

The moral and spiritual development, The final happiness, Not only of myself, But also of other human beings;

To affect the history of the universe 

By my decisions.

This power is so tremendous, So far reaching,

The responsibility of using it is so great, That, had You left the decision to me, Dear Lord, I would have shrunk away, Frightened and reluctant 

To receive the gift, To assume the responsibility.

But You have not left it to me to decide. 

It is Your Will that I, Unlike all other lower creatures, Should think and decide for myself. 

Your decree has lifted me to this high level. 

Therefore, dear Lord, Accepting gratefully and trustfully 

Your gift of freedom,

I now resolve- 

And I ask you to confirm my resolution — 

Never to use my free will 

In opposition to You, Never to act in the spirit of disobedience. 

This I resolve and promise, First of all, out of love for You, And in appreciation

And if this motive of love, The desire of pleasing You, Were not strong enough

You have made my freedom so complete, Dear Lord, That for the time being, And within this little kingdom of mine, I am truly supreme. 

If I should choose to be so foolish, so wicked, I could rebel against You, I could bring Your Will to naught, I could wreck Your plans.

In a word, I am at liberty

From any such abuse of freedom as this, Dear Lord, deliver me ! 

It seems wholly beyond belief

Thus rebellious. 

But bitter experience has taught me 

That frequently I seek my own will, Even at the cost of disobeying Yours. 

Now that I realize 

What it means to disobey You, I am both frightened and ashamed. 

Forgive my foolish, wicked conduct of the past, All wise, all kind, all Holy God. 

Teach me to use this divine gift in the way

Save me from the greatest evil

Teach me,

Then it would never again be attractive to me

Unspeakably horrid, and vile and loathsome.

7. EVIL 

Plainly, neither I nor anyone else

Will ever be able to get anything worth having 

By defying Your Will.

I can never advance a single step 

Towards real joy and ultimate happiness, Except in conformity with Your plan for me.

Every thought, and word, and deed, and omission 

Which is against Your Will 

Partakes of the vile qualities of sin. 

This is why in Your eyes, And in the eyes of Your Saints,

In the judgment of Your Church, And according to the testimony 

Of my own conscience,

Every sin, however trifling the matter with which it deals, Is always to be avoided at any cost, Is always essentially wrong,

Always contains within itself 

Measureless possibilities of evil.

Just as a deadly virus unimaginably small, Makes the difference 

Between death and life, So any disobedience to You, Any repudiation of Your Will,

May begin a sequence of events 

Involving endless evil and final death. 

Dear Lord, With Your help I now set my face 

Resolutely against all misuse of my will, Against all sin and all disobedience. 

Never again will I depart from Your plan. 

I dedicate myself to You 

Whole-heartedly, unreservedly. 

I promise to seek Your Will always, To be Your servant and disciple at all times 

And in every place. 

I aim with the help of Your grace 

To abandon the company of sinners, One of whom I have become, By unwillingness 

To deny myself and to follow You.

I renounce and I promise to avoid 

All deliberate disobedience,

Even small transgressions, venial sins. 

For every deliberate defiance of You- Implies kinship with all doers of evil, And involves the possibility 

Of an ultimate fall to the lowest depths 

Of wickedness. 

I hope this present resolution of mine, Even though faint and feeble, Will quicken the first stirrings of holiness 

Within my soul, And bring me at once into kinship 

With Your disciples. 

From every point of view, And for every rational motive, I must accept Your Plan, As the one possible path 

To the Kingdom of God 

And to Your presence,

The way to happiness, to holiness, The road to the fulfillment of my destiny.

Dear Lord, I begin to perceive 

What Thomas à Kempis meant when he said 

That to be separated from You, is Hell, And to be united to You, is Paradise. 

I begin to understand 

That the fatal quality of sin is 

Discord, disagreement of will, Between You and me. 

Sin alienates me from You, Whenever in any degree 

It takes possession of my soul. 

When I sin, I disbelieve You, I distrust You, I insult and injure You. 

However small the matter of my sin,

I destroy the order of Your universe 

To that extent. 

And the evil done by my sin remains

So far as my own power is concerned, Irreparable.

Foolish me! 

What have I done?

It is the cry of every sinner 

Throughout the ages, "I have left You, the Fountain of living water, And dug for myself broken cisterns, Which hold no water." 

I acknowledge my foolishness, my fault. 

Like Adam and Eve, and David and Peter, I seek forgiveness, I beg for mercy, I promise amendment. 

There is no health, nor joy, Nor fair prospect, no hope of life, Or of happiness, or of heaven, but in You. 

And the way to You, Is the doing of Your Will. 

Help me to put resolutely away, Help me to keep always far off,

The things which displease You 

Even in the least degree. 

For they form a barrier between You and me, And I want no barrier.

They shut out from me some of Your love, And I need it all. 

They lead me from the path to heaven 

And I cannot find my way back alone.

I cannot climb up again to the height 

From which I plunged down. 

So that I dare not hope 

Unless You, O Lord, in Your great mercy, Will lift me up 

In the arms of Your everlasting mercy, And carry me back again

Of the divine quality of Your Mercy. 

Little wonder that souls

Penetrated through and through

A flash of that eternal light, Which is Your Truth, Made it possible for them

On a path leading to disaster; 

Let them know that sin

That to indulge self

Help me, dear Lord, always to see this truth

Help me all life to be aware my 

That happiness in the last analysis 

Is the same as holiness. 

Help me to be always as determined

In the joyful doing of Your Will. 

Nothing could be more plain

And my best interests. 

Nothing could be so foolish 

As an act of disobedience to You.

For just as surely as You are God, So sure is it 

That You are Infinitely Loving, And Infinitely Wise, And Infinitely Powerful. 

How could it ever happen then 

That defiance of Your Will 

Could get me something worth the having? 

No matter what the outward appearance of things may be,

No matter how temptation May distort the facts, and confuse the mind, May I always perceive this central truth: 

Your Will is always good, Sin is always evil. 

And so, dear Lord, To my acknowledgment of Your Goodness, And my gratitude for Your gifts to me, I add now unending trust in You. 

I acknowledge, And I hope always to acknowledge, That nothing can be profitable to me

In conformity with Your Law.

To those that love God,

A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, Neither can an evil tree bring forth good fruit.

Your Will.

This then, is my free choice: 

Your Will today and tomorrow and forever.

No one acquainted with the story

The Bread of Life, 67

The Creator.

He is the Source of the holiness

Of every good and meritorious deed,

For all that You have done, O Holy Spirit of God,

Since the beginning of the human race, And especially

During the nineteen centuries of Christian history,

For the Apostles and the Martyrs, The Confessors and the Virgins,

That has shone into human minds, And for all the high ideals 

That have inspired human hearts — 

The fruit of Your influence upon men. 

And now I beg You to bestow on me,

Give me more Hope. 

Give me more Love.

Dear Lord, I know I should be mindful 

Of the presence of the Holy Spirit Everywhere. 

In Church, especially, I am reminded of His presence

By the sight of the Tabernacle 

And by the little glowing flame before the Altar. 

Then again, many a time, Face to face with nature, I come upon the manifestations 

Of His Power . . . His Wisdom... His Beauty.. 

Sunset, and flowers, and the sea, And moonlight, and morning star, Are so many reminders 

Of God beneath the surface, The Great Reality sustaining all, Holding the universe in the hollow of His hand. 

Often alone, undistracted by men, Untroubled by things,

I look into my soul, and find Him there, The Hidden God, Ever good and patient towards me, Ever gentle and loving and divinely merciful, And infinitely generous. 

And so, day by day, going about my duties,

I am reminded that He is here - As I pass along city street, or country road, Walking or driving, or being driven, As I stand in shop or railroad station,

As I enter schoolroom, parlor, chapel, Or dining hall, or recreation room 

Wherever I am, He is also,

Everywhere and always present, Ever and always the same, Infinitely Wise, Infinitely Kind, 

Infinitely Patient and Helpful and Loving. 

O Holy Spirit, Let me never forget Your Presence: 

Never fail to remember Your goodness!

Since in every place I am reminded of You, In every place will I worship You.

Since you are present every moment, At every moment I will remember You, For every place is Your place, And every moment, Your moment. 

The whole earth is Your temple. 

All time is Yours. 

And my song of thanksgiving, My hymn of praise, My act of adoration is always called for. 

Here and there and everywhere, Always and ever, You are my God. 

And I am ever and always Your creature, Bound to you by ties of duty, But also by my own free and loving choice. 

If there could be a place where You were not, A place wherein You might not fittingly be praised

Remembered and acknowledged,

For not even a demon, nor a lost soul, Can deny that You are the infinitely good, The absolutely perfect God. 

To me, Your creature and Your servant, Your follower and Your child, You are indeed Master, and Lord, and King, Father, and Friend, and Lover, Always and ever. 

The sun, giving light to my day, The moon, giving glory to my night, These are symbols of Your loveliness, O Beauty, ever ancient, ever new! 

May I always be

O God, all holy and all gracious, O God, my love, my life, Let me never forget You, At any time or in any place!

Dear Lord, I fear there are few souls 

Who constantly remember

The Indwelling Holy Spirit. 

Far too few, indeed! 

Here and there, a great saint, Now and again, some simple, loving, obscure disciple. 

But by the many, He is neglected and forgotten; 

Just as we, to our own great shame, So often forget You, our Lord, Waiting in the Tabernacle, Present on the Altar. 

But the consciousness 

Of that Divine Presence in the soul,

The gracious influence of the Indwelling Spirit, Make those who remember it 

Grow steadily more and more like You.

In their lives one sees ordinary men and women 

Reflecting the sublime beauty,

And the splendid wisdom, And the perfect holiness, Which belong to God Himself. 

O God, forgotten by so many! 

I will strive to remember You. 

Multitudes of men may overlook Your Presence ; 

None of them 

Can have so many reasons as I have 

To keep You in mind. 

What a long series of claims You have upon my loyalty, You, Who made me ! . . . Who redeemed me! 

Who saved me, not once, but repeatedly, From the fatal consequences 

Of my own foolish and wicked conduct. 

How could I ever forget You! 

I am humbled 

When I find You faithfully remembered 

By many men and women 

Who have not been favored by You 

As I have been.

The poor, the halt, the blind, the crippled, The deaf and the dumb, find time to remember, To praise, to give thanks. 

So doing, they put me to shame.

Others make me painfully aware of the contrast 

Between their loyalty and my fickleness, Because they, never having forfeited your friendship,

Nor lost their baptismal innocence, Are constant in the giving of thanks, Whereas, I, a relapsing sinner, Fail even to keep in mind

The many times that I have been pardoned 

Over and over again. 

Help me, dear Lord, To find a lasting source of joy 

In the thought of Your Presence within me ! open, Help me to keep the eyes of my soul 

And the ears of my soul unstopped, So that from morning until night

10. GOD'S VOICE 

Every day and every hour, dear Lord, Your Holy Spirit speaks within my soul. 

Not for a moment 

Is the Hidden God within me silent.

He teaches, He warns, He commands. 

He blesses, and soothes, and pardons. 

He communicates Heavenly secrets, And uplifts, and encourages. 

As only the willfully blind never see Him, So only the willfully deaf never hear Him. 

For He is the Voice 

Whose "Fiat" created the universe,

He is the Light which enlighteneth every man 

That cometh into the world. 

And to all He reveals the wisdom of faith, The beauty of unselfishness, The precious value of pain, The horror of sin, The great universal eternal law that "Happiness is identical with holiness."

In a word, to all who look and listen, He speaks words of Heavenly Wisdom,

He shows Himself Infinitely True, Infinitely Beautiful, Infinitely Good.

This then is true knowledge, This is life eternal, To hear and to heed the voice of the Holy Ghost 

Speaking within the soul. 

O God within me, I cannot realize that I have been thus blind, Thus deaf.

As I think of the opportunities I have lost, I am filled with regret. 

As I recall Your patient kindness with me, I am overcome with shame. 

Help me to be no longer dull, foolish, Reckless, ungrateful. 

With Your aid, I undertake in the future 

To remember Your Presence, To heed Your Voice,

To accept Your Judgments, To look at life through Your eyes, And to measure values in the light of Eternity.

Thus and only thus, can I know the truth, Gain happiness, grow holy. 

Only thus can I fulfill my glorious destiny, To become like You. 

I believe, O Lord. . . Help my unbelief! 

You, then, I choose, O Hidden God within my soul, To be always my Guide and Counsellor, My Ruler and my King. 

O Light Divine, be my Light! 

O God, be You Yourself my Lover! 

Be You my Beloved! 

Now and in Eternity!
