GENERAL REVIEW 

The Department of Revenue is the State's major revenue collection agency. The accompanying financial statements disclose the financial condition as of June 30, 1974, for each accounting entity administered by the Department or from which the Department has received an appropriation and the results of operations in each accounting entity for the fiscal year then ended. Not accounted for in accompanying financial statements are fixed assets and some of the Department's accounts receivable. 

Because of the enterprise nature of the Liquor operations, a complete additional set of financial statements is presented immediately following those of the entire Department of 

Revenue. These statements of Liquor Division operations provide comparative data and fiscal information consistent with statements provided in prior years.

GENERAL REVIEW 

In Montana, liquors and wines are sold to the public and to other retail liquor establishments through 147 state-owned liquor stores located throughout the state. All liquor and wines are supplied to these retail outlets from the central warehouse located in Helena. In addition to the merchandising operations, the liquor division issues liquor, beer and wine licenses and permits. The State Tax Appeal Board in the Department of Administration hears appeals of the 

Department of Revenue's decisions concerning the issuance, transfer, suspension, or revocation of licenses.

Because of the enterprise nature of the liquor operations separate statements on a basis consistent with prior years are being presented to show comparability with prior years. 

These statements show combined activity of four separate accounting entities used in the Statewide Budget and Accounting System (SBAS) to account for the liquor operations. 

These statements also show information which is not included in the SBAS due to present limitations of the system and therefore do not necessarily indicate the same financial condition and results of operations as the SBAS statements presented with other Department of Revenue accounting entities' financial statements. 20
