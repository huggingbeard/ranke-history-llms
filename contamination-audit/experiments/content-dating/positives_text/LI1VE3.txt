FOREWORD 

Pennsylvania's rank among the leading agricultural States of the Nation, together with county rankings with the Commonwealth were compiled by the Pennsylvania 

Crop Reporting Service. 

These "Rankings" were developed as a result of numerous requests for this type of information from students, newspapers, farm periodicals, research specialists and others. These "Rankings" serve as quick and useful tools in studying and understanding the location and concentration of the State's principal agricultural enterprises. 

The Keystone State ranks well among the top agricultural State's in the Nation. 

In the important dairy industry, Pennsylvania ranks among the top four States. In the poultry and egg industry, Pennsylvania ranks usually among the top three States. 

Method of Ranking: Rankings for the State, as well as the counties in the 

State, were predicated upon the relationship of Pennsylvania among all other States, and the counties upon the relationship to the State totals. 

For crops, the acreage and production for 1959 served as the base. For livestock numbers, the inventory numbers as of January 1, 1960 were used. Livestock products related to the 1959 production. 

Assigning Numerical Ranking: In determining the numerical rankings for the 

State among States, and for counties within the State, where more than one county occupied the same numerical position (or, a tie), the States, or the counties were given the same numerical ranking.
