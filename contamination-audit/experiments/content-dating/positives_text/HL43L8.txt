A JOINT RESOLUTION Proposing an amendment to Section 8 of Article III of the constitution of Louisiana, to provide for annual sixty day sessions of the legislature. 

Section 1. Be it resolved by the Legislature of Louisiana, two-thirds of the members elected to each house concurring, that there shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana, for their aproval or rejection in the manner provided by law, a proposal to amend Section 8 of Article III of the constitution of Louisiana, so that the same may be made to read as follows: 

§8. Annual sessions; duration; bills and joint resolutions; vacancies

Act 773 Section 8. A. The legislature shall meet in regular session at the seat of government at 1:00 P.M. on the second Monday in May, 1973 and annually thereafter. The annual sessions thereof shall be general sessions and shall be limited to sixty days.

B. No new matter intended to have the effect of law shall be introduced or received by either branch of the legislature after midnight of the fifteenth day of its session except in case of emergency and then only by a yea and nay vote of two-thirds of the elected members. No proposition for amending the constitution shall be considered unless introduced in the legislature within the first twenty-two days of its session except in case of emergency and then only by a yea and nay vote of two-thirds of the elected members. 

C. Should a vacancy occur in either house, the governor shall order an election to fill such vacancy for the remainder of the term.

Section 2. This proposed amendment shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana at the next election for Representatives in Congress, to be held in Louisiana on the first Tuesday next following the first Monday of November, 1972.

Section 3. On the official ballot to be used at said election there shall be printed:

FOR the proposed amendment to Article III, Section 8 of the 

Louisiana Constitution, to provide for sixty day annual general sessions of the legislature. and also:

AGAINST the proposed amendment to Article III, Section 8 

of the Louisiana Constitution, to provide for sixty day annual general sessions of the legislature. 

Each elector voting on this proposition for so amending the constitution shall indicate his vote relative thereto in the manner provided by the General Election Laws of the state of Louisiana.

Affects: AVOYELLES, RAPIDES AND ST. LANDRY PARISHES ACT No. 184 By: Messrs. Blair and Friedman. 

Senate Bill No. 82. 

Proposing an amendment to Section 2 of Article XVI of the 

Constitution of Louisiana by amending Paragraph (a) thereof, to reduce the maximum annual tax which the Board of Commissioners of the Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District may levy annually on all taxable property, situated within the alluvial portions of such district subject to overflow, from five mills to four mills.

Section 1. Be it resolved by the Legislature of Louisiana, two-thirds of the members elected to each house concurring, that there shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana for their approval or rejection, in the manner provided by law, a proposition to amend Section 2 of Article XVI of the Constitution of Louisiana by amending Paragraph (a) thereof, so that the same may be made to read as follows: 

§2. District taxes; Orleans Levee District tax and refunding bonds; Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District

Section 2. (a) For the purpose of constructing and maintaining levees, levee drainage, and for all other purposes incidental thereto, the governing authority of each district, with the exception of the Board of Levee Commissioners of the Orleans Levee District and the Board of Commissioners of the Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District, may levy annually a tax not to exceed five (5) mills on the dollar on all taxable property situated within the alluvial portions of said district subject to overflow. The board of commissioners of the Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District may annually levy a tax not to exceed four (4) mills on the dollar on all taxable property situated within the alluvial portions of said district subject to overflow.

Act Section 2. This proposed amendment shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana at the next election for representatives in the legislature to be held in February of 1972.

FOR the proposed amendment to Paragraph (a), Section 2 

of Article XVI of the Louisiana Constitution, to reduce the maximum annual tax which the board of commissioners of the Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District may levy annually within the district from five mills to four mills. and also: 

AGAINST the proposed amendment to Paragraph (a), Section 2 of Article XVI of the Louisiana Constitution, to reduce the maximum annual tax which the board of commissioners of the Red River, Atchafalaya and Bayou Boeuf Levee District may levy annually within the district from five mills to four mills.

Each elector voting on said proposition for so amending the Constitution shall indicate his vote relative thereto in the manner provided by the General Election Laws of the state of Louisiana.

§53. Family court for parish of East Baton Rouge 

C. Judges; qualifications; election, classification. The legislature in the same manner as additional district judgeships are created may, from time to time, create one or more additional judgeships in the family court. Each judge is to be elected to a specific division of the family court. Such newly created judgeships shall be filled as prescribed by law. The first term of such judge or judges shall not extend beyond the term of the then existing family court's judge's or judges' term; thereafter, all family court judges' terms shall be the same and shall at all times remain identical with the terms of the district court judges within the parish. Each judge so elected must posses* the same qualifications required of district judges and shall occupy the same status as district judges.

F. Continuous session. The family court shall be in continuous session throughout the year.

Section 2. This proposed amendment shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana at the next general election for members of the state legislature in Louisiana in February, 1972.

ACT NO. 192 

Proposes an amendment in respect to the Traffic Court of 

New Orleans

Affects: ORLEANS PARISH ACT No. 192 House Bill No. 217. 

Proposing an amendment to Paragraphs (d), (e) and (f) of 

Part II of Section 94 of Article VII of the Constitution of 

Louisiana, entitled Traffic court, to authorize additional jurisdiction and authority for the Traffic Court of New Orleans, and otherwise to provide with respect to said court.

Section 1. Be it resolved by the Legislature of Louisiana, two-thirds of the members elected to each house concurring, that there shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana, for their approval or rejection in the manner provided by law, a proposal to amend Paragraphs (d), (e) and 

(f) of Part II of Section 94 of Article VII of the Constitution of Louisiana so that the same may be made to read as follows:

II. Traffic court 

$94. New Orleans; municipal and traffic courts; personnel; jurisdiction; appeals

Section 94. 

By: Mr. T. A. Casey. 

(d) Each of said judges shall receive a salary as is now provided by law, payable monthly by the city of New Orleans, 12

Act on his own warrant, or as may be augmented by the city of 

New Orleans. (e) The jurisdiction of said court shall extend to the trial of offenses against the ordinances of the city of New Orleans regulating traffic within said city of New Orleans. The jurisdiction of said court shall further extend to the trial of offenses involving traffic and the regulation thereof punishable by state statute including violations of the Criminal Code of Louisiana involving traffic except as hereinafter provided. 

The jurisdiction over state traffic offenses shall be concurrent with the Criminal District Court for the Parish of Orleans. In addition, every prosecution in the Traffic Court of 

New Orleans under state law shall be filed in said court by affidavit under the provision of state law defining said offense and such prosecution shall be brought by the city attorney of New Orleans. The legislature may enact laws conferring additional jurisdiction upon said court for the trial of violations relating to street and highway regulatory laws and such other state laws as relate to the operation of a vehicle. 

The said traffic court shall have no other jurisdiction, and shall not have jurisdiction over the trial of any state offense that now or hereafter may require a trial by jury. (f) The procedure in the traffic court and the trial of all cases therein shall be as now provided by law governing such court. Said court shall have the power to adopt such rules and regulations governing the operation thereof as may be necessary for the proper functioning of the court. The court shall have the right to issue all necessary writs and orders to carry out its jurisdiction.

Section 2. All laws or parts of laws including portions of this constitution contradictory hereto are herewith repealed. 

Section 3. This proposed amendment shall be submitted to the electors of the state of Louisiana at the next election for members of the legislature, to be held on February 1, 1972.

Section 4. On the official ballot to be used at the said election there shall be printed:
