HOW TO MAKE PROSPERITY NOW 

WE are living through a period that has brought to the people of the United States new records in business failures, in bank failures, in falls of value structures both in commodities and in securities, and, as well, serious value declines in real estate and in practically all our capital holdings. Our general opportunities for securing profits in business operations of all kinds have been most seriously abridged. Millions of our workers are unemployed. Our governmental and charitable agencies have been called upon to relieve conditions of widespread suffering and have had to operate so as to prevent the actual starvation of many in our midst. We have gone through all this burdened with the heaviest taxes in our history, to which greater burdens are constantly being added. We have been expected to believe the statement repeated over and over again for more than a year-"Prosperity is just around the corner."

The wealth value destruction that we have seen throughout our own country is but part of the same process that has been going on all over the world.

The outstanding difference is that in most of the other countries the sufferings have been greater, deeper and more widespread. In some of these countries unspeakable miseries abound with insurrections, revolts and wars of recent happening or existing or threatening, all as added horrors of the situation.

An anomaly of the present which is baffling to many of our leaders is that there could possibly be any serious business disaster in the United States. 

These men, limiting themselves to a provincial viewing, refuse to believe, admit, see or deal with the actual facts of the situation. They look at and deal only with a local part of the entire structure. They reason around and around in the same limited circle. They make motions but accomplish little. We have been witnesses of a fact fighting spectacle that has been tragic in its consequences to millions of our people, and which has involved us all.

We are all well aware of the mighty strength of our nation which stands at the moment like a beneficent giant, shorn of power. Our bank deposits total more than fifty billion dollars. Our 

Federal Reserve System is the soundest money structure extant with great potentialities for the common good not yet used or even mentioned. Our gold holdings are the largest in the world. Our in-

terest rates are at extreme lows, inviting the building up of a tremendous credit structure. Our natural resources are unimpaired. All these things appear to some as being irresistible elements of a tremendous prosperity structure for our people. 

This viewpoint explains the constant cry-though of late with less vigor-"Prosperity is just around the corner."

In spite of all our assets it is estimated that ten billion dollars of wages were lost to our workers in 1930, and at the same time our businesses suffered casualties beyond all our previous experiences. 

Why was it possible for the United States to have its present wealth disaster with such great and widespread business failures and such great and widespread unemployment? The answer should be clear to us all by this time. The reason is that the United States, as far as business is concerned, is not a self contained entity, nor for that matter is any other civilized money using country. 

If our nation was a self contained entity then it would have been impossible for us to have had more than a temporary credit collapse which with all our assets would have been repaired before now in view of the extraordinary efforts made to accomplish this end. 

No one is able to fully cope with or help in solving our present wealth disease or for making

any substantial contributions for our future prosperity until the realization comes crystal clear that factors of world proportions make or break our prosperity no matter what our assets may be. This realization rests upon first principles. 

It is because we have the power to shape these world factors which affect our prosperity that we have the remedy for our present crisis. It is in the ability to extend our horizon and to view world wide the business interests of humanity that our business salvation is made possible. We are seeing the light when we view with deep concern and with a helpful attitude any business catastrophe in any part of the world, and are able to see in every such catastrophe eventual harm to all our businesses. 

Our viewpoint is correct when we look at all humans everywhere as our neighbors, and further see all our neighbors as our customers or as customers of our customers. We all live upon the services of each other in a world sense, not merely in a local sense.

It helps our farmers, manufacturers, transporters, and distributors but little in a business way if they have good bank balances and have good facilities for service when the buying power of their own customers and of their customers' customers has been cut in half or entirely depleted. Add to this situation which obtains at present the further fact

that large percentages of goods and services are selling at less than cost and we face not only a loss of profits but as well a destruction of businesses. 

When profits cease a business dies. It is high time for us to discuss and consider in a comprehensive way buying power and its destruction if we are to build up a new structure of profitable business and build up thereby a new structure of prosperity for our people.

While it is self evident that we have been stricken with a wealth disease of world wide proportions that has carried and left poverty and suffering in all nations, yet the realities of the situation have been obscured, suppressed, or glossed over with us as a matter of public policy. No recognition has been given to the fact that we are participants in a world disaster, instead our troubles are discussed solely as of local origin and influence and the situation has been continually held out as a slight temporary business disturbance to be ignored or to be passed over lightly with a laughing comment indicating an attitude of blase confidence that is without foundation.

It would be difficult to point out or imagine any period of history that reveals a deliberate program of action more juvenile or dangerous than that pursued in our midst when millions of our workers and their families are suffering the consequence

of unemployment and we are expected to ignore, smile at, or pass off with a laughing jest such a serious situation of outstanding public concern. The stern demand of the moment is for a return to reason.

Some of those in control of the shaping of our material destinies have seen fit to discount the intelligence, courage, and ability of the American people to understand and solve our common problems. The sufferings and perils of the situation have been increased by the widespread and stupid endeavor for more than a year to fix firmly in all our minds the absolutely erroneous idea that we were going through a simple depression. This was about as sane as telling a person who has pneumonia that a simple cold is his only trouble and that this will cure itself shortly. Such action plainly is not a sensible way to handle a serious situation. 

The carrying forward of propaganda calculated to disarm the public mind by substituting optimistic blindness in place of common sense and the further efforts which sought the suppression or rose coloring of essential and necessary information for the public to have, were all parts of a moronic campaign which has accomplished its natural, ultimate, and only possible objective. Public confidence has been destroyed to an immeasurable degree. 

The pressing need of the moment is truth and

honesty. The obvious truth is that we are in a world wealth panic which from the standpoint of widespread value destruction is one of the greatest in history. The further truth as we shall see is that this world panic will not cure itself. It can be cured only by following a logical procedure of facing facts, recognizing symptoms, identifying and interpreting causes, and providing a real remedy that will bring a real cure. This is the only course of action that can possibly bring full relief for our world wealth plague. This is the only sure way to bring back material happiness to humanity. All this can be done, and the way to do it will be set forth in this presentation as we go forward carefully examining our premises step by step and then proceeding to outline a program of action by which there can be inaugurated as a matter of cause and effect the greatest era of prosperity in the annals of humanity.

The time has come for the American people to act upon the matter of helpless and misguiding leadership in our present crisis. There is real work to be done, and if our present leaders are unable to do it, then we must get those who can. In the meantime, we must set to work ourselves. We have a job to do that must be done intelligently. This means that we must concentrate and work together. 

We must determine what has to be done and how

to do it if the damages of the storm are to be properly repaired and things are to be righted correctly.

We must, first of all, start in the right direction or all our work will be merely makeshift efforts. 

We do this first by facing the realization that we are in a world panic. Secondly, we face the necessity of joining our efforts with the peoples of other countries upon a world wide course of action to cure the diseased parts of the world wealth structure that is causing such tremendous havoc in all parts of the world. We face, thirdly, the requirement of correct and speedy action because, obviously, the continuance of the present world panic carries certain potential threats of further fearful consequences far flung in their operations. It is important, fourthly, that we consider with the utmost care the aftermath of the present panic and determine the best way to handle it. 

An aftermath is sometimes far more terrible in action than the panic that produced it. Certain aftermaths in the past have brought about great upheavals in the social order and have carried poverty and misery to succeeding generations of people who in their sufferings eventually lost sight of the original source of their troubles. Our present panic has an aftermath that is pregnant with terrible potentialities, as we shall see when we con-

sider the causes from which both the panic and its aftermath result.

We are fortunately in a position to quickly and vigorously check the present world panic and its aftermath. We can do this by a world wide cooperation that faces realities frankly and fearlessly and that deals honestly, justly, and sympathetically with the rights of all humanity in all parts of the world.

This is the plain and common sense way to peace and prosperity. This way is available for our use and awaits our action. If man, however, chooses to avoid this path for any reason, then the present panic and its aftermath must go on as man's decree against himself. 

Wheat is not going to five cents a bushel. Silver is not going to five cents an ounce. Other values are not going to similar points of almost complete destruction. Certain value plateaus will fight further declines. Moreover, a limited and crippled prosperity structure is being created from existing factors to aid man. Such efforts, however, can bring only limited and crippled results with considerable suffering. Healthy prosperity structures are built only when healthy essential factors are available and used. The controlling factor which shows clearly in the trend will act against us all until it is changed to act for us all. Man is at the cross roads

and may choose the way to future poverty or the way to future prosperity. It is not a blind choice if we will but note the path and shape the working factors of the situation. 

Straight thinking, straight talking, and straight acting are the sane commands of the day. He who closes his own eyes or seeks to close the eyes of others to the actual facts of the present can hope to accomplish nothing more, no matter how high his motives, than to increase the disaster. Too much precious time has already been lost evading, dodging, and making light of a serious world malady.

We are living in a period that demands the best in progress from men of conservative and constructive vision and action. It is just such a period as called forth the best efforts of the Fathers of our country to serve the progress not only of our own interests but, as well, the interests of humanity in general. It is a time to weigh carefully both assets and liabilities and to note both the way and the pitfalls of the way. We are a living part of an event of the centuries.

Standpatters, radicals, propagandists, and theorists in our midst cannot help us in this crisis but they can and may seriously impede us. It is difficult to judge who have held back more the progress of the American people, whether it has been the

standpatters who have been mentally dead to all conservative progress or whether it has been the radicals who have been mentally unaware of the practical. 

Other countries furnish us with examples of progress destroyers, some like and some unlike our own. Moreover, the further we look abroad the more we are stunned by the sufferings of large areas. One of the main causes becomes apparent when we see virulent types of misery makers at work as persistent and constant menaces of widespread evils. We see these busy disciples of blind greed and adepts of Machiavellian strategy stealthily operating spiderlike with a cold calculating cunning that is aimed to serve and is able to serve only the ends of special interests. We see them dissemble under a cloak of subtle, smooth, polite, and beguiling manner and language as they seek to disarm and then pilfer all honest efforts that seek to serve humanity. Much of the misery of mankind is due to the machinations of these over-shrewd gentlemen who live to outwit their fellows, and who would be the last to sense that in the long run they outwit themselves, because, to speak in their own language, profits can be gained more surely and more permanently by building up the prosperity of others than by tearing prosperity down or holding it back.

The influences and powers of the Machiavellians are great in shaping the destinies of humanity. 

Such leaders constantly strive to form alliances to suppress the rights and control the destinies of other peoples. The American people sensing these things have very conservatively followed the advice of Washington to avoid entangling foreign alliances. Our statesmen sometimes forget this warning, but our people never forget it. These selfish schemers cannot help us in this crisis, but they can and almost certainly will try to seriously impede us. If they act thus at a time when millions are suffering and in need, then their punishment should be made a matter of certainty as a long delayed example to the world of the proper reward for duplicity.

The American people are ready now as always to co-operate with all nations for the common good of humanity. The mighty power of our great nation rests in the public opinion of our people. We favor constructive action for all nations, but we refuse to engage in any destructive or suppressive action to any people.

The time has come to address the common sense of the American people, and through them the common sense of the people of the world. We 

Americans have never yet failed to eventually solve our public problems in the right way when the will

of our fully informed people has had full opportunity to express itself on a matter of common interest.

When our leaders fail us in a crisis like this and stand helpless before us while millions of humans throughout the world, through no fault of their own, are compelled to take food or doles from the hands of charity or starve, then a serious situation is at hand that calls for serious attention and the time for safe and sane general action has arrived. 

Now we must revive and call together again the Demos, as in former times, for careful consideration, for progressive action, and to provide the means for our safety from those dangers to which a helpless leadership has left us exposed. This we are compelled to do that we may be spared the consequences of a further spreading of outbreaks of thoughtless violence, the source of which lies in those millions whose confidence has been completely shattered, and who face the future with despair. To these latter we say, 'Stop and listen. 

We bring you sincere hope and a real remedy to cure our common woes.'

Now that the Demos is in session let us consider our situation and its perils and let us determine what it is that we must cure and how it is that we shall revitalize our material well being. 

We see millions unemployed because the major-

ity of businesses, both big and small, and in almost every line throughout the civilized world, now have lessened demand for their goods or services. We see the general destruction of the value structure is so complete that few businesses can get back, figuratively speaking, a new dollar for any dollar put into the business. There is a double peril here, one to the unemployed, the other to the business. 

Every business, large or small, that is unprofitable becomes thereby self-consuming, and if this situation continues long enough the business eats itself out of existence.

This heavy stream of business failures throughout the world must be stopped, not by a dam as some are futilely attempting, but instead the flow must be stopped at the source, thus drying up the stream. We must protect all businesses, big and small. In that path lies our way to safety. 

Another menace of our present calamity is the immense burden of national and international bonded debt that has piled up all over the world since 1914. These great debts were based upon values existing at the time they were incurred, and every fall in the value structure swells this burden in the opposite direction.

A further menace is the heavy weight of taxes pressing down upon people all over the world carrying stresses that may well develop repudiations,

renunciations, and despair. This situation may easily break into further revolutions and wars. It is easy to reason that a revolt to throw off heavy taxes has everything to gain and nothing to lose, this in spite of the fact that the penalty for such action is ruin.

A still further outstanding peril of the present that cannot be ignored is the claim that our world leaders are doing nothing in this world panic because we are simply victims of a diabolical plot of capitalists to reduce us all to a state of debt slavery. There is just enough shrewd diagnosis in this statement to make certain money powers wince, as we shall see later. This is undisguised Communistic propaganda that cunningly seeks to divide us in this time of our great distress. No Communistic demagogue can hope to mislead any real American with such a statement no matter how great our perils. Such statements, however, may unfortunately find ready belief in some countries where educational opportunities have been limited and where despair will grasp at any straw that offers even the faintest hope.

Let us all remember in our deepest troubles that in Communistic Russia there has been and is now more real suffering than in any other part of the world. We have under our Capitalistic form of society, so-called, a simple and effective remedy for

our present troubles which is set forth here and which will bring the world a greater and more widespread prosperity than ever before. Communism can carry no such message of hope to its suffering millions. It lacks the foundation upon which to base such a hope.

Communism in Russia is primarily a transitory and impossible experiment in Atheism that carries the seeds of self-destruction. Its wealth system is a secondary adjunct to the main issue that permits neither freedom of personal action nor freedom of wealth action. Communism has no appeal for any real American. We live with and deal in positives, not negatives. Our motto is the same now as it was in the beginning with our Fathers, "In God We Trust."

Let no one be asleep to the dangers of Communism, for though it offers no solution and no solace for any country in our present state of world misery, yet there can be no question but that Communism or Socialism in some forms will win many new converts and many new followers throughout the world from our surface thinkers, an indication of which is shown by the steadily growing movements of this kind throughout various countries at present. Inevitable conflicts will result in the near future from this political situation unless a remedy is quickly secured and

applied, by which our world wealth structure is restored to a vigorous and firm health before these Communistic and Socialistic movements gather further strength and increased momentum to carry these movements to new heights and to gain further new political control in certain countries. 

He reads the historical records of humanity, and the signs of the times superficially who does not realize that the terrible pains and sufferings of this world panic have gone so deep and so wide and to so many millions that as a consequence the Capitalistic system in its present form is now indicted before the Bar of public opinion. The sufferers from our present grim world tragedy who are suffering through no fault of their own are already challenging the forms of our present wealth system that not only brings about but permits such suffering. Drastic and speedy action, conservative or radical, sane or insane, is inevitable to change not only the present situation of distress, but in addition to prevent as far as possible its recurrence. Millions of men are thinking today not only in the terms of bread and butter, but even more in terms of preserving their own self respect and the self respect of their families. 

Many leaders of many nations now stand abashed at their inability to offer any real reason or any real remedy for the present woes of their

own people. We may also state that these same leaders not only incorrectly thought, talked, planned, and acted provincially before the disaster but with equal incorrectness, they are doing the same things now in striving to find a remedy for the disaster as they discuss and deal with symptoms instead of with causes. Each one of these is looking for a remedy that may be applied solely to relieve the ills of his own. These observations do not apply to all the shapers of our material destinies. They do not apply to those whose greed governed their course of action regardless of the frightful consequences that had to come in the wake of their greed.

It is highly important in this time of common distress that we determine why those of our world leaders who actually tried to help us and failed have so acted, and that we further ascertain the real reason why they took the wrong paths and took us to the depths.

The helplessness of our world leaders to provide a convincing reason and a real remedy for our woes has been due to the fact that they have lacked advisors equipped with the proper methods to diagnose and cure the present wealth structure plague which has swept through the world and stricken high and low.

Our present wealth plague is similar in a way

to former health plagues which swept through the world and carried disaster to millions of all classes. 

Leaders of world thought and action were as helpless then in a health disaster as they are now in a wealth disaster.

Up to a comparatively recent time the health doctors held that disease was a matter of spontaneous generation. This was a philosophy that was both erroneous and lacking in a technique of action.

They had no plan of attack that would get results. 

When a health plague came then, carrying its dread results far and wide, the leaders were helpless for the simple reason that their health doctors were helpless. Then came Pasteur with his new science and technique based on the control of germs and germ environment. This science and technique was carried forward by Lister, Koch, and others to serve the welfare of humanity. 

Since the coming of Pasteur with his science and technique of action which supplanted the old fallacious philosophy, our world leaders have been able to call upon doctors equipped and ready to bandle efficiently any threatening health disaster. 

Results since then have justified the confidence, support, and co-operation of all intelligent people everywhere, and the general and continued provisions for the safety of our health we now accept simply as a matter of course. The time that our

world leaders have failed us on serious health matters has passed. The health miseries of the past have given way under the scientific cause treatment of the present.

Our leaders of world thought and action have lacked a plan of attack that would get results in our present wealth plague. They have had to rely upon the old wealth doctors called Economists. 

These wealth doctors, like the old health doctors, have a philosophy something similar to spontaneous generation as being the cause of wealth diseases. Their best remedy for hard times is to lower wages. Their remedy for handling a business panic is to increase poverty. Selfishness, greed, and conflict are the outstanding idols upon their altars. 

These they claim are the figures that it is necessary for us to embrace and follow for success in the wealth field.

Because Economics is a philosophy that is lacking both in a science and in a technique of action, the Economists are unable to make a diagnosis that reveals the causes of the wealth plague. It follows, of course, that they are unable to provide a remedy, and so the disaster has gone on unchecked. They chanted for over a year, "Things will be better next week." Their tune then changed to "Things will be better next month." Later in a minor key we heard, "Things will be better next

year." The Economists have tried to help us by guessing about symptoms, but with no confidence because they actually stand in awe and fear of these symptoms. They say in effect man builds a structure but after it is built it runs the man instead of the man running it. In fact, the man cannot run the structure he has built. It is in accordance with this philosophy of the force of things that they drive fear and terror into the hearts of their followers by the teaching that the forces of things are as irresistible as are the forces of nature, and that nothing can be done to stop or control these forces. This fallacious theory unfortunately has rather widespread acceptance on faith. 

This philosophy of theirs sums up to "Let things alone and let things take care of themselves." This is the old "laissez faire" doctrine of the Physiocrats who helped to bring on the French Revolution. It was at the shrine of the Physiocrats that the founder of Economics, a moral philosopher who never had a single day's experience in the worka-day world, received his basic tenets, which he later expounded as Economics a wealth philosophy which basically is the same now as it was in the beginning. As a matter of fact, as we shall see later, not only are we in the midst of a world wealth panic, but we are also in the midst of a world wealth war which is going on right under the

eyes of the Economists and not a single one of them has launched or led an attack against this terrible monetary onslaught which has already brought death to millions and terrible disaster, it is estimated, to about a billion souls. These are the direct sufferers of this world wealth war, and we are, as the indirect sufferers amongst the second billion of the world's inhabitants, reaping the harvest of woes which have been set forth here and which we are considering.

Is it not an amazing fact that in spite of the great progress made in so many fields by man by reason of his advancement in ideas, thoughts, strategy, and action, that the clouds of mystery so completely enshroud our wealth structures in their operations that we are constantly getting erroneous impressions or distorted pictures presented to us of what is actually taking place in the wealth field.

There is a reason, of course, for all this. Welogy, the new science of business and wealth making, sets it forth in illustrating the technique of shaping operating policies in business organizations. Welogy demonstrates that the destinies of man are shaped and controlled most effectively by those who operate in the field of apperception rather than by those who operate simply in the field of perception. 

When we interpret and realize the full significance

of this truth of Welogy we are then in a position to see why it is that the Machiavellians are able to upset our wealth structures right out in the open and to pillage humanity from time to time as modern brigands while the public, unconscious of what is going on, do nothing about it. The public for their own welfare and safety must know all about this situation.

The pirates of the past seized physical things and crudely operated in the field of perception. Their actions and the results of their actions were easily and immediately ascertained. They received general condemnation and were dealt with summarily. 

The pirates of the present adroitly seize and control abstracts, with which they cleverly operate in an area of apperception. Their actions and the results of their actions are not easily or immediately ascertainable to all. Consequently, these modern pirates have not received general condemnation and summary treatment, but instead they have received honors and praise. The Demos should decide to change all this by a full illumination and by proper action.

Is it any wonder that wealth structures of various countries go to smash repeatedly when the false philosophy of Economics flourishes as the highest and final word in handling national and international wealth matters, and the Machiavel-

lians are permitted to operate without control or check.

Is it any wonder that in our present world wealth crisis the world leaders of our material destinies stand not only helpless but stunned as the Economists advise them quietly but firmly that further woes are inescapable, for the reason that wages must be cut and that people in general must accept in the future a lower standard of living, and that all this is inevitable before a firm spot can be secured upon which the wealth structure can safely rest.

Meanwhile, for public consumption there is spread a propaganda of optimism to salve and ease the increasing pains of the people as they slowly but surely fall to lower standards of living which the Economists are certain the people must not only accept but learn to endure as their state of existence for the future.

The common sense of the American people has defeated similar programs in the past and will defeat this program. We call upon the intelligence of the world to join with us now in safely and surely raising the standards of living in all nations as a world wide movement to raise the civilization of the world from our present disaster as one of the outstanding contributions of this generation. 

The Demos may well launch such a program of

action as worthy of the fullest and best efforts of all peoples of all countries, for in this plan is carried not only a drive for the general increase of material happiness everywhere, but as well the most practical program of action yet devised to establish peace within troubled countries and between the nations of the world.

The wealth progress of the American people in the past has come in no small part as a consequence of ignoring Economists and their teachings. Our great basic and well established principle of protection could never have been set in operation had our Fathers acted otherwise, for every real Economist must condemn and does condemn that principle of protection, which, when we have used it properly, has brought us great material blessings. This same principle holds further great potential benefits which we may develop for the future. 

The peoples of the various countries throughout the world are not as helpless in this present wealth crisis as the Economists would have us believe. 

Moreover, we are no longer dependent upon Economists to guide us to new depths and to greater miseries with a fallacious philosophy. We now have a science and technique of business which enables us to make diagnoses and to perform operations in businesses in the wealth field with the same degree

of certainty and success as we secure at present in the health field.

We now have a business science and technique called Welogy which sets forth the obvious truth and principle that the germs of businesses are movements, and that as we control movements and the essence and the environments of movements we secure just as complete a control in handling businesses as doctors since Pasteur have gained in handling health. 

The best proof of the worth of any science comes from an actual demonstration that reveals what it can accomplish. We, therefore, call for such a demonstration of Welogy now, right here before us in the Demos. We do not want a demonstration that is sc professional in its language and in its nature that after it is all over we are left confused and unable to understand what it all means. We know that doctors, professionally trained, must conduct actual health operations, and that professional training to a similar degree is plainly necessary for the successful performance of delicate business operations. 

We shall be able to make up our minds as to the worth of Welogy to us by a simple demonstration that deals with the large outstanding factors of the situation, and which leaves the details for later professional handling.

Let us carry on our Welogy demonstration to-

gether. Let us first of all identify the world panic as a world of sick businesses. Let us follow Welogy's teaching that when one, many, or all businesses are sick then a study of the factors and the germs of business, namely, movements will always reveal the causes. It cannot be otherwise because business reduced to its simplest terms consists of these factors and their forces, namely; men, materials, and money, and the movements of each operating in the fields of apperception and perception. This is a tested technique now used in the actual work of making accurate diagnoses of all business ailments whereby new methods and means are set up for making new and greater profits in a business. The world needs now another contribution to its material welfare the same as California brought to us. We are ready and able to supply this need. 

We know that one billion six hundred million people are in dire and immediate need of more honest, sound and hard money to handle their own business and to do business with us, and we further know that a large number of these people are distressed with money that has been deliberately crippled.

Welogy recognizes the realities of the world panic situation, both in its diagnosis and in its remedy. Welogy sums up the situation as a lack of money movement power in the world of business to move men and materials. Welogy prescribes first the restoration of the money use value of silver, and secondly the creation of more hard, sound, and honest money to insure a decent minimum standard

of living throughout the world. These accomplishments may be effected by one simple, sound, and sane proceeding.

Here is the remedy by the use of which we are able to re-shape the material destinies of humanity for the future – "Monetize silver throughout the world, make it on its bullion value basis equal in standing with gold in the payment of all national and international trade and governmental transactions."

The moment that this remedy is carried into action great things will happen. The money use value of silver will be restored and will gradually assert itself in an increased buying power. The demonetization of silver will be past history. The people everywhere will have more, hard, sound, and honest money to move men and materials in and among all nations and these movements will inevitably develop to such world-wide proportions as to amaze all as undreamed of prosperity comes to all. This picture is not overdrawn. It is a recital of the obvious. It is stating simply a matter of cause and effect. 

The moment that silver is monetized everywhere, is the moment that the troubles of the pres-

ent world money disaster and its aftermath will start to fade out and a new era of peace and prosperity, the greatest in the annals of humanity, will be started for all nations.

The world-wide demand for silver for money use will increase its value so that it will gradually rise to over a dollar an ounce. Governments will profit by silver purchases and their financial obligations will be thereby reduced. Consuming power will increase proportionately with buying power and movements of men and of materials will be worldwide as humanity with this new source of power goes forward to a new and higher standard of living than ever has obtained before.

Let us avoid the mistakes of Rome and intelligently plan our material destinies.

Let us at last put aside the Economists as prehistoric relics of the dark past. It is time to shake off the gruesome clutchings of these poverty and misery makers with their wage cuts, their lower standards of living, their strikes and their struggles. It is time to shape and control the destinies of things instead of having things left to shape and control our destinies.

Let us follow the path that Columbus brought to Europe and that California brought to us. Let us lay a new, sound, practical and strong foundation of material security for a new and mighty world

that will increase its tempo and carry men, women and children everywhere to the greatest general plane of prosperity in history. 

Let us secure our wealth redemption by storming world leaders everywhere with an irresistible demand that silver be monetized throughout the world. The day that this is done will mark an event of the centuries. The occasion may well be celebrated everywhere as another Armistice Day of deep and full rejoicing.

The Demos is adjourned that the people may act.
