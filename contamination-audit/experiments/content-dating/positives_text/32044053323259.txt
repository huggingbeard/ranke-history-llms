RESEARCH ON THE MIDDLE EAST External Research List No. 4.26 

This publication records social science research on the Middle East currently in progress or completed during the period January through December 1966 and unpublished as of the latter date. It is one of a series of lists based on the catalogue of social science research on foreign areas and international affairs compiled by the Office of External Research from information submitted by scholars and research centers throughout the United States.

Persons desiring copies of completed research reports should address their inquiries directly to the author whose academic affiliation (department, if applicable) or home address is given in parenthesis following his name.

Data for each citation are as complete as the Office files permit. Where available, annotations, supplied by the author or prepared by the Office of External Research, follow the bibliographical information. The 

Department of State assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of the statements of fact, nor does it necessarily endorse the conclusions expressed in the annotations. 

The Office welcomes letters from scholars and research organizations, giving the current status of unfinished projects already in the External Research Catalogue and reporting new projects under way. Scholars are invited to send annotations with their research topics for inclusion in next year's lists.

Copies of these lists are sent to the libraries of universities that offer graduate degrees in the social sciences, to directors of area study programs, and to faculty members who are engaged in relevant teaching or research. The catalogue itself is available at the address below for use by students and private scholars as well as by government officials.

Reports of recently completed, published research, although not incorporated in these lists, continue to be entered in the catalogue where they are available for consultation.

In order to help cope with the critical problem of access to the rapidly expanding bibliography of social science research, the Office of External Research has begun to develop an automated processing system for data storage and retrieval. The publication of the 1967 External 

Research Lists marks the first time that this system has been used to publish the lists. The implementation of the later stages of the External Research Publishing and 

Retrieval System (XPARS) will make possible not only the mechanized preparation of the various research lists but also will provide a better means of retrieving information about studies in External Research Files. 

Further information may be obtained from the Office of External Research, Room 8733, Department of State, Washington, D.C. 20520.
