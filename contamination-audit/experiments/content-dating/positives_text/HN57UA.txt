PREFACE 

This bibliography sime to be a reasonably complete listing of the works of Timothy Alden (1771-1839), the first president and founder of Allegheny College, Head ville, Pennsylvania. It includes considerable material relating to him and to the College either directly or Mr. indirectly. While the list of works by the Rev, Alden is extensive and much larger than any compiled before, it is not exhaustive. The remainder of the bibliography is less complete, and has obvious gaps in certain places; for example, there is nothing included about Cincinnati, although he lived there for more than a year. Practically nothing has been done with newspapers as sources. 

Although in the course of compilation a straight alphabetical author arrangement was used, this bibliography has been classified to give unity and coherence to the information. The list of works by Timothy Alden is arranged chronologically by the first date of publication or of writing. The works about Timothy Alden and the historical background of his life have been arranged chronologically as they refer to various periods of his life. The references concerning Allegheny College are given chronologically by the first date of publication or of writing.

The items to follow up and the sources

iv of information are arranged alphabetically by author. 

For each entry I have tried to supply: author (full namo with dates), full title, full imprint, paging, and a descriptive or critical annotation (excepting the sources of information). This has been possible in all but a fow Cases. Books which I have examined are noted with an asterisk (*) preceding the mumber assigned to it.

The diagonal line (/) has been used throughout to imicate brackets (℃) na they are used in cataloging and for those entries which I have been forced to copy from library catalogs. Cross references are indicated by the number of the item as well as by the common designation of the book.

The location of copies has been given for practically every item included. Much of this information has been gathered from printed library estalogs, and so contains the possibility of error in that the library referred to may no longer possess a particular item. 

The index contains entries for authors, titles, and a fow subjects, while information given in the amotations has been indexed also. The numbers in the index refer to the number of the item and not to the pare. 

Although the regularly numbered itoms had been collected and arranged, only the section of works by Timothy Alden had been typed when the bibliography was presented
