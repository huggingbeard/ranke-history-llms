EVERY woman who has a son and every girl who has a sweetheart should read this astounding and sensational novel which exposes with daring frankness the ruthless cunning and contemptible intrigue now being employed by large interests to cause a new World War. 

These heartless, inhumane enemies of civilization and progress use money and lurid publicity lavishly, but their most effective weapon of inciting international distrust and of obtaining secret information is the passion-inspiring charm of a beautiful and clever woman without morals.

The Duchesse Malouines, formerly Rose Sylvestre from the upper fringe of French harlotry, now Mistress Extraordinary and Temptress Plenipotentiary, was the inflammatory lure employed by Hector Gilberg and his nefarious associates. Not since the days of the dazzling Mata Hari have Prime Ministers, Ambassadors, and Secret Envoys been subject to the alluring temptation of such a glamorously intriguing invitation to treason.
