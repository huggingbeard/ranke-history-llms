INTRODUCTION 

TO THE GROUP LEADER: 

Are you acquainted with the astonishing number of resources upon which your club members may call in planning their program? Has your group realized that there are innumerable speakers, films, demonstrations and trips in which they might be interested, and which may be enjoyed at little or no cost?

Your group may wish to concentrate upon one phase 

Should they desire to of interest during a year's pro gram. do so, there are ample faciliti es: for example, a Mothers' 

Club may wish to direct its program towards "Homemaking". 

The accompanying list points out the numerous resources upon which they may draw, ranging from speakers on Health or Housing to demonstrations of new recipes for bananas and movies on flower arrangements.

On the other hand, your club may prefer a varied program: if so, you might check through the booklet to see what might have the most appeal for them: speakers on "How to be "Charming", suggestions for Picnics or Educational Trips, Travel 

Films, etc.

We have also included, under Section II, a list of free or inexpensive courses and consultation services which are open to leaders.

-2- This booklet is primarily designed as an aid to program-planning, and to demonstrate the wealth of material available in or near Boston. The editors have attempted to include both resources which have time-honored popularity and those of lesser fame. They realize that the list is incomplete and subject to change, but hope that staff workers and volunteers will send their criticisms and new ideas in to the United Settlements
