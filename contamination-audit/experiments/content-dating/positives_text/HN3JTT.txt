INTRODUCTION 

They closed the book about witches and gnomes 

And told him where cereals grew,

Of slow waving grains, of fruits on the trees, Of flowers where clear skies are blue. 

And he said right away as he came in from play, "Please tell me more tales that are true." 

They read him bright stories day after day

Of children just like those he knew, Of learning the health rules in work and in play, And laughing the happy day through. 

And he said after while, with a twinkling smile, "Please tell me more tales that are true."

It is conceded quite generally that children in the lower grades are most interested in the experiences that are their own or that could have been theirs. Using this well-recognized fact as a basis, we present this book of health tales that are or that could have been within the child's own experience.
