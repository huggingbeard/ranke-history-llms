PREFACE 

The purposes of this small book are to bring before the people of this community the history and lore of their habitation, to help the citizenry to know more of the background of their vicinity, to give the pupils some real experience both in compiling a book and in English sentence structure, and to impress upon the people the importance and value of our school's work.

We hope to bring before the reader some of the facts relative to the rich history and lore of this little town, which lies on the Kentucky bank of the great and peaceful Ohio. Ii man lives only as he knows, the more a citizen knows of his community the more will he honor and love it. When the members of the 

1933-34 graduating classes, the chief authors of this small volume, learn more of the life and lore of their homes, they will love and honor their community and in turn make it a better place in which to live. We have a large number of citizens who are of recent arrival and who do not know the story of their town. If all can know the facts and look into the future fron the same

When we instill a knowledge of the beautiful and the noble contributions of our honored citizens, and of the efforts of the past expended in the production of the present community, we shall have a wide-awake people who are more able to meet the encroaching problems against which they must ever struggle. 

Let us all dream of the glorious pat and view the more glorious future. Can not all the churches, schools, political parties, fraternal and civic organizations abolish the fake differences if there be such, and consolidate their efforts for a better community in which to nourish their future civic spirit?
