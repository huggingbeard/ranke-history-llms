This preliminary edition of the Checklist includes only publications of the House of Delegates and the Sections of the Association. A checklist of 

Standing and Special Committee publications of the Association is in preparation and will be published in a preliminary edition in the near future. This edition of the checklist contains entries for all Association publications added to the Cromwell Library by June, 1960. 

The Library has attempted to make its collection of American Bar Association publications as complete as possible. However, it is expected that there are publications which are not in the Library and consequently will not be entered in the checklist. The Library would welcome information about such publications as well as copies of such publications. 

There are entries in the checklist which lack full citations, especially informaion on place of publication. In such cases, the Library welcomes additional bibliographical information. Any other additions, corrections, or suggestions will be welcomed as well.

The entries for publications are grouped. The heading for each group was chosen because it was considered descriptive of the publications entered under it and because there were a sufficient number of publications to create a heading. It is possible that other headings may be more appropriate. Therefore after completion of the Standing and Special Committee publications checklist, the existing headings will be edited. The headings appear on the attached pages. 962294

HEADINGS USED ADDRESSES AND ARTICLES 

Addresses delivered at meetings; some of which are reprinted from other publications; others of which are unavailable in any other form. When an alternate published source is known it is given but no attempt has been made to search each address.

BY-LAWS 

Separately published by-laws.

CONSTITUTIONS 

Separately published constitutions.

DIRECTORIES 

Listing of members which may or may not indicate committee membership also.

HANDBOOKS 

Although the publications are primarily topical law publications, any one titled Handbook is included in this heading. 

MANUALS 

Publications included in this heading are generally to be used by the section for internal operations rather than being topical law publications found in Handbooks. 

MONOGRAPHS 

Publications found under this heading are not true monographs in a bibliographical sense of a substantial work on a single topic. Publications found here are non-serial items that do not fall into any other non-serial heading. 

Many of the publications found here are pamphlets. 

PERIODICALS 

Publications that appear more frequently than once a year that do not fall into any other serial publication category such as proceedings, programs, etc. usually a section or committee organ.

PROCEEDINGS 

Proceedings of meetings, largely annual meetings.

PROGRAMS 

Usually lists of meetings planned and occasionally lists of membership and chairmanship of committees. 

(ADVANCE) PROGRAMS 

Lists of meetings planned usually distributed prior to the meetings.

RELEASES 

Primarily news releases of a public information nature.

REPORTS 

Publications that do not belong in either of the two following headings.

ii

REPORTS COMMITTEE 

Reports of Section committees to the Section or to the meeting, primarily to Annual meetings. 

REPORTS 

SECTION 

Reports of the Section to meetings, primarily to Annual meetings.

RESOLUTIONS 

Usually statements presented to Annual meetings.

ROSTERS 

Similar to directories except the committee membership is usually listed.

SYMPOSIA 

Joint meetings or meetings devoted to single topics of law.

SERIALS, TOPICAL 

Publications on topical law questions that are issued at less than periodical frequency but do not belong to any of the other serial publication headings such as reports, proceedings, etc.

Note. 

Headings may include more types of publications than the heading indicates. 

That is, if a publication contains Rosters, Committee Reports, and a Program the publication is listed under the first type of publication listed on the title page, Rosters. In this preliminary checklist, no attempt has been made to supply cross references under related headings such as a note under Committee Reports and Programs indicating that these publications are to be found under the heading Rosters. However, cross references will appear in the final edition.
