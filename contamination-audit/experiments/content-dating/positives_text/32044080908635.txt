They were silent for a while, and then young Whitney turned to Rosma, gathered her in his arms, and pressed her close against his madly beating heart. "I love you, dear," he murmured. "I'll always love you. I'll love you all my life." 

Rosma, resting contentedly against his heart, knew, somehow, that he would.

They were silent, with that sweet silence that only sweethearts know, for a while, and then they began, in low voices, to talk of future plans and the wonder of the whole thing.
