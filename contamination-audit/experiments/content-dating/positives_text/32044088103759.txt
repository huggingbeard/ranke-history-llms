WE MUST 1. Defeat the Axis powers completely. 

2. Make it impossible for the Axis to attack other nations again. 

3. Make the Axis evacuate all occupied countries outside their own definite borders. 

4. Punish with justice all persons who have been guilty of crimes, torture, starvation, slavery, looting, rape, murder. 

5. Help the occupied countries with food, clothing, finances, equipment, seed, live stock, materials drugs and medicines through Lend- Lease or otherwise, and require the Axis powers to send food, clothing and household goods to their victims so far as possible. 

6. Occupy the Axis countries, including Berlin, Rome, Tokio, and stay there until a new dependable anti-Axis government is definitely in control regardless of the time required. 

7. Require all Axis powers to return all prisoners, civilian or military, deportees, white slaves, and workers. 

8. Return all gold, machinery, railroad equipment, art treasures and other items stolen from occupied countries and redeem the worthless money they have circulated there. 

9. Dismantle all munition plants and deliver the equipment to the 

Allies where wanted, otherwise destroy it. 

10. Surrender to the Allies unconditionally all military and naval forces, with all guns, planes, tanks, ships, etc., used in war. 

11. Require the Axis to disarm completely and stay disarmed. No more conscription, military training, uniforms or marching. No military forces of any kind. Restrict aeroplane operation for 50 years by the Axis powers. 

mor 12. Require the Axis powers to renounce for the future all war, all aggression, all conquest, and the Nazi, Fascist, Jap philosophy. No more teaching of such. No more Swastika or propaganda. No conlying to their own people, or suppression of radio listeners. No control of the press, religion or radio. 

13. Establish a new League of Nations with teeth in it, an army, navy and air force, a code of international law, world court and an isternational Bill of Rights. Alternatively an alliance with the British 

Empire and those who wish to join with us. 

14. Under this new League, plan and organize a world wide economic effort to stimulate trade and peace time pursuits with the same kind of energy and co-operation as we have used for war. A moderate, not drastic, reduction in tariff barriers, and trade restrictions; and freer access to raw materials, would encourage trade and help many small countries.

15. Outlaw war once and for all as a method of settling international questions. There must be an agreement signed by the nations requiring that all questions between nations shall be settled by arbitration or submitted to the New World Organization for final settlement through a World Court. 

16. Find a land of refuge at once for the homeless and dispossessed of Europe. America does not need any immigrants now as it has too many underdigested foreigners at present. 

17. Require the Axis powers to renounce all claim to those of 

German, Italian, or Japanese origin in other countries and the persecution of their relatives. 

18. Stop inside Axis countries persecution of Jews, Catholics or those of other religious or political beliefs, and their imprisonment, robbery, torture and death. 

19. Require Japan to give up all islands in the Pacific. She fortified them against the agreed conditions. She used them to attack the Allies. Never again. 

20. All Aliens from Axis countries should be returned there unless their loyalty to their adopted country can be established. In that case they should become citizens. 

21. Require that all Quislings shall be held in or returned to the occupied countries for just treatment by their own people, or to the Allied powers including such as the Mufti of Jerusalem and Rashid 

Ali of Irak. 

22. All Axis agents, saboteurs, spys and propagandists to be punished and then sent home. 

23. Stop all attempts by Axis powers to stir up revolt, insurrection, disloyalty and enmity in other countries. 

24. Require that all land, buildings, equipment, mines, banks, etc., in any countries, stolen or taken over by the Axis powers, their companies or agents, shall be turned back at once to their rightful owners. 

25. Require that most Axis merchant ships shall be turned over to the Allied nations to replace ships destroyed by the Axis. 

26. We MUST require the Axis powers to join the New League of Nations. They should pay their share of the upkeep but should not furnish any men for the armed forces. 

27. We must change international law to provide that diplomats who abuse their immunity by acting as spies, and subversive agents shall be subject to trial. iniquity 28. We must insist that that pest hole of inquiry, torture and death operated in French Guiana as a prison colony shall be removed from the Western Hemisphere. 

29. We must require that all money, jewelry, land or other property, either in or outside the Axis countries and claimed to belong to those who are guilty of Axis crimes, shall be turned over to the Allied nations.

30. We must see that the Axis countries revoke all laws favoring war or the war parties and that all war parties shall be barred from any position of influence. 

31. We, America, Britain, Russia, China, must sell to ourselves and the world the idea that war does not pay. The Victor loses. Another victorious finish to a world war and there won't be any world left. 

32. We must notify the Neutral powers soon that they must not allow any Axis criminals, Nazis, Fascists, or Jap war party leaders to take refuge in neutral countries for they must be turned over to the 

United Nations for trial and punishment. 

33. We must continue to arrest, punish and deport Axis spies, agents and saboteurs after the war. We must not permit the formation or operation of any more corporations, societies, or groups under misleading titles, to carry on subversive actions or propaganda, nor permit foreigners or foreign governments to contribute assistance in any form to such groups. 

34. We must smash once and forever the German Cartels that have undertaken to build up and control monopolies in dyes, chemicals, synthetic rubber, patents and other essentials of modern international industry and commerce, and smash connections with American individuals and corporations. Great American corporations were duped into tying up their activities with these German Cartels, patents were exchanged, production was either fixed or entirely squelched, prices were frozen. 

Markets were divided, America to our corporations, the rest of the world to Germany. A more dishonest, unfair and diabolical scheme was never tried on business. The purpose was to make Germany and German business stronger for both peace and war. 

35. We must urge our Allies to educate and train their people for real democracy, otherwise some of them may drift into dictatorship as some did after World War 1.

WE MUST NOT 

1. We must not disarm completely. We tried that before. Hitler and the Japs cannot be trusted to keep peace. 

2. We must not allow any individuals to make a peace agreement without authority from our own government. 

3. We must not listen to any Axis peace plan except unconditional surrender. They might fool us with a fake reformation if they can, and take twenty years more to get ready for a more diabolical attack. 

4. We must not allow any single political party to have all the delegates to the peace conference, nor send any crack pot theorists. No one to represent any selfish groups. We suggest people like General 

McArthur, Cordell Hull, Wendel Wilkie, H. P. Kaiser, Queen Wil helmina, Chiang Kaichek, Winston Churchill, no isolationists, no Wheelers, Lindbergs or Hamilton Fishes. No labor racketeers. 

5. We must not at the war's end allow a few pious "softies" to persuade us to be nice to Germany and Japan. Only firm measures will keep the Axis from doing it again. 5

6. Allow political crack pots to get us into an international W.P.A. or new deal whereby the U. S. A. will commit itself to spend billions of our people's money on some wild world wide experiments. Our people are willingly paying taxes and making sacrifices to win the war, but they will not do so to reform the world on an experimental basis. 

7. We must not mix up purely national questions in the war settlement with the Axis powers. We must not confuse local questions such as religion, social problems, women, labor, national customs and economics. These are not international questions. We should not try to tell the United Nations what they should do on internal questions. 

Each of them can settle these at home without war. 8. We must not start quareling among the United Nations as to division of territory or the spoils of war, who won the war, nor future trade routes, steamship and air lines or spheres of influence. 

There should be no territorial changes more than are necessary. 

9. Allow any one to divert us from a new League of Nations on e ground that it won't work. Neither did the U. S. A. in the first attempt from 1783 to 1789. It MUST work or we are sunk. 

10. Allow enemy aliens to live in the United Nations after the ar unless they can be loyal to their adopted country, become bonafide citizens, and agree not to affiliate with alien groups, societies, propaganda or spy work in any manner. 

11. We must not allow the Axis leaders to find a haven in any neutral country. 

12. We must not accept any Dictator to run America nor allow 

America to become the Dictator of the nations. We have just had a taste of Dictators in Hitler and Mussolini. An absolute despotism ay be a good form of government in the hands of a benevolent kindly xrson, but in the hands of a mad man or cruel monster it is no good. he world went through that centuries ago many times. Democracy slower and often wasteful, but infinitely better in the long run. 

13. We must not go on increasing the public debt which will soon 

200 billion dollars. That is $1500 per person or an average of $6000 er family. To pay the interest and pay off the principal is a burden. 

14. We must not interfere in India. That is Britain's problem. mericans would resent interference in our relations with the Philipnes, Porto Rico or Alaska. 

15. We must no longer boast as a nation that we are the biggest nd best. Stop talking about "the master race," "God's chosen people,' the middle Kingdom," "Rule Brittania" and American braggadocio. All ations have some splendid qualities. 

16. We must not expect to reform the world the next week after e war ends.

We do not want revolutions nor shocking changes in ir mode of life. The world has moved upward by slow and steady volution, not by sudden application of pretty theories of impractical oliticians.

17. We must not go to a peace parley to argue religion. In the 

United Nations there are a thousand sects, Catholics, Protestants, Buddists, Mohammedans, Brahmins, Confucions. Do not mention it. 

18. We must not force upon any conquered country any officials of 

Axis sympathy, Quislings or Darlans. 

19. We must not allow the Axis powers again to move into other countries to control transportation lines, air terminals, or large land areas as they did in Central and South America to attack the Panama 

Canal or other vital spots. 

20. WE MUST NOT be fooled by the situation in Spain. 

The Franco Government is definitely committed to the Nazi-Fascist type of philosophy and sympathetic to Hitler. Spain has never recovered from her terrible Civil War. Her people are poorly fed and suffering from malnutrition, but the majority favor democracy. Franco might make any move to aid the Axis or at least furnish an asylum for the Axis leaders when they are defeated. 

21. WE MUST NEVER AGAIN allow our defences to descend to that low stage which existed in 1939. We must stop appeasing agressor nations. We must not depend even upon a new League of Nations until we are convinced that we are safe from any future Pearl Harbors. 

22. WE MUST NOT allow Hitler and his gang to fool us into a German peace by telling us that we are in danger from Bolshevism. 

Hitler spouted against Bolshevism for years until he made that peace pact with Russia in August, 1939. Then he stopped those tirades and yelled against the Jews and the democracies until he was ready on June 22, 1940, to attack Russia. Now Russia is driving the Nazi armies out and Hitler once more is holding up the scare-crow of Bolshevism. We must not allow him to fool us again. We must subdue Germany this time and stop all her atrocities.

Brazil, Canada and China, if those countries agree. Using ex-soldiers and discharged war workers until they are absorbed in peace time industry. Those extra ships, trucks, jeeps, bulldozers, planes and camp equipment could be used. The army engineers built the Alcan highway promptly and well. They can build new type dual highways anywhere.

Colonize it with refugees, orphans, and homeless from the war areas.

This would be the greatest peace promoter of them all.' Children now waste years in learning to spell English. Other languages are little better.

6. Establish a new international Bank, financed with American gold. (We have 23 billions.)

11. Adopt the Centigrade thermometer, printing the Fahrenheit gradations and titles on one side and the Centigrade on the other. In time the Centigrade could become standard. 

12. The Germans have tried to exterminate the intelligencia of 

Poland. We might send a few thousand Polish-Americans over there to help train and teach the younger Poles. Similar plans could be used in other occupied countries. 

13. We might ask the nations to agree that they will not permit their governments or people to monopolize, "corner the market" or combine with others to force a high and unwarranted price for any commodity, but shall sell freely at regular market prices to the people of all nations.

POST WAR AMERICA 

1. We should start planning, designing, and preparing for construction, machines, commodities, services of all kinds in 1943 so as to be ready with little delay when we change over from war to peace time industry. We should include a broad sensible program of public improvements. 

2. We should stop discouraging investment and business development with high taxes and burdensome restrictions. Inventors, promoters and investors will not develop a new business or extend an old one unless they can hope for a reward for the risks involved. 

No more "Cracking down on business."

3. We should let business make all the money it can, develop, expand, promote, buy materials, employ labor, pay wages, pay low taxes, pay good dividends. Then and then only put the bigger taxes if necessary on the individuals who get the money after it has passed through the business, any thinking person would rather have a good income and pay taxes out of it than to have business and employment discouraged and no jobs out of which to pay taxes. 

4. We suggest group insurance for everybody covering death, accident, sickness, doctor bills and hospitalization. Surely the health of the people is more important than the risk of fire on a building which is always insured. 

5. Most important is jobs; the secret of peace, prosperity, health, hope and happiness. Any unemployed person knows the supreme change and satisfaction which comes with a new job. It means food, shelter, clothing, a home, education, opportunity, encouragement, life. So jobs must be had in the post war period. Steady jobs at decent wages. But no more leaning on a shovel; that's out. If the government will cooperate and help support business, jobs can be provided. 

6. We suggest a simple profit sharing plan for business. Pay a fixed dividend of say 3 to 5% to the stockholders. Pay a fixed wage to employees say as of January, 1940. Then divide the rest of the profits between capital and labor on a fair basis. This would gain the support of all parties, increase co-operation and produce more profits. 

8. We suggest that a worker could in some cases be elected to a board of directors,-not some ignorant bum, but a man of age, intelligence and good sense with the interests of all parties at heart. 

9. Build a few large super ocean liners with a capacity of 5000 passengers to carry tourists to Europe at a low wholesale rate, $50 without meals. All one class, with comfort, not luxury. The ultra rich and the man in haste can go by plane, or on luxury liners. It would help ship-yards, give us passenger ships, give Americans a chance to learn. by travel and the European countries will be glad of the local business. 

10. Stop the demands of pressure groups on the government for favors and money. Millions are wasted yearly. 

11. Require those government departments in thousands of not needed employees, now loafing. departments that need them, or let them go home. is a national scandal.

This terrible waste 12. Most women in war work should go home and care for their families. Otherwise there will be many broken homes and much unhappiness. Money isn't everything and the home is the foundation of the nation. 

13. We should go back to a shorter week say 44 to 48 hours and raise the wages of that vast army of workers who got no gravy out of the war boom, but suffered from the high cost of living. 

14. We should move heaven and earth to keep industry going strong for twenty years to pay off that terrible debt. 

It will not be easy but we can do it.

15. We suggest that the size of the Social Security card be doubled to make it a card of identification. It should include a photo, finger prints, date and place of birth, weight, height, color of eyes and hair and nearest relatives and addresses. Renew it every five years. 

16. We might stop speculating or gambling in grain, meat, cotton and other necessities. Most sales are on "margin." Just put a high tax on all purely gambling sales. To buy commodities and hold them for higher prices in war time is treason. Here is a legitimate source of revenue for the government, but the brokers and gamblers may not like it. Should stocks be included? 

17. Nazis and their Axis friends may try to enter the U. S. A. after the war. They should be stopped. 

18. We could save much money for other purposes by getting rid of those political jobs and commissions where men get big salaries for doing nothing. Worthless political hacks get $5000 to $20,000 per year of the taxpayers money. Abolish those jobs. 

19. We should stop our officials from paying their political debts by appointing worthless people to public office-people who couldn't get a real job anywhere. 

20. We must not allow our country to drift into a dictatorship through control of a large number of activities by government agencies; activities which were formerly handled perfectly by private interests. 

21. We must adopt a different defence policy for peace time. Heretofore we have maintained a standing army, a navy and a few planes. 

But we had no tanks, no jeeps, no mechanized artillery, practically no up to date equipment for the army. This was in spite of the fact that it was America that invented the aeroplane and the caterpillar tread and excelled in the production of motor driven machinery. In our military maneuvers of 1939 our equipment was simply a joke. To spend millions on maintaining an army and then have no equipment is criminal; it is treason.

We neglected to make any proper use of our own inventions or develop them for defense purposes. Hiram Maxim, an American, invented the Maxim machine gun. The U. S government would have none of it. He sold it to the British government and was knighted by 

Queen Victoria. Today the modern machine gun is used by every army. 

It was six years after Wright brothers invented and flew the first plane before Congress appropriated $25,000 to buy one. The Nazis developed and used our inventions to conquer and enslave Europe and planned to do the same to us.

In view of the close call we have had and the terrible cost of the war, we should in the future spend a few millions per year in necessary developments, and build whatever experimental equipment may seem promising and have on hand a safe quantity of the better models as well as the necessary facilities for producing them promptly. This means bigger and faster planes, tanks, jeeps, motorized artillery, and even completely new items for defense. 10

We have hopes that the New League of Nations will abolish war, but until we are sure of it we should be prepared to equip our armed forces with the best that can be provided. 

22. We must provide through our government, or otherwise, substantial supplies of basic materials not produced in this country such as tin and rubber. While we had plenty of notice that war was imminent as early as 1938 and the spring of 1939, no attempt was made to stock up on vital materials until long after the war started. 

23. We must find a legal way to stop waste of time and money in our government offices and bureaus. There should be some authority who would be continually on the job to make government employees work a full day for every day's pay or get out. The same authority could also stop waste of materials. 

AIRWAYS AND BASES 

We should acquire a few extra bases in the Pacific and Western Hemisphere, but NOT "all over the world." Bases themselves are not self protecting nor self supporting. America lost Guam and Manila;

Britain lost Hong Kong and Singapore. The Dutch lost the East Indies. 

The French lost nearly everything. 

We should allow private air lines to expand just as they have done in recent years except that German, Italian and Japanese air lines should be confined to those countries and nearby airports. The development of 

American, British and Dutch air lines has been most successful. Each large country can and should encourage it's own nationals to operate local lines. Certainly Americans would hardly agree to allow lines of other countries to operate inside of the United States because we have our own system well developd.

The last thing Americans would favor would be government operated air lines either here or elsewhere unless that becomes absolutely necessary in the distant future.

THE ATLANTIC CHARTER 

We would vote for the principles in the Atlantic Charter except that paragraph three needs some clarification. It says, "They (the two countries) respect the rights of all peoples to choose the form of government under which they live." This is quite correct as regards the occupied countries of Europe, also China. But we do not believe that the 

American people would give up Alaska, Porto Rico, nor any part of the U. S. A. even if the people of that area should claim the right to choose some other form of government, nor would the American people go to war to help Malaya, India, or the Dutch East Indies secede from their present governmental connections. After the American Civil War the states that had attempted to secede were brought back into the Union. 

America helped the Philippines and Cuba to obtain their independence from Spain but that was only after they had been misruled and a settlement was made after the Spanish-American War. Such questions cannot be settled by any arbitrary rule. They can only be settled as each case arises and then on the merits of the case. 

THE GERMANS 

The writer has many friends in Germany. He likes the German people in time of peace. The Germans are nice "home people"; their life is fine. Germans have made many valuable contributions to civilization. Why then do they go off on a war rampage every few years?

In visits to Germany the writer found that the women are kept at home. Except in big cities they do not go out at night. Women do not get much education. They are told to go in for kinder, kitchen and kurch. Hitler told them to stop the kurch, so they have children and. cooking left. 13

Most Germans are a simple minded plain people. Their conversation is on ordinary subjects. Their popular songs are simple little ditties on a par with our nursery songs. Their music in beer gardens is usually like the "German band" played in the back alleys in New York, or like the local band plays in a country village here. The writer visited night places in a town of 10,000 in Germany-not a woman was there. isn't the custom," he was told. "It

Such a people, sincere though they be, are easily led by men of Hitler's type who comes along with a new philosophy based on the old concept of Germans—“the master race." He, like other prophets, offered them prosperity, glory, conquest, a great future, the spread of German Kulture throughout Europe and the world. The German people fell for it as was to be expected. A young lady in Munich in 1937 said to the writer, "You must admit that German is the best language, and German Kulture much superior to that of other countries." Other nationalities feel the same. But they don't go to war about it. 

Hitler put his doctrine over, it is in effect a new religion. He persued it just as zealous fanatics prosylitize for a new faith. The minds of the German people were fertile soil for his propaganda. Those who opposed him were quietly led away and disappeared forever. The most diabolical schemes were used to force Nazism on the whole people. 

When elections were held nobody dared to offer an opposing party or candidate. Wherever the writer went in Germany he must "Heil 

Hitler" to everybody. A silly procedure, but it kept Hitler's name on everybodies' tongue and Hitler became Chancellor by almost unanimous vote, a great national hero, almost a god.

His plans were adopted, Germany must prepare for war and conquer the world. Conscription was again adopted; men, women, boys and girls were marching, marching, marching all over Germany. The 

British consented to Germany having a navy one-third the size of Britain's.

Hitler tested out France and the British by invading the Rhineland contrary to the existing treaty. He got away with it. He invaded Austria-no resistance. He made demands on Czecho-Slovakia. Chamberlain sold the Czechs "down the river." The rest we know.

Now the Germans come to us and tell us they are still fine home loving people, and they are, millions of them. But having allowed their government under the Nazis to commit horrible crimes, it will be inadvisable to allow Germany hereafter to have freedom of action again until the other nations feel it will be safe to do so. And that goes too for the other Axis powers. We want no more of the Kultur that allows a people to become beasts and barbarians. And if Germany goes amuck again, like Carthage, she must be destroyed.

But the Allies must not and will not cheat, lie, steal, imprison the innocent, torture, starve, murder and destroy as has been done by Hitler's gang and the Japs. We will not punish radio listeners, control the press nor issue worthless money. 14

We do not believe in killing off millions of the healthiest young men and boys, maiming millions of others, and then breeding the next generations from the weaklings who were not healthy enough to be in the armed forces, thereby causing a physical decline in the human race. 

Farmers breed from the best stock only, and thereby build up the quality and strength. Humans should do the same. 

We want no more wars.

JAPAN 

According to reliable authorities the Japanese have been a cruel and terrible people for 2000 years. The wars between the clans have been bloody and the women and children were massacred. 

Lieut. Col. Clear, U. S. A., from Corregidor, who had four years in the Japanese army says: "I saw crowds of Japs murder thousands of inoffensive Koreans, I saw shreiking Korean men, women and children hacked to pieces. . . . During Kato's invasion of the mainland of 

Asia his troops decapitated 37,700 Chinese and Koreans, their ears and noses being cut off and sent back to Kioto. . . . Principals of burned school buildings allowed children to perish in the flames, but committed hari-kari because of the failure to rescue the Mikado's picture." "The child's military training begins at six years. Each yokel is decended from the gods. . . . His emperor is the son of heaven, an incarnate god. . . . Even the portrait of Hirohito is divine. I saw the recruits receive their rifles. . . . Each soldier bowed reverently before the rifle. . . . At grand maneuvers these men reeling with fatigue had marched 122 miles in 72 hours under rifle, 150 rounds of ammunition, and 40 pound pack; with four hours sleep! And now double time! The Lieutenant, who had collapsed, died in the hospital."

One of our vessels shot down a Jap plane and threw a life preserver to the crew, not a Jap would touch the lines. They preferred death to rescue and surrender. On Batan 500 Japs held a position. Our men took it. Three hundred and fifty Japs died fighting, 150 committed mass suicide."

The most difficult job for the individual everywhere is to distinguish between the true and the false. 28

When an advertisement claims that a $10.00 article is being sold for $5.00, it is usually false. Either it was not sold for $10.00 before or the store overcharged their customers terribly.

When Hitler says something, it is usually propaganda to deceive his own people or the rest of us.

When a politician spend lots of money or accumulates wealth it is evident that he is in politics for his own pocket and he is dishonest and grafting. Therefore anything he may say is likely to be a lie. 

When radio talkers tell you that a particular brand of quack medicine is so much better than anything else, one should know that he and his sponsors are getting good money out of it. Have you noticed how some radio talker will talk for years about a certain item, then change sponsors and lie about the new item? What a farce!

When an oil company tells you it's gasoline is much better than any other, they are lying, for any brand will take your car as far and as fast as the government will allow. 

When a religious fanatic tells you that he has the only true religion or philosophy, that the other two thousand sects are wrong. It is two thousand to one that he is lying.

When a union leader tells the members he is protecting "their interests" or getting them more money for less work, it is pretty certain that he is more interested in the big money he makes for himself out of it. 

Whenever any talker, writer, or leader or any concern or group get money or power out of your gullibility you can discount what they say by ninety per cent. Never believe what is said because it is printed in a book or magazine or newspaper or told over the radio or in a speech. 

Liars are all using these methods to get suckers or converts or money. 

CRIME 

Americas greatest curse is crime. We have more crime than all the rest of the world put together. Three per cent of our people are criminals. Several billions of dollars are lost yearly through crime. 

We should stop this waste. Why do we have it? Because police, lawyers, judges, juries and the law are entirely too easy on criminals. 

We have leaned over backward to be fair to criminals, thereby being unjust to law abiding citizens. 

Murderers and crooks are often not caught at all, if caught sleek lawyers for big fees or for notority get many of them off on technicalities. Judges let them out on easy bail given by professional bondsmen for criminals. They often free them because the papers or the evidence are not exactly so. Months of delay follow, juries disagree or are swayed by silly sympathy for the poor crook. 

After a small percentage finally reach prison many are pardoned by governors or parole boards under pressure from the criminal's friends. and accomplices. The criminals are turned loose to prey again upon innocent people. This whole business is a farce and ought to be stopped. 29

Lynching in the South can be stopped if the public knows that punishment will be dealt out quickly and surely by the courts. Letting a murderer live from one to two years after he is caught is ridiculous.

THE INDIVIDUAL 

Those persons who think that the peace after this war is going to bring the millennium are mistaken.

Those who think that life will be soft, easy, no work to speak of, and plenty of money from the government are fooling themselves. 

The cost of living will be high, the value of the dollar will be low. 

Life Insurance, rent, interest, income of any kind will have shrunk, measured by what it will buy.

Advice to the ordinary young man. 

Get all the education and training for your work that you can. Get a good job with a reliable concern, work hard, save your money, put it into bonds, a home, or life insurance. Vote for decent men in office. Keep yourself clean and sober. Look over a hundred girls and keep your eyes peeled for a nice clean healthy girl with decent habits and who wants a nice home, and some fine children-marry her. Don't hook up with one of those painted, worthless female bums who drink, smoke, lie, gamble and stay out all night. They are no good. 

Advice to girls is similar. 

You have two chances now for comfort in your old age. You can still save, invest and buy a little home. Besides that you may get quite an income after 65 from the Social Security. Play safe and have both. 

YOUR PERSONAL SACRIFICE 

There are millions of you so-called Christians in America. You pay homage to one who made the supreme sacrifice two thousand years ago as you say in order that the world might be better. Now we wonder just how many of you people can make a personal sacrifice for the good of your country. Can you make the following "pledge?" "I am one of many employers, managers or bosses who are getting a salary or income much larger than the average, or I am one of those few million workers who are now making as much per week as many others make in a month. "I hereby pledge that I will not seek nor accept any more income until the average pay of millions of other poor devils shall be brought up to a point where it will compare favorably with mine, and that I will use my influence to see that those who are grossly underpaid shall receive a square deal."

If a few million managers, businessmen, bosses and working men will sign that pledge, peace will reign in industry and business will BOOM. 

Will those who are most favored at present, sacrifice this much for the good of their country and their fellow men? 30
