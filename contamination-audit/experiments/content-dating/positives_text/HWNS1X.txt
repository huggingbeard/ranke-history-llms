God's creative acts, therefore God fashioned him after Himself, that he should not only bear the physical likeness of God, but that eventually through God's redemptive plan he should be like Him spiritually. (We shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is. 1 John 3:2.)

What Must I Do To Be Saved? 

OD has decreed that man shall have a life after this life, and what that life will be is determined by the account he must give of the deeds done in the body.

Therefore within the heart of every mortal being, whether he admits it or not, there is the consciousness that he will live again. As a result of that consciousness, every sane man or woman has at some time or other asked the question, How can I be saved and have eternal life?

Therefore God made salvation so simple and easy to attain that no mortal could ever stand before His throne and state that he could not meet God's demands. 

Only a loving Heavenly Father could make salvation so simple that none need be lost (God is not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance. 2 Pet. 3:9). God says, "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved."

You can accept Him in the workshop, alone in your room, out in the field, anywhere you may simply bow in humbleness before your God and accept Christ as the Lord of your life.

O sooner does a soul accept Christ as his Saviour, than Satan tries in every conceivable manner to regain such an one for his own kingdom.

His first attack is to try and place doubt in the believer's mind. Fellowship with Believers

ELLOWSHIP with believers on the part of a new convert is vitally necessary, as one coal by itself does not constitute a fire and will soon die out. When it is added to other coals, there is a continuous glow which radiates light and heat. God knew this and consequently instituted the Church. 

However, no one should get the idea that every Church is a body of believers, for we are living in the closing days of this age when there are a lot of dressed-up dudes in the pulpit and a vast number of mummies in the pew, who no longer believe in the Virgin Birth, to whom the Bible is an antiquated book, and man the creature is placed upon a pedestal and his marvelous virtues extolled since his evolution from the slime of the ocean. From such turn away. 

For any new convert to seek fellowship in such an icehouse, means spiritual death in forty-eight hours.

Far better for a few to gather together in a room in some humble home, where the Bible is upheld, and faithfully taught, for there Christ has promised to be in their midst, and where Christ is, there He will manifest His marvelous power. 

There miracles will commence to happen, the sick will be healed, men and women will be convicted of sin and be saved, and ere long a church of believers will arise with everyone on fire for God, and telling abroad the marvelous workings of His grace, until the surrounding countryside is dumbfounded at the manifest presence of the Master. Baptism 

HERE is nothing which arouses so much opposition from Satan's camp as There is which in which the Bible gets it forth. 

Baptism was instituted by God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ as an outward form to show to the world that the believer is now vitally joined to the Lord Jesus Christ by a new relationship which He Himself instituted. 

The manner in which the Bible sets forth baptism is unmistakable. Christ went down into the river Jordan and was baptized by John the Baptist. 

Again and again the Bible sets forth that we are buried with Him (Christ) in baptism. No one would think of burying a natural body by sprinkling some dirt over the corpse. Yet countless numbers of men are trying to do away with the reproach of the Gospel, desiring to make Christianity popular by merely sprinkling a few drops of water on the individual and calling it baptism, having an idea that there is a back gateway into Heaven which will allow a man to carry on in the world, maintain his popularity with the devil's crowd and still enter Heaven's pearly gates. The reason for so many defeats in the average Christian's life is because they have never come to the point where they have made a full surrender of all the faculties of their being, spirit, soul and body, to the Holy Spirit's control.
