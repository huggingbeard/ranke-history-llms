If they want to believe that the mighty scientists of this world who in making their discoveries are certainly upsetting the religious structure in its accepted sense today-if they want to believe that these men are all wrong-then let them believe it. I would not rob them of the pleasure they get out of the thoughts of millions of humans roasting and sizzling in "hell-fire" for millions of eons, even if I could.
