To all my friends

General Nuisance was written to make people laugh and forget their troubles or anything else that may be worrying them. If it makes one laugh but once and forget his troubles for a moment, it has served its purpose well. Every city and town has its Hank Lang and the readers may find some likeness to him in 

General Nuisance. The book is printed as ori ginally written there are no revisions or corrections. Some people get more pleasure in finding mistakes, than doing anything else. The 

Author wishes to make them happy also, so as to serve all.

BIOGRAPHY 

Henry Boulanger was born in Lawrence, Mass. on March 2, 1912 of Canadian-American parentage. His education consisted of a few years schooling of which many days were missed. He has been writing for the past three years and has published a book of poetry, "AFTER THE 

BLACKEST NIGHT". He has traveled and worked at more different jobs than he cares to remember but will relate them to you in his future books.
