I So in the beginning a maskéd zone 

Resumed His enigmatic face and tossed

The stars into the spheres above embossed With first despair, conceived by Man alone. 

With roar of beast and bud of bloom a crone

Was born, so named the seeds by beauty crossed, Thus nature flings her nurtured grains all glossed By He who sits upon His mighty throne. 

Returned to seed the further bounds of space, A thing ne'er planned by mind nor softened heart; 

All marked the disc on which the forms revolve. 

Even as the moon is dead so is the race Unspun as spidered web of thought, a part Returned to worlds the Two can never solve. [1]

II 

And through the night the dusk-inwoven shades 

Are steered to course of shallows near the stars;

Though ceaseless as the fleeting light, these spars 

Are tossed upon the strands recalled from Hades.

These dark-born Sons of Ham are wed with maids 

Who dance upon the mists that rise from Mars;

So flinging wreaths of garlands round the bars, A pagan light is loosed in ravelled braids.

Loose flung these tresses down the nape of neck, Loose flung the skirts of dancers round the ring; 

Like flying sparks this ribboned whirl is fleet. 

To race the fiery steeds, this jewelled beck 

Of lip to lip commands the swarthy king

And slaves around the baser rung, His feet. [2]

III 

And through the day the sun god, Ra, this gem 

Of deities must issue life of blood, Or else he gushes forth a feverish flood

Of power only central might can stem.

Thus for the sight of seeds must clouds condemn The splendrous glamour only He can brood Upon, or else the mists that cling as food 

Are born again by dint of stratagem. 

To part the clouds and pour forth streams, a glimpse

Of life beyond the death is needful gift, Or else the mold is bud without a bloom.

Still taunts of beast are cast at Him who limps Unto His throne, so nobly does He sift The dross from grains that grieve their way from womb. [3]

IV 

And on the stars the glimmering shapes of sprites

Recapture youth as dancing bound they weave The spell of mage, so might the swimmer cleave To flow of land-drawn waves, these anchorites. 

Into the night the flux of feasting rites

Compels its way until the dawn of eve 

Is nigh, so might an echoed sigh bereave

These hosts of feline orbs, but satellites. 

As naked eye is drawn towards twinkling gleam, So is the image mirrored, Precious Stone, So is the fleeting glimmer but desire. 

The one is other but in guise of dream,

For though the flame of one can ne'er atone The need that burns, these stars incept Man's fire. [4]

V And on the moon the mountained breasts of She 

Are shaded lightly, and the mists that taunt

Are delvings deep ino the spheres that flaunt 

Their mystic power, almost like dash of sea.

This nymph-like peer into the worlds to-be 

Is fraught with blindness blent with message gaunt,

So might the depth of cave be but the haunt 

Of gods, these mongers of fantastic glee.

Though light confines the spirit-form in arc And closes limitless, a vast compact 

Of death is made with birth of tautened rope. 

Each cell of Man renews its retorched spark

Like fisher thrusting line, thus to attract The bait upon the hook, this life-drawn hope. [5]

VI (To William)

Reborn the sun at dawn, this scarlet disc Of light compelling earth to be a mass All motionless and calm with beggar's gas, This web suspended like an obelisk. 

Because the rays are pillared light the whisk 

Of broom is seen propelling through the pass 

Between the tropic glory of a glass

In which the barriered face is mirrored risk. 

Inert as in a swoon of latent fire The sun is scrolled across the sky in burst 

Of flame that dashes down in ghost acclaim.

Life-giving god of might, his toil is dire 

With hidden need and fraught with frenzied thirst; 

His lips but parchéd split for thunder-game. [6]

VII Reborn the death of blackened stars when drink 

Returns to plains of salty waves, this lust

For fevered life but ghost of fevered dust, So is the destined chain a rattled link. 

A link between the contrast formed by brink And score of depth now stands in space august, Now stands a Presence doomed by mortal gust

Of gale, resounding through the curious chink. 

Resounding vast and deep the echoes sent

By messengers all donned in robes of gold, On chariots dashing down the dappled way. 

Unearthing all the treasured flashes rent

By light this earth soon sparks the story told, Retold and told again in night and day. [7]

VIII 

As space shoots through the murmured wells of time The Father Chronos stands superb, a voice 

That splits asunder giant caves to poise

The world upon its spinning disc of mime. 

Each jot of seed upon the stalks is prime 

With puerile gropes that go beyond the choice 

Of life and death, and signs that reel rejoice 

To muffle in the echoed rock's upclimb.

On rafts of rearing tufts dark waves unroll A splendour caught in webs of daring warp; 

Down shoot the stars within their emerald cage. 

Up! Up! as though the thrall now coursed through shoal; 

On! On! the stream of light that sears the harp

With vastness lit by time, this Wonder Age. [8]

IX As time shoots through the corridors of space, The mingled seeds of Man are protoplasm With bounds all limitless with restless spasm Thus motive force combines the stone-grimace. 

This carving out of toil unreels the race 

To rest on laureled wreaths of blunter chasm,

And thaws the ice to split the star-phantasm That sinks, an orb without a boundary trace. 

On shoot the marks, these myriad grains but pith Of life and death without the name of each; 

For this the waves brush back the sands and sing.

On shores of endlessness the swish is myth 

Of gods and She whom none the dream can reach,

Except in eyrie-chords, this sea on wing. [9]

So in the end a pontiff sprinkles balm 

Upon the overladen breast and tears

No more are shed, because the weighted years 

Dissolve in aura round the sun so calm.

With cast-off cloak the lordly glance of psalm 

Is given freely, not with braggard's cheers,

But more with gift of goodness only Peers 

Of Presence would in homage pay salaam.

As death is summoned by the will, so might Of life may seek the star-in-wrought apex, This inkling that transcends the future curse. 

As wells are dug, so wish is flamed to fright The skull from solving oracle of sex, Thus fathomless is He, our Universe. [ 10 ]
