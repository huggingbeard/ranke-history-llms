We have given to man the right to vote, to speak and to publish, but we have failed to provide him with the right to live. He has the right to cast his ballot, but often not the bodily strength required to take him to the polling place. He has the right to free speech, but often he is lacking in the nutrition that makes speech possible. And he may have thoughts and the power to write, but often he is too weak to think and there is no place for him to do his writing.
