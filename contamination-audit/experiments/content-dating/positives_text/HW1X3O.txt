Introduction 

YOU and I are about to begin a wonderful jour- You ney together. As we tread this path, hand in hand, you will be supremely happy, as the dazzling brilliance of the path we are treading dawns on you. Joy will be unspeakable, full, and running over. And as we walk and talk together by the way, you will be conscious of the fact that for many years you have hoped against hope that someone would show you-the path that leads to God.

That is where the path we shall tread together, leads. 

At the end of the trail, your eyes will see some of the glories-some of the burning bliss of the Realm of God. So amazingly wonderful will be this experience, that your soul will cry out as the depths of the love of 

God became known to you.

The experience which will come as a result of our little journey together, will be such that your whole earthly life will be transformed. The close association you will make with God, and the permanent consciousness of the Presence of God, which will come to you at our journey's end, will be the fulfilling of your soul's deepest longing. 

For you want to find God. Otherwise, you would not be making this little journey with me. You have sought many times, and in various places for the rich consciousness-the soul satisfying Peace, Power, Love, which only direct communion with God can bring. It is likely that, as we begin our travels together, those desires still are unfilled. You still find, deep down in the hidden recesses of your life, an indescribable longing to know the superlative beauty which invariably comes as the result of perfect, constant, blissful communion with the ineffable Power which gave Life in the first place, and which moment by moment sustains that Life in you. 

As the fulfillment of your dream comes to you, the tears will stream down your face. But they will

not be tears of sorrow. Nor of defeat. Nor of despair.

For these things do not exist where we are going. No -the tears will come to you-because you have, for perhaps the first time in your life, come to the pathway at the end of which is-GOD. 

This world has not yet found the path you and I shall tread together. For I discovered this sacred trail all alone. There are a million paths which lead to God. 

Many of them, however, are very steep. Others are tortuous. Still others wind and twist and turn around steep cliffs, up steep hillsides. The traveler along some of these paths is very apt to become discouraged, and falter by the way. 

And he may slip. He may bruise himself. And, in despair, he may decide that it is impossible to find God along those hard trails which honest, earnest theologians have sign-posted as the way to God. 

Life has many of such sign-posts along the way. 

And, undoubtedly, if sufficient perseverance, strength, courage is displayed, they all will lead to the Realm of the Spirit of God-that Realm whose glory cannot

be exceeded, by even the brightest star. That Realm which needs no light, for the Presence of God-the sacred, hallowed, mellowing Presence of God is the Light thereof. And that is where we are going. 

From early youth there has been in me, an indescribable "something" which harnessed itself to me. It would not let me go. That indescribable "something" was the Spirit of God, striving to reveal It's lovely 

Presence to me. For it drove me on. And on. And on, until one day, after traveling over the very same path 

I shall lead you along, the scintillating beauty of the glory of God come upon me.

That experience melted me. It brought to me an actual experience of the wondrous Power of Almighty 

God. It made me live for but one thing-to bring that experience to others. To take them by the hand, as I shall take you by the hand, and gently, kindly, lovingly, understandingly lead them up the pathat the end of which is-GOD.

These other trails will, undoubtedly, sooner or later, lead the honest seeker, to God. But I tried many of them. Sometimes I was lost among the maze of ritual-

ism, while at other times, God was held so far away from me by creeds, that I despaired of ever knowing the fullness of God while on this earth. 

When I would lose hope, and become despondent because of my inability to know the fullness of the Power of the Supreme and Mighty God, I would rest for a while. Perhaps it would be on some lonely mountain-side. Perhaps, by the restless, heaving sea. 

But there, no matter where it might be, I would pour out my heart, crying in soul anguish for that Eternal Light which ever lights the pathway, and which always dispels the darkness, doubt, and fear which so many of the usually trodden paths to God are cumbered with.

One sign might proclaim:-"Lo-this is the pathway to God." And millions, their hearts buoyed with an eternal hope, would start down that path. 

Alas-many would never reach the goal. 

Then another sign-post might say: "Lo-God is down this path," or perchance, still another sign-post might proclaim: "This is the only path to God—all these other paths are false and will lead you astray.'

While still quite a young man, I looked upon these sign-posts, and, in my eagerness and anxiety to find God, I travelled quite a way down many of them, only to be turned back by some bridge which was unsafe for me to cross. Some chasm I could not leap across.

Perchance, some boulder along the trail which I wasn't able to climb over or push to one side. 

And then-just when it seemed that all was hopeless, the Spirit of God came to meet me. Gently, lovingly, and patiently, the Great Master of us all said to me "Just put your hand in Mine—and come with Me I'll show you a path which, had you trodden it, would have led you to Me long ago." 

You see the Spirit of God, knowing I was hopelessly lost, had come out to meet me. 

From that point on, having complete and supreme confidence in the Power of the Spirit of God to direct me, I was shown the path which I shall lead you along. 

I have travelled this path so many times that I know it pretty well now. I have travelled it both ways, countless scores of thousands of times. First-I travel it up to God. There, in that shining Presence, I re-

dedicate myself to my life's work of bringing others to the place at the end of the trail, where God is. 

Arriving there, I rest for a few moments. Then, I retrace my footsteps, filled with the Power of the 

Spirit of God. 

You see I am leading others along this same path.

For God has seen fit to use me as a guide. He first showed me the path, then He said, taking me gently to one side:-"Look out there the fields are ripe unto the harvest-won't you, knowing the path, bring in as many as you can? Truly the harvest is great-but the reapers are few."

And so, out of a heart flowing with gratitude to 

God for ever revealing Himself to me, I give my life walking back and forth along this blessed pathway which leads to God. I come back alone. I go to God with many. So you see I know this path. You may safely trust yourself in my hands for a little while. 

After all-I am not the one who is leading you- I am but the messenger of God, sent from God to help find the blessed assurance of the Presence of Godyou right here on earth where you are. You will remember

-when I was lost, the Spirit of God came out to meet me. 

Now that same Spirit sends me out to meet you. 

And we have met. And you have signified your eagerness to enter this most wonderful of all human experiences-consciousness of God in you. You have given me your hand. I shall not let go of it. 

You can if you wish-but you won't wish to. For you will not have taken steps along this hallowed path until you know, beyond the peradventure of a doubt, that, just as God came out to meet me, God will come out to meet us both, as we travel along together. There is Great Light where God is, and it is that 

Light I see before me as I lead others into the Realm of the Spirit of God. That light shines by day-it shines by night. Just as long as I can see that Light, even had I not travelled this trail before, I should be so very sure of myself.

For the Light I see is God. And the Light that is 

God-that "Kindly Light" will lead both you and me until that glorious moment comes when, standing

in awe and amazement at the beauty which surrounds the Realm of God, we are conscious of the fact that we are "at home" with God, where we always should have been.

One sight of God will be enough. We shall be very happy and willing now, to give our lives to the beautiful work of being "guides" to God.

Never again shall we take either our eyes, our minds, or our thoughts from this Realm of ecstatic glory. For God is in the midst of this Realm, and to be with God, though still here on earth, is peace indescribable. It is Life abundant. It is joy unspeakable. 

And these things all lie at the end of the trail we are now starting together. We shall end our journey very close to the heart of God. I have made it as easy as possible for you to find the Power of God. Our journey together will be taken in six easy steps. I shall not hurry you. The trouble will be that I shall have difficulty in holding you back until you have taken each step.

There are some who can easily take three or four

steps at a time. But they might slip. Then, they would have it to do all over again. So it will be easier for us both if we just quietly take each of these six steps one at a time. Then we shall be sure to reach the top. 

May I suggest to you that you set aside a certain hour each day for your little journey down the trail that leads to God. You know-this is the most important journey you have ever taken. So much depends upon it.

If you let go of my hand, you can wander off into the darkness and that will mean retracing every step you have taken. You don't want to do that.

So shall we set aside say fifteen minutes or half an hour each day? You might make it before you retire. 

Or, better still, get up fifteen minutes earlier every morning, and let us take our little walk at that hour.

There is another suggestion which you will find will help. Be as quiet as you can while treading this path with me. Shut out as many disturbing influences as you can. For you will find before long, that while the Presence of God will make you cry out for joy, at the same time the Spirit of God in a Spirit of Infinite 

Peace will be with you.

Out of that Peace comes Power. Out of that Peace comes Love. Out of that Peace comes Strength. Out of that Strength comes overwhelming Victory. So I suggest that you keep quiet and be as undisturbed as possible while we are travelling together. 

When the Peace of God comes into your life, it will bring with it more Power than you can ever need. 

Then, filled with the Power of God through the Peace of God, you too may go out into the fields, already ripe-yes overripe-unto harvest, and then you can do what I am doing-lead men and women to God.

The Pathway to God

THE FIRST STEP Be Able to Recognize God 

HE first step we shall take will, of course, deal directly with God. That is all we are interested in. God. And God only.

When I allude to God, I allude to the Great Spirit of all Life. I mean the Intelligence which, before the earth was, existed as Life, as Power, as Intelligence, and certainly as infinite Wisdom. In other words, before there was any physical manifestation on this earth -before there was animal life, plant life, or human life-yes-before the earth had any form at all, there existed a Great Invisible Spirit which was God then, and is God now. It is the same God.

This is the only definition we need. Anything more than this may hinder instead of help us find God. On this one fact alone, we shall rest. We cannot introduce the many concepts of God which our theological systems have brought into being. These can only hinder and becloud the simple truth of the existence and character of God.

If we get the picture clearly in our minds that God is the invisible Spirit of Life first of all, and many other things after that, we shall have little trouble in discovering the greatest Spiritual Power or Force this world can ever know.

But if we wander off into creeds, beliefs, rites, rituals, etc., we shall wander off the pathway we are beginning to tread. Our search for God will end in complete failure, and we shall get hopelessly lost if we deal with any other concept of God than that advanced here. 

When you say that God is the Maker of the heavens and the earth, and all that in them is-you have said all you need to say.

So then, in our travels together, we shall at the very outset, discard any theory of God or any teaching

about God which would introduce any other picture than this.

The invisible Spirit which created this universe is all we need to discover. We want nothing less than this, and certainly there cannot be anything more. This creation has been admitted by geologists and scientists to be at least 500,000,000 years old. Some of them admit of even a far greater age. 

However that may be, we most certainly know that the creation of both the earth and man, took place a very long time ago. Certainly it took place long before there were systems of religion. These, are comparatively recent. Our own Christian religion is not yet 

2000 years old. But God—the invisible Spirit-God, created this earth millions of years before man was ever even thought of, except in the mind of God. 

To take anything man has written then, as the last and final word concerning God, we may slip away from the facts. So we shall not do that. In travelling along this wonderful pathway together, we shall make no mistake if we discard, or lay to one side, any theory of God, or any claims about God, which claims originated with man.

If God exists, and we know God does, because we exist, then we cannot get off the path if we deal direct with God. What man may say about God will be subject to error certainly, for man is full of error and mistakes. (I was before I found the exceedingly beautiful Spirit of God in my life). 

Yes the very first thing for us to do while walking along the pathway that leads to God, is to remember to deal only with God. To deal directly with God. The 

Power that made this universe, and man, certainly is great enough to reveal itself to man. It is great enough, wise enough, powerful enough, to reveal Itself to man without any aid from man.

As a matter of fact, man cannot help God reveal Himself to man, the human race, nor was there ever a time when man can add anything to the glorious revelation of the Power of God which God Himself brings to man. I can go one step farther and say to you that God never dealt with a man through anyone else. 

God did not ordain any human organization to be the agency through which Almighty God is presumed to either deliver "salvation" or anything else to the human race.

Whenever anyone finds the Power of God, that one will find it by dealing with the Great Spirit of God direct. Human approaches fail. Human approaches to 

God must ever be human approaches. But the God-approach to God-well, that is the only safe and sure method of finding God. I might go so far as to say that the Power of the Spirit of God cannot be revealed through anyone.

So then, the first thing for us to understand is that we are dealing direct with God. Human rites, rituals, creeds, etc., are all good. But they are only good in the absence of an actual and literal experience of the Power of God in human lives. When God becomes a Living 

Reality to men and women, these other things, many of them relics of a pagan past, at once disappear. They are lost. They have no worth or merit.

All they ever pretend to do anyway is to reveal God to the human race. But it is quite evident that 

God is practically unknown on the earth today. The organizations which have presumed to teach what they believe to be truths of God-well, they flourish. But it is very significant to note that while this earth is full

of systems of religion, the actual Presence and Power of Almighty God is a completely unknown factor among us.

This is not as it should be. It is evidence that these good people, while earnestly believing what they presume to teach about, are impotent to reveal the Presence of God to any of us. Most of them, however, predicate their teachings on the theory - the very human thory that God may not be known on the earth. There is no major system of religion which does not confine most of it's teachings to "the life beyond." 

These good folks tell us that we must not even try to deal direct with Almighty God.

They tell us that all that is necessary is to believe what they believe, and then, after we die, we shall perhaps know something about God.

Now, that is not what the Spirit of God revealed to me. Nor is that the pathway to God which we are taking. It may be one pathway to God-but you and I will arrive at our destination while we live. We shall find the actual Presence and Power of Almighty God, right here on the earth. We shall find it very speedily.

That once accomplished, what more can we ask? If you and I can find and use the Power of God here and now-do we not have in our hands the key which unlocks the vast storehouse of all the treasures God has? Of course we do.

But, if we are convinced that we just simply cannot get acquainted with Almighty God here in this life, then we are of all folks most miseraable. Fortunately, this theory of the absence of God, which humanity has believed for so long, is not true. It is not of God. For the whole object of this creation was to reveal to manhere and now the fullness of the Power of the Spirit of God.

When man is dead he ceases to be man. He has lived his life on this earth under the very grave misapprehension that while he was on the earth he could not know the glory, the joy, the wisdom, the power of Almighty God. If that should prove to be untrue, then what a vast number of human beings have wasted their lives.

As far as the soul of man is concerned, no religious organization on earth knows anything about the soul.

In any event, after man has died a physical death and is no longer man, the indwelling Principle while that man was alive, belongs to God. No man-no religious organization has anything to say about what happens outside of this earthly span of life. If he attempts to, he is merely rushing in where angels fear to tread.

These well-meaning theories of God have filled a certain place in the long evolution of man. But they have left the world without actual knowledge of God. 

They have demonstrated very forcibly the fact that there is much more to God than they have been willing to give God credit for revealing.

It is entirely possible that had religion, as we have known it, been able to present to this world the actual Power of God, and the Presence of God, which we shall find before we finish our little journey together, this brutal war which is decimating the human race never could have happened. 

It is completely within the bounds of reason that it is the "absentee" ideas of God held by the religious organizations of the world which are directly responsible for the world not knowing the Power of the 

Spirit of God today.

The idea of an absent God, only knowable after death, came from an age when ideas of God were very pagan and far-fetched. Inversely to the remoteness of time has been man's ascent into the temple of Spiritual knowledge and truth.

The path you are walking along with me had not been discovered then. The Power of the Spirit of God has had to make its ingress into the human mind in the ratio by which man has attained the capacity to receive and appreciate it. The Pekin Man, The Neanderthal 

Man, and The Cro-Magnon Man, all of them human beings, even as you and I, could not possibly have received or appreciated the staggering truths of the present existence of the Spirit of God. 

If we tread back the meandering pathway of human history, we find that every step in the receding process brings us to a lower plane of intelligence and a state of mind quite thoroughly encrusted with ignorance and superstition. We find then, and reason dictates that it is wrong to look to the past for true concepts of God. 

Only now, when the earth is being seared to its very heart with misery, suffering, and blood—only now-

at this very time, are the actual truths of the existence and power of Almighty God being brought to the human race. 

What satisfied humanity, 10,000 years ago, did not reveal God. The religions of the present day certainly cannot reveal God. At least they have not. And as a result of the almost complete absence of authentic knowledge of the Presence and Power of the Creator, look at the world today. Do I need to say more?

I have the most profound respect for the eleven major religious systems of this earth. But I have a more profound respect for the ability of Almighty God to reveal Himself, with all His Power, to this suffering world, and to do that now. 

If humanity discovers the Power of God as you will discover it as a result of our little journey together, may I say to you that this earth will blossom as the rose. How long do you think the present misery could last if even one jot or one tittle of the absolute Power of God were known on the earth? 

Not very long, I assure you.

So, then, the first thing incumbent upon us before we start our happy travel into the Realm of God, is

to get the correct perspective of God. Let us then, realize that the only God we are interested in knowing is the Great Invisible Spirit which created this universe, the stars above, the waters beneath, the human race, and every created thing that has life. 

Instead of looking back to the Cro-Magnon Man and the rest of the incomplete specimens, and their theologies, let's anticipate God a little bit. In any event, let us take God as we see Him through His works. 

Let us deal with a present existing God, and the first thing for us to do is to learn to recognize God when we see him.

Many today are very apt to mistake a clergyman for God. Many are inclined to make the mistake of attributing some theological building as the residence of 

God. But you and I are not travelling down that path. I did travel down it for many years until God, the infinite Spirit of God met me on the way, and, taking me by the hand, turned me completely around, and set my feet on the path which did lead to God. 

That is the path I shall lead you along. It is the only known and proven pathway to God.

Evidence of the existence of the supernatural Spirit of God is not very hard to find. Evidence of the hand of the God we are going to find is all around us. We must be blind indeed if we cannot see it. 

The shrinking violet, blooming in some crannied nook bespeaks the very Presence of God. The crocuses which are just now poking their little yellow heads above the earth round my home-these are living evidence of the existence and Presence of God. The painted pansy, decorated by the hand of God as no artist could decorate these too are living proof of the existence and Power of God.

It would not be correct to say that all of God lived in those flowers. But it would be categorically correct to state that these beautiful things are a living example of God in actual operation. For the life of those flowers is part of the Life of God. 

This Great Life manifests in a million different ways. 

The flower-realm is from God just as surely as you and I are. A different manifestation of creative Power, but the same original Creator. 

What Power do you think makes the lovely flowers

so beautiful? What Power do you think it is that, after an all-winter sleep, awakens then in the spring, giving them life enough, and grace enough, and power enough to grow again, beautifying every home fortunate enough to have them growing hard by? 

Take the pouting, painted pansy. Do you know of an artist who can cause life to flow in the almost perfect models he can make of these flowers? The reproduction can be perfect-but it will lack the life. 

No-both the life of these wonderful things, and the sheer beauty of their coloring come direct from the hand of God. The actual Life and Power of 

God is plainly visible in the growth of both violet, crocus, and pansy, and every other form of plant life. If 

God were not in those creations—they never could have life. 

So learn to recognize God when you see God. 

Do not make the very common mistake of thinking that in order to get close to God you must attend some "house of worship". God has not asked anyone to "worship" Him. The great heart of God is ever open to reveal His fullness to every created human soul. But that

is only that that soul may come to know the fullness. of the Power of God. It is only because the Creator wants us all to know the vast ocean of invisible Power instantly available to us all, not because God is removed from us all, but because God lives in us all, making each one of us living manifestations of what God can create.

The farther we travel together along this pathway, the more shall we be convinced that with every step we take, we are learning more about the actual Presence of God. And we shall discover something very unusual. 

For the revelations of the Power of the Spirit of God which will inevitably come to us, will come direct from God. 

This writer will not ask you to "believe" anything that you see, or experience in your own life. For instance—before too long, you will know, beyond any doubt, that the Spirit of God is making It's presence known to you in a very marked manner. 

Now this would happen if you were in the middle of darkest Africa, or anywhere else. The path you are now treading is quite free from any human concept

of God. It is quite free from any human teaching about God. If it is possible to bring to the whole human race, the actual Power of God, do you not see how the whole human race can be unified by and through this mellowing Power?

Religion, as we have it, and as we have had it for many thousands of years, has been directly responsible for more than one brutal war. Two different ideas about God have met and clashed. In some instances, our religious organizations have been more interested in becoming a political power than they have been about discovering the pathway that leads to God. 

So-God has been sort of left in the discard. It must not be inferred here that I mean to suggest that any major system of religion is dishonest. They just simply live in the past. Their minds are made up that the traditions of 2000 years ago, while totally incapable of proof, must be the foundation upon which must rest a religion which while well suited to the mind of man 2000 years ago, is not suited to either the mind or the need of man today. 

If civilization progresses, religion must progress

with it. If it does not, it will stagnate and die. What has become of the Pterodactyl the Dinosaur the 

Tyrannosaurus? They outlived their usefulness - and they became extinct. If the theories upon which our present religious systems are founded have outlived their usefulness, they too will stagnate and die. 

After all there is a God. That God must needs be the most vital, powerful, dynamic Spirit in the universe today. If that Spirit was great enough to bring this amazing creation into being without help from man, certainly it is great enough to bring peace, joy, happiness to the entire human race. 

To this writer, it borders on blasphemy to assume that Almighty God is powerless to crush world-disturbers and wholesale murderers. To me, that is admitting God to be impotent. Either that, or it is admitting the complete impotence of any major religious organization to demonstrate any of the Power of God to this bleeding, suffering human race. 

And this has been so since time began. This is not the first time dictators have arisen with insane dreams of conquest of the world by brute force. But never

yet has any system of religion anywhere been able to bring such mad-men to their knees through the superior 

Power of the Spirit of God.

So then, we find here in our own land, out of nearly 135,000,000, less than 15,000,000 ever attend their churches. More than that hold membership, but only that number actually attend. Here, then, do we not find complete lack of understanding of God? Certainly there is a complete absence of the Power of God. 

That absence, however, cannot be the fault of God.

It must be, then, our utter lack of authentic information about God. The concept of God prevalent on the earth today cannot be the true concept. It is not. The works speak for themselves. Where the Spirit of God is, there is freedom, there is liberty, there is peace and utter harmony. Where God is not, the very opposites manifest.

Let us then, as we travel along this new pathway to God, deal only with God, where we find Him, as we find Him, and when we find Him. Let our view not be colored by religious beliefs of the past. 

They may not be correct beliefs.

All about us, all around us we find absolute evidence of God in actual manifestation. This is not Pantheism. 

I simply state the fact that all life was originally created by God. In the human family, the only family on the earth capable of understanding the Power of God, we have vast potentialities of the Power of God in human lives.

God does not confine His acts only to the human race, however. The little mongrel dog now lying at my feet while I write to you-who do you think gives him life? Where did the life on the earth come from originally? From God. Then let us admit and agree that God not only is the original Creator, but that God lives in His creation. 

We shall find the actual and literal Power of God if we do this. Admitting that God does exist and manifest on the earth in and through all life, and granting that God must sustain all life, regardless of it's physical manifestations, we must also admit that it must be possible for the human race, in whom God manifests to a greater degree than in any of His creations, to know that life to the very full.

If man were an automaton, incapable of reasonincapable of comprehending the acts and works of God, that would be something else. But as long as the Life which is God exists on this earth, and as long as the Life of God exists in man, does not that make it completely possible for man to draw upon the Power of 

God to the very full? I think it does. In any event,

God has seen fit to use your writer as a "guide," and, as a result of the God-inspired vision brought to me, hundreds of thousands have walked along the paths you are now treading. They have found God. More glory and honor to the Power of the Spirit of God. 

For credit certainly does not belong to me.

So then on this little journey, we have learned first to recognize God wherever He is. That is everywhere. But-listen-that "everywhere" means GOD 

IS IN YOU, TOO.

THE SECOND STEP 

Cultivate the Companionship of God

UR first step along our pathway to God, disclosed the fact, very plainly evident, that God is so close to us all that we may not only find God, but may cultivate the companionship of God. And remember, the more we cultivate God; the more we come to know God, the more are we made aware of the limitless ocean of Power which is available to us, any time we feel we have need of it. That will be most of the time. With me, it's all the time. 

To have the actual Power of Almighty God instantly available, is a statement which will take a lot of digesting. You may not grasp the full significance of this

staggering statement on your first reading. But lay the book to one side. Think for a moment or two of the transcendently beautiful statement I have just made to you.

Remember first of all, what God is. The Supreme 

Creator of the universe. And you have all the Power of the Creator, available in all it's fullness to you. 

How long do you think it would take to end this war if the American grasped this statement fully? Well, the statement is absolutely true.

This wondrous path we are treading together is full of revelations from God. They are staggering in their import, to be sure. But the amazing thing about it is that these statements are not only known to me to be true, but can be proven by you to be true. 

You can, whenever you desire, call upon any attribute God has, and that attribute will respond to your needs. This means that all the indescribable beauty of 

God, all the marvelous wisdom of God, all the omnipotent Power of God - these and many more — are available to you and will be more and more available, depending upon how ardently you cultivate the companionship of God. 

---

One might be tempted to ask me at this point:- "Well, but where does the Power of God come fromhow does that Power come out of the Realm of the 

Spirit of God into my life?”

That is a logical question. The answer is just as logical. The Power of the Spirit of God does not come out of the skies into your life. There is no need of that, because, the Power of the Spirit of God already exists in your life. As a matter of fact, the Life that is God, the Power that is God, the Breath that is God-was given to you when you were born. That means that the 

Power of the Spirit of God has been living in your physical body, ever since your parents performed the physical act which made your physical birth possible. 

If this is not so-where does your life come from? 

Or perhaps you prefer to call it "nature" and let it go at that. This you may do if you care to. If you do, you will be leaving the path along which I am leading you. 

So shall we be honest with one another? Where does your invisible life come from? Who is the author of all life? Is it not Almighty God? Of course it is.

Then it must be a fact, easily proven, that so long as

this physical body of yours has life, which life came from the Author of all life-Almighty God, then, do you not see, the very life you live must of necessity be the life of Almighty God. 

And if you have the Life of Almighty God in you, do you not have in that Life, the very Powers of God also? How can Life, created by God, exist apart from 

God? It cannot. So then, it must be absolute truth that just as long as you live on this earth, the Spirit of God. or the Life of God must be your invisible Life. More than that-if this is a fact, then how come you do not have, in the Life of God in you, all the Power of 

Almighty God too? 

You do have, only, you don't know it yet. You have never been told that before, have you? It is for this reason our second step asks you to cultivate the companionship of God. 

Let me now prove to you scientifically that all the 

Powers of Almighty God live in you. If I can prove that, and you can agree with me, you have successfully covered the second step in our wondrous journey, and you are ready for the third step. The poet put it most beautifully when he said:-

"I know not where His islands reach; 

Their fronded palms in air: 

I only know I cannot drift, Beyond His love and care."

I shall show you now, on this path we are travelling, why you cannot drift beyond His love and care. The reason is that the Spirit which is God, is too close to you for that. Just as long as you live, Almighty God, with all that means, is closer to you than life itself. Let us see. And note please how this new pathway we are treading together goes straight to the heart of God. 

There are no by-paths. We deal with no man-made creeds or theologies. Instead we go direct to the 

Author of life, and we analyse the beginning of our physical life. 

And there-right at the heart of the problem, we find exactly when and where God came into your life. 

We scientifically prove beyond any question of challenge, that not only did Almighty God breathe into you the breath of Life-His Breath and His Life, but we discover that God has never withdrawn that Breath of Life from you. Therefore, just as long as that breath

of life remains in you-so also does God remain in you. 

For God is not only that breath of Life-GOD IS

YOUR LIFE. 

And long after your physical body has been placed beneath the sod, the motivating impulse which manifested in you as Life-will still be Almighty God. 

Self-existent, whether in your body or not. So then, try to cultivate the companionship of God. It will pay. 

It will put at your disposal such an ocean of the Power of God that you will marvel why someone did not see this amazing truth long ago.

We wandered a bit, but we'll come back. Your parents cultivated each others companionship, just as I am asking you to cultivate the companionship of God. 

They met they loved they married - and nine 

months later you came along.

Now just exactly what was it your parents did to bring you into physical existence? How far did they go, and how far could they not go. Well we know that after marriage, they made a physical union, and thus made it possible for two different kinds of physical life-germs to meet. And that's all they did. That's as far as they ever went.

They made it physically possible for the male and female physical (note well-physical) cells to unite. 

And then they had accomplished all that they possibly could accomplish. But was the child immediately born? 

Did they actually give "birth" to you there and then? 

I think not.

Let us look carefully and see what really did happen. 

Let us see whether your parents gave you life, or whether Almighty God gave you life. What we shall see will prove to you that we are, at least, on the pathway which leads to the bliss of full knowledge of our Creator-Almighty God.

After the physical union had been made by your parents, let me re-state that they had done all they could do. Their purely physical part of the operation ended right there. They had no way of knowing whether the child would be male or female. They did not know whether the birth would be single, double, or triplicate. They really did not know whether or not a child would be born.

After this physical operation, there stepped into the picture a far greater Intelligence and Power than any

possessed by your parents. This outside Power took complete charge of the building operations from that point on. Where your parents were forced to stop, another Power, the Power of God, took up the task of producing another human soul. 

To do this, It performed biological problems which no chemical laboratory can ever reproduce. Starches were changed into sugars. Bones were formed from practically nothing. In fact, there took place, in your mother, the most amazing demonstration of the Creative Power of the Spirit of God. Yet these parents probably treated the whole operation as nothing out of the ordinary.

But wasn't it? Yes it was. For there in that home, Almighty God performed an operation which, for sheer majesty, this world has not known the equal of. Nine months later, you came along. The physician was called. He cut the umbilical cord, and presented to the world, another separate individual. 

Now let me ask you a question. You will agree that it was the Creative Power of the Spirit of God which gave you life. You will admit that outside of the fer-

tilization process, the entire operation was conceived and carried out, not by the intelligence and power of your parents, but by the Power and Intelligence which is Almighty God. Now-here is the question do you think that after Almighty God had completed His task of making you, He turned you over to your parents, and at once withdrew his presence, intelligence, and power from you? 

Had God done that you couldn't be here. When 

God withdraws His Spirit from anyone, that human body dies. But you are still alive. And what Power is it that keeps you alive? It is the Power of the Spirit of 

God which made you, built you, and which has never been withdrawn from you from the day of your birth.

This means that you have in you, as your very life, here and now, the wonder-working Power of God. In addition to that, because God, with all His matchless miracle-working Power still lives in you, every attribute of God also is within you. So you see, it's not going to be quite as difficult to cultivate the companionship of God as you might have imagined—is it?

When we deal with God alone, forgetting human

concepts of God, the beautiful handiwork of God unfolds like a scroll. And the human mind can comprehend the staggering fact of the existence of God in each one of us. It requires no stretch of the imagination to grasp the Truths of God.

Theories of God, impossible for the most part, cannot be either grasped or understood. And to ask the human mind to "believe" something the human reason cannot grasp, is asking us to perform an impossibility. 

But along the pathway we are travelling to God, we are not bothered much with "believe" alone. What anyone "believes" about God is entirely beside the point. "What are the facts" is what really matters. In your birth, and in the birth of all human beings, it has been seen plainly that the Great Creator of the universe not only did the building of your physical body, but still maintains His presence in that body. 

This puts at your disposal, all the Power there is in the Realm of God, and this could not be otherwise. 

What a picture. What a Power. Can world-disturbers stand against the Power you have in you? Of course not.

This revelation of the Power of God in you puts you

in a rather peculiar situation doesn't it. You possess in you, all the Power of God, yet did not know this amazing fact until you began to tread this pathway to God. 

And now that you know you possess the Spirit of God in you, you still do not know just what a revolution this will cause in your life or affairs. You still do not know how you may use the Power of God in you. If you are conscious of the Power of the Spirit of God in you, but do not know how to use this wonderfully beautiful Spirit, you still are in a rather peculiar situation. 

But you promised to put your hand in mine. You promised to let me lead you, gently, lovingly, tenderly down this new trail. And I, in turn, promised you that at the end of the trail you shall find God. I repeat that promise to you now.

I have seen a few hundreds of thousands of lives unfold under the matchless Presence of the Spirit of God. 

And as I see your life unfolding, a great joy sweeps over me. The peace, and the Power of God come again into my life, and I know that I am doing my work well. 

And you know that too.

So just try to hold fast to my hand. I'm not much of a writer. I possess no great amount of ability. I'm not supposed to. I shouldn't want it even if I did possess it. 

But there's one thing I do possess; I've been over this trail to God so many times with so many different men and women that I possess the assurance which comes from long association with God, that practically everyone who travels along this pathway with me, will most assuredly not only find God, but discover how to unite his or her life with the Great God. When that happens, what a change.

I, remember many years ago, I was spiritual leader of a large evangelistic choir. Every Sunday evening we split up that huge choir and went to different churches assisting with evangelistic meetings. One of my favorite songs went something like this:- "Soon will our Savior from Heaven appear;

Sweet is this message and it's power to cheer; 

All will be changed by a glance from His sight; 

Walking by faith changed to walking by sight".

I was travelling down one of the many paths then which, eventually, I suppose, can lead to God. And, even then, I was trying in every way possible to have 

God make Himself a Living Reality to me.

Since I have been used as a "guide" to countless thousands, many a time my mind wanders back over the old trails upon which I tried to find God. I often think of this old hymn. And I know now why I was not able to find God along the other trails. 

You see-the old idea of God was of an absent God.

Not even the evangelists knew how very close they were to God. They did not even suspect that all the Powers of the Spirit of God were, even then as they preached, closer to them, at least as close as life itself. 

They taught us that God was "in Heaven", and, as this song implies, will soon appear and change everything. Even change "walking by faith into walking by sight." But you know now, even though we have just begun our little walk together, that you are not walking "by faith". You know something else—you know that Almighty God is not "in Heaven", but in you. 

That eliminates the "walking by faith" idea. Before

we reach the end of this trail we shall see that "walking by faith" is a poor excuse for the actual and literal Presence and Power of the Spirit of God. Incidentally, it's interesting to note how that type of religion has almost completely faded from the picture. Yet when I was a boy, it was all the rage. 

What you already possess, you do not need to have "faith" for. Until you possess the overwhelming Power of God in your life, then "faith" that you some day will know God, is, I suppose all right. But by what rule of logic does present religion rule out the manifestation of God here and now?

Who said that God is a great unknowable Being, hidden from mortal ken and beyond the reach of mortal man? Why not believe that the great Creator of this universe is abundantly able to manifest all the Power He has, not after we die but while we are alive. If the 

Power of God can be manifested to us here and now, then don't you see what a comparatively easy thing it would be to bring complete and universal peace and happiness to man?

All that is necessary is to tell man of the amazing

Power of God available. Just lead men and women to 

God-that's all. If religion in the future follows the trail trod by religion in the past, and insists on an "absent" God, unknowable until after death, then there will be no religion of the future. For religion, and God, are going to have to prove themselves before men will accept them.

There will be those who sought to find God through other paths than the one we are now treading, and they, of course, will insist on hanging onto the tapestries of ritualism for a while. But the future of the human race hinges on the actual discovery of God-here and now. Religion as we have known it will not be the religion of the future. Humanity will demand an actual demonstration of the Powers of God, or it will discard God, in which case the future will be blacker than the past.

All efforts of rulers to bring to this earth a just an equitable peace will fail until the hearts of men are changed. But the hearts of men cannot change until they come into intelligent companionship with God. 

Religions of the past have not been able to disclose any

intelligent companionship with God. Therefore, their concepts of God must be wrong. Any system of religion, and any concept of God which does not reveal the actual Power of God to man, cannot be a true system of religion.

An absentee God, or an impotent God is not what we shall find down the pathway we are treading. Instead we shall have a glorious experience of the Presence and Power of God in our lives. We shall be able, to not only talk about God, but to demonstrate the Power of God in our every action. Seeing the truth, the world will come to us for the Power we have found. 

A celebrated "divine" said to me in New York recently "But Dr. Robinson, the trouble with this world. is that it does not want God". I replied: "You mean

Dr. that the world does not want your God, and perhaps the reason the world doesn't want your God is because it has never seen any demonstration of the 

Power of your God anywhere. When you bring to humanity a God whose Power and Might can be demonstrated here on the earth, never you fear Dr. the world will make a beaten path to your door". 

---

And it will. But this is an age of reality. There is a grim reality in life. There is a grim reality in this brutal war. And before religion can have any effect on the world, it will have to become a living reality to men. When religion talks about God, it should be able to prove the existence of God, and it should be able to demonstrate the Power of God. Failing in this, it has no right to even mention the name of God. 

Let me impress upon you the fact that there is only one thing wrong with the human race. It does not know the normal, natural relationship which exists between God and it. The reason it does not know of this relationship is because no man, or no system of religion has revealed the true relationship to the world. But nowa change has come. You and I are travelling down an entirely new pathway to God. Down this trail, we shall not just talk about the wondrous beauty of God "above the sky"—instead, we shall have a wonderful experience of these beauties "below the sky". I have never been able to understand why the old church fathers of past ages insisted that God must be unknowable. That argument isn't even logical. The logic of God-the

truths of God cannot be discovered until man makes a personal contact with the Power of the Spirit of God, and makes that contact here and now. That has never been done to date.

There have been a few isolated souls who have bled through to God, but by and large, even our churchmembers do not even faintly suspect what a bottomless ocean of God-Power is available to them if they only knew it. Harriet Beecher Stowe had this experience, for she wrote: "Still, still, with Thee when purple morning breaketh; 

When the bird waketh and the shadows flee; 

Fairer than morning, lovelier than the daylight; 

Dawns the sweet consciousness-I AM WITH

THEE. 

Alone with Thee—amid the mystic shadows!

The solemn hush of nature newly-born; 

Alone with Thee in breathless adoration,

In the calm dew and freshness of the morn. 

Still, still, with Thee-as to each new-born morning;

A fresh and solemn spledor still is given;

So does this blessed consciousness awaking, Breathe each day nearness unto Thee and heaven.

When sinks the soul, subdued by toil to slumber; 

It's closing eyes look up to Thee in prayer; 

Sweet the repose beneath Thy wings o'ershading,

But sweeter far to wake and find Thee there". 

You see Mrs. Stowe had gone down the same pathway we are travelling along. She knew that God was not at a distance. She knew that she was still (quiet) with God, not only in the morning, but in the evening too. She knew that the Power of the Spirit of God was with her every moment of the day and night. And, knowing this, she drew others, and her name will live for many years. She cultivated companionship with God.

Webster says a companion is a comrade, a mate, or a partner. So then, when you cultivate the companionship of God, you make of God a mate, a comrade, and a partner. This is getting close to God. There will be a few more steps yet. We are not at the end of the journey. But if you make God, as little as you so far know of God, a companion, you will be ready for the next step.

There is an overwhelming passion in the soul of the one chosen by God to act as a "guide" down this pathway which leads to God. He, or she, cannot get away from this passion to bring men and women to a conscious knowledge of the Power of the Spirit of God. 

If I did not have this passion, I should be unable to guide you, and I should not be fit for my task. Nor should I attempt it. 

But you see I have trod this pathway before you.

I have seen, so very many times, the light of enchantment which has illumined the faces of hundreds of thousands when the consciousness of the Presence and Power of God fully dawned upon them. So I am so very anxious that I guide you aright. I am so very anxious that you reach the Haven of God as speedily as you can. Then, I can come back and lead another group over the same path. They too will find God. 

I shall not hurry you. I shall speak as simply as I know how to speak. And you will know that I know wherof I speak when, at the end of the trail, you make the greatest discovery man has ever made-the discovery of the consciousness of God in you.

That is all you will ever need know. For, having discovered the Presence and Power of the Spirit of God in your life, you will spend the rest of your days making known this Pearl of Greatest Price to those you daily come in contact with.

THE THIRD STEP Turn Companionship Into Friendship 

HE third step is to change the companionship of God into friendship. This will be easy for you. The first step is to recognize God whereever you find God. The second step is to cultivate the companionship of God. Now, we shall turn this companionship we have found into a friendship. This, in turn, will grow into something better. It will grow into a tie which shall bind us forever to God. 

You probably have a very close friend. It may be wife, or husband. You perhaps first cultivated the companionship of that friend. Then, as you both began to know each other better, that companionship ripened

into friendship. That is what will happen as we travel another step down this most blessed of all trails-the trail that leads to God.

There is a fellowship in friendship which does not always exist in companionship. There is a deeper bond in friendship. You grow more like your friends. You have the same likes and dislikes. You have the same objectives in life. You love the things your friend loves. 

You travel much in each other's company. You really get to know each other.

So it is in this friendship with God which you are now cultivating. There is quite a possibility that this friendship will ripen into love. It may go farther than that. 

It probably will. I know of no closer and more perfect and more productive friendship than that between 

God and man. Between God and you. 

You see when you have a friend you always feel perfectly free to consult and advise with that friend. 

If you should run into trouble, your friend is the first person you will take it to.

When your companionship with God has progressed to friendship, you will do the very same thing. There

may be moments, even after you have found God, when you won't be sure of the path which lies ahead. Then, if you are wise, you will stop and have a quiet talk with your newly-found Friend-the Spirit which is God.

You will discover when you do that, the advice, the love, the power which will come to you from God, your friend, far exceeds anything a human friend can do. 

For, when you make a close personal friend of God, you have made a friend of the greatest spiritual and material Power in the universe. Spiritual first, because it was the Spirit of God which gave you life. Material, second-because the Spirit of God always manifests in a material way, and always will, just so long as you are a material body.

If God should be separated from you as a material body, only manifesting spiritually, that could not be a very close friendship. For there would be no connection by which all the Power of God could be changed into the material help and power you so badly need. 

It isn't much help to tell the weary soul, tired and worn by the struggles which always beset a life lived without conscious knowledge of the Power of God that,

in some future realm, at some future date, in some future land, God shall wipe away all tears from the eyes.

That is not the true vision of God. For if you are weary, worn, and sad here in this life, then it is here in this life you need the help of your newly-found 

Friend. Therein lies the wondrous beauty of friendship with God. This Great Spirit of the fullness of all power, is so very capable of providing the material answer to your problems as well as the spiritual answer. 

It is not true to say that God can only manifest spiritually, not materially. Yet so many say just that. 

For some reason or other, God created a material earth. 

He created man a material being. The flowers, the birds, the bees, these are all material. They may be spiritual, but the only part of them we see is the material part, is it not? The life in them is God. 

Then it must follow as the night the day, that if God expended so much time and effort in producing a material world, filled with material beings, most certainly the God of the Universe can, and must, manifest His Power in a material manner. Spiritual manifestation from God, not coupled with material mani-

festation would not be manifestation at all. There is nothing such manifestation could do to aid us materially in our material troubles. And all the troubles of life we are capable of recognizing, are material troubles. 

Now then, if it should be a fact that Almighty God intended us all to be able to manifest materially as well as spiritually, don't you see how very far behind 

God we have lagged? Don't you see how unfair you have been with God in not giving Him credit for being able to bring material blessings as well as spiritual blessings? I'm sure you do.

A profession of religion which does not manifest the Power of the Spirit of God, bringing with it complete and absolute victory over every trial, hardship, want, and fear, is a lop-sided profession of religion. It admits the ability of God to provide spiritual blessings above the sky, but denies the ability of God to do very much for us in a material way. This may be a piece of religious strategy or propaganda. 

As long as religion confines itself with what goes on "beyond the tomb", it's veracity cannot be questioned. 

For none knows what goes on "beyond the tomb".

When religion offers little else than "salvation", and that at some future time, religion is putting itself in a position where it cannot prove it's thesis, and you cannot disprove it. Which leaves us high and dry insofar as God is concerned.

Only a religion, or a religious professor who is not sure of his ground, will adopt such a concept of God. 

There is nothing to be proved, nothing which can be proved, and the entire category of theological conclusions may be completely false insofar as proof is concerned.

How much better is this pathway. For when we have arrived at the end of our journey, we shall be able to prove most abundantly, not only the actual Presence of God, but we shall be given Power to manifest materially too. That will prove to a material world that God exists, and can successfully handle every problem which can confront man.

The problems confronting this blighted civilization of ours are not spiritual problems, they are material problems. If my young son Alfred, who is flying a torpedo-bomber, never comes back to me-if he is killed.

in action, that will be a material death. And the hearts which will break will be material hearts. 

This whole war is a material human slaughter. It has been brought upon us because religion has not been true to its trust. It has slandered God. It has told the world that Almighty God exists only to "save the soul." It has told us to be "pilgrims, walking along a thorny path, with no hope outside of the preachments of itself." That is the reason this earth is bathed in blood.

Religion has denied that Almighty God is capable of material manifestation. 

If, in these dark days, religion would first find the Power of God, and then teach that God wants to manifest in a material manner on the earth, humanity would receive such a baptism of Spiritual and material Power that this earth would stand amazed at the Power of God.

But religion does not do that. It tells us that 2000 years ago, Almighty God, born in the womb of a virgin who, incidentally was a married woman, was crucified on a cross for the sins of the world. It tells us that all who do not believe this are automatically damned,

unless they make a public confession that they do believe this.

This is religious tapestry only. This is the concept of 

God which will never, never, never disclose the Power of the Spirit of God to the human race. This is the concept of God which can do nothing in the most bloody crisis humanity has ever known, to bring world dictators to their knees.

This concept of God very effectively prohibits God from doing the thing God is dying to do for us-manifest His fullness of Presence and Power. With our churches teaching us that foolish theology, it is evident that the manifestation of God will have to be brought to earth by someone who does not believe such a low concept of Almighty God. 

I am very glad that I am not attempting to lead you down that by-path to God. I'm afraid we should never get there. Not long ago, a close personal friend, a "fundamentalist" Christian said to me:- - "My,

Frank, what a wonderful work you could do for God if you would only work in my church." My reply shocked him. "Jim," I said, "If I were working in

any present-day church, instead of revealing the Power of God to humanity, I should be deliberately hiding 

God from the human race." 

He cannot see it. To him, everything concerning God is wrapped up in the Christian theory of God. And this friend will be that way until he dies. What a wonderful life he could be living if, instead of telling men they "must be born again and believe that Jesus was Almighty God," he preached the actual Presence and

Power of the Spirit of God here and now, operating freely for all, and regardless of what man-made theory of God he believed in.

The Christian theory of God is less than 2000 years old. It cannot therefore reveal God to the human race. 

No system of theology which makes the discovery of the Spirit of God dependent upon what a church believes, can possibly be of God. For that Great Spirit, millions of eons ago, was at work, creating in His own way, the foundations of what you and I are today.

After all is said and done, I know that those who travel along this path with me find God. They know 

God. They are abundantly able to manifest the Power

of the Spirit of God. By their fruits ye shall know them. 

What a religionist may say means nothing much. What he is able to do, through the Power of God, means everything. That tells the story.

So, then, we are not going into any by-paths no matter how easy and alluring they may be. We are going to cultivate our companionship with God. We are going to turn that companionship into friendship. 

We are going to follow the Light that leads. For that 

Light is God. Cardinal Newman knew something of the Light that is God when, his ship lying becalmed, he wrote those lovely words which I have painted on the door of my down-town study. Here they are:- "Lead-Kindly Light-amid the encircling gloom; 

Lead Thou me on:

The night is dark, and I am far from home; 

Lead Thou me on: 

Keep Thou my feet-I do not ask to see the distant scene-one step enough for me.”

The reason Cardinal Newman did not ask to see the distant scene was because he knew the present scene.

He knew the absolute Presence of God in him. No one who gets to the place of friendship with God can ever be interested in "the distant scene." The thoughts, the aims, the ambitions, yes, the whole life of such a one is indissolubly linked with the present scene, with all it's suicidal madness.

The best way to be led to God is to follow God in His heart-breaking attempts to tell this world that all the Power of God exists here and now for every created human being. Enlisted in that cause, there is little time left for thinking about the future. There is little time to think about one's own "salvation." The man or woman in tune with the Infinite will live with the Infinite. 

And there is neither time nor space there. "So long Thy Power-(here it is) has blest me, sure, It still will lead me on;

O'er moor and fen, o'er crag and torrent, till the night is gone: 

And with the morn, those angel faces smile, which 

I have loved long since-but lost awhile."

Cardinal Newman knew what his life's work was. 

Had he not been becalmed on that ship, this beautiful

hymn never would have been written. But note the thoughts which were uppermost in his mind, as, with time dragging heavily on his hands, he unintentionally disclosed where his treasure was. The actual Presence and Power of God. It made no difference to the Cardinal where he was. Nor did time nor space. Out of the fullness of his heart he cried: "So long Thy 

Power has blest me-sure, It still will lead me on."

And It did. And It always does. And It always will. 

Blessed is the man or woman who discovers that. Thrice blessed is the man or woman who,knowing the fullness of the Power of God, brings that divine message to others who, wandering along many by-paths seeking God, have become lost in a maze of theological preachments and, sometimes I think, idolatrous practices. 

I remember some years ago I was having a period of forced idleness. I was making a trip through the 

China Sea. The night in question was dark. The sea was rough. Not a star in sight. It was after midnight. I was lying on my back on the upper deck of that huge liner as it ploughed it's weary way through the waves and storms which only the China Sea knows.

I was lying in the fore-part of the ship-the focsle I believe they call it. Suddenly, from out the night, I heard a shout from the "crows-nest"—"Light on the port bow, sir." Arising to my feet, I looked over to the left, and there, sure enough, were a few little twinkling lights dotted along the shore of the Island of Formosa. 

Approaching one of the ship's officers, I said to him: -"What are those little lights, sir?" "Oh!" he replied, "they are the little lights in the cabins of the tea-growers on the Island of Formosa-we always look for those little lights because we know when we see them we are on our course.' 

And my thoughts recalled another verse of beautiful hymnology. And I thought how appropriate that verse is to the present situation:— "Brightly gleams our Father's mercy; 

From His Lighthouse, evermore:

But to us, He gives the keeping of the lights, Along the shore." 

So I repeat, thrice blessed are you and I who are walking along the pathway that leads to God. For I

know, just as surely as I know I am alive, when you have entered into the blessed Presence of God, which experience you will have when you come to the end of our little journey together, you will keep your "little light" burning brightly so that others, tempest-tossed on the waves of theological myths and traditions, may find the Lighthouse which is God. "Trim your feeble lamp, my brother: 

Some poor sailor tempest-tossed; 

Trying vain to make the harbor, In the darkness may be lost."

The Great Lighthouse which is God is not feeble. 

Nor will your little light be feeble when you replenish it from the oil of the Power of the Spirit of God. 

Sailors, tempest-tossed, lost, discouraged, are always made supremely happy when someone throws them a "light" or a life-belt. And on this human sea of driftwood, nations as well as individuals flounder round in the darkness. Theology, too, it flounders round in the same darkness.

For the Presence and Power of the Spirit of God

is unknown on the earth today. The by-paths cannot save anyone. Chances are, it will be so long before they reveal the Spirit of God that humanity may have destroyed itself before that happens.

We shall now advance rapidly in our travels to God. We have learned to recognize God wherever we find Him. In the flowers, the trees, in every beautiful thing that God has so bountifully supplied. More important than all, though, we have definitely established the world-shattering fact that the Spirit of God actually lives in, and is the life motivating the physical body of every human being.

So our foundation is very sure. If some Christian theologian should be inclined to doubt the statement that God Almighty lives in us all, refer him to his own Bible, in either first or second Corinthians, (I forget which) will be found this statement. As the 

Christian believes his Bible to be the "divinely inspired word of God, true from cover to cover" he must accept the following statement as coming from God, therefore true. Here is the statement:-

"What-know ye not that your bodies are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God liveth in you?"

So, then, we are absolutely safe in building our experience on the staggering fact that we have, in us, the Power of the Spirit of God. But regardless of what any "bible" teaches, we have established firmly the scientific fact that God does actually live in our physical bodies. If the contrary were true, we could not live another moment.

Without Almighty God, life is not possible either on this earth or anywhere else. Without Almighty God in our physical bodies, there can be no life in them. 

So our next step is to begin to get acquainted with this Guest we have been entertaining all unknown, for so long. We are changing companionship with God for friendship with God. So at this point in our travels, we shall begin talking to our Friend. And I mean actually and literally, talking with God. God is the essence of intelligence. He is the Author of intelligence. 

So there can be no doubt but what God can hear what we say to Him. Nor can there be any doubt of His

ability to answer us in language we can understand. 

Furthermore, God actually living in us, there can be no need for group gatherings in which God is addressed as some distant Being, incapable of being either known or contacted here and now. There will be no need to address God as "Our Father which art in heaven." For while it is true that God, being everywhere, must be in "heaven" provided such a place exists, it is also more abundantly true that God is in us. 

We know little about heaven. We know less about 

God in heaven. But God is us-actually living and abiding in us here and now; well-that's something else. That brings all the infinite love, power, wisdom, intelligence which is God, right home to us. It places 

God where He has ever been, all unknown to us. 

Had the nations and their leaders but known the staggering facts of the whereabouts of God as you are learning those facts, what a different world this would be.

So we're going to talk to God. We are going to talk to God intelligently, and we are going to hear God speak back to us, equally intelligently. When I first

ran my advertising copy which states "I TALKED 

WITH GOD," howls of indignation went up from all churches. Letters of complaint went into the Post 

Office authorities in D.C. stating that in claiming 

I "talked with God, actually and literally," I was, in reality, blaspheming the name of God, because no one can talk with God.

Here we have the ludicrous situation of seeing men, and churches, which are supposed to be bringing God to men, making the statement that it is blasphemy to state that anyone can talk with God. No wonder our churches have not been able to reveal God to the world. 

They evidently have not enough faith to believe that God exists on the earth. They denied, and still deny, that it is possible for anyone, anywhere, to actually talk with God. What a pity.

Their concept of God should be changed, certainly. 

For to teach a God with whom one cannot speak, to teach a God whose existence on earth, in us, is denied, is to be teaching a God who does not exist. Of course, the trouble here lies in the fact that all major religions have made the fatal mistake of putting a "go-between"

in the place of God. A "mediator," they call it. But if

God lives in us-if by His Spirit he gives us life—if Almighty God in us is actually our life, then there can be no need of a "mediator" of any sort. 

All "mediators" and probably "moderators" can do is to hinder the free intercourse of the Spirit of God. 

When any theological organization makes the statement that it is necessary to go through them, or through some other "mediator," even though they assume Godship to the "mediator," they are teaching a false philosophy of God. Of course, they each have a different "mediator." They each have a different "Christ." None of them can deal directly with the Spirit of God, because to do that would rule out their entire structures. 

So it will be necessary to get rid of all "mediators," whether they are called "gods" or men. Not until that is done can we talk with God, thereby drawing to us whatever Power of God we need. For to draw upon the fullness of the Power of the Spirit of God is to have an eye single to the glory and Power of God. Nothing can be allowed to come between us and God. If it is allowed to do this, we cannot see or commune with God.

We cannot worship the messenger instead of the message. 

Now just how may we talk with God? Just how may we turn companionship into friendship? Well you will talk with God just exactly as you will talk to any other friend. This will be something new to you, but as you get better acquainted with God, in you, you will spend a great deal of your time in intimate, personal conversation with the Spirit of God, in you. 

When you do, and when you hear the voice of God speaking back to you, telling you what to do, you will enter into a life which, by it's sheer beauty will enthrall you. You will still live on the earth. You will still be a physical being, as you are now-but conversation and communion with God will put the best that is in you into the Realm of God. Your whole heart and life will be under the Supreme Guide, the one I represent in a small sort of way, as a humble guide. 

As your conversations with God continue, and as the matchless beauty, charm, and Power of this Realm of God are discovered, you will find that there is coming into your consciousness, a new sense of the existence of

God. Not into your life understand, because there already exists in your life, in your physical body, more of the Power of God that you will ever be able to use. 

But, continued friendship with God-continued "little talks" with God, will bring the Power of God into your conscious life. Then-you really will begin to live. 

You may ask me at this point just what form shall your conversation with God take? The same form that your conversation with me would take. With this difference. If you were talking with me you would be able to see me. But in talking with your unseen Guest, you will have to treat Him, for the time being as just that-YOUR UNSEEN GUEST. 

You will, however, find this type of conversation far more pregnant with Power than any other conversation you can engage in. You will find it much more satisfactory. For, after all, to be able to know that God is listening, in you, and to be able to talk to God just as you would talk with me, is the most staggering human achievement yet attained.

Remember this, too-the Spirit of God you are talking with created this universe. It has unlimited Power.

It is more willing and anxious to manifest materially and spiritually in your life than you are to have it manifest. It is God. It is the beginning of the end of your search for God. And you found God in the last place you would ever think of finding Him.

Let me tell you how close God and I are. Not to brag, because the one in tune with the Infinite never brags. I think, though, if I tell you what my procedure is, you can safely follow it. The first thing in the morning when I awaken, I close my eyes. I shut out all visible things. Then, before I dress, I'll say something like this: "Spirit of the Living God, reveal more of yourself to me to-day. Make me more and more conscious of your Presence. Show me the way-tell me what you want me to do. Take me, break me, make me-but use every talent I have-use my whole life in a manner which will best reveal the Power of the 

Spirit of God to this sin-cursed earth."

These may not be the exact words, for I talk with God so often, and under so many different circumstances, that it is impossible to recall the actual conversations. You will understand first, that there is

a great love between God and your writer. Nothing means anything to me but God. Not because He is supposed to have died on a tree for my sins, because God did nothing of the kind. But do you know what makes me love God so?

Well, the answer is simple-it's just because He loves me and has seen fit to reveal Himself to me. 

That's why I love God. How can I do anything else?

When Almighty God takes a mediocre man like me and breathes the Breath of the Spirit of God upon him, how can that one think of anything else? The matchless unseen glories of the Realm of God, in us, melt every human heart which comes in contact with them. The outside world knows nothing of this. It cannot see the operations of the Spirit of God while this Spirit is revealing itself to you. But when the Life of 

God reveals itself to you and me, the world knows, through our message that we have been with God. Quite often I'll be making a long journey on a train. 

That is where I like to talk with God best. There, all alone in my compartment, the train speeding along, I

can pour out my soul. And the Maker of that soul of mine knows what it's all about. Yes-God knows far better than I can tell Him what the innermost yearnings of my heart are.

Often, I'll stand there, looking into God. Looking into the invisible Realm of the Spirit of God. Then, it may be that I'll tell God anew how my heart bleeds for this mad world. I'll tell Him anew that I am only waiting for Him to show me the way. Invariably, after my little talks, alone with God, the world shut out, I'll keep very still for a long time. You see I'll be listening to what God has to say to me. 

After all, it makes little difference what I say to God. 

The Great Law which is God will operate in spite of anything I might say. But what God says to me—what 

God wants me to hear that is important indeed.

For this is the message I have waited for. When God speaks to me, I am always so very anxious to listen. 

The burden of my talks with God is the burden of my heart. You see God has revealed to me the pathway which leads to Him. He has commissioned me to guide as many others who will be guided into

that most blest of all havens-the haven of the Peace and Power of God. And so, in the quietness of a blustering train, when God and I meet, I remind God that I am ready for the task He has given to me. 

The revelation that God lives in us all, with all the glittering Power involved, and the further revelation that all may know the Power of the Spirit of God to the very full, was an astonishing revelation to bring to any little man like me. But it came. And it came from the Author of all revelation. I must make known to the world the staggering fact of the existence of God, right here in every life. That-is my task. I long for the day when this brilliant vision shall burst, in all it's wondrous beauty, on the entire human race. 

So you see I cannot under any circumstances get very far from God, can I?I might be bothering myself with some personal problem just the very moment 

God might want to be speaking with me. So I listen. 

And the more I listen to God, the more real does God become to me. Before too long, the faith which, for 56 years has buoyed me up and carried me through many a fiery furnace, will be rewarded. The Spirit of God

will descend on the earth, and, through this simple guide this incompetent messenger of God, the world will know the glorious brilliance of the Presence of God. 

That is the plan, and I suggest you follow it. Be alone with God. You can do that any time. The noise and bustle of the mad city cannot close the door between you and God, for, you will remember, God is in you. You simply close the avenue by which worldly things register on your consciousness, and there, clothed in all His wondrous beauty-stands the loving Spirit of God-the Creator of this universe-and my Creator too.

So you see, finding God is not a matter of attending a church, although I suppose that can do no harm. It is, however, an individual matter of your finding the Power of the Spirit of God in your own life, right here while you are living on the earth.

When you find God, the consciousness of the invisible Spirit of God in you, is merged, if you please, with the physical consciousness, and you are united with your 

Creator. You and God are one. You live your life with

the full consciousness of the Power of the Spirit of God in you, all about you of course, but primarily in you.

Your life then becomes a well-rounded life. It becomes exactly the kind of life God intended it should be in the first place, before religious leaders took man's eyes off the indwelling Spirit of God, and placed them on a human-made structure which, with no authority whatsoever, takes it upon itself to interpolate beween 

God and man, a "mediator." I love everyone who is trying to make God real to the world. I love every member of every church. My work as a "guide" is to all, church-members included. There, my greatest heartaches lie. From there, my greatest persecutions have come.

Before too long, the glory the amazingly rich sweetness of the Presence of God will be made known to the whole earth. When that happens, truly the glory of the knowledge of the Spirit of God shall cover the earth from corner to corner. It will reach into the most remote quarters. Nations will be born in a day. Man will have turned from his idols, to God.

So make your little talks with God private. Make them often. Let the burden of them be the revealing of the fullness of the Power and Presence of the Spirit of God to you. Let this one thought be the predominating thought of your life. If you give God even the faintest opportunity to reveal Himself to you, the work is done. God never disappoints. And God lives in you. 

What you have not known, you know now. From now on, your life will be just as beautiful as God can make it. The degree of beauty depends upon the degree of the consciousness of God you allow to enter your life. 

Don't forget your closest friend.

THE FOURTH STEP Turn Friendship Into Love 

HE pathway to God is very similar to the pathway two personal friends will take. They first would recognize each other. Then, as we did, they will cultivate the companionship of each other. 

Then, they will seek to turn that companionship into true friendship, if they really like each other. After that, the friendship will automatically turn into love.

It is exactly like this in the Realm of the Spirit of God and it's relationship to the human race. It is easy to recognize God. It is easier to cultivate the companionship of God. It is still easier to turn that companionship into friendship. The change from friendship

with God to the love of God seems to be the easiest step of all.

For it is practically impossible to know very much about God and not fall in love with this Great Spirit of triumphant Power. In fact, I will say to you as we travel down this sacred path together that it is not possible to know the greatness and the goodness of the 

Spirit of God and not fall in love with it. I do not believe that can be done.

It cannot be done because the Spirit of God is too close to us, and is too loving toward us. You cannot hate one who loves you, and certainly this Great Spirit of all Creation, living moment by moment in you, giving you life, peace, joy, abundance of material supplycertainly it would not be possible to consciously live with this Great Spirit of God and not fall in love with it. 

You see whether you know it or not, you do obtain your life from the Spirit of God within you. That part of the Spirit of God in you is not, of course, all of God.

But it is all you will ever need. As it exists in you, it is more than abundantly able to make your life so scintillating bright and beautiful. You will never be able to

comprehend fully what it means to have Almighty God living in you.

Should it be that the Spirit of God in you is not, in such small proportions, able to bring to you every good thing life can offer, then this indwelling Spirit of God is in total and complete contact with all the Spirit of God there is in the universe. And the God who created this universe is most certainly abundantly able to bring into your little life, a super-abundance of every good thing, and a thousand times more.

Knowing this, then, and realizing the permanent existence of the Source of all supply, in you, how can you fail to fall in love with the Spirit of God? It would be an absolute impossibility. It would be very foolish to even attempt it. Of course, it is entirely possible for one to have full knowledge of the existence of the Spirit of God in him, and then completely ignore that blessed Spirit, choosing to live out one's life alone, disdaining and ignoring completely the indwelling 

Spirit of God. 

Along that path lies madness. Hitler's madness-for that is exactly what he is trying to do. Don't let anyone

tell you that this insane murderer does not know of the existence of the Spirit of God. He most certainly does. 

In his crass egotism, he has decided that he is a bigger personality than God. What a terrible end that poor fool will come to. If our religious organizations only knew the Power of the Spirit of God, instead of a lot of old unprobable theological traditions, rites, and rituals, how very fast could the Spirit of God eliminate him, and his ilk. 

Yes you can ignore God if you wish, but, after having had authentic knowledge of the existence and 

Power of God in you brought to you, I don't advise anyone to attempt to live his or her life ignoring that beautiful Spirit. Disaster, swift and permanent lies down that trail. Don't do it.

But to come back. To recognize the shining Power of God, either in you or anywhere else, is to love God. 

Not because God died on a cross 2000 years ago for your "sins" because God did nothing of the kind. But you love God because that great Spirit, even before you knew it, placed at your disposal more than an abundance of Power, Might, Intelligence, Love, and a million

other things, all awaiting your appropriation for your own use. And for your own use from the very day you recognize the staggering import of what it all means to have all of God available to you here and now. 

A bank-roll, completely inexhaustible-a bank-roll of spiritual and material Power has been placed in your hands. You can write the check because the money is in the bank. And God is the banker and God owns the bank. The only "sin" in this world is not to know the limitless source of everything, which God has placed at your disposal. All crimes are directly tracable to this one and only "sin." That "sin" is either ignorance or doubt of God. To know God and to draw freely upon the material and spiritual wealth of God, that is honoring God. That is loving God. And the bigger you make the check the better does God like it. It shows complete assurance and faith in God when you write a big check, and call upon God for some big things. 

May I repeat myself-there is nothing, absolutely nothing, either material or spiritual, the indwelling Spirit of God cannot bring to you here in this life, any time you need it, if that thing is for your own good.

If it is not for your own good you won't be very apt to ask God for it. For it is not at all a matter of asking anyway. It is a matter of taking. The idea of getting on your knees and asking God for something which He might, and would withhold from you´if you did not "pray," is rather a monstrous practice. It has no basis of fact in the Realm of God. The fact is that Almighty 

God, living in you, already possesses every good thing you can possibly need. To get these good things you do not, therefore, ask God for them-you take them.

I have a son. I love that son. He is a Naval pilot, flying a tropedo-bomber. All through his High-School days, about twice a week, Alfie would come to me and say, "Will you loan me a buck, Pop?" Now he knew that I had that "buck" and he knew that I would let him have it. Well, there was so much of that "loan me a buck, Pop," that I said to him one day-"Alfie-I'm not going to 'loan' you any more 'bucks,' I'm going to give you so much every week."

After that, every Monday, Alfie would get from me several "bucks" which he could spend as he saw fit. Now the Spirit of God is a far more abundant provider

than I am, because that Great Spirit of God owns far more than I own. So it must follow then, with that 

Spirit living in us, an overwhelming supply of every good thing is here—all the time-awaiting our taking.

We do not need to ask God to "loan" us anything. 

We do not need to ask God to "give" us anything. We take what we want, and the reason we feel so free about taking it is because God placed His Spirit in us for no other purpose than to provide us with every good thing we can possibly need. Not "after we die," but right here and now, while we are consciously alive physically. To "pray" then, is to doubt both the existence of the Spirit of God in us, and the ability of God to provide, freely and without limit.

Praying does something else. It establishes a chain of thought predicated upon an absent God. It completely ignores the Presence of God within us. Those of us who know the present existence of the Power of the Spirit of God in us, never pray. We never ask God for anything. That would be just like asking ourselves. 

My little Florence, if she needs clothes or school-books or anything else, goes into the store and buys them.

She says "My Daddy will pay for this." And her

Daddy does. And so it is when one knows from actual experience that the Spirit of God, the Creator of this universe, actually lives, with all of His might, in each one of us.

So then we do not ask. We appropriate from the 

God of the universe, the things we must have for our complete happiness, joy, and eternal peace.

I wish our good Christian friends would learn this lesson. When they discover how wondrously beautiful the existence of the Spirit of God really is, the tears will stream down their faces. Their joy will be unconfined and limitless. They will have found God. 

There will be no need then for their minister or priest to read some prayer out of a book. There will be no need then for the priest or preacher to stand before an altar, dressed in sparkling raiment, chanting masses and burning incense. Oh, no. There is neither need nor room for that where the Spirit of God is. 

That is all so un-called for. It can only happen when the presence of God is an unknown factor in the life. 

The only ones who look for God in that sort of per-

formance are those whose mentality will not allow them to understand that the Spirit of God is not dependent upon any man or any system of religion, to manifest His Power to them.

Doubting God, and following customs which originated in a pagan and idolatrous past, these good deluded folks, not able or not wishing to stand on their own feet before God, depend upon either preacher, priest, or rabbi to act as a "go-between." By this childish, unthinking act, they hope to escape the terrible torments which they have been told God has provided for them, always, of course, "after they die." Nothing for them here though. I feel sorry for these poor fellow-Americans. They are due for a rude shock one of these days, when the fullness of the glory of the indwelling Spirit of God in each one of us becomes known.

Almighty God ordained no church organization to represent Him on this earth. Nor did He give to any organization the sole and exclusive bargain rights between God and man. Those who believe that God did, are automatically living a life in which the Power of

. 

God can never manifest. Those who are teaching such childish doctrines are hiding the Spirit of God from the human race-not revealing it.

I was literally dumbfounded when one of the clipping bureaus last year sent me a clipping from one of the large Press Associations. This clipping stated that on the eve of the infamous "back-stabbing" of France by Italy, priests of the Roman Catholic Church, (an Italian system of religion) went in and out among these Axis soldiers, "blessing" them and "praying for victory for our Italian troops in order that the flag of the House of Savoy (Italy) may wave forever over the holy sepulchre in Rome."

I thought the democratic nations were fighting the Axis and its philosophies. The thought here is that we have an example of what self-claimed "agents of God" really are interested in. This Italian system of religion, according to this statement, is only interested in keeping the Italian flag flying over the Vatican in Rome. 

It is not interested in the ever-abiding Spirit of God which actually lives in the physical bodies of those Italian soldiers. Nor is it interested in telling men and

women that the Power of the indwelling Spirit of God can eliminate all wars, making the earth blossom as a rose. It is only interested in "keeping the flag of the 

House of Savoy flying over the Vatican."

Yet, we, a democratic nation, at war with the Axis and Italy, and its Axis philosophies, send an ambassador to Rome, the headquarters of a system of religion which, while knowing nothing whatesoever about the Spirit of God in human lives, tells its followers that, unless some priest "save their souls," hell-fire or purgatory is their inevitable doom.

This is the sort of thing that has hidden God from the world. We have looked to the churches for knowledge of God. We have looked to them for the Power of God. What we have been offered is not what we were seeking. We did not want, and we do not want a theory that Almighty God, 2000 years ago, sent His son (if that were possible) down to earth by the questionable path of a married virgin's womb, to die on a cross, to rise again, and to disappear-all this because we were "born in sin and shapen in iniquity." 

No, we were not. The birth of a child involves all

the Power Almighty God has. It is the miracle of all miracles to us human beings. We have found that it took the Power of the Spirit of God to make that child, and we have seen that as long as that child lives, the Spirit of God lives in that child. Yet all major systems of religion teach today that if that child dies "in its sins," it never can go to heaven, without being "washed in the blood of the lamb."

The legend of the "blood of the lamb" originated in an old Mexican system of religion which was extant nearly 1500 years before Christianity was ever heard of. The old Mexican "holy-men" would take a sheep, cover it with human blood, and then chase it out into the hills until a rainstorm came. If the sheep wandered back before it rained, they would chase it back into the hills again. Then the rain would come, and wash all the blood off the sheep, which would then be free to return to the fold. This nice piece of theological nonsense was then practiced wholesale. The return of the sheep was welcomed as "the lamb of god which taketh away the sins of the world." The whole theory of God being a "lamb," slain on a cross for the sins of

the world, was known to a dozen systems of religion long before the Christian religion was born. It is then, shocking as the fact may be, a pagan conception of God, originating in a dark and idolatrous age.

I don't want to hurt anyone's feelings here, although, if telling the world the truth about the Power of the Spirit of God should hurt their feelings, that cannot be helped-but I must-it is my duty to tell the American people what the truths of God are. Just as long as any system of religion interpolates anything at all between the Spirit of God and man, that religion is hiding from man, the very truths and the very Power which can liberate this world from complete ignorance of the existing Spirit of God.

It makes no difference if they call the interpolation God. That cannot change the basic facts. We all know that God Almighty did not need to lower Himself to such asinine means to reveal His Power to the human race. It is those "agents of God," who, without the slightest experimental knowledge of the Power of God, brought these idolatrous teachings into being. 

The result of them is evident in the world today.

They cannot reveal God to man, although they can, and do, reveal creeds, rites, rituals, and other idolatrous practices in the name of God. They all mean well. They are mostly honest, I believe. But their philosophies have failed to reveal the Spirit of God to the world. 

They have failed to do one thing which has helped banish wars, crimes, strife, and wholesale pillage and murder. They cannot do these things so they cannot be of God.

Let's stop fooling around with the name of God. 

Either God exists or there is no God. If God exists, His Power must be able to manifest itself to a suffering, bleeding, dying humanity, writhing in perhaps its death agony because it does not know either God or the Power of God. When we have come to the end of our little journey together, we shall see how the Power of God, discovered and used by humanity, will automatically bring to the war-weary nations of this earth, a day replete with such resplendent glory that we shall look back with shame on the practices upon which we were told to depend for our knowledge of God. 

A religious revolution is here. The world does not

want, and will not have much more to do with systems of religion which, while making a lot of talk about God, do positively nothing to reveal the actual Power of God to humanity. Before this war has ended, men and women will have discovered the measureless might and the ineffable love which automatically come to every human heart when it realizes that Almighty God, in all His blessed fullness, lives in that life, here and now.

It is not a case of angels worshipping round the throne of God in heaven. God wants no one to worship 

Him. All God asks is that men and women everywhere find the remarkable fact of the existence of the Spirit of God in them all, and, having realized that God does live in us all, use the indwelling Power of God to do any good thing we may need.

God created the human race in order that He might reveal the fullness of the Power of the Spirit of God to it. Man, to date, following wild, unauthenticated systems of dark-age religions, has utterly failed to see the gloriously illuminating truths of the present existence of God in man.

No. God wants no man to worship Him. Why should 

He? In any event, the best way I know of to "worship" 

God is to allow the fullness of the Spirit of God to manifest unhindered through man, so that others may find this matchless Pearl of Greatest Price. That's all

God is interested in. Dr. Washington Gladden, whom they kicked out of the Presbyterian Church, had the right idea. He knew the truth of the Presence of the 

Spirit of God in us all when he wrote:- "O Master, let me walk with Thee;

In lowly paths of service free; 

Tell me Thy Secret, let me bear;

The strain of toil-the fret of care. 

Help me the slow of heart to move; 

By some clear, winning word of love; 

Teach me the wayward feet to stay; 

And guide them in the homeward way. 

Teach me Thy patience, still (quiet) with Thee; 

In closer, dearer company;

In work that keeps faith sweet and strong; 

In trust that triumphs over wrong.

In hope that sends a shining ray; 

Far down the future's broadening way;

In peace that only Thus canst give; 

With Thee O Master, let me live."

And the Presbyterians, the same religious body which murdered Servetus for denying the doctrine of the trinity, kicked Dr. Gladden out of the church. He probably did not have as much "blood of lambs" as these old Scotch religionists would have liked. So out he went. 

But these old theological tapestries are doomed. God will never go. Instead, as man-made creeds, rites, rituals die by the weight of their own error, the peace, the joy, the happiness, the material and spiritual abundance which will come direct from the hand of God, living in man, will bathe this blood-soaked earth with the glory of God. 

So let us not condemn then. Let us remember that the few easy steps you are taking into the Realm of the 

Spirit of God could not have been possible fifty years ago. Let us remember that man will slowly, by his own theological mistakes, discover what the truth about

God really is. It's a growth. We grow day by day into the marvelous, priceless truths of God. When times become dangerous, and it seems as though hope is to be forever dimmed, God raises up a prophet who, though his path must inevitably be thorny and hard, will show the world what God really is.

If I write plainly, and seem to criticise, it is only because, as a true prophet of God, I must, kindly and lovingly, point out to those honest souls who believe

God to be at a distance, the supremely beautiful fact that what they have been promising in the future, and after death, can be secured here and now, because the Spirit of God dwells among men. In them, to be accurate.

I remember an incident which happened while I was going through theological seminary many years ago. cross. We want to ask you to keep very humble before

God, because we are all convinced that the Spirit of 

God will some day speak through you." 

That was a prophetic remark. However, I am afraid

I shocked those good men and shocked them hard. For

I did not want any teachings about God, what I wanted was a revelation of the Power of God in my life. 

I had not had such an experience as yet. All I had done was to make a public declaration that I trusted in 

Jesus, the "god" of the Christian Church, as my own personal "saviour." This was done in a revival meeting where religious emotion ran high, and in which the evangelist played with the emotions of us youngsters. 

There had been no contact with God as a result of that experience and I was somewhat mystified because of this fact.

I had been promised that if I "believed" in Jesus,

some great load of "sin" would fall from my shoulders, and I should be free. But I was not free. No great load had been lifted because there never had been a great load there. I simply wanted to know, by actual experience, that God lived, and was able to disclose His Power in my life. That is all I wanted. It is all I have ever wanted. Later, I had that experience but it did not come as a result of anything I "believed." It did come when I recognized the priceless majesty of the Spirit of God, and freely took of the Power which was revealed to me when, instead of looking to man, or men, or religious organizations, I took my troubles direct to God. Then it was that I really began my ministry which is not, as those good men predicted, "drawing the whole world nearer to the cross," but which is drawing them away from all religious crosses. Men and women are finding God, but they are finding Him entirely outside of Baptist, or, indeed, any

theory which teaches the immaculate conception, the virgin birth, the crucifixion, the resurrection of Almighty God in the form of a man. But, I pointed out, I had studied them apart from any religious institution. I had studied them as contemporary religions. 

Then I threw a bombshell into that little group of Godly men. (And they were Godly men. They were living up to the light they had. Unfortunately, they had not yet seen the True Light, which is the Spirit of God. The discovery of the Power of that Great Light had not yet been made.)

I told these good men that as I studied the origins of all the major systems of religion, I had discovered there a similiarity which I wanted explained to me. I told them that every fundamental theory of the Christian religion existed years before in many religions. 

I told them that the doctrines of immaculate conception, virgin birth, crucifiction, resurrection, were so prevalent in older systems of religion that, to me at least, they bespoke a common origin, that origin being long before the Christian religion had even been born. 

I wanted this explained to me, and asked these good men to explain it. I told them further that I had discovered more than a score of supposedly virgin-born "gods" had, according to ancient religious story and tradition, come down from heaven to "save the world. from sin." And I asked them to explain that to me. I asked them to tell me why, if their theory of God was original, practically the same story, even to names and places, existed in a religion of India, fifteen hundred years before the Christian era. 

I gave them the names of a dozen of these "gods" and asked them to tell me how I could be expected to

believe the story they were teaching when, thousands of years earlier than the Christian era, millions of people knew the story by heart. They could not answer me. 

I dont like to tell this story, and I shall not dwell on it. Suffice it to say these men could not answer my questions. So I walked out of that Bible School some thirty-five years ago, and have never been inside one since. They were teaching the Christian "belief" about 

God. I did not want that "belief" unless, as a result of it, the mellowing, hallowed Power of God came into my life. I found the peace, the joy, the power, the satisfaction which can come only from the actual Presence of the Spirit of God in the life. It never takes an honest seeker very long to find God, provided that seeker seeks where God is. But he can search for God a million

No. These dreams of bringing the Four Freedoms to humanity are dreams, impossible of fulfillment. Laudable dreams to be sure, but dreams nevertheless. 

Even if it were possible to eliminate all European and Asiatic political intrigue-even if it were possible to distribute the hours of work and the wealth of the earth even if it were possible to set up a system of world government in which peace and plenty were the order of the day, such a government would not last. Such a wonderful state of world affairs will never be until man is placed in his proper relation to God. 

That cannot be done by legislation. It can only be done when man finally comes to realize what the Power of 

Almighty God can do for him, right here on the earth.

Union with God is man's natural state. Without that conscious union, even in the conditions the Four Freedoms aim for, man would still be incomplete. He would be living a lop-sided life. He would be attempting to set up a perfect world order on a foundation of imperfect man. He would be attempting to usurp the

office of God, and he would be attempting to bring peace and perfection to the human race on a foundation of human blood. Both are totally impossible. 

Neither the blood of "gods", nor human blood can have anything to do with straightening this old world out. No. This world cannot be straightened out until man realizes the fact that the ever-existent Spirit of God lives in man, and can be, and should be the controlling factor in every man's life. The Spirit of God, the Creator of the universe is all-powerful. That Spirit is allcreative. It is almighty. We of this earth want power. 

Some of us want peace. Our American citizenry wants a fuller, richer life. It talks about "abundance." Yet it believes that such a state of bliss can be brought about by the sword. And while it believes that, it completely eliminates the One Great Power which can bring all these things to man, and will do so in the moment it's staggering Power becomes known.

So then, until the Power of the Spirit of God becomes universally known, this earth will not know peace. Look through past history. It is one bloody record of war after war. Brutality after brutality. Dictator after dictator. And all the while, all these warring nations have called upon God to come to their aid. Religion has been in the middle of all of these ghastly conflicts, just as it is in the middle of this one. 

That is where it belongs. For religion, as it exists today, founded as it is on traditions of thousands of years ago, is a purely human product. As such, it belongs in the middle of this purely human cataclysm which threatens to destroy half the population of the earth. And the One Power-the Power of the Spirit of God-which can bring the Axis fall overnight, is completely unknown on the earth, in spite of eleven major systems of religion. This little guide is bringing knowledge of that Power to humanity. I may not live to see the day dawn when the glory of the knowledge of the Power of the Spirit of God shall cover the earth as the waters cover the sea. Then again—I may.

So-in these fantastic days, fantastic statements will have to be made. Sweeping changes in our ideas of God will have to come. The Power of the Spirit of God that invisible Creator-will have to replace the theories of God which have never done other than bring bloodshed, misery, and disappointment in their wake. 

Yes. These are fantastic days. They are fast-moving days. A nation can either rise or fall overnight. Believe me when I say to you that the nation which first discovers the Spirit of God will be the nation through which the dazzling brilliance of God shall be manifested to humanity.

Once the fire is kindled-once men and women discover the existence of this amazing God-Power in their lives, it will not take long for the whole world to rush to the Source of that Power. I hope that somewhere, somehow, through the Power of the Spirit of God, some national leader will catch the beautiful gleams of God which, even now, are to be seen faintly over this bloody horizon. That national leader has not yet appeared. Perhaps he never will. But there is one thing which is as sure as the rise of tomorrow's sun-that is-

the manifestation of the Spirit of God on this earth will have to come before this war is ended, and before the world is permanently made aware of the priceless gem of the Love and Power of God. Humanity is wasting it's efforts to bring peace without first discovering the Power of the Spirit of God in human lives, and building the future with God the Supreme Ruler, instead of being dragged around in the mud by every political organization which masquerades under the name of "religion".

Man without the consciousness of the Power of the Spirit of God in his life is incomplete. And until man finds that consciousness, he always will be. They followed them faithfully. They listened to what preacher and priest had to say. And they went away broken hearted. They went away emptyhanded. They did not find God. Nor was any hope held

out to them that they ever could find God this side of the tomb.

That has been going on since the human race came to this earth. It will go on until someone calls attention to the fact that humanity has been looking in the wrong place for God. It will go on until the intelligence of man is willing to give Almighty God credit for being able to reveal His Power to man, right here on the earth where man needs it most. The very idea of a 

God who cannot, or will not take a hand in the present conflict against such human monsters as Hitler, Tojo, and Mussolini, is repulsive to those of us who know the sweet Presence of the Spirit of God in our lives. And make no mistake, God is a Reality. The greatest Reality humanity can ever know. Yet we have lived in utter and complete ignorance of this very real God. We have been led astray. Strange paths have been suggested. 

Strange "gods" have been offered to us. We have tried them. We have weighed them in the balances and found them wanting in everything God should be able to do for us. 

Now-thank God-we are travelling down the trail

which really does lead to God. You know-between every paragraph of this book I lean on the Power of 

God for guidance. I want to say the right things. More than that, I want the Spirit of God to be revealed to the reader through what I am writing. And I have the blessed satisfaction of knowing that, with God in my life, it cannot be otherwise.

Thousands of people have marvelled at the staggering growth of my Movement-Psychiana. Large international magazines have called it to the attention of the world. Some have poked fun at it. God forgive them. Others have had this, or that explanation for the phenomenon it is. But few know the Source of the Power behind the Movement. Few know the agonizing years spent trying to find God in present-day religions. 

Few will ever know the lonely days and lonelier nights when, on some lone mountainside, before the mornings light, I would wrestle with God for the answer to my longing for God.

tion masquerading as God. I wrestled until I found the sweet peace, the joy, the Power which, had I but known it, was in my life all the time, because the Spirit of God, my Creator, lived in me

Am I not fully conscious that all about me, as close as life itself, is the overaweing, overwhelming, omnipotent Power of my Maker. What circumstance can ever come into my life which the Spirit of God cannot guide me through? My own ability—you say? Oh no.

No beloved-not my ability. Not that. But let me tell you what has done it. It has been done because, when I realized the fullness of the Spirit of God in my life, I fell in love with God, just as you will when you know God. Falling in love with God means to stay with

God as much as possible. For where my heart iswhere my love is-where my Treasure is—there you will find me. 

And so having discovered the Power of the Spirit

of God living in me, all I did was to completely submerge my life with the Life which is God. I had no more ideas of my own after that. For to me to live, now, means the constant Presence of God. And I'm satisfied. Perfectly satisfied in my task of fighting religious superstitution which tells us it is God. Perfectly satisfied because there is, fighting with me, the Great 

Spirit of God. And with perfect union of my life with the Great Life of God, I can smile at any storm which the winds of the world may blow up.

The newspapers and magazines wondered. States Senate".

mighty Power of the Spirit of God. And I knew that

I had it. Therefore, I let my troubles be God's troubles. 

And what happened? Well what always happens when

God is in the life. The Spirit of God in me proved vastly superior to all the schemes of all religions, and, though I felt these attacks, I never worried about them. 

I knew that with the Power of God in my life, the fight was not mine-it was God's. And God has a habit of doing all things well. And God has a habit of completely protecting the one that leans on God for shelter in time of storm. And so, while Federal District Attorneys and a dozen other men were tearing their hair in an effort to eliminate your guide, he sat in his home smiling at the puny efforts of man to interfere with the works of the Spirit of God. 

Why did I smile? Was the smile a sarcastic smile? 

Oh no. Nothing like that. I smiled because the Great

Spirit of God in me knew that I loved Him, and I knew that no earthly power can compete against the Power of Almighty God, and my confidence was fully justified. You see—my life has been completely hidden in the Great Overlife which is God. The Spirit of the

Eternal God, Whom I love and Whom I serve, put

Its protecting arms about me. And there, in the cleft of the Rock of the Love of God, I stayed, fully sheltered and protected from all earthly harm. 

There shall I ever stay. I wouldn't want to live if

I had to live without the consciousness of the Spirit of 

God in me. I couldn't live. 

I couldn't live. For life without God just wouldn't be life at all. It would be the drab thing so many lives are without God. Great Loving Hand of God.

There would be no object in my leaving the God I love and believe in. What would be accomplished?

Nothing. For to me, life means God. And God means 

Life. And, so long as I keep my little earthly life hidden in the Great God I love, let the storms come.

day bow in love and honor. Not because God wants anyone to bow to Him, but because the Infinite Love which is God some day shall be known to all men and all nations. That day may not be too far away. It cannot be too far away.

Humanity has reached a crisis in its affairs. This crisis calls for the Presence and Power of God. That is the day which is coming. That is the future of the human race. The quicker we come to know the True God, not the "god" of religious myth and tradition, the sooner shall we be free to live our lives, individually and nationally, in the full consciousness of God in those lives.

THE FIFTH STEP 

Marry God

HE next step we shall take along the pathway that leads to God is a very important step. 

It involves a marriage ceremony. You are going to marry God. By the consummation of this marriage, God and you will become one.

The Robinson family had a wedding in it this week. 

Our son, Ensign Alfred Bruce Robinson, a Navy pilot, married his schoolgirl sweetheart. The wedding ceremony was performed in Miami, Florida where Alfie is stationed for the present. 

In this present marriage, Alfie and his sweetheart,

Annette, were joined together in the bonds of matri-

mony. They became man and wife. They became, so the ceremony stated, one. That means they will share together all of the many experiences of life. Their joys, their sorrows, their ups and their downs will be carried jointly.

In sickness, in health, this sweet young couple will walk along life's pathway hand in hand. No matter how stormy the sea may become, these two, wedded together for life, will share the tempests along with the calm. No matter what life holds for them, they will be found together, sharing alike the future. They truly love each other, so the natural sequence was a wedding. 

You changed friendship of God for love towards

God. The next step then must naturally be a marriage between you and God. Marriage is usually a blessed event, but more so when the contracting parties are God and man. Earthly marriages can be broken, but a marriage with God-never.

Now in this Robinson marriage, Alfred will provide 

Annette with whatever she needs for her complete happiness. He will love and cherish her until death parts them. And I trust that will be a long way off, al-

though with a Naval Torpedo Bomber you never can tell. However, as long as they live, this is what Alfred will do. 

Annette, in turn, will love Alfred. She will live with him. Their lives will be one life. They are united. 

Whenever Annette needs help, Alfred will provide that help. Whenever she needs cheer and comfort, my 

Alfie will provide that cheer and comfort. Whenever she needs rest, she can rest upon his shoulder. And, the love and affection being mutual, as a result of this marriage the Law of God will be completely fulfilled. 

That is an earthly marriage.

But, at this stage of your journey, you are about to enter a marriage of far greater significance. You are to marry God. The great God whose mighty Power was sufficient to cause this earth, and probably a million more to fly from His fingers at a word. This magnificent Power will be united with you in exactly the same manner as my Alfie and Annette are united. 

Whatever Alfie has Annette can have. And she wont need to ask for his possessions, she merely takes them because she and Alfred are united for life. Their lives

are the same. Their lives are one. And the wealth of one becomes the wealth of the other. 

This means that all the Power, all the Might, all the Love, all the Security there is in the Realm of the 

Spirit of God becomes yours when, wholly and unreservedly, you give yourself away to God. In this earthly marriage, someone had to give the bride away. But in this marriage of God and you, you give yourself away. 

You do this because you love God. You do this because you know God. You have had an experience of the Power of God while walking along this wonderful pathway together. You know that God loves you so much that He placed at your disposal, for your instant use, everything God has. He has been willing to give much to you, so you, in part, are willing to give much to God. In fact, after carefully weighing the matter, you are willing to give yourself. That's better than giving anything you have.

For when God and man unite, man has nothing he can give God. There is nothing he can do for God. But there is much that God can do for man and give man. 

He will give his all to anyone who will take it. God

will give to those who love Him, first of all His very Life. Indeed God did that long before we even suspected that had been done. But now that we know a little about just how much God has placed at our disposal, we gladly give back our physical life, and all our efforts, that this life and these efforts may be put to work for God, under the guidance of God, and filled with the 

Power of God. 

In other words, when you are married to God, your physical life becomes the channel through which the invisible life and Power of God are manifested to others. God, so to speak, entrusts you with His choicest gifts, wonderful gifts they are too, and you, loving

God, and seeing the Godless condition of the human race, are used by God to carry the message of freedom to those who, while probably very religious, have never really known God or any of the Power of the Spirit of God. For it is not possible to know God,-to be married to God—and not immediately go to work, carrying the liberating message of the Power of God to the whole universe.

If you carry out this commission faithfully, it is

quite likely that God will make of you a guide who shall bring many to the glorious knowledge of the Power of God. That is all God exists for. That was the object of the creation in the beginning, and it makes no difference where you place that beginning. 

What difference does it make whether God instantly created man from the dust of the ground, or whether He made the process of evolution the channel through which His Spirit could make man? The method of the creation is a bit aside from the immediate point. It has significance however, but not here. For no matter what the method of creation may have been, the object is constant.

God has never had any other objective than to reveal His Presence and Power to the human race. It has taken a long time to bring man to the place where he is even willing to admit that, as so many tell me, "there must be some sort of spiritual power that can step into this ghastly world picture and bring peace". 

Men and women everywhere are subconsciously coming to see that we have been told very little about God which is of so constructive a nature that it can help us here and now.

But men and women are beginning to think. And when they do that, God comes closer. And when God comes closer, He draws man closer. If man makes another step closer to God, then the Infinite God makes another step towards man, and the first thing you know they are in each other's arms. What a hallowed moment that is. God and man united in a sacred wedding which all eternity shall not disturb. All eternity wouldn't want to if it could. For man and God, they live together through all eternity. 

There is no period of "testing" after death. There is no session in "purgatory". Nor is there a big book in the skies which must be opened to find out whether we have made the grade or not. All that, I repeat, is theological tapestry. When man discovers the amazing Power of the Spirit of God, and consciously unites himself with God in a marriage, as you are doing, that man can never leave God. If their lives are lived together here, they will be lived together through all eternity. For God is life. And Life is Life and not death in any sense of the word. As a matter of fact, if we, here on earth, could get as close to God as it is possible to get, we

should be able to destroy that last enemy of mandeath-physical death.

God can do that. God has done that. And God can do that working through us, if we will let Him. Unfortunately we have so little faith in God that we do not even believe that God can bring to us, through the Power of His Spirit in us, what few things we need materially and spiritually to make our lives like His Great Life. 

So there's little chance of our ever being able to destroy the last enemy of man through the Power of God until we have a bit more confidence in God than we have now.

Yet it is completely possible for us to live so close to God that what God can do, we, through the Spirit of God in us, can also do. Is this asking too much? Is this too far-fetched, think you? How can it be if the 

Spirit of God lives in us? And we have seen that this

Spirit most assuredly does live in us-otherwise we could not live at all. But to be married to God-to unite the life which is God with the Life in us, is just simply giving back to the vast ocean of Power that is God, the little bit of life God gave us in the first place.

That's all God asks. He says: "Give it back to me.

I gave you physical life, and put My Spirit in your physical life—now you give that physical life back to me, and I'll show you how beautiful a thing I can make out of it". God gave you life in the first place. For some unknown reason, religion, as we know it, sepaated man from God. It taught that we were on the high-road to hell if we did not believe "this" or "that".

Ignorant man, not having the capability of judging the soundness of these theological theories, believed it. And the harder he believed the farther he got away from God. Now, in this age when all humanity will either find God or perish, God says "Come back to me. Give me that life back again. Come home where you belong. I want to give you a super-abundance of everything I have, but I can't do that if you wont come to me and get it”.

So, for hundreds of thousands of years, man has been afraid of God. He saw God in the lightning and was afraid. He was taught to be afraid by false religious leaders. He was told that the wrath of God would run him down, and finally hurl him into hell

unless he followed the preachments of the "holy-men". 

And man listened. Poor superstitious man listened. 

And the more he listened, and the more afraid of God he became, the farther he got away from God. 

And, I'm sorry to say, in spite of the enlightenment of the past few decades, man is still listening to theological preachments which, instead of revealing the Spirit of 

God to man, separate God from man. 

And all the while, God is saying: "Come back to 

Me. Come back to me. I want to give you in abundance the very things you are hoping to find without Me. 

And I want to give you those things here and now, not in heaven where you wont need them". But unwise man, listening to those who would underwrite God for him, is still following down paths which might lead to God at some later date, but which certainly cannot disclose the Power of the Spirit of God to man here and now.

It is incomprehensible to me why our religious bodies insist on teaching such inadequate and futile concepts of Almighty God, making it almost impossible for humanity to find God, when, if they only had one look at

the beauty which comes into the life through the indwelling Power of the Spirit of God, how much brighter would the world be.

Men and women want God. They would far rather go to a place where God is really to be found than they would sit, Sunday after Sunday, listening to impossible theories of God which never have been able to reveal God to anyone. Let alone save the world. This world needs saving. The American people know that it needs saving. They also instinctively know that somewhere there must exist the Spiritual Power which can save it. Yet, with all this heart hunger for God, our religious bodies insist on telling us we cannot ever find God unless we believe that Almighty God, after creating man "in sin and iniquity”, died on a cross to save man from the sins he was born with. 

The creation of man was an event of far greater import than the "salvation" of man. Yet no cross was necessary for that world-shattering event. And neither is a cross necessary in order that man may find God. 

The legend of the cross goes back some 25,000 years. 

The original cross was the Swastika in reverse. It re-

That fact is there. The whole world knows it. The 

Christian Church knows it. And you and I know it.

Therefore, regardless of whether it hurts the feelings of religionists or not, I must make the statement and repeat it o'er and o'er, that this story is not the way to God.

I don't care how many people say they believe it. 

I don't care how many preachers, rabbis, and priests preach it. It is not the way of God, nor can it be. 

If that story was of God, it would be able to save

this world from this mad orgy of death and destruction. It would be able to bring conscious knowledge of the Power of God to humanity, and, through the 

Power of God, humanity would be able to knock off the Axis crew in short order. It could do that through the Power of the Spirit of God, not through tanks, submarines, bombs and other hellish contraptions.

The apostle Paul was walking along a road one day and he saw a sign, we are told. That sign said: "TO

THE UNKNOWN GOD." Then Paul told the folks that the unknown God they ignorantly worshipped, he, Paul, would declare unto them. But he did nothing of the sort. The point here is that the churches of that day had "AN UNKNOWN GOD." The churches of our time still have an unknown God. If they would, they can be shown the matchless truths of God. But they will not. They love their traditions too well. 

Now, in the face of the fact that this world does not know the Power of the Spirit of God, how should those of us who have married God and are living with 

Him act? What should be the daily routine of our lives? Well, let us see.

The very first thing is to keep the connection you have with the Spirit of God, alive and the fire burning brightly. This means a constant getting together alone with God. True, the Spirit of God is always with you, but, as you do with your husband, you must have definite periods which are set aside for quiet communion with God. Quiet talks with God. These will be the mainspring of your life with God. In these moments you will be filled anew with the conscious Power which only actual contact with God can bring.

So have as many of these moments in which you talk with God as possible. You will still be in the world of course, but you will not be of it, because in the very moment your life is consciously joined with the Life of God, you belong to God. You are no longer your own. As you make these rendezvous with God, you will look forward to them. They will call you from the cares of this world. 

There, all alone with God, you can make all your desires and needs, if there should be any, known to the 

Father, and while you are making them known, you can rest in the absolute assurance that no sooner are

your desires made known than they are granted. That is satisfaction. That really is God.

There will come to you in these moments of communion with God, a hallowed peace the like of which you have never experienced before. It will be the peace of God. It does pass all understanding. It is permanent. Coming from God, your earthly body may be hard put to feel that peace and be able to contain yourself. 

But as you live longer and longer with God, this blessed peace will become deeper and deeper. Soon, you will discover it is fathomless. Just as fathomless as Almighty God is. For God is that Peace. 

There will be moments of superlative joy, too. Moments in which the tears will stream down your cheeks, as the very fullness of the Power of God is allowed to shine into your soul for just an instant. You could not stand the glory of the fullness of the brightness of God, and live. You are still a human being, having the Spirit of God in you. But these moments with God, wellthey are so full of the joy and peace of God that I would give my life any time to be able to enjoy the peace and the Presence of God eternally. Some day I

shall. Some day, all who have discovered the Spirit of 

God here, will know the fullness of God. That day will not be "in the sky." It will be right here on earth.

I am not saying that some day, far hence, this material earth shall not be destroyed, although I can see no sense in God making such a wonderful world and then destroying it. But some day, every man and woman on the face of this earth shall know the shekinah glory which comes only from a consciousness of the fullness of the Spirit of God in the life.

This glorious day could come in tomorrow if the people would have it so. If our religious bodies exchanged old threadbare theories of God for the dazzling, scintillating brightness of the Presence and Power of God, they could bring that day in. But they wont.

So you and I shall have to do the work they should be doing. 

My mother was a great Christian lady. A very devout Christian, who was big enough to know that God was a bit bigger than Christianity. She loved God. 

There's no question about that. She really loved God.

She seldom talked about Jesus-it was always God. 

She had a favorite hymn which is running through my mind now. Here is part of it: "Oh, to be nothing, nothing, Only to lie at His feet;

A broken and emptied vessel, For the Master's use made meet."

Her son is not like that. He does not want to lie broken and empty at the feet of God. He wants action. 

He wants to be up and doing. He wants to see the glories of the knowledge of God brought to humanity as fast as possible. God cannot use broken vessels. They don't hold anything. Nor can God use men who are content to lie at His feet while humanity is blasting itself into perdition so fast that if it is not stopped quickly, it will never recover from its wounds. 

Do you wonder why the Spirit of God drives me into action when I see the desolation on the earth today? 

Do you wonder why I tear myself to pieces in an attempt to bring knowledge of the saving Power of the 

Spirit of God to humanity, when I know that Spirit

exists here and now, just begging men and women to recognize it? There's no wonder about it in my life. 

I know why I do it. I just cannot stand to know the fullness of the Power of the Spirit of God in my life, and not shout the news of it from the house-tops. So will it be with every man and woman upon whose heart Almighty God has laid the burden of the absence of 

God from the world.

We are told that the man Jesus, of the Christian Church, one of the greatest spiritual prophets this world has ever known, stood on a hillside overlooking his favored City-Jerusalem, one day. He was a Jew -a Syrian Jew. Yet in that Jewish heart pulsated the Love and the Spirit of God.

Standing there, and looking upon that City which he loved, He said: "Oh, Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou who stonest the prophets and slayeth those sent unto you, how off would I have gathered you, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wing-BUT YE 

WOULD NOT."

And I can understand how His heart nearly broke as He stood there. You see-this Jewish prophet knew

the Power of the Spirit of God. He had that Power in Him. And, as is the case with your author, He knew that, through the Power of the Spirit of God in Him He could have gathered not only the City of Jerusalem unto God, but the whole world. But Jerusalem would not. Nor would the world at that time. 

When it gets through with this war, which hasn't even started yet, it may be ready for the truths of God. you

Now a word to Christians-if anyone ever tells that Jesus had any mission on this earth other than to reveal the Spirit of God to men, you correct them. 

I know the Christian Bible. I know what it teaches. 

The whole life of that Jewish prophet who was baptized with the Power of the Spirit of God as I am baptized, had but one mission. That was not to claim to be Almighty God, but to reveal the Spirit of 

God the Father to the whole world. Read your Bible and watch for this if you do not believe me. 

Yes. He stood there. Tears streaming down his sad face. For it must have been sad. The face of every prophet of God must be sad. The weight of the sufferings of humanity make it sad. Colliers Weekly said

of your author: "He has the look of a tired matinee idol." No. I'm not a matinee idol. I am tired though, and if I did not know how to renew my strength through the Power of the Spirit of God, I'd have passed out of this life long ago. 

But you see, the Eternal Spirit of God is my refuge just as it will be your refuge, now that you are living with God. And something else, underneath you, all around you, as close as life, are the everlasting arms of the Father-God. How can you fail? How can you be discouraged? How can you fail to respond to the promptings of the Spirit of God with such a great 

Power in you, and available to you every moment of the day and night.

If earthly problems arise, and they will, then at once get alone with your Friend-that Great Source of all Power. Take those problems into the Great 

Heart of God. You know you can do that. Because, in you, closer than your life, is the Spirit of God. And it will not take much of an effort to get alone with God, and there, in the sanctity of that hallowed room where 

God is, you can then not only take your burden to God,

but you can get instant relief. You leave your problem there. You tell the Spirit of God what it is, you throw the Power of God into play against that problem, and then forget it. It's out of your realm of influence then. 

God has it. And He knows what to do with it. 

Life with God, then, becomes a beautiful thing. 

It is a constant joy. You overcome every one of life's perplexities. And you really don't do it at all. God does it. You just know where the Source of all wisdom, power, and help is, and you at once run to God, audibly tell God what the trouble is-and then forget it. If necessary, the very heavens will fall before you will ever be disappointed. It is not God's job to disappoint us. It is God's job to supply our every need, answer our every problem, and then, when full confidence in God is manifested, to lead us upward and onward unto that Perfect Day which is coming, and in which, the glory of the Presence of God shall be the 

Guiding Light of all life, here on earth.

How long it's going to take this world to awaken to the staggering Power available to it I do not know. 

The radio was playing a hymn while I was eating lunch.

Let me give you the first verse and then make a few comments on it, to try to explain to you what I mean. 

This old hymn is a favorite of mine, and has been for years. Here it is: "Eternal Father, strong to save, Whose arm has bound the restless wave; 

Who bidst the mighty ocean deep,

It's own appointed limits keep: 

Oh hear us when we cry to Thee, For those in peril on the sea."

Now we all know that the Eternal God of this universe the Creator of the universe, must be strong to save, or to do anything else needed to bring peace, joy and happiness to this badly distorted human race. We also know that it is God's Power that keeps the boundless oceans in their appointed place. We know, if we look above some moonlight night, that it must be the mighty Power of the Spirit of God which keeps you dazzling array of little lights called stars in their appointed orbits, too. We know all that. 

But at this point, this wonderful hymn gets away off the track. It begins to doubt the ability of God to do

for us now, the things we need. For it asks God to "hear us when we cry to Thee." Now let us look at that statement. In the first place it is predicated upon an absent God. A God who is far away somewhereprobably in the sky. Then again, the verse asks God to hear us. This assumes that there is a possibility of 

God's not hearing us, which, or course, brings us back to the "absent" God, separated by millions of miles from the family he created-incidentally, His greatest creation.

We seem to insist on the "absent" God all the time. 

Now as a matter of fact, there is no absent God. Nor is there any need for us to ask God to hear us. There is a great need for the world to recognize God, and I shouldn't be a little bit opposed to humanity crying out to God for help. It will do that before this war is over. But, so long as we humans consider God either dead, crucified, or absent, we cannot grasp the significance of the fact that God is not dead, was never dead, was never killed by anyone, but, to the contrary, is the greatest, most dynamic Spirit on the earth today.

Moreover, it never has been necessary for us to ask

God for anything. But here is this concept. The whole world of religion has it. Teaches it, in fact. But the fact is that God has already given Power to us, because the Spirit of God lives in us, to "save those in peril on the sea" or those in peril anywhere else. I wonder how long it will take America to wake up and have a little grain of confidence in the fact of the present existence of Almighty God. I wonder how long it will take America to awaken to the staggering fact that it could, if it really wanted to, find God instantly, and, through the Power of the God it found, manifest the Spirit of God on the earth to the very fullness of its Power.

I wish I knew of some more effective way to call the attention of this warring, brutal civilization to the Power of the Spirit of God. Frankly, I see no hope for it until someone tells it the truth about the Presence of God on the earth. It looks now as if the nations are going to continue to slug it out, murdering, pillaging, torturing fellow human beings unrestrained. Yet, all the time, if America only knew it, there does exist a spiritual Power of such magnitude that it can step into

this bloody picture, and reveal to mankind how this ghastly slaughter can be instantly stopped, because the

Power of the Spirit of God still lives in us. 

Every so often someone calls for a "day of prayer,” and here is the old story of the absentee, impotent, departed God again. Prayer is not the answer. We can "pray" till the crack of doom, and when it cracks, we still shall have received no benefit whatsoever from God. We cannot be helped by God through the avenue of prayer. It is foolish to ask God to do something He has already done. It is foolish to ask God to bring peace to the human race when Almighty God has already done every single thing He can do to bring peace. 

He has given us the Power of the Spirit of God. 

We have that Power within us now. Yet we absolutely refuse to recognize it. The chances are that we do not know we possess any such marvellous Power as the Spirit of God. Our religious bodies do not tell us that. 

They are the originators of the "absentee god" theory. 

And the American people still seem to subscribe to that theory, false as it so manifestly is.

But the day will have to come when men and women

the wide world over will discover this God-Power. 

When they do, and when they look back on the ruthless human butchery they engaged in, they will hang their heads in crying shame. Their only excuse will be:

Why didn't someone tell us about the Power of the Spirit of God before; we did not know anything about it; we thought that God was Jesus who was killed on a cross.

Yes, that will be the only excuse they will have to offer "WE DIDN'T KNOW." When that day comes, God could, if He would, remind them that ignorance of the Law is no excuse. But He won't. In 

His great and infinite love he will say to the warring nations: "NOW YOU DO KNOW. NOW YOU DO KNOW THAT MY SPIRIT LIVES IN YOU WITH ALL OF ITS POWER. NOW YOU CAN GO AND LIVE TOGETHER IN PEACE, HAP- PINESS, AND JOY. FOR I DONT WANT YOU TO WORSHIP ME, I JUST WANT YOU TO RECOGNIZE THAT I HAVE GIVEN YOU, FREELY AND BOUNTEOUSLY, ALL THE POWER THERE IS IN ME."

That's about what God will say to the nations. But how much better would it be-how much human suffering could be saved if, here and now, our religious organizations would bring to men and women, not a theory of God which is over 100,000 years old, but the actual working knowledge of the Power of the Spirit of God, living within us, and abundantly able to bring into our lives the fullness of the Power of God. In fact, that fullness abides with us now. God has already brought it to us. What we must do is discover that fact and take advantage of it.

So, then, in living with God, let the object of this glorious union always be the equipping of yourselves to be able to intelligently bring to men and women, knowledge of the Power of the Spirit of the Living God -in them.

I don't know that I can give you too much personal instruction after this point. Another and Greater Guide than I am will take control of your wedded life, and you will be far safer in the hands of the Spirit of God than you can be in my hands. I can lead you to God. I can take you down the pathway which, in your case,

had led to God. I can put you in touch with the Power of God, and the marvellous brilliance and glory and Power there is in God. I have brought you this far. 

Now I shall have to leave you. I shall have to go back over the same trail and bring another group to 

God. I shall leave them here as I am leaving you here. 

What you do with your life now that you have met

God will be an individual matter between God and you. With your life completely submerged with the 

Life which is God, all there remains for you to do is the very same thing I am doing. Go out into the highways, the byways, the hedges. Seek the lost. Compel them to come in and partake of the bounteous repast God has prepared for those who realize the Power of the Spirit of God in them. 

I don't doubt for one moment that this earth will soon see the greatest manifestation of spiritual Power in history. God will speak. Humanity will not be allowed to completely annihilate itself. For it so happens that there are a few people who know God. To some of these God has given the Spirit of God without

measure. When necessary, those favored souls will speak. Rather, God will speak through them. 

Let me give you one parting little word of advice.

The closer you keep to God, the more you will become like God. The more God-like you become, the more men and women will you be able to bring into the fold of the love and Power of God. So shall we not concentrate on laying our plans so that God, through us, makes His wonderful Presence known to as many as possible in as short a time as possible. 

Remember you cannot take a single step without God. Every step you take, regardless of how daring it may be, cannot be too big a step for God. So step out. 

God is real. God is true. God is very powerful. Let go your all. Some day you'll find it again.

Some day, when the storms of life are all over, you and I shall stand in the shining presence of God, knowing that we have done our little part well. Then He will call us up higher into the fullness of His glorious beauty. It will be nice if, when we arrive there, we can hear the words: "Come ye blessed of my Father-enter into the joys which I have prepared for you."

That will be a happy day beloved. Here, down here, we piddle along in our weak way, and were it not for the omnipotent Power of the Spirit of God in us, we should have fallen by the wayside long ago. But we don't fall. Some day, not too far off, we shall lose ourselves in that Great Love which, before the world was, loved us with an everlasting love. "Were the whole realm of Nature mine, That were a present far too small: 

Love so amazing, so divine, Demands my soul, my life, my all." 

Shall we not give it back to God? 

Moscow, Idaho, March 1, 1943.
