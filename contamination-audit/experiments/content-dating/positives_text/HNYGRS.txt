PREFACE 

S children at school, before we were able to judge for ourselves, we were taught that "The Earth is round like an orange." This idea, having been drummed into our ears, gradually became a fixed belief; and as the majority of people never trouble to investigate the subject for themselves, meekly accepting the statements of their early teachers, they unconsciously receive as fact the popular though evolutionary theory of a globular earth. 

Now it is significant that the argument adduced in support of this theory will not bear critical examination. 

If man uses the senses which God has given him, he gains knowledge; if he uses them not, he remains ignorant. Mr. R. A. Proctor, who has been called "the greatest astronomer of the age," says: "The Earth on which we live and move seems to be flat." Now he does not mean that it seems to be flat to the man who shuts his eyes in the face of nature, or who is not in full possession of his senses: no, but to the average, common sense, wide-awake thinking man. If the earth seems to be what it is not, how are we to trust our senses? Who will tell us then, where is the mistake?

The whole weight of the Roman Catholic Church was against Galileo and Copernicus. The decree of March 5, 1616, voiced by the Pope, was not in favor of their teachings. The "Catholic Belief" today ostensibly gives the "infallible" teachings of the "R. C. Church." This book is in favor of the work of Copernicus, and teaches, as truth, a theory once pronounced as "absurd," "false," "heretical," and "contrary to scripture." vii

The truth will always find advocates-men who care not a snap of their fingers for popular opinion, whatever form it may take, whilst they know that they are the masters of the situation. Because an opinion is old and well established, it is no evidence it is truth, for there are many gray-haired errors in the world; and truth crushed to the earth will rise again, and though the weeds of tradition have grown in rank profusion about its grave, it will have a resurrection morning.

Now I do not make claim to entire originality in the ideas presented in this book. However, I have tried to follow the maxim of some good thinker who said: "Think for thyself." And though I have thought for myself, I have not scorned to glean from other fields, on the principle that a borrowed tool is better than no tool at all. 

But these borrowed thoughts are now my very own and none can ever rob me of them. For matter contained in this book we are indebted to such men as: Dr. Samuel 

B. Rowbotham, Prof. Wm. Carpenter, Prof. Alex. Gleasen, Albert Smith, Fred H. Cook, C. S. DeFord, and others; nearly all of whom are now dead.

That the earth is an outstretched stationary plane, founded upon the waters of the deep; the sun, moon, and stars in motion above; over the whole being the firmamental vault, or floor of heaven, represents briefly and fairly the astronomy and cosmogony taught throughout the Bible. The Bible refers to no world other than the earth upon which we live. The sun, moon, and stars are described as lights that were made specially to serve this earth. Above the stars is heaven. The fact that there is such a place as Heaven, according to the Scriptures, does not require much demonstration. We never read in the 

Bible that heaven, besides being above the earth, is also below it; neither do we read that heaven is all around. 

Our Saviour gave thanks looking up to heaven, and viii

taught his disciples thus to pray. Christ ascended up to heaven, and the promise is he will return again to this earth. Surely some difficulty would be experienced in locating such a "speck" in "space," dashing about so many different ways, at such a speed as we are taught to believe, were the earth a revolving globe. 

Now the plain proved facts will present to the reader, the stupendous and startling truth, that astronomers, and scientific philosophers, have been weighed in the balance of exactitude and found wanting! It will be seen that modern astronomy has not one fact to support it. Their whole argument is based on their "ifs," "buts," "suppositions," "assumptions," and "hypotheses."

S to the origin of this earth, when and how the world began to evolve itself, we are told that in the beginning there was gas, or a "nebulous cloud," according to scientists and evolutionists. This is rather a difficult subject to deal with, because there was scientifically, no beginning just an "eternal evolution of substance." 

Anyway there was a time when this "nebulous cloud" arose-never mind where it came from, for no scientist has yet ever attempted an explanation on this point, although its existence requires some accounting for, considering it was inorganic matter, and it possessed the powers of "feeling and inclination." According to La 

Place, "The particles forming the cloud were very hot,' he was not there to see, but I only mention this because some scientists, like Herbert Spencer, state that the "embryo universe" was cold. Anyway, hot or cold, the particles by universal suffrage, or by some other method, unknown to scientists, took upon themselves to form the "Solar system"; therefore, it was necessary that this "diffused fire mist" should condense a little and move its particles a little closer together, "according to Newtonian laws."

There is a difference of opinion among the men of science concerning the origin and destiny of this earth. 1

Some geologists inform us that this earth had its origin in fire, others in water; some contend that it will become extinct by a lack of water-a drought; others by too much water, a flood; others, that the end is likely to come by fire-a burn out; others that it will be caused by intense cold—a freeze out. They all feel sure of their opinions, and yet no two of them quite agree. 

Now concerning gravitation, C. Vernon Boyd, F. R. S., A. R. S. M., M. R. I., in his paper "The Newtonian Constant of Gravitation," says: "It is a mysterious power which no man can explain, of its propagation through space all men are ignorant,"-Proceedings of the Royal

This is certainly an honest and authoritative confession of astronomical ignorance of their fundamental position.

Newton himself strongly felt that the impossibility of rationally accounting for action at a distance through an intervening vacuum, was the weakest point of his theory. The doctrine of universal gravitation then is a pure assumption."-Modern Review, October, 1890. 

This "absurd" law, or "mysterious power, which no man can explain," the existence of which has never been proven, and of which its supposed operation through space "all men are ignorant," amounts to nothing more or less than an empty assumption.

Astronomers themselves consider that gravitation is absolutely necessary to their system, in fact the very

foundation of it all. But I deny that there is any such force in existence, and will express my denial in the language of Sir Isaac Newton himself, written in a letter to his friend, Dr. Bentley, February, 1692, just before the death of Newton; "That gravity should be innate and inherent in matter, so that one body can act upon another at a distance, is to me so great an absurdity, that I think no one who has, in philosophical matters a competent faculty of thinking, can ever fall into it." 

I agree with Sir Isaac, for I, too, think it "so great an absurdity" that I shall never "fall into it."

INERTIA OF MATTER AND INHERENT FORCE 

Inertia is that property which renders a body incapable of putting itself in motion when at rest, or coming to rest when in motion. When a stationary body begins to move, or a moving body comes to rest, it is not through any power of its own, but because it is acted upon by some external agency, which is called force. 

That no inanimate body can put itself in motion, is evident from our daily experience and observation. The rocks that we saw on the earth's surface years ago, are precisely in the same place today that they were then, and there they will remain forever unless some force removes them.

A moving body gradually comes to rest, when the force which put it in motion ceases to act; this is also due to the resistance of the air, and not by any agency of its own. 

A projectile may be thrown with such force as to be borne some distance in a straight line, without having its direction altered either by its weight or air resistance; but when the force is spent, its velocity diminishes, and it comes to rest.

Now since it requires force to act, whether that force be a pull or a push, I might inquire how did the planets ever tend to group themselves together about the sun, or speed off into space as we are taught to believe? Do they run on their own power? Does that force reside in them? Is it inherent force? It must have some source, some origin, some place of abode. Are the planets living organized bodies, or are they composed of dead unorganized matter? When they left the supposed nebula mass, did they separate, "move off" or "drop behind, on their own power or by some other force. Can a mass of dead matter at rest start itself into motion, or stop itself when once it is in motion? Can a pebble or your penknife start or stop itself? Then if, according to the inertia of matter and the first law of motion, a mass of dead matter can neither start nor stop its own motion; can it pull on some other mass of matter at a distance and start that mass into motion? These are pertinent questions, which, when carefully considered, lead to the conclusion that universal gravitation is a gross delusion, a presumptuous assumption.

EXHAUSITIVE ENERGY OF FORCE 

And now, in case the planets can and do pull on the sun, and the sun on the planets, as we are told, how does that pulling force act? If that force does not reside in the planets, if it is not innate force then the planets do not attract or pull on the sun, or the sun on the planets, and the force is not an attractive force. If, however, that force does reside in the planets, does it go out of the planets, pass across the intervening space between themselves and the sun, and on reaching the sun does it reverse itself and come back to the planets when they pull on the sun, and vice versa? Does that force

flow out from the planets to the sun and back again in a steady constant stream, or does it go in impulses or waves? If so, would it not meet itself coming back and thus interfere with its own motion, or counteract or nullify itself?

But does force ever reverse itself and return to its own source? Does force act that way? How often can you explode a stick of dynamite? What becomes of the force when dynamite is exploded? Does it return and concentrate itself in its original source ready for subsequent action? Does force push or pull when it leaves its source?

Does dynamite accomplish the purpose for which it is used by a push or a pull? What is your answer? But is not force exhausted and wasted when it leaves its source or center instead of returning to that center? It is utterly impossible then for the planets to attract or pull on the sun or the sun on the planets, or on anything else, because force never reverses itself and returns to its own source, thus pulling on some object at a distance. On the contrary, when force acts and passes away from its source, no matter what that force may be, it radiates in all directions from that source and the farther it goes, the weaker it becomes, until it is finally entirely exhausted. Force then exhausts itself. It spends its strength in its action-it wastes its own power or energy in moving away from its source. Furthermore, when force is exerted and reaches an object, if that force is strong enough to move the object, it would push on the object and drive it farther and farther away from its source instead of pulling the object towards itself. Force pushes, it is repulsive not attractive; hence, we have here incontrovertible proof in the exhaustive energy of force, that gravity is an absurd assumption, a scientific myth.

ACTION OF ALL KNOWN FORCE 

If there is such a force as gravitation, as taught by our astronomers, then the action of that force violates the law of all other known forces. Now the action of all known force is eccentric, expansive, outward; that is, repulsive. This of course, is the direct opposite to attraction. The force of dynamite, powder, steam, heat, electricity, gas, air, water, lightning, mineral, vegetable and animal oils, and all combustible and explosive materials and elements, are all eccentric, outwards, and therefore repulsive, and not concentric or attractive forces. Repulsion is not attraction. All these forces are exerted eccentrically. They operate outwardly from their centers or sources. So then, if matter exerted any force at all, it would be eccentric, repulsive, instead of concentric or attractive; and bodies would be driven apart instead of drawn towards each other. 

Now we know from experience and observation, that the action of all force is expansive, outward, repulsive. 

Dynamite rips up the earth, steams bursts the boiler, powder speeds the bullet, lightning splits the tree, electricity runs the dynamo, wind topples over buildings and devastates, water breaks the dam and inundates. It is only by some intelligent and mechanical device that force can be made to pull, or draw objects concentrically. The hand may reach out and draw objects inwardly, but even then the pull is a push. This is the nature of force. Force pushes; it is repulsive, not attractive. The wheels that speed along our highways and run our factories of industry, exert a repelling force. All rotary movement, like a wheel in motion, whether it be a water-wheel, or a millwheel; all exerts a repulsive outward force. The grindstone that sharpens our tools, throwing off the water, exerts such

a force; and so would this earth if it were in motion-a revolving globe; nothing would remain on its surface. 

Even a magnet does not "draw" a needle towards it.

The needle, or a piece of soft iron, when brought within the magnetic current, which enters into and flows through the magnet, is pushed or impelled towards it. But the other "pole" of the magnet will "repel" the needle, because the current comes out at the negative end. So that there is quite as much evidence in the action of the magnet for "repulsion" as for "attraction"; but magnetic currents account for both.

So then, if gravitation exists, the nature of that force must be similar to the nature of all other known forces, which are eccentric, repulsive, expansive, and not concentric or attractive. Now, since all known force is expansive, repulsive, outward,-if there is such a force as gravitation it must, by the law of analogy be like all other forces; and therefore it would be a repulsive and not an attractive force. Therefore, we have conclusive evidence, by a knowledge of all known forces, that the supposed law of universal gravitation is an unproved and unprovable assumption.

EQUILIBRIUM 

My next proposition is that equal mass balances equal mass, and equal force exhausts the strength of equal opposing forces. Every mass of matter whether smooth or rough, regular or irregular, must have within itself a center of gravity, according to this universal law, or scientific principles of physics. The center is that pivot about which all its parts are balanced. The center of gravity is nothing more than the center of weight. Each mass retains its own weight, but inherent weight is a

very different thing from extraneous gravitation, or a pull from something outside a body. 

When a body is of uniform density, its center of gravity coincides with its center of magnitude. When one part of a body is heavier than another, the center of gravity is shifted and lies nearer the heavier part. Take a wheel for instance, of uniform density; both the center of motion and the center of gravity correspond with the center of magnitude. But let the same wheel, with its two lower spokes be of lead, while the center of magnitude and motion remain, the center of gravity has fallen or changed. Those who have made a study of natural philosophy or physics, are familiar with these laws. 

When two bodies, say lead balls, of equal weight are connected by a rod, the center of gravity will be in the center of the rod, they will balance each other when placed at equal distance from the fulcrum. They are then said to be in equilibrium.

But when two bodies of unequal weight are so connected, the center of gravity will no longer be at equal distance from the pivot, but will be nearer the heavier one. This same principle is illustrated by the teeterboard balanced on a bench, or by the common balance or scales used for weighing. The balance weighs correctly only when the arms of the beam are exactly equal. The weight of one exactly balances the weight of the other, so that the one side could not lift a pound more than the other without destroying the equilibrium, or throwing them out of balance.

In other words, we have mass acting against mass, equal weight working against equal weight, and equal force pitted against equal force; and just that instance either side exerts a greater weight, force or strength than

the opposite side, the law of equality is violated and it becomes unequal. The weight of one is all employed in balancing up the other; and all the weight, force, power, pull or gravity exerted by one is exhausted in neutralizing that of the other, so that neither body could attract or exert a force on anything beyond an equal mass, or outside its own circumference.

Two boats of equal weight, A and B, are connected with a rope, a man in A pulls the rope; action and reaction being equal, not only will the boat B move towards him, but the boat A, which he is in, will move with the same velocity towards B. If another boat were attached at either end, the law of equality would be destroyed, and they will not move with the same velocity towards each other. Hitch two horses together of equal strength, and let them pull against each other. Where will they move to? Will not each one exhaust all his strength on his opponent and not move him? Then how could they pull on some other object at the same time!

EXPANSIVE ENERGY OF HEAT 

The sun is said to be the center of universal gravitation, and the greatest of all bodies in the known universe possessing gravitation. But, on the principle already propounded in the foregoing proposition, the sun being a hot body could not possess the power of gravitation. 

Heat is expansive, it is eccentric, it is repulsive. Consider

the following quotation and estimate how much gravity is possessed by a body of so great heat. "The sun is a fiery ball one million three hundred thousand times larger than our earth; and several thousand times hotter than the most powerful electric furnace. The sun is pouring forth enough heat every second to melt a column of ice two and a half miles square, reaching from the earth to the sun, and in eight seconds of time it could turn the column from solid ice to steam." Some heat! Strange we ever had a "Glacial Period"-if the sun melted the ice formation of that period, it could have prevented it; therefore it never existed. 

Just figure out if you can how much "attraction of gravity" a body of such immense heat would have. Do not our scientists know that they teach that it was that eccentric, outward, repulsive force of the nebulous mass, a portion of which it is claimed still composes the sun, which originally "threw off” the earth and the planets? 

Then was it not the repulsive force of heat that threw them off? And now they would have us to believe that the same force which formerly repulsed is that force that attracts. Strange philosophy! But what reversed its action? Is repulsion attraction? And on the principle of the repulsive energy of heat how much "attraction" would "the most powerful electric furnace" exert on a fly, a wild goose, or an airship? And on this same principle of the repulsion of heat how great would be the "gravity" of this earth, with its supposed hot and molten interior, having only a thin shell or crust surrounding this molten matter, so thin that it frequently bursts through in a volcanic eruption? On this principle of the expansion and radiation of heat, it is positively certain that neither the sun-an alleged fiery, nebulous, seething mass; nor the supposed molten interior of "the globe"

would ever "tend to draw" anything towards its center or towards itself. Is it not the repulsive and expansive force of the sun's heat that affects the temperature of the air, land and sea, producing atmospheric changes, air currents, ocean streams and land conditions or change of seasons; melting ice, bursting buds, and generating warmth and light? The sun, then, being a hot body, is not an attractive force; hence there is no universal law of gravitation. And so clear is this that I state again that universal gravitation is a universal fake, because it is an unfounded assumption.

FORCE A DESTRUCTIVE AGENT 

Our present Newtonian-Copernican system teaches that the earth we inhabit is a planet, of very subordinate rank, that it is dashing and spinning about through space, rotating on its axis, equatorial motion, at the brain-reeling rate of 17 miles per minute; and that it is also moving in its ellipse or orbit, 18 miles per second, describing annually a circuit around the sun of about 600,000,000 miles, breaking all speed limits and speed records. The sun, at the same time, we are told, moves through space at the rate of 10 miles per second, around some mysterious center of gravitation, which center of gravitation must again revolve around some other center of gravitation still more mysterious; and so on indefinitely, because the law of universal attraction knows no rest. 

Now, let a force, it matters not what kind of a force it may be whether it be a wind force, a steam force, a dynamic force, an electric force or the force of gravity, whether it be a pull or a push, strong enough to move this vast earth, either on its axis or in its ellipse, pass over its surface, at such a terrific speed and note the result -it would be disastrous. If it were a pushing force,

everything would be crushed and driven into sea and earth; if it were a pulling force, everything would be lifted bodily off the sea and earth, and in either case the whole face of the earth would be devastated. 

Since motion creates friction and friction produces heat, if this earth so vast were dashing and spinning so many different ways through space, faster than any bullet, which speeding through space is often disfigured; or even faster than lightning speed; the friction set up by air resistance would cause it to disintegrate, or burn up. 

According to Duncan's "Astronomy" Pg. 260, the average velocity of meteors is about twenty-six miles a second, and at this speed most of the meteors burn up completely. Only a few comparatively ever reach the earth and are found. Now the speed of a meteor is only one-eighth of the alleged speed of the earth, yet the earth does not burn up or even warm up from that cause. 

But the sun is not only pulling the earth, but the earth is attracting, too. But the sun, being 1,300,000 times larger than the earth, is therefore that many times stronger than the earth, and therefore is doing the most of the pulling. The greater pull of the sun must overcome the weaker pull of the earth, even though the earth resisted: like a boy pulling in his kite, or a fisherman pulling in a fish. But the earth is constantly pulling the sun toward itself, or itself toward the sun, and does not resist. So that, if gravity pulls, when the sun got the earth started it would draw the earth to itself. But if it pushes, it would drive the earth farther and farther away from the sun all the time, so that in either case it would result in utter destruction or total loss. 

A very gentle wind moves light objects, but no wind storm, hurricane, cyclone strong enough to move this

earth has ever been known. There have been horrible earthquakes, which have swallowed up cities, destroying thousands of lives, and damaging property; fierce storms have raged, hurricanes and cyclones have devastated, and terrible explosions of dynamite, nitroglycerine, gas and other explosions have occurred, causing the loss of life and property, but none of these have shaken the earth or moved it out of its place. These disasters were caused by the force of these destructive agencies. What, then, would be the result of a force of gravity passing over the surface of this earth, if that force is great enough to move and actually does move this earth, at the terrific rate we are told? We feel the force of the slightest wind, but we do not feel this force of gravity. We know the destructive power of these other forces but as there are no such disastrous effects experienced, we can but logically conclude that there is no such cause or force as gravitation.

CONCENTRATION 

The astronomers tell us that "all bodies have a tendency to rush together into one mass." And this "tendency" which brings them together, we are told, is this law or force of gravitation. One writer goes so far as to inform us that the planets are "anxious" to all rush together, and that the nearer they approach each other the greater their "anxiety" becomes, so that at least it is "two-fold." But we might ask, why do they not "all rush together into one mass," since nothing lies between them but "empty space"? Bees swarm or "all rush together," whenever they have the "tendency" to make the attempt; and what is there to hinder them from doing so, for there is nothing between them but empty space; exactly as it is with the planets? And this "tendency,' 99

this "anxiety" to rush together, we are told, is constant and persistent; therefore the planets are always making a constant and persistent attempt to rush together. How is it then that they cannot accomplish their purpose? It is a foolish attempt for the astronomers to invent another force, which they call centrifugal force, to hinder the planets embracing one another. 

Just think of it. Nothing between you and your wife but "empty space," she anxiously, constantly, persistently tending to rush to you, and you attracting, pulling her with a force directly proportioned to the mass of your attractive personality, and still a persistent, repulsive force forever preventing her embracing you! Well, this is some science, some profound philosophy! And mind you, that pull of yours, together with her persistent tendency to rush to you is what prevents her from rushing off into space. So there she stands, poor thing! A pity some way cannot be devised whereby bodies attracted and pulled and tending to rush together may be enabled to do so. 

Here, then, we have an attractive force constantly and persistently pulling everything inwardly towards everything, and another force just so constantly and persistently pushing everything outwardly, away from itself.

A DEAD STANDSTILL 

We are told that the planets once did all rush off into space from their primitive nebulous mass. Something stopped them in their mad rush. That something was gravity. It was the attraction or pull of the sun that stopped them. The sun is working overtime in a mighty effort to prevent the planets from rushing off into space. 

This hard work of the sun is no doubt what keeps him so hot and red-faced.

Now, it requires more force to start a body at rest, than it does to keep it in motion after it is once started; hence, if the sun's centrifugal force exceeds its attractive force, when the planets were once started they would have rushed off forever into space.

A moving body is a resisting body. It takes more power or force to stop a body in motion than it does to start a body at rest. Therefore, if the sun continued to exert the same force on the planets after it got them stopped, that it did to stop them, they would be drawn back again to the sun; but if centripetal and centrifugal forces are equal, then the planets are at a dead standstill, for equal opposing forces prevent motion. Try it and see!

Now this principle is illustrated by the philosopher's third law of motion. Action is said to be the force which one body exerts on another subjected to its operation. 

Reaction is the counter-force which the body acted upon exerts upon the body acting. Reaction then, is always equal to action, and opposite to it in direction. So then, we try to push a cart up an incline; and we feel the reaction in the resistance it offers, and the force of its weight. 

A boatman pulls on his oars against the water; the water reacts and drives his boat in the opposite direction. A bird, when flying, strikes the air downward with the

wings; the air reacts and supports the bird.

Reaction often nullifies action. Take two balls of equal size and weight, suspended by a string; throw A against 

B with a definite force and a definite result will be produced. If A met with no resistance, it would rise to about the same height on the opposite side. But, encountering 

B, it imparts to it a portion of its motion, and both move on together though only half as far as A would have gone alone. The reaction of B is clearly equal to the action of A; for the latter loses just as much motion as the former gains, hence the reaction of B brings A to rest. 

A boy in a basket tries to lift himself by pulling on the basket but the power of resistance prevents him; action and reaction being equal, or equal opposing forces preventing motion, he remains at a dead standstill. It is like an irresistible force coming in contact with an immovable body, or like Eugene Field's gingham dog and the calico cat-they "eat each other up."-So these forces would eat each other up-neutralize themselves. If the law of gravity were true, and attraction and repulsion were equal, the earth would be at a dead standstill. GRAVITY AND WEIGHT 

"Gravitation" is a term derived from Gravitus, meaning weight. But how one weight can cause another weight to come towards it by reason of its weight, is a thing that no thinking person can understand.

Weight is scientifically defined as "a measure of the force of gravity." But weight is inherent in matter. It is merely a comparison of bulk with bulk as regards density, solidity and compactness. There is a distinction between inherent weight and extraneous gravitation. They may call it "gravitation" if they choose; but when an apple falls to the earth, it falls by its own weight when released from the branch on which it grows, and not because the apple has been pulled by the earth or the earth by the apple.

Why do not objects fall upwards? Gravity is supposed to act in every direction, especially upwards, while weight tends downwards. If gravitation were strong enough to move this vast earth and make tides it certainly would pull all loose and unattached objects away from the earth before it could move the earth, and so they would fall upwards. Objects move in the direction they are impelled or acted upon. 

A balloon will ascend, because bulk for bulk it is lighter than air. It will rise to an altitude or position, at which elevation it will come to rest, having found its balance or equilibrium. Should it lose its bulk by an escape of gas, it will collapse and descend to earth again, for the simple reason that its weight is greater than that of the air it displaces. Wood floats in water, while a piece of solid iron sinks, because mass for mass the wood is lighter than the water it displaces, whereas bulk for bulk the iron is heavier than the water. The denser a body the greater its weight, and recognizing this truth the scientists tell us, gravity is another name for weight. 

Let a man and a horse cross a swampy stretch of ground. They both will sink into the mud; but the horse will sink deeper because he is heavier than the man. 

This is said to be the "measure of the force of gravity" on the two bodies. But in reality it is a comparison of bulk with bulk. The greater bulk of the horse compared with the lesser bulk of the man, displaces a greater mass of mud than the man displaces, so the horse sinks deeper than the man; and each sink into the mud because they present to the mud a more solid and compact mass than the mud which they displace. 

Drop a stone from a bridge into the water below. 

The stone passes through the thin air, then the more

solid water into the mud below, and comes to rest on the solid earth beneath the mud. The solid earth is unyielding, the mud more yielding, the water still more so, and the air most of all. The earth stops the stone because it has the greatest power of resistance. Thus universal gravitation has been tried at the bar of Reason and it has been found to be an unsupported hypothesis, a gross delusion.

FIRING PROJECTILES 

If the earth went through space at the rate of eleven hundred miles in a minute of time, in a particular direction, as we are told, there would unquestionably be a difference in the result of firing off a projectile in that direction and in a direction the opposite to that one. If a projectile be fired from a rapidly moving body in an opposite direction to that in which the body is going, it will fall short of the distance at which it would reach if fired in the direction of motion. Now, since the earth is said to move at the rate of nineteen miles a second, in a certain direction, it would make all the difference imaginable if the gun were fired in an opposite direction.

The circumstances which attend bodies which are caused merely to fall from a great height prove nothing as to the motion or stability of the earth, since the object, if it be on a thing that is in motion, will participate in that motion; but if an object be thrown upwards from a body at rest, and again from a body in motion, the circumstances attending its descent will be very different. In the former case, it will fall if thrown vertically upwards, at the place from whence it was projected; in the latter case, it will fall behind, the moving body from which it is thrown will leave it in the rear.

Now, fix a gun firmly in the ground carefully plumbed in a true vertical position, fire off a projectile, and it will fall by the gun. CENTRIFUGAL FORCE

REFRACTION 

When an astronomer or globularist finds he cannot reconcile his theory with plain earth facts, he dips his pen in ink and writes a long article on "Refraction." 

But what is the cause of refraction?

When light passes from above through media of varying density it is deflected or reflected, out of a straight course. The density of the atmosphere alters with its altitude; hence light is refracted as it descends and passes through atmospheric strata of various density. 

But in the case of the observer and the ship at sea both are in a medium of unvarying density, in the air above the surface of the ocean; so there could be no refraction. 

Place a spoon or penny in a glass of water and you will have a good example of refraction. When a ray of light strikes the water, a denser medium than air, it is refracted or bent out of its normal course, and the image of the objects are elevated and projected forward, so that they appear to be where in reality they are not. But if these objects were out in the air, or the observer down in the water, there would be no refraction in either case. 

Divers are never deceived in locating objects under water.

So as a ship and the observer are both in a medium of uniform density, the air, there is no refraction.

PERSPECTIVE 

We come again to consider the law of optics or principles of perspective that ever present optical illusion known as perspective. It is manifest, the views we get on the retina of the eye are apparent, in other words we see objects in their relative positions, which does not necessarily show their exact positions. Our eyes are globular in form, and all within our range of vision, the length of which varies with different persons, converges to a point on the retina of the eye. From numerous experiments it has been proved that all objects below an observer's eyeline, and all objects above the eyeline respectively, come up and down, and vanish into the eyeline, the length or limit of the eyeline forming the observer's horizon. But our eyes being globular in form act as lenses, hence, perspective operates right and left, as well as above and below. So lines that are parallel to each other, appear to converge to a point directly before us, but never appear to cross from the right hand to the left, or vice versa; the reason being, the lines converge and vanish into the vertical eyeline, or axis, in a similar way to which parallel lines above or below the eyeline perspectively vanish.

Examples of perspective are seen about us on every hand. A row of telephone poles, or street lights, a rail track, a row of houses, in a long street, the horizontal joints of the brickwork in a long wall, all give plain and unmistakable evidence of the operation of the laws of perspective. When this simple law, so well known to artists, architects, opticians, and many others, is clearly understood, it will explain the vanishing of the bird, and the airship in mid-air, the rising, culminating and setting of the sun, moon and stars, the passing of the clouds and the disappearance of ships at sea. SUN'S DIAMETER 

The "exact" figures given by modern astronomers concerning the size of the sun are as varied and as unreliable as the distances given. We are told that the sun is "more than a million of times larger than the earth"; with a diameter variously estimated in harmony with their different ideas as to its distance. Russel declares the diameter to be 882,000 miles, and Ball, of "exact" figure fame, has found the diameter to be 866,000 miles. 

Now if the sun were so many million (1,300,000) times larger than the earth, and at so great a distance, then all the direct rays of light that this "little" supposed globe could ever receive from it would come in parallel lines. All diverging rays, right and left, must fall outside of it, and only vertical rays could fall upon it. But since we know that the sun's rays do not all fall upon the earth vertically or in parallel lines, it is therefore convincing evidence that the sun cannot be anything approaching the size astronomers affirm it to be. And since we do see such divergent rays, and sometimes very divergent rays, in almost all parts of the earth; it goes to show that the sun is a comparatively small body and

not very distant from us. Furthermore, if the earth is such a "speck" compared to the size of the sun, darkness would be impossible. This is a condition that is commonly overlooked. The light from the sun radiating in all directions from itself, would completely submerge the earth. The sun being so much larger than the earth, its light would sweep entirely round it so it would amount to no more to the sun than a pinhead in the rays of an electric light. The light in passing the earth, would entirely envelop it on all sides, so that one on the side opposite the sun, with it hidden from their view, would be like a person sitting in the shadow of their house, with the light all round them, reading the news. Night would be no darker than the shade of your house at noonday, if the earth and sun are the size assumed by our scientists. 

Now we have proven to our own satisfaction, that the sun is nearer the earth than what the astronomers postulate, therefore it follows without argument that it is a small body. But how small or how large remains to be proven. There are some simple rules or methods by which we may determine the facts in the case. Let a person stand by night directly under a light and it would cast no side shadow. If you move northwards your shadow will fall towards the north, if you go south your shadow will fall southwards. If the light were extended over your head, say for ten feet, then on moving that distance under the light, you would still see no shadow.

That is the vertical rays of light would cast no shadow for a distance equal to its own extent. As long as you were under it you would know that its rays were vertically to your position, as you walked about you would learn that the size of the light was equal to the area of all its vertical rays. Before you entered it from the north side you could see the light from an angle less than the vertical and south of you; and when you left it on the south side, you saw it at an angle less than the vertical

SECTION VI  A COMMERCIAL IMPORTANCE 

VEN the commercial importance of this subject may be seen in connection with navigation. If mariners in Southern latitudes are supposing the configuration of the earth to be that of a globe, instead of, as it is in fact, one vast outstretched plane, then we can see the cause for many mistakes navigators have made in Southern waters. These mistakes have no doubt, led modern mariners to navigate the seas by Mercator's Chart, which is an approach to the truth, representing the earth and the seas as one vast but square plane. I think we can safely affirm that in the whole range of commercial navigation no sea captain or master mariner would ever venture to take a globe to sea to sail by, or attempt to navigate his ship in Southern waters by a globular chart. "Plain sailing," the system of navigating adopted, "is sailing a ship, or making the arithmetical calculations for so doing, on the assumption that The Earth is 

Perfectly Flat."-Navigation in Theory and Practice, 

P. 66; by Prof. Evers, LL.D. From this standpoint alone the subject is of sufficient importance to arouse the interest of reasonable and intelligent men. 

THE MARINERS' COMPASS 

That the mariners' compass points north and south simultaneously is a fact as indisputable as that two and 118

two make four; but that this would be impossible if the thing were placed on a globe with north and south at the center of opposite hemispheres. On the equator the north point of the needle would point to one of the northern star constellations, while if it were taken far south of the equator it would point to the stars over the equator.

PARALLEL LINES OF LATITUDE 

The parallel lines of latitude only, of all imaginary lines on the surface of the Earth, are circles, which increase progressively, from the northern center to the southern circumference. The mariner's course in the direction of anyone of these concentric circles is his longitude, the degrees of which increase to such an extent beyond the equator, going south, that hundreds of vessels have been wrecked because of the false idea created by the untruthfulness of the charts and the globular theory together, causing the navigators to be constantly getting out of their reckoning.

The British ship Challenger completed the circuit of the southern region, indirectly to be sure, but she was three years in making the voyage, and traversed nearly 69,000 miles-a stretch long enough to have taken her six times round on the globular hypothesis, and the cruise cost the nation the extravagant sum of over one million dollars. (See Encyclopædia Britannica on the Antarctic circle.) This is a startling proof that the earth is not a globe.

MERIDIANS 

As the mariner's compass points north and south at one and the same time, and a meridian is a north and south line, it follows that meridians can be no other than

straight lines. But since meridians on a globe are semicircles, it is an incontrovertible proof that the earth is not a globe.

It has already been shown that meridians are necessarily straight lines; and that it is impossible to travel round the Earth in a north or south direction; from which it follows that, in the general acceptation of the word "degree," the 360th part of a circle-meridians have no degrees; for no one knows anything of a meridian circle or semicircle, to be thus divided. But astronomers speak of degrees of latitude in the same sense as those of longitude. This then, is done by assuming that to be true, which is not true. But the plain-earth philosophy does not involve this necessity. If the Earth were a globe, the distance round its surface at, say 45 degrees south latitude, could not possibly be any greater than it is at the same latitude north; but since it is found by navigators to be twice the distance, to say the least, or double the distance it ought to be, according to the globular theory, it is a clear proof it cannot be what astronomers tell us it is-hence not a globe. 

WHERE IS THE SOUTH POLE? 

What assurance have we that several south polar expeditions, beginning at different places on the antarctic circle would converge in one definite area? Not any!

We have many reasons to doubt that they would. There is no south polar star, and if there were, who knows when he is exactly under a star? The explorer accepts as certain, that which is yet unknown. He clings to the chart which shows a south pole, and which gave its theoretical location long before anyone ever attempted to go there, and he proceeds to discover the place which in theory has no diurnal motion, yet he is not conscious

of this lack of motion and feels no different than when in his native land, which is said to partake of a diurnal motion of nearly one thousand miles an hour. What other proof is needed that the earth is stationary and partakes of no motion whatever except that caused by earthquakes. 

The south pole was conceived in theory, charted in theory, and now discovered in theory. When these explorers reach what they calculate to be ninety degrees south, they know they have reached the south pole. But an infinite number of points ninety degrees south of the Equator would be found in a vast circle of ice which surrounds the earth and seas and forms the earthly boundary of man's universe.

DAYS LENGTH VS. NORTH AND SOUTH 

Navigators who have journeyed a sufficient distance within the Arctic circle have been able, at a certain time of the year, to see the sun above the horizon, continuously for months at a time. As a consequence of this, we have in Arctic circles what is known as the midnight sun. Now those persons who have penetrated to within the same distance south, report that at a corresponding season the sun is seen every day, but that it always sets in the evening and rises in the morning.

If the earth were a globe, and things were co-equal; and there was a south center similar to the north center or pole, there would be a season of the year when the sun would be visible for months at a time—a midnight sun, from the south center. But there is no such season -no such midnight sun, hence no south center; and no south polar star to determine a center; and its absence is proof enough that the earth is not a globe. 

At Stockholm, Sweden, latitude 59° 21′ north there are eighteen and one-half hours sun. At Hammerfest, Norway, latitude 70° 45′ North, there is continual sun from 

May 11 to July 22-three months. At Spitzbergen, 78°

North latitude, the longest continued day is three and one-half months. The longest day we are able to find on record in the south, practically observed, is given in the "Antarctic Cruise," by Capt. Wilkes. Pgs. 133-135, though very indefinite, gives us a day something over nineteen hours long in latitude 66° South. But to contrast we give St. Petersburg, Russia, latitude 59° 56' North, nineteen hours sun, the latter being about six degrees, or over 400 miles nearer the Equator, yet about the same length of day, each of these ever having their summer or longest days. Prof. J. Morrison, of the Nautical Almanac Office, Bureau of Navigation, Navy Department, Washington, D. C., makes the following official statement: "On December 21st and 22nd at South Shetland, about 70° South, (more correctly between 60° and 63°) sun rises 2h. 3m. 30s.; total 19h. 53m. But one important fact to keep in mind is, that concerning all the navigators on record, who have penetrated the farthest south, and reached the highest austral latitude, no record is made of any long sunny days during the summer months, which are the most favorable seasons for south sea expeditions.

But in the writings of these explorers, there is a constant mention of the "next day," the "next morning." They more often travel in the "night time," because the sun on the snow in the day causes "snow blindness." Then again the heat of the sun in the "day time" softens the snow and has a tendency to impede their progress, while at "night" it will freeze and form a crust and thus enable them to facilitate their journey. This goes to show that there is a "day" and "night" in the summer season of the Antarctic regions which is not true of the summer season of corresponding latitudes north.
