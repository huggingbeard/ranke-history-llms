Carlene Brien Myers was born at Nashville, Tennessee, and educated there in a church school. After her marriage, she resided near Helena, Arkansas, where her husband was a planter.

She is a member of many women's organizations, including the American Legion 

Auxiliary, Founders and Patriots of America,

D. A. R., U. D. C., Arkansas Authors' Society, Pacaha Study Club and Twentieth 

Century Club. At one time she was President of the Federated Churches of Helena. 

She did much army relief work during the 

World War. 

After rearing a large family, she took up the study of the short story and the novel at Hollywood, later taking a course from 

Harvard University. 

Now her versatile talent has produced a moving tale in her first novel.
