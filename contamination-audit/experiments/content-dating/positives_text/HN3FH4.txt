EXPLANATION 

KC 14246 During the period of distribution of the first 4,000 books printed, many times the question has risen, why am I using my own time and money to publish this book?

In this edition of 50,000 books an explanation is given for every reader that may desire to know. The receiving of the Blessed Holy Spirit has given me great desire to serve and please God, and after having been born with the 

Spirit of God, a dream followed me practically every night, and in this dream I was going from door to door and ringing the bell. After three years of receiving this dream a great aspiration came to me that I should write my first experience with God, so at this time I wrote what the's written in the first and second pages of this book and I had 2,000 copies printed. When I began to give them out the dream stopped. After the 2,000 copies were distributed four and one-half months went by that I was not giving any literature out, and during this time the dream was coming back again; at this time I wrote the little book and when I began to give them out the dream stopped again, so I hope that this will help you to believe that this is God's work and not my own. 

This explanation was written in 1942, today 1948 God still speaks to me concerning this work. Also in a vision I have seen my hand doing the writing.

THE GRAND NIGHT WITH GOD BY CONFESSION 

I thank my sweet Saviour and my God for His wonderful grace, and hope that the readers will realize my sincerity, as I have no other interest but to please my sweet Saviour and my God by trying to lift the human faith toward the One who gracefully had lifted mine. 

On the night of January 5, 1938, I was in my own room alone, and a little while after I had prayed for a short time, I spoke to God with faith and sincerity, and said: "You know all about myself," and as I finished saying these words the Holy Spirit of God fell upon me as a gust of wind had blown on my face and entered in my heart and soul and I was in a supernatural joy and glory, and I could feel the presence of God and the Blessed Spirit working within me, and this is a joy unspeakable and full of glory so much higher than anything we call joy in this life, that I don't know how to express it, and this great joy was manifested in me for about one and a half hours and then my life was changed. I found myself free from all the desire of worldly pleasure, but filled with joy and peace and love of God in my soul. 

My faith was established without doubt that Jesus Christ is my Saviour and my God, and that all things that He said are true and I am not only saying that I have faith in Jesus Christ, but I say I know that Jesus Christ is the Way, the Truth and the Life, and today I still have joy (1)

.. and peace settled within my soul, and enjoy it day by day as I am serving my sweet Saviour and my God.

This experience was received in 1938-written 1941. 

Today, 1948, I still have the joy, and peace of the Lord within my soul, and even greater than I had in 1938; also with power to live free from sin.

THE DREAM ABOUT THE WARS and THE GOING DOWN OF CHRISTIAN RELIGION 

In the year of 1938 I had a dream in which I saw two men fighting. When they had fought for a while they stopped, and one of them, after they had stopped fighting, walked away about fifty feet from the other man that (14)

he had fought with, but the other man stood still for a while, and then he started to go in the opposite direction from where the other man was, but as he had walked about ten feet when the other man came right over and started to fight with him again and they did this very same thing over and over for a few times. Every time they would stop fighting, the same man would walk away and then he came back as the other started to go away and he started to fight with him again. After they had fought for a few times they were friends, and they walked away together and they went on the side of a house and there were some stairs and they went down the stairs and they never came back. Then I saw a priest coming out of the same house, and he was dressed the same as when a priest is at the altar during a service, and he went down the same stairs and never came back. 

And I believe that God gave me the understanding about the dream. The two men fighting meant a great war or maybe more than one war and the same two men going down the stairs and never coming back, it means the end of the wars. And the priest going down the stairs and never coming back it means the going down of Christian religion sometime after the end of the war. 

This is not very clear but I believe that there will be a war yet, after the going down of the Christian religion. which God Himself will destroy the great army. 

THE DREAM ABOUT THE HIGHWAY 

In the year 1938 I had a dream that I was walking down the road of Glenwood Glade, Oakland, California. (15)

As I was walking down this road I saw many men working making a new highway and they had trucks, steamshovels, tractors, horses, and all the equipment that is used to build a highway and the highway was under construction and I saw some of the work that was already done, and when I came to the end of my walk, I was standing on the new highway, and that was about 40 to 50 feet from some business property that I own, and this was the end of my dream. Now at this time the state was planning to construct a new highway in that community, but they had four different places before them and not knowing which place would be more convenient they had surveyors and engineers working on the four different places, so that they might know the most convenient one to take. At this time the cities of Oakland, Piedmont, Berkeley, and also the Community Improvement Club, agreed all together and selected one of the four places and voted that the new highway should go through that place, but the highway that I saw in my dream was not in the same place that they voted on. So when I had conversation with someone I expressed myself by saying that the new highway is not going to be in the place where they had chosen it to be constructed and they were getting angry at me, thinking that I was against them, so I kept my peace, but in the year of 1942, three years later, the state decided on the same. place where I saw it in the dream, and they started to move the houses, and I want to thank God for his wonderful advice. Praise His name. (16)

THE DREAM ABOUT THE ROOF LEAKING 

In the month of December, 1940, I was living in Turlock, California, and I had a dream that the spirit of God had picked me up and I was traveling in the Spirit of God, and after I had traveled for a while I found myself inside of a store building that I owned in Oakland, California, and there was a man of Oakland whom I know and this man had a stepladder and he placed it against the wall and he went up it and as he got to the top of it the water was coming down through the roof and he turned his face toward me, smiling as if to say the roof is leaking, and then the man went away and as I was there alone I saw a big rat going across the floor and this was the end of my dream. In the morning 

I was thinking about the dream, but I didn't understand why I had such a dream, but in the same day a woman whom I know asked me if I would like to go to Oakland.

I could have a free ride as she was going and she would give me a round trip, but I kind of hesitated as I didn't know any reason why I should go to Oakland. When she saw that I hesitated she said, "Well, if you desire to go you could let me know as I am not going until tomorrow about 10:00 a.m." So I thought about it all day, but I could not make up my mind, but about the end of the time, something helped me to make up my mind, so the decision came and I went. Now, as soon as I got to 

Oakland I went to my place and as soon as my tenant saw me, with his finger he pointed to the roof, to the very same spot where the man in the dream showed me that the roof was leaking, and said the roof is leaking (17)

right there, and then I went to another tenant in the same building and as soon as she saw me, she said please put a screen in the window back of the store as I saw a great big rat. So I called the woman up and told her that I couldn't go back to Turlock as I must remain in Oakland, and I got on the roof and found that the roof was in a very bad condition in many different places. Then I started to work and it took me 15 days to repair the roof and just as I was finished, and I was still on the roof when it started to rain and I could not have repaired the roof any more for a long time as the weather was so bad for a period of six months, and I want to praise God for the wonderful way He takes care of His children.

THE VISION ABOUT THE TENT and THE WHITE SOUL and THE BLACK SOUL · 

In the year of 1940, in the summer time, I was living in Turlock, California, and I had a vision as I was sitting in my own room alone, and a round tent had appeared before me, and two little men about as tall as an inch were six feet from the tent, and they both walked into the tent together, and one was white and the other was black, both their color covered their whole bodies, and I had a big laugh because I thought it was very funny but I didn't understand the vision, but in a day or so God gave me the understanding, and it became clear in my mind about the meaning of the vision. God showed me how small the men are before God, and also God does see the soul of man, and He showed me how (18)

the soul of man looks before God. The black man is the soul of the man who is in his sins and has not received the Grace of God, in which He is the Blessed Holy Spirit in which it was spoken by the mouth of our Lord Jesus 

Christ when He said: "Truly, truly, I say unto you except a man be born of water and of the Spirit he cannot enter into the kingdom of God." John 3:5. And the white man is how the soul of man looks after having received the Grace of God and followed our Lord Jesus Christ. 

Thank God for this wonderful vision.

THE VISION OF THE HAND 

In the year of 1941, from April to July 1, I was living in Prescott, Arizona, and I was attending at the Pentecost Mission and also the Salvation Army, and they asked me to preach for them, and I did. I preached twice to them, but the last time I was not satisfied with my preaching, so when I got to my room that night, I was disgusted and I spoke to God by saying, "O Lord if you want me to go ahead you must do something,' and then I went to my bed, and during the night as I was asleep I felt something coming upon me so by that 

I was awakened from my sleep, and I quickly understood that it was a spirit, so I thought I would see how strong he was, and I pushed up against him with my arm, but he quickly pushed my arm down and then before he left me, I felt his spirit going through my body, and through my bones and it was a wonderful feeling, and he revived (19)

my whole body and made me feel very good, and then I saw a beautiful hand on the cover of my bed, and I can't express in words how beautiful that hand was, but something about that hand that was very pure and angelic, one could see that he was from above. 

Praise God for the wonderful way He answered my prayer.

HEALED BY THE GRACE OF GOD 

About August in the year of 1938 I began to feel a pain in the right side of my intestine, and I thought it might go away, so for a while I didn't do anything about it, but the pain was getting worse so I called on four different doctors, but I was not helped by any of them. 

One of the doctors, after he had taken some x-rays, he told me that unless an operation is performed the trouble will remain. I thought this over for a while and 

I decided to take it to my Saviour, Jesus Christ, in prayer and I began to pray for healing and I also had requested prayer of the Pentecost Mission where I have attended many times. For about three years I prayed and also had the Mission pray for me several times, but I had not received the grace. But after all this time, about the night of November 18, 1941, as I was in my room alone and had prayed for a short time and then I spoke to God very sincere and desperate and said, for three years I have been praying for this grace and I have not yet received it, and then I put the question before 

God by saying, what is the matter with me? And as I (20)

was talking to God this way the power of God touched my head and went down in the very spot where I had the pain and thank God I was healed. I want to praise and thank God for His wonderful mercy to me.

It kept moving up and down and while I was looking at this bright jewel I saw an angel where the bright jewel was. Then the angel came down to me, and when he was about fifty feet from me I saw that he had the baby Jesus Christ in his arms and he came about ten feet from me and then vanished. 

Then I looked back to the bright jewel again and the bright jewel was still moving up and down, and while all this was going on in my dream, I was filled with the Glory of God, and then I awoke and I really had the Glory of God in my soul. God was realling blessing me. 

Praise His name. 

GREAT MIRACLE 

About the 24th of January, 1942, I was attending a meeting at Twelfth Street Pentecost Mission in Oakland, pil

California, and I saw a man on crutches and he dragged himself on these two crutches to the front of the Mission and his body was trembling and shaking from the top to the bottom, and looking at his condition brought tears to my cheeks, and as he testified his voice was only heard part of the time as most of the words were choked before they came out of his mouth, but I understood that he did not expect to be on this earth much longer as he felt very weak in his body. But that he had the joy and peace of God in his soul, and he expected to see Jesus soon.

I saw this man again several times at Ninth Street Mission, Oakland, California, and his condition was even getting worse and his testimony was about the same, but he was doing much praying. One night he got five people to pray with him and they had all-night prayers. 

During the night of March 12, 1942, as I was present in the meeting I saw the great miracle of God. A PEN PORTRAIT OF JESUS (Copied from the New York World) 

Rome, from the dust of more than 1900 years, a studious, modern Rome, has rescued a pen portrait of Jesus Christ. It was drawn in one of the letters that 

Publius Lentulus, who was a Roman pro-consul in Palestine, and knew the Saviour in Nazareth, wrote to a friend in Italy. "There has appeared here a man of strange virtue,"

Publius Lentulus wrote, "His disciples call him the Son of God. He cures the sick, and raises the dead to life. He is a very handsome man, and worthy of attention. His hair is blond and curled, falling over his shoulders, and is parted in the middle after the fashion of the people of 

Nazareth. His forehead is smooth, and serene, without marks or wrinkles. His countenance is pink, his nose is well formed, his beard is the same color as his hair, is parted in the middle. In his gaze, is an expression of wisdom, and of openness. His eyes are blue, and shine terribly when he reproves people, but in conversation, they are amiable. His observations are expressed with liveliness, although he always remains calm. Nobody has ever seen him laugh, but he often weeps. Of a good height and straight figure, he has very beautiful hands and arms. His manner of speaking is serious. He speaks but little, and is modest. In short, he is as handsome as a man may be. They call him Jesus the Son of Mary." (99)
