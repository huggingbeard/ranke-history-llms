FOREWORD 

The problem of taxable abilities among the West Virginia counties has been brought into acute focus by the findings and recommendations of the recent comprehensive report on the state system of education prepared by Dr. George D. Strayer and his associates. The general property tax, already the mainstay of the local tax structure, must, in the event of increased local participation, contribute still further in the task of financing the public school system. For this reason it becomes increasingly important that taxes levied by each of the fifty-five counties bear a reasonable relation to the tax base for that county.

This source book presents essential economic and tax data which may be utilized by the reader in relating the taxable abilities of his county with the effort made to support public functions. Most of the data included in this bulletin have been drawn from such official sources as the Biennial Reports of the Test Virginia Tax Commissioner, the Census reports, West Virginia County Basic Data Sheets, the West Virginia Blue Book, Sales Management, the Bureau of Internal 

Revenue, and the Strayer Report. These data are presumed to be accurate, but the author disclaims any responsibility for their origins or their derivation. 1
