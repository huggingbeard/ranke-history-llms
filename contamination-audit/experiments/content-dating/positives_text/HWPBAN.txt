Her affair with Alex Ingalls was something of a nine-day scandal in the small town of Glencreek, so Ruth Mellstock decided to look for greener fields in New York, where supposedly wives are less prudish and men more exciting. 

As the landlady of a theatrical boarding house inherited from her aunt, Ruth soon made things hum. Practically, she insisted that her histrionic tenants pay rent. Since several of the "actresses" knew only one way of earning a sizable sum quickly, that led to complications.
