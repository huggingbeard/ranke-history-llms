Kent Kirk shivered a little, crouching deeper into his seat, despite the warm clothes which he wore. Resignedly he gazed about at the enveloping storm which had swirled about the plane now for hours. Then he leaned forward a little, shouting above the roar of the propeller. "How's the needle now, Ray?" 

Ray Clark, busy at the controls, facing the storm like a blond viking, his face set and strained, flung back one brief, uncomforting word. "Crazy!"

Both boys relapsed into silence. The snow drove and whirled in a dense cloud, making it impossible at times, to see more than a few feet.
