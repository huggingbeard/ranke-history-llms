II "LITTLE LUCIFER" 

As the Flying Buddies glided unwillingly earthward, Austin glanced ahead to see into what sort of place they were going to land, and saw a deep valley, about two miles wide, through the center of which rushed a swift, clear river that looked as if it had worn its way through many centuries to its present level. On both sides of it, growing high and close to the sweeping banks, was a tall, dense forest whose great massive trees wove their thick branches into a mat on which dozens of monkeys, parrots and other brilliant tropical birds and animals swung and flew. The place reminded Jim of the one Corso and Yncicea Haurea had led him under the first time he was taken to the Emerald Temple and he wondered if it were into some such passage or hide-out they would be taken now, but for the life of him he could locate no spot where the "Lark" could be brought to a stop. 23
