PREFACE "New Forms of Practice in Civil Actions" is offered to the Bar as a second or supplemental volume to "Forms of Practice," published in May, 1924. It was thought advisable to adopt this method of publication in order to avoid the necessary duplication of forms that a second edition would occasion, and also to place in the hands of the lawyer a later and larger compilation of forms. For this reason a careful and exhaustive survey has been made of cases lately considered by the Supreme Judicial Court of the Commonwealth as well as of cases heard and pending in the Superior Court for Suffolk County, and a selection made from those pleadings that will meet the needs of the general practitioner in his daily practice. It has also been thought advisable to include in this book some of the more simple forms that did not appear in the first work, in view of the numerous inquiries and requests for same.

The readiness with which the Bar accepted the book of 1924, is an indication that the lawyer of today has not the time, even if he has the patience and ability, to devote to the preparation and drafting of his pleadings, but prefers to have some form that has already been before the Court to use as a basis for his practice forms. 

The research of the writer indicates that the Courts of today do not look with favor on forms that are replete with the quaint expressions and phraseology of former days, but favor a simplicity in the wording of all pleadings, and while it is essential to include all allegations material to the issue, care should be taken to be as brief as possible.
