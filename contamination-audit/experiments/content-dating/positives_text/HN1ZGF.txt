GIN WIFE-The story of a girl who wakes up one morning after an orgiastic gin party and discovers that she is married. 

WANDA HARTLEY-The beautiful, fun loving cashier who loves one man but marries another during a drunken orgy.

JOE WHITTAKER-Idealistic son of a large real estate promoter who goes off the loose end after Wanda's gin wedding.

MIRIAM PATTEN-A former burlesque chorus girl who is out to make Joe Whittaker because he is a good catch. 

DICK KENNEDY-Black sheep son of a socially prominent family who stages the gin wedding.

She slid into the deep tub of scented water, drowning in its depths rounded thigh and tiny waist, but leaving revealed her shapely breasts made more firm beneath their ruby crowns by the caress of liquid warmth. Enveloping folds of creamy lather heightened her sense of the perfection of all things. 

Tonight was the night! Tonight-Joe Whittaker would be coming to her apartment. Tonight, to the accompaniment of shaded lights, music, drinks, and a hand picked assortment of friends, Wanda meant to win her battle with Joe's reticence.

Momentary doubt traced a frown across her smooth forehead. She had known Joe so long!
