FOREWORD 

This brief annotated list of books, pamphlets, and articles on disarmament, arms control, and related topics was prepared as a preliminary introductory guide to the increasing volume of scholarly and popular writing in this field. 

For the most part it includes titles dealing with arms control developments since 1945. Several titles cover the period between World Wars I 

and II. 

This compilation includes citations representing a wide variety of viewpoints and approaches to the problems of disarmament and security. The general scope, content, or viewpoint of each title, when not self-evident, is indicated in a brief descriptive annotation. In the main highly technical or highly specialized works are omitted. Also omitted are titles difficult to obtain by purchase or on loan from a town or college library. The inclusion of any title in this list carries no implication either of approval or disapproval by the United States Disarmament Administration of the views expressed therein. Since this bibliography is intended to be only a tentative introduction to the large body of disarmament literature and is by no means exhaustive, there is appended to it a list of bibliographies listing additional sources for those who wish to conduct further research. In addition, standard publication guides, such as the Readers Guide to Periodical 

Literature, the Public Affairs Information 

Service, and the International Index to Periodicals, can be consulted.
