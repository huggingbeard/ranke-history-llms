The tables which follow have been prepared by the staff of the Committee on Foreign Relations in order to assist the committee in its consideration of the proposed Mutual Security Program fol 1953. The summary table on page 1 shows several trends which are elaborated in the more complete table which follows. The tables show, for example, that the trend for military assistance to non European countries has been upward since 1950 as has the trend for economic and technical assistance to non-European countries. Mili tary assistance for Europe, which reached its peak during the current year, will be slightly less for next year if the President's proposals are accepted. Economic and technical assistance for Europe has gradually decreased since 1950 but the President's program for fiscal 

1953 calls for a slight increase.
