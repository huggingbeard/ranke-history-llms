DRILL EIGHT TWO-STEP PROBLEMS 

A complex, or two-step, problem contains a main problem and a subordinate problem. This subordinate problem must be found and solved before the main problem can be solved and the desired result obtained. 

What you are asked to find in the main problem is always set out for you, but in a two-step problem one or more of the facts necessary for its answer is lacking. Finding this missing fact is what makes the subordinate problem.

The question of the subordinate problem is hidden and can be determined only by careful thinking. Answering this hidden question solves the subordinate problem and gives the missing fact needed to solve the main problem.

A complex problem is solved for you in the space below. Study it and solve all other two-step problems by the same plan. 

1. The total weight of John, George, and Henry is 298 lb. John weighs 101 lb. and George weighs 105 lb. What is the weight of Henry? 

MAIN PROBLEM-What are you asked to find? The weight of Henry.

What facts are given? The total weight of the three boys is 298 lb. John weighs 101 lb. and George weighs 105 lb.

What fact is missing? The sum of the weights of John and George. This becomes the question of the subordinate problem which must be solved before the main problem can be solved. 

SUBORDINATE PROBLEM-What are you asked to find? The sum of the weights of John and George. 

What facts are given? John weighs 101 lb. and 

George weighs 105 lb.

CALCULATION 

What process must be used? The word sum indicates addition. 101 105

What facts are now given? The sum of the weights of John and George is 206 lb. All three boys weigh 298 lb. 

What process must be used? When you have given the total of three numbers and the sum of the first and second, subtract the sum of the first and second from the total to find the third. 

Is your answer reasonable and correct? Yes, because 92 is less than 300.
