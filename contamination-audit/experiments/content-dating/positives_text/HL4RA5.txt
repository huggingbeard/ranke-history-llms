c# Under the provisions of Section 406 of the Omnibus Crime 

Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (Public Law 90-351), as amended, the institutions of higher education named in the following list are participating in the Law Enforcement Educa tion Program (LEEP). 

LEEP provides student loans and/or grants to State and local public law enforcement agents and persons who are committed to careers in the criminal justice system. Loans may be made to cover direct educational costs plus additional demonstrated financial need, not to exceed $1,800 per academic year. Grants are available only to currently employed criminal justice agents and may be up to $300 per semester or $200 per quarter, not to exceed the cost of tuition, fees and books. 

Actual awards of loans and/or grants to students will be made by the Student Financial Aid Officers (SFAO) at the participating schools. Persons interested in applying should contact the SFÃO at one of the participating institutions. 

For further information, contact your appropriate region al office of the Law Enforcement Assistance Administration.
