Re your Missing Cash Savings.

When the total amount of the missing cash savings of the people of the United States of America is ultimately verified, it will, in the opinion of the author hereof, be found to reach the huge total of somewhere between 49 and 61 billions of dollars, more or less.

Of this huge total of missing cash savings, an amount of approximately 37 billions of dollars thereof, comprises and consists of and is equivalent to the amount of investment securities presently held by the Commercial Banks of the United States, heretofore acquired by them from the American people on credit, through the operations of the prevailing system of bank credit exchange, without the formality of making cash payment in exchange therefore. 

This is the amount of investment securities heretofore acquired by the American commercial banks on credit, and for which cash payment to the American people has never yet been made. And this is the amount of genuine cash money which must be forthwith fabricated by and placed on deposit with a suitable money depository, in favor of the government of the United States, in trust for the American people. And this is the amount of cash money presently required to place the monetary exchange system of the United States in a liquidlysolvent condition. And this is the amount of genuine cash money which must be forthwith restored to the American people to end for once and all the current evil economic conditions arising from the use in the United States of gold and bank credit and other associated processes for monetary purposes.

American readers of this book should read the word American and the words United States instead of the words Canadian and Canada wherever the latter words appear herein, as in all respects, other than the actual volume of money to be fabricated, this new system of genuine money as proposed for Canadian use is likewise intended for American use as well as for international exchange purposes.

The amount of the missing cash savings to be restored to the people of the various nations would be the only variation in the processes disclosed herein.
