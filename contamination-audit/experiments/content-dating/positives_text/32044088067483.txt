The human race has built many beautiful civilizations, but none of them has been permanent. We have had the Sumerian, Egyptian, Assyrian, Babylonian, Phoenician, Persian, Jewish, Chinese, Grecian and

Roman civilizations in Europe, Asia and Africa and the Aztec and Inca civilizations in America. We now have an oriental, Hindu, Mohammedan, Slavic, Teutonic, French, Italian, Spanish, Scandinavian, English and

American civilization; and they are all collapsing.

HARVARD 

UNIVERSITY LIBRARY 

MAR 11 1942 Emplicate Money 

Since the beginning of time, civilizations have collapsed. Civiliza tion has now circled the globe and the collapse is world wide. Hitherto, it has been city wide, state wide, nation wide and continent wide. Why is this happening again? Is it because the human race is incapable of building a permanent civilization, or is it because of inherent weaknesses or defects in human nature that prevent it? When We are all endowed with more or less worthy ambition. ambition degenerates into greed, civilization begins to decline. Civilization goes forward or backward; it does not stand still. If you read Isaiah in the old testament, you learn that all civilization before his time (about 700 B.C.), went to pieces because of plunder and corruption. That is the end of all progress. If you read other prophets, you get the same information. If you read the history of nations, you get the same answer. 

The most striking example of a collapsed civilization, is that of Rome. A little kingdom city became a world empire in a few hundred years and controlled most of the known civilized world in Europe, Asia and Africa, embracing about one hundred million persons. That isn't much compared with the population of the world today (about two billion), but it constituted a tremendous empire two thousand years ago. 

Wealth increased and the people finally became callous and corrupt. The golden calf flourished. Justice and government were bought and sold and nearly everything else was purchasable. There was little or no pride left in the people who were the heirs of many generations of hardy citi- During the last stages of the Roman republic and empire, the slaves outnumbered the citizens several times. The masses were poor and the few rich. This was the final result of an economic system based on private monopoly. zens.

The story of Rome is the story of all other civilizations before and since that time, and civilization is collapsing for the same reasons. In nearly every nation, a handful controls and wealth-power rules. Private monopoly and governmental extravagance have undermined the economic structure and when that goes, everything else goes. That is what happened in Rome and in other countries, and that is what is happening in the United States; only the speed is faster.

In the latter days of the Roman republic, the rulership of the country was lodged in the senate. The senators were the leading monopolists. 

Each senator had his band of followers who looked to him for support. 

Those were the days of "bread and circuses". The poor were fed and entertained at the expense of their senatorial patrons. It was evident to those who thought, that such condition could not endure. Rome was losing her prestige and glory. The climax came when Cæsar, a popular general, tried to wrest control of the Roman world from the senatorial

oligarchy, about 44 B.C. He was assassinated in the senate chamber. 

For many years before and after this event, triumphant rulers confiscated the property of those who opposed them for office, by a system known as proscription. The names of the victims were posted in the forum and those on the list were considered legitimate objects of plunder and assassination. 

There have been many proscriptions in the United States and in other countries since that time, though they were not called by that name. 

The names of the victims were not posted in the forums. Those pro- scribed constituted the entire populations who were exploited by private monopoly and victimized through public plunder. 

Every nation that ever existed, developed the same way, through the thrift and industry of its inhabitants. In time, certain systems develop―economic, political, educational and religious. When a handful grabs up what most persons produce and individual wealth-power becomes dominant, these systems begin to crumble. Character disappears. 

Oliver Goldsmith expressed it in unforgettable language in the "Deserted Village," where he said, "Ill fares the land, to hastening ills a prey; where wealth accumulates and men decay." The moral fiber is destroyed in those that have too much and in those that have too little. The latter struggle only to exist. Bribery, plunder and corruption become general and humanity loses its finer qualities. This is the usual course and the people of today are no different than the people of other times. When the economy of a nation produces extremes of wealth and poverty, and this gross inequality is not corrected by law, wealth is always destroyed by violence, and civilization perishes. 

In most countries, this condition of extreme wealth and poverty was produced before the first world war. For fifty years, the people of the United States were busy producing and grabbing. They thought little of anything else. The whole philosophy of life was how to make the most money and have the "best time." During that same period, a handful through trick and device, grabbed up what most persons produced. There was the Rockefeller group, the Morgan group, the DuPont group, the Mellon group, the Ryan group, the Guggenheim group and other groups. They acquired control not only of industry and finance, but of politics, government, education and religion, as well. They were the country's leading monopolists. They controlled banks, mines, utilities and industrial monopolies. Their aggregate wealth ran into billions. 

As a result of their economic conquest, all systems were corrupted. 

Politicians, "leading citizens" and public officials became subservient; educational systems were doctored and religious systems were subverted. 

Government became the instrument of big business, which aimed only

at further conquest. The press was suborned so that instead of serving the people, it served the money gang and deceived the people. 

The money gang came near losing its power as a result of the first world war, but the loss of ten million lives and the exhaustion of the people generally, saved them. The people were too busy trying to live, to think of economic correction. The money gang still controlled and the newspapers fed the people falsehood and trash to keep them ignorant and occupied. The educational systems helped keep them subnormal and the religious systems kept them fairly satisfied. That was what the money gang wanted-to keep the people happy in their own ignorance.

At the head of the world economic structure were the Rockefellers. 

Their wealth was estimated in billions, obtained through dishonest competition and business suppression and oppression. They controlled nearly all large industry in the United States, and they and those associated with them, controlled the government and industry in nearly every other nation. Their empire embraced the world and Rockefeller Center was the business capital of the world. It was the only private "free port" for imports in America. 

In Italy, the Rockefellers through Standard Oil, were equal partners with the government under Mussolini in a thirty-year oil monopoly. 

In Persia (Iran), they had an 88-12 partnership with the government. 

In France, their partnership was 75-25; and in China, their partnership was 90-10; the government in each case receiving the smaller end. In 

Germany, they did business with Hitler on a barter basis, taking tank steamers, harmonicas and canary birds in payment for oil. They even did business with him in the United States. They loaned money to governments for commercial reasons and they and the British oil crowd controlled nearly every spot on earth.

When the second world war began, it was obvious that the Rockefeller interests would be affected more than any other. It was their civilization and their world empire that was being hit. When Russia went bolshevik twenty years before, they lost their influence and industry in that country. They now see a chance to get them back. 

The economic system based on private monopoly in the United States, reached its logical limit before the first world war in 1914. Those who were at the head of the system then are now dead; only their inconsequential heirs remain. It is because these heirs aim to hang on to what they have, that we have another world war. Hitler and Mussolini are only incidents. They will pass like others before them. Wealth-power still rules and other agents will serve. In Italy, the industrialists control; also in Germany. In Japan, the oligarchy, composed of two dozen richest families, still rules. In England, the money gang is still in power and

in the United States, the crowd headed by the Rockefellers, is still on top. Wealth-power still dominates in all warring nations and until wealth-power is dethroned, there can be no peace on earth. It is not sufficient that one set of commercial despots is dethroned and another substituted. The system must be changed. 

How can the system be changed without violence and so that war will cease? By limiting what the few can have and taking back the rest for society. It is only because of large private possessions in other countries, that the few are willing that the people should continue to destroy themselves. By this means, the few hope to be able to hang on to what they have. Every civilization dominated by the rich, wound up the same way. They rule and they ruin, and the people suffer. Transfer the bulk of their wealth to the governments, and their rulership is ended. The governments, representing all the people, will follow a different course, and war will cease. Limit what the few can have and one of the prime causes for war is removed. 

The money gang in the United States has already lost part of their possessions in other countries and will lose more if the war continues, but they hope to recover their losses after the war, if they are still in power. What they would not surrender peacefully through a tax on private capital, they are losing by violence. In my book "Rockefeller or 

God-Who Will Rule?," published in 1938, I said on page 60, “If

Rockefeller gives back to the nations what he possesses in those nations, and keeps only a small part of what he possesses in the United States, wars will cease and be avoided. If he does not do this, wars will continue. The Lord awaits Rockefeller's decision."

The present John D. Rockefeller is incapable of ruling the industrial empire that he possesses. His possessions span the earth. He did not earn any part of what he has and he is not able to properly administer it for the good of all. All his wealth came from the people. His father once said that he was only a trustee of the great wealth that he possessed, though he acted like an industrial executioner. The present John 

D.'s power is sustained largely by those who serve him. They are all on the payroll and anxious to hold their jobs. Their vision embraces only service to their employer, and with an income estimated at two hundred and fifty million dollars a year, the Rockefellers are well able to support them. In politics, their agents rule politicians and political parties. 

In education, they control educators and educational systems. In religion, they support various denominations and sway many religious leaders. 

In government, they rule the rulers. In other words, they rule civilization and it is their civilization that is collapsing. The people are only the victims. 

How did the Rockefellers acquire this vast power? Mainly through the acquisition of the greatest fortune through unmoral business methods. 

In order to be able to maintain this fortune and this power, they financed newspapers, magazines, movies and radio; and they financed so-called

civic and reform organizations. In fact, they financed everything and everybody for their purpose. It is the civilization that Rockefeller and his agents built, that is collapsing. 

God and Mammon cannot rule together on this earth. One or the other must give way. If the war lasts long enough, most of the Rockefeller wealth will be destroyed; so will the people. At the present time, the Rockefellers, personally, are conspicuous in government. Nelson

Rockefeller is an important factor in Washington and David, another son, just resigned as secretary to Mayor LaGuardia in New York City. 

Nelson Rockefeller's job is to try to "co-ordinate" cultural relations between North and South America, where the Rockefellers own tremendous wealth. In New York City, their wealth is estimated at one billion dollars including real estate holdings, city bonds, utilities and other properties. That is about one-fifth of their entire possessions. The rest of their possessions in the United States are worth about twice that amount, while their possessions in other countries are estimated to total that much again. 

How long can such vast ownership in one family continue? It is true that they contributed to colleges and hospitals, but virtually all that they contributed came out of the people and was saved from taxation, hence the people made these contributions through the Rockefellers. It is true that they contributed to the cost of parks, but this money also came from the people and was saved from taxes. The incomes of the vast Rockefeller holdings in Foundations were untaxed. It is also true that they contributed to hospitals and missions in China and other foreign countries, but they received concessions worth many times the amounts of their contributions. It is also true that they contributed to rehabilitate France after the first world war, but they were partners of the French government and controlled newspapers and other institutions there.

Every dollar of Rockefeller wealth was made out of the people and every dollar of it should be returned to them, except a small amount. 

The late John D. Rockefeller started with nothing; his family should atone for his immorality in business. It is unmoral to own an excessive private fortune today, particularly by those who did not earn it, and particularly since human beings are being sacrificed to sustain a wornout and decadent economic system that permits it. The United States is a political democracy, ruled by an industrial oligarchy headed by the Rockefellers. It is time this rulership was ended, so that real democracy can be restored. Neither Rockefeller, nor Morgan, nor any other of their clan, is capable of ruling in a changing world, and their agents should be made to serve the government instead of their individual masters. It is high time that oligarchical rulership based on private wealth possessions, be abolished and that government for all the people, be restored.

In a real democracy, the wealth-power of the individual is limited,

so that no person or group of persons is able to control. The incentive for excessive wealth is restricted and the impulse for worthy ambition, stimulated. This can only be accomplished now by limiting by law what the few can have and taking back the rest for society. All that is needed is nine words in the United States Constitution-Congress shall have the power to limit private fortunes. If the constitution can be amended for such superficial purposes as prohibition and woman suffrage, we certainly ought to be able to amend it for a fundamental purpose, to save the state of society. Individual effort would be stimulated if excessive private fortunes were limited by law and the interests of society would be better served. Had this been done before the first world war, there would have been no war then and no war now; one is the outgrowth of the other.

Had excessive private fortunes been limited by law through a capital tax before the first world war, there would have been no communism or nazism in Europe. Communism is the other extreme of unrestricted capitalism and nazism is a hybrid combination of both. The law of limitation would also have been adopted in other countries and the people would have learned to use their heads instead of their bodies to correct economic wrong. There is a capital tax now in Italy, France and other countries, but only for war purposes, and there is confiscation in every country under Hitler rule. All this could have been avoided by a capital tax in peace time, such as I have been advocating since 1913. This would not only have corrected economic hardship, but it would also have saved the few from the follies of excess wealth, and heartened and encouraged the many who would be saved from excessive taxation. 

The cost of government is becoming prohibitive and more taxes will be imposed in city, state and nation. It is a sad mistake to tax meagre incomes when excess wealth is available in the hands of those who never earned it. Taxing the incomes of the excessively rich, means little or nothing to them, so long as they control monopoly. Taxing a small income means a great deal to the small man and his family. He has no surplus wealth to fall back upon. Inheritance is only a privilege granted by the state. That privilege should be sharply curtailed and the only way this can be effectively done now, is through a tax on private capital. 

The cost of federal government is now ten times what it was in 1914. The interest bearing national debt is fifty times what it was then. 

The cost of government in cities and states is increasing and before long, the bulk of incomes will have to go to the government. Why not stop the trend now by limiting what the few can have and take back the rest for society? If this is done, the government becomes the largest stockholder and bondholder in all monopoly and draws the bulk of revenue therefrom. Most taxes could be wiped out. Governmental processes would be greatly simplified and the cost of government would be greatly reduced. 

The only alternative to such a step is city, state and national bankruptcy, $7

preceded by the bankruptcy of most of the people. The nation is bankrupt politically. The people are without vision. Excessive taxation. ruined many nations and it will ruin ours. The people have neglected their collective welfare, but that is no reason why their interests should not be protected, if only to follow the precept of Jesus that "Inasmuch as ye do it unto the least of them, ye do it unto me." 

We know the result of wealth-power government. It has led to disaster. Let us try real democracy (demos and krato-rule of the people) and see what happens. It can't be worse than what we have todayworld destruction. Representative government is not a failure except when it becomes oligarchical government. Then civilization must fail. 

Civilization is failing because the philosophy of Moses and Jesus is as futile today as it was in their day, and as is the principle of the brotherhood of man, the federation of the world. Both have been used only for abstract preachment. 

Jesus fought the money gang and achieved his earthly triumph when he drove the money changers out of the temple which they profaned. 

Moses fought the money gang and achieved final success when he smashed the "golden calf" by instituting laws for the periodic redistribution of private property every fiftieth year and the cancellation of private debts every seventh year (Leviticus 25 and Deuteronomy). This latter step is confirmed in the Lord's prayer "forgive us our debts as we forgive our debtors".

Let us limit excessive private fortunes by law and save civilization. 

Everything in life is limited including life itself, but there is no restriction on greed, which should be automatically restricted so that each generation can apply its own limit.

It is up to America to save civilization. I made this plain in my books "America Use Your Head," published in 1932, and "Rockefeller or God Who Will Rule?" published in 1938. If the United States constitution can be amended for prohibition, woman suffrage, and income tax, we ought to be able to amend it for a capital tax, to save the state of society. 

Think this over and act before it is too late.
