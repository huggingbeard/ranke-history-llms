Those who find the courage to raise their eyes and look will find TRUTH reflected from the bright face of danger, and finding truth, will be able to transmute deprivation, hardship and anxiety into hard, clean, moral and physical health. To those, this tempering in the fires of conflict will prove a boon beyond price, and the dawning of peace will find them ready and eager to rebuild their lives, and civilization, into new and nobler patterns.
