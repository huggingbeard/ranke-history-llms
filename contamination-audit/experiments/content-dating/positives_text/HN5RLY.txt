The long, low house looked out from its mountainside over the Alleghenies, range after range to the west; its back door led into a sheltered, sunny garden full of the smell of box. From the open hall gray stone steps led down between the old box bushes to where the roadway climbing from the village circled and came to an end. There at the first step Frances Dean stopped the gray gelding she was riding and, dismounting, dropped the reins into the small groom's waiting hands. She stood for a moment by the door, then shut it slowly, and with the closing of that wide, white door a barrier within herself, kept relentlessly up, went down.
