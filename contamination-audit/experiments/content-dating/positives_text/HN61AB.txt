II. TWO-STEP PROBLEMS 

A complex or two-step problem contains a main problem and a subordinate problem. This subordinate problem must be found and solved before the main problem can be solved and the desired result obtained. 

What you are asked to find in the main problem is always set out for you, but in a two-step problem one or more of the facts necessary for its answer is lacking. Finding their missing fact is what makes the subordinate problem.

The question of the subordinate problem is hidden and can be determined only by careful thinking. Answering this hidden question solves the subordinate problem and gives the missing fact needed to solve the main problem.

A complex problem is solved for you in the space below. Study it and solve all other two-step problems by the same plan. 1. 

The reading on a gas meter in January was 37,500 cu. ft. In 

February it was 42,500 cu. ft. At 80 per thousand cu. ft. what was the amount of the bill? 

What are you asked to find? The amount of the gas bill for January.

What is given? The price of gas per thousand cu. ft., or 80¢, and the two meter readings.

What is lacking? The number of thousand cu. ft. used. 

What is given to help find this? The January reading was 37,500 cu. ft. and the February reading was 42,500 cu. ft. 

How can the total number of cu. ft. be found? Subtract 37,500 from 42,500.

How can the total amount of the bill be found. 

Multiply the cost per thousand cu. ft. by the number of thousand cu. ft., or 5 × 80¢. 

Is your answer reasonable? Yes, because 5 × .80 is 4.00. 

Is your answer correct? Yes, because of $4.00 is 80¢.
