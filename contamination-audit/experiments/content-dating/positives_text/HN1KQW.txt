FOREWORD 

A book is a living organism. It not only has a head and body, but it contains a heart and life. It has a voice that speaks forth the mind of the author and its truths are the very pulsations of his heart and life. 

We call books whose ideas have enriched our lives "Our Friends." Over and over again we peruse their pages, as we would look into the face of a friend, to glean every truth that illuminates our minds and awakens within us anew the blessed fellowship that feeds our hungry hearts. A great responsibility rests upon one who sends forth another book, fraught as it is with influences for good or evil. 

The author was in his seventieth year, when his first book "The Kingdom of God" was published by 

Fleming H. Revell Co. This was followed by four other books, "The Beast, Modernism, and the Evangelical Faith"; "The Rapture-The Translation of the 

Saints"; "God's Great Gift-The Holy Spirit”; and "Revelation-Christ's Return." Now, twelve years after his first book, comes this one, "Heaven's Kingdom on Earth," which he sends forth with his heartfelt prayers. In none of these other books did he feel more joy in writing, nor did he write them with greater ease and alacrity. He has felt the same urge, been guided and guarded as in his other books, and his

PART I KINGDOM DESCRIBED

CHAPTER I KINGDOM NEW AND SPIRITUAL 

"Repent ye; for the kingdom of heaven is at hand" (Mat. 3:2). With this startling message did John the

Baptist arouse the Jewish nation from its sleep of centuries. Jesus takes up the same cry, "The time is fulfilled and the kingdom of God is at hand" (Mk. 1:14, 15; Mat. 4:17). Not only was this positive declaration heralded up and down the Jordan valley and over the Judean hills, but the preaching and work was to prepare the people of all classes to be ready for this coming kingdom. In order that the whole nation might be reached, Jesus sent out the twelve saying, "Preach, saying The kingdom of heaven is at hand" (Mat. 

10:7). Finally as the work became so urgent, "The 

Lord appointed seventy others and sent them two and two before his face into every city and place, whither 

He himself was about to come" "to say unto them

The kingdom of God is come nigh unto you" (Luke 

10:1, 9). Matthew says "Jesus went about all of the cities and villages preaching the Gospel of the kingdom" (4:23; 9:35). Then Jesus in many parables explained the nature of this kingdom and taught his disciples to pray "Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, as in heaven so on earth," which shows it is to 15

be a heavenly kingdom on the earth. Once He said "There are some here of them that stand by, who shall in no wise taste of death, till they see the kingdom of 

God come with power" (Mk. 9:1). He even gave "the keys of the kingdom of heaven" to Peter to open the kingdom while he was upon the earth, and Jesus would ratify it in heaven (Mat. 16:18). It was a 

Proclamation of the coming kingdom, not an offer.

KINGDOM TO COME AT PENTECOST 

It is very certain that this kingdom was not only "at hand" but that it would come before some who were then living should die; and it would come with 

POWER. All of Palestine was aroused with this new proclamation from the first word of John until Jesus "was speaking the things concerning the kingdom of 

God by the space of forty days" after his resurrection.

Not only is there no hint of a postponement of the kingdom or even a change; but with persistent force, the heralding of its coming and the preparation for it wa's so great, that all of the forces of earth and hell could not stop, or even retard it, until it swept on into a glorious fulfillment at Pentecost. Instead of the 

Transfiguration scene (Mk. 9:2-8) being a fulfillment of such strong statements as Mk. 9:1, it was only that which was to occur at Pentecost in miniature. Jesus knew that when the kingdom came at Pentecost it would be invisible (Luke 17:20, 21; 19:11) and that also the king himself could not be seen, so He revealed to the trusted three his glory, that He would have when He sat down at God's right hand (Acts 2:34,

WHAT IS THIS KINGDOM? 

It is a NEW kingdom having never existed in reality before. God says through Daniel "In the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed" (2:44). As

John and Jesus came in the days of those kings-Au gustus and Tiberius Caesar (Luke 2:1; 3:1)—what kingdom could it be but this kingdom of heaven of Daniel's prophecy. Not a word in Daniel about restoring the kingdom of Israel; but on the contrary "The God of Heaven shall set up a kingdom." It had not existed before. It was to be set up-have a com-

mencement.

How well does this agree with all we have seen in the Gospels. It was not "putting new wine into old wineskins"; neither were they to say "We have Abraham for our father." "The axe is laid at the root of the tree." A life of fruitage for God is demanded of the citizens of this coming kingdom. 

Nicodemus was startled when "the teacher sent from 

God" said to him "Ye must be born again." "That which is born of the flesh (fleshly Israel) is flesh," but "That which is born of the Spirit is spirit." So, “Except one be born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God." This is the same kingdom that John and Jesus, the Twelve and the

Seventy had said was "at hand," and it is entered by a new birth. No other way to enter into it except to be "born anew." Thus by a new birth does one come into this "new creation," which shows that it is a new kingdom, a new life, a new covenant, a new priesthood and a new King after Melchizadek-King of Righteousness and King of Peace" (Heb. 7:2).

"Behold, The Lamb of God, that taketh away the 

Sin of the world!" (Jno. 1:29, 36). ever.

This was John's introduction of Jesus to his disciples. Before there was any opposition, John saw 

Jesus as the Lamb to be slain at Jerusalem instead of being crowned. SIN, that had made even the theocracy impotent, and the kingdom of Israel a miserable failure, had to be destroyed before the Kingdom of God could become a reality in the earth and endure for- So Jesus came in the flesh that He might die for the world's sin. Every slain lamb offered for a sin offering, from that of Abel down to the Christ, was showing forth the necessity of the cross of Christ, where He was lifted up to "draw all men unto himself" (Jno. 12:32, 33). He called himself the Son of man more than He did the Son of God. Not the son of a man, but the Son of humanity-the human race. 

As all had sinned "He tasted death for every man." 

God's throne is established upon Law and Justice. His kingdom is a kingdom of Righteousness. He cannot be indifferent to his own laws even if man is. Man, who has trampled these Holy laws under his feet cannot come into this eternal kingdom of God without 22

being pardoned and cleansed. So Jesus the sinless 

One, as man's only representative, "laid down his life that He might take it again." "Who his own self bore our sins in his body upon the tree, that we having died unto sins, might live unto righteousness" (I Pet. 

2:24); "So that God might himself be just, and the justifier of him that hath faith in Jesus" (Rom. 3:26).

The cross not only shows God's great LOVE (Jno. 

3:16); but it shows his infinite wisdom and Righteousness. Instead of discarding, God magnified the Law, and kept His Holiness unsullied. There was no other way. But not only was it necessary for man to die for his sins through the efficacy of Christ's death, but he must live again. So "Christ was delivered up for our trespasses, and was raised for our justification" (Rom. 4:25).

THE GOSPEL, THE POWER 

While the facts of the Gospel are that "Christ died for our sins," etc., yet the crowning fact is that Christ "arose from the dead according to the Scriptures" (I 

Cor. 15:1-3). Other religions have their self-renunciation, but they have not this resurrected life that is so essential. Jesus says "I came that they may have life and may have it abundantly" (Jno. 10:10). 

So Jesus had not only to conquer Death, by destroying sin in its guilt and its power and its terrible consequences by His own death and resurrection; but He commissioned his church to preach this powerful Gospel to the uttermost corners of the earth, that it might as the good seed produce the children of the kingdom.

There could be no real Kingdom of God on earth while 

Jesus was in the flesh. "Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a grain of wheat fall into the earth and die, it abideth by itself alone; but if it die it beareth much fruit" (Jno. 12:24). Hear Jesus again, "Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer, and rise again from the dead the third day; and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name unto all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem" (Luke 24: 

47). Repentance could not bring Remission of sins until the death of Jesus. Jesus not only as the Lamb of God died for the sin of the world, but after his resurrection He went into heaven "and when He had offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down on the right hand of God; henceforth expecting till his enemies be made the footstool of his feet" (Heb. 10:12).

All this was necessary for the coming of the kingdom of God with Power at Pentecost. The "Holy Spirit could not be given until Jesus was glorified" (Jno. 

7:39), and Peter could not use the keys to open the kingdom until Christ was seated at God's right hand to "bind in heaven what Peter bound on earth," and had prayed the Father that He would send the Holy 

Spirit-the other Advocate-that He may be with you. forever" (Jno. 14:16).

KINGDOM IS INVISIBLE 

In the parable of the Pounds, Jesus gives briefly the order of the events of His Kingdom (Luke 19:11-27).

The occasion of the parable: Jesus "spake a parable because they supposed that the kingdom of God was

immediately to appear" (vs. 11). The reason for telling the parable was to show that the kingdom would not "immediately appear." This statement may seem to be against what has been stated in the foregoing; but a careful study clears up the seeming contradiction and throws much light upon our theme. It does not say that the kingdom would not come, but that it was not to appear or be visible. There was to be no open manifestation of the King or of His kingdom. It was not to be a material kingdom to be seen; for says 

Jesus "My kingdom is not of this world" (Jno. 18: 

36). "For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and Joy in the 

Holy Spirit" (Rom. 14:17). It is a spiritual kingdom. 

When asked by the Pharisees "when the kingdom of 

God cometh" Jesus said, "The Kingdom of God cometh not with observation;. neither shall they say, Lo here! or there! for lo, the kingdom of God is within you" (Luke 17:20, 21). He does not say "it is in your midst," for then it would be tangible and they could say "Lo here! or there!" It is clear that Jesus meant it was to be invisible when it came. Neither does Jesus say that the kingdom would be in the Pharisees. They wanted to know when it would come. 

Jesus' reply is that you cannot observe it when it does come. Those present cannot say, "Lo, here! or there!" for it is hidden within. It will be invisible-it will not appear when it does come. This becomes apparent in the parable.

JESUS RECEIVING HIS KINGDOM 

"Jesus said therefore, A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom and to return" (Luke 19:12). There are four clear facts stated here: (1) Jesus is this nobleman; (2) He is going into a far country, which is heaven; (3) He will while there receive his kingdom. He is to be there "a long time" (Mat. 25:19); until "God makes his enemies the footstool of his feet" (Acts 2:34, 35); (4) then He is "to Return." Verse 15 says He does not return until He has received his kingdom, neither does He give his awards until He returns, "having received his kingdom." It is very evident that the ten servants (vs. 13) represent the members of his church, who are to be awarded for their faithful work while 

He is away. Then his "citizens hated him and sent word we will not that this man reign over us" (vs. 14. 

See Ps. 2:1-3). This is the world, over which He will eventually reign. These enemies are to be destroyed at the battle of Armageddon (vs. 27; cf. Rev. 

16:16; 19:19-21). The main events that occur in the unfolding of the kingdom are illustrated by this parable. 

Jesus ascended to God's right hand "to receive for himself a kingdom." So God said to him "Sit at my right hand till I make thine enemies thy footstool." What is his kingdom? It is composed of the regeneratedthose born again (Jno. 3:3, 5). When Peter opened the kingdom with the keys, by giving the terms of Pardon to those who had been convicted by the "Word (seed) of the kingdom" (Jno. 3:5; Acts 2:38, 41),

those that obeyed entered into the kingdom and Jesus received them by giving them not only forgiveness, but the Holy Spirit, as the seal of their acceptance, and the earnest of their inheritance (Acts 2:38; Eph. 1:13, 14). Then the number became 5000 men (Acts 4:4).

Then He received the Samaritans and Gentiles even to the present time. For nineteen centuries He has been receiving his kingdom and that covers his work until it is received. About 57 generations have already passed to be with Jesus. He received them when they were converted. He also received their spirits when they died, as He did that of Stephen (Acts 7:59) and the spirit of Paul (II Cor. 5:6-9). But just before

He returns, He receives them all collectively in their glorified bodies in what is called "the Rapture," which is described by Paul (I Thess. 4:13-18) and also by 

Jesus (Mat. 24:29-31; cf. Rev. 17:14) and by John (Rev. 6:16, 17; 7:9-17). Then after "the marriage supper of the Lamb" (Rev. 19:7, 8), Jesus Returns with his "Elect" Bride (Rev. 17:14; 19:11-14, 19; 

20:4). At this time when "He had come back again having received his kingdom" then the thrones are given, which show the rewards that his faithful servants receive (Luke 19:15; Rev. 3:21; 20:4).

THE CHURCH AND THE KINGDOM 

Again in this parable we see that clear distinction between the church and the kingdom which has troubled so many. On heaven's side we see the kingdom of God; for here is Jesus the King receiving the regenerated, who are "born from above" by the Word,

"water and Spirit" and thus enter the kingdom and are received by the Christ. But on the earth side we see the servants, preaching and teaching the Gospel whereby the children of the kingdom are born. But their organization is the church and is visible. It is the only thing that is visible; for the powerful work of the Holy Spirit and the work of the Word-by which one is begotten-cannot be seen. It is invisible like the wind, seen only in its effects, says Jesus (Jno. 

3:8). But the church is composed of those in the flesh. Its work is done openly-obeying the ordinances and fulfilling all that is given in the Great Commission (Mat. 28:18-20). It is the recruiting station for the kingdom of God. It is divine for it is the body of 

Christ. Christ is its head while the Holy Spirit is its heart and life. It is the bride of Christ-"the Lamb's wife" (Eph. 5:25-30; Rev. 19:7).

This is the same distinction that Jesus makes in 

Mat. 16:18, 19. "Upon this rock (Peter's confession) I will build my church," but He immediately adds "I will give unto thee (Peter) the keys of the kingdom of heaven." When Peter opened the kingdom of heaven by giving the law of pardon, 3000 entered into this kingdom of heaven (Jno. 3:3, 5; 

Acts 2:41), which was and is invisible-it does not outwardly appear; but the visible organization is the church-built upon Peter's confession-the Rock. "The dream of a common-wealth of peace, and righteousness and general welfare for all, of equality in a classless society, in an earthly state, in which wages will be commensurate with human need," etc., is no

where promised in this Christian dispensation. The kingdom of God cannot appear in visible form until the children of the kingdom are born and resurrected with bodies and hearts like Jesus. As fast as a generation of obedient believers, who have tried and partially lived the sermon on the mount, pass on, "they are kept by the power of God unto a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time" (I Pet. 1:5; cf. Rom. 8:23).

"Out of the great tribulation" where "Men are fainting for fear" up into the place where "they shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more, neither shall the sun strike upon them nor any heat." "." From the very beginning, God has been drawing out of mankind a people for Himself. Patriarchs and prophets like

the Gospel of Christ, a people so vast that "no man can number them." It is of this people, called the man-child that we shall further study. 

CHRIST'S TRUE CHURCH TRANSLATED 

"And the dragon standeth before the woman that is about to be delivered that when she is delivered he may devour her child. As the great concern of the dragon is about this child and his destiny, we share the same anxiety. We may differ in our interpretation of the woman; but let us be all ears and attention to be sure that we understand rightly the meaning of this "SON-the man child" or "a Masculine SON" as Rotherham translates it. Some interpreters say it is 

Christ and him only. But why these peculiar qualifying names, "man child" or "masculine son" when it is clearly stated he is "a son." It is certainly Jesus the 

Christ; but this is "a great sign" and this is a book of symbols and we have a right to see if it is not Christ and something besides, that illustrates his great work and victory. It can certainly refer to Christ and his body the church. Christ's church is his body of which 

He is the head. We are exhorted to "build up the body of Christ" to "attain unto the knowledge of the

Son of God unto a fullgrown man unto the measure of the statue of the fullness of Christ;" "that we may grow up in all things into him who is the HEAD, even Christ; from whom all the body fitly framed and knitted

together, etc." (Eph. 4:12-16). Christ would not be complete without His body-the church. Then this is 

Christ's Home-coming with his body the church that 

He has won out of the earth. Why should it dwindle into a historical fact-that Jesus ascended God's throne? That is a great fundamental truth that no believer disputes. It needs no sign to substantiate it.

Christ's body. "Out of the great tribulation" (Rev.

OVERCOMING Satan 

"Now is come the salvation, and the power and the kingdom of our God and the authority of His Christ; for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, who accuseth them before our God day and night. And they overcame him because of the blood of the LAMB, and because of the word of their testimony; and they loved not their life even unto death" (Rev. 12:10-11).

This "war in heaven" and the casting down of Satan is so dramatic that it furnished much material to that great genius, John Milton, in his great epic poem, "Paradise Lost and Regained." Nothing appeals to the imagination more than this mighty conflict between good and evil; light and darkness; Christ and Satan and Heaven and Hell, that has continued with unabated

fury from the gates of Eden to the present time. Here we find the four names of "The Adversary" (I Pet. 

5:8); "great dragon, the old serpent, the Devil and 

Satan" (Rev. 12:9; cf. 20:2). Devil and Satan are given in capitals, showing that they are the proper names, while dragon and serpent are descriptive names. 

He is also called "The deceiver of the whole world" (cf. 20:3). Jesus adds, "He was a murderer from the beginning" and "He is a LIAR and the father thereof" (Jno. 8:44). Jesus had no doubts about a personal

Devil. His only work is to "accuse the brethren" which he keeps up "night and day." Since he had his head crushed at the cross, while nibbling at the Savior's heel, he can only "accuse the brethren." He is the same old sneering devil as when he slandered Job.

His great contention before the cross was that as man had broken the eternal, perfect law of God, that he was eternally lost. Truth is forever truth and right is forever right. The moral law of God could not be changed or set aside; so man, the sinner, was forever doomed. But when Jesus the Son of man-humanity -received the penalty upon himself, the believer in CHRIST answers the law through Him, having died for his sins through his substitute. Now, man by a new birth, comes into a new reign of righteousness. 

So now the Devil's only work is to tempt and then to accuse the brethren by showing that, while saved from the Guilt and penalty of sin which is death, nevertheless they are imperfect, because of their fleshly bodies. 

But when Christ presents the church to himself with their glorified, spiritual bodies, Satan is overwhelmed in

defeat. His mouth is shut. Then Michael, the High 

Sheriff of heaven, arises with his angels and cast the 

Devil down to the earth (Rev. 12:9). God and Christ are patient, looking upon the Christian, as he will be when he is perfected and not as he is while in the flesh. 

THE ELECT BRIDE OVERTHROWS SATAN 

But, there is another aspect to this scene in heaven. "The war in heaven" was not ended by Michael and his angels; but it is the Bride Elect of Christ that overcomes Satan. She has this exalted privilege, not only to "also overcome" when she descends with Jesus to destroy the beast, etc. (Rev. 17:14); but she, in her ascension with Christ as in this scene, overcomes 

Satan; for it says, "And they overcame him (Satan) because of the blood of the Lamb, and because of the word of their testimony; and they loved not their life even unto death" (Rev. 12:11). To get the full force of the above statement we must envisage the whole scene. Here is Satan, who from the days of Job has been slandering and accusing every child of God, who has though imperfectly been reaching out and up for God and light and LIFE. Whenever he can tempt one to go wrong, he is like the spider. After entangling his victim in his web until he is helpless, he then gives him the "ha! ha!" and goes and accuses him before God. How he did "desire Peter to sift him," but his glee was spoiled by the "prayer" and "look of Jesus" so that Peter went out and wept bitterly. Oh, the tears and heart-aches and bitter regrets, that this old Adversary has caused the human

race since "Eve's lamentation" on leaving Paradise, until the utter destruction of the "Ancient Serpent" in the Lake of fire (Rev. 20:10). He has relentlessly pursued every one of God's chosen, and with great skill and cunning, he continues up to this very hour when Christ catches them out of the mouth of the dragon that was open to devour them. It will be a holy joy to see him writhe amidst the wonderful happiness of that mighty throng, as the Elect-bride arises and overcomes him with her united testimony.

The Devil is overwhelmed with shame and humiliation and is cast down to the earth with the great voice in heaven, saying, "Now is come the salvation and the power, and the kingdom of our God and the Authority of the Christ; for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, who accuseth them before our God day and night." NEW STARTING POINT

The triumphal home-coming of the Christ into the

It is like the clearing of the city of the women and children before the battle begins. Beast and False Prophet. It is only "for a little moment" (3½ years); but it is long enough to show what man will do without God. This lawlessness is

seen even now over the earth in nations breaking sacred peace-pacts, and the disarmament conference failing; nations of Europe repudiating their just debts; and this latest Economical Conference in London which is more disturbing than stabilizing in its results, as it has come hopelessly to its end.

"And the great dragon was cast down, the old serpent, he that is called the Devil and Satan, the deceiver of the whole world: he was cast down to the earth, and his angels were cast down with him." Prophet, which endures only forty two months-3½ years (Rev. 13:5). The woman also, whom the dragon persecutes, is protected in a prepared place 113

from the face of the serpent for "a time and times and a half a time," which is said to be in verse six, 1260 days. A time is 360 days-two times are 720 days and one half a time is 180 days, which are 1260 days or 

3½ years (Rev. 12:6, 14). Then the Beast is to make war with the saints, and to overcome them" (Rev. 

13:7). But these saints are not Christ and his church, for when the beast wars with the Lamb he himself is overcome (17:14). So these saints may be the 

Israelites "the holy people" of Dan. 12:7. In Rev. 

11:1, 2 we find that the temple has been built and accepted by God; but the court that is without the temple is rejected (not measured). "For it hath been given unto the nations: and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months" (Rev. 11:2).

The holy city here agrees with "the holy people" in 

Dan. 12:7, and the time is the same, for 42 months is 

3½ years, the same as Dan. 12:7. The time of the reign of the beast is the same (Rev. 13:5). And the work is the same for "the treading under foot" will "break in pieces the power of the holy people” (Dan. 

12:7). Then "the two witnesses prophesy 1260 days clothed in sackcloth." This shows mourning and covers the same time of Dan. 12:7. They are slain by the beast (Rev. 11:7), which must be near the time of his own destruction, for he only reigns 42 months (3½ years). Now the time of the reign of the beast, and that of the people he deals with, is stated SEVEN times to be just 3½ years. one, given with an oath. "The False prophet" (Rev. 

13:11-18; 16:13; 19:20; 20:10) is to be co-existent with the beast; and "Babylon the harlot" is to be destroyed by the beast and ten horns; and there is to be great rejoicing in heaven over her destruction, before the beast is destroyed (Rev. 17:16, 17; 19:1-4, 20).

CHARACTERISTICS OF THE BEAST 

This beast, though first mentioned in Rev. 11:7, is fully described in Rev. 13:1-8; 17:3, 7-17; Dan. 

7:15-27 and II Thess. 2:3-9). It has striking characteristics. It is a government like the one that controls 

Russia today. (1) Multitudes-the Proletariat. It "comes up out of the sea" (Rev. 13:1). Water means people (Rev. 

17:15). Sea is universal. "The waters which thou sawest, where the harlot sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations and tongues." In verse three we find "the harlot sitting on a scarlet-colored beast."

So this beast is composed of "peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues" (17:1, 3, 15). As this is a special definition of this beast, let us notice this group of words. There are seven groupings of these words in 

Revelation (5:9; 7:9; 10:11; 11:9; 13:7; 14:6; 

17:15); but only in this last verse is the word "tribes" (phules) changed to "multitudes" (oxloi), which means "the common people opposed to the rulers"

(Grimms Wilkes, Greek Lex.-J. H. Thayer). This lexicon has a special reference to Rev. 17:15. This describes the rule of the Proletariat-the Communistic government of the Soviet Union of Russia today. It is a government of the "multitudes"; and it is their aim to overthrow all power that comes from individualistic effort, in thrift or in independent success. "The common people against the rulers" or the successful, called "Capitalists," is what differentiates communism from "Fascism" of Italy, or the present government of Germany under Adolf Hitler. (2) RED is its color, which is also that of the devil (Rev. 12:3). It is a brilliant red-"A Scarletcolored Beast," when it becomes the Devil incarnate (Rev. 17:3). This color is fastened on the Soviet

Union of Russia all over the world. Their army is the "RED Army"; and this is the color given to her diplomacy as she is propagating her doctrines throughout the earth. Italy has her Black shirts, and Germany has her Brown shirts; but the word RED covers everything distinctly Russian. (3) Satanic in organization. "A great Red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns and upon his heads seven diadems" (Rev. 12:3). In Rev. 13:1 the beast is described, "Having ten horns and seven heads, and on his horns ten diadems and upon his heads names of blasphemy." Note that while the number of the heads and horns are the same, yet the diadems are on the heads of the dragon for ruling, but only names of blasphemy are upon the heads of the beast. But on the ten horns of the beast are the diadems, which

show that these horns are now ruling and active, while the heads of the dragon are crowned for counsel and control. This is made very emphatic in Rev. 

13:2, "And the dragon gave him his power and his throne and great authority."

IS THE BEAST A PERSON? 

Because this beast is personified, having the pronoun "he," etc., is no more reason for calling it a person than the pronoun "she" would indicate that the great "Apostasy," called "Babylon the harlot" is a beautiful, entrancing woman, seducing the nations of the earth, with her charming personality. No one is looking for such a woman to arise. There are many reasons for saying that this "beast" is a government and its characteristics are not those of a man; but of a universal communistic government as we see today in Russia which is spreading throughout the world. (1) John saw this beast coming up out of the sea as Daniel saw "four beasts come up from the sea."

These beasts of Daniel we know are universal governments. (2) In the defining of this beast in Rev. 17:1, 3, 15, the "woman is seen sitting upon a scarlet-colored beast," and she is sitting also upon many waters, which are said to be "Peoples, multitudes, nations, and tongues" (vs. 15). This is certainly a government spreading throughout the earth and not a person. (3) There is no human Person at the head of Christ's body, the church. Christ is the head and dwells in his body by the power of the indwelling

Spirit. He controls it and at His appearance in the cloud He will catch it up in the cloud "to meet the 

Lord in the air."

So Satan controls those that are his, and has them organized for the purpose that when he descends he may complete his plans in gathering his hosts into a government which he himself will rule with consummate skill. The Beast government is the devil incarnate and is the Proletariat government throughout the earth of whatever name. He has seven heads and ten horns, and so has this beast (Rev. 12:3; 13:1). And with the two-horned beast-the Ecclesiastical branchthey will rule the world until the battle of Armageddon. It takes both of these to make the Antichrist (chap. XIII). It is "the number of a man" or man's number which is four. Four plus two, one-half of four-man's number brought to its highest point-is six. This number repeated three times makes the superman, "666." It is man exalting himself above

God in a Super-government (II Thess. 2:4; Rev. 

13:8; Ps. 2:1-3). This beast government may have a head, like Gen. Foch was with the Allies, and will be, probably, located at Moscow or at Rome; but the biggest earthly thing about this government is its ten horns, which are crowned for ruling and action. But there is no "notable horn" (Dan. 8:5), but all are equal.

TEN HORNS-TEN DICTATORS 

While the Beast government, by its propaganda, as we see it today is reaching into every nation; yet it

comes into its controlling power when it federates the ten horns "who give their kingdom unto the beast" (Rev. 17:17). Another thing is noticeable, viz.: The heads on Satan are mentioned before the horns, while the horns on the beast are mentioned before the heads. 

This shows that the heads of the dragon, given with sevenfold power are guiding with Satanic wisdom, while the power to execute is given to the ten horns. 

What is a HORN? He is a king or ruler without a kingdom. "Who have received no kingdom as yet; but they receive authority as kings with the beast for one hour. These have one mind and they give their power and authority unto the beast" (Rev. 17:13).

A horn, then, is a ruler that has no kingdom, but receives authority as a king. Such a ruler we call a 

Dictator. We have four full-fledged Dictators already.

The last one, Adolf Hitler, is a fine illustration. He is a ruler "who has received no kingdom as yet." He is a Dictator that "has been given authority as a king" by the German nation. But he has no kingdom as yet.

He says, "The time has not yet come for that, so I will be a Dictator for four years." If the ex-Crown Prince had been invited to restore the monarchy, and had accepted, he would not be a Dictator, but a king with a kingdom, so would not be a "horn." But Adolf

Hitler as a Dictator has more power than Kaiser Wilhelm ever had. He came into power on the crest of the wave that a turbulent Germany in its restlessness has cast up. He is not a usurper, but is there by the votes of the people and by the indorsement of President Hindenburg. (Though written over one

year ago yet no change is needed.) He believes in a representative government, but its Reichstag must have but one great party and that is to be under his control. 

This is exactly the case with Mussolini and Italy. 

Mussolini has no kingdom, and makes no such claim, for he keeps the present king on the throne. He is there by the consent of the Italian people and rules with a power that is more absolute than any king on earth. He has no throne but dresses and lives as a citizen of Rome. The same is true of Josef Stalin, Dictator of Russia. He "has no kingdom as yet."

But no king has such power. He is Dictator by the consent of the governed. Another Dictator is Mustapha Kemal of Turkey. KINGDOM GATHERED OF ALL DISPENSATIONS 

How blessed is the thought that our Heavenly

Father has ever been seeking to save out of all the dispensations those who desire to be conformed to "His Image" through Christ. How He planted Abraham and the Hebrew nation in that strategic place- Palestine, between the two great centers of civilization, Babylon and Egypt. When these were subdued by 

Grecian culture and wisdom and brought under Roman law and government, for the dissemination of the Gospel of the Kingdom throughout the earth, then Jesus inaugurated the invisible kingdom of God, which is in outward form His Church-His body. How could 

Jesus have done His work and started His kingdom a century and a half earlier in the time of the Maccabees, when the Hebrew nation was gasping for breath or in a few decades later, when Jerusalem was destroyed and the Jews were scattered. "But in the fulness of time," which was the only time possible in the history of man, the "Star of Bethlehem" appeared over the divine BABE, causing the world to reckon time from that event. The earth quaked when it drank in the Life-blood of the Son of God-the Son of mankind, by which price Christ redeemed this earth for

His Kingdom of Righteousness. The "Tongues of

Flame" at Pentecost signified the presence and power of Christ's mighty advocate-the Holy Spirit, whereby "This Gospel of the Kingdom should be preached in the whole world for a testimony unto all the nations and then shall the end come" (Mat. 24:14). This verse shows that God in His infinite LOVE is not going to end His gathering of the children of the Kingdom, until every nation has within itself a witness of His marvelous Grace in the gift of His LOVE (John 3:16). Who knows, but what in His coming in contact with all the nations during the 4000 years B. C. that this was his great purpose. He sent Jonah to bring Ninevah to repentance and "Ninevah will rise up in the judgment and condemn this generation," says Jesus. He had Daniel and his fellows to show 

His power in Babylon, and Mordecai and Esther to manifest His Providence in Persia. Through Joseph

He impressed His wisdom and power upon Egypt, out of which He finally brought forth His chosen people with a mighty hand overwhelming all gods by His power. Thus He was making His impress upon all the nations of antiquity and has moved mightily upon all the nations to the present time. The God of Israel was no "tribal-God"; but is the God of the universe and for all Time. He makes no mistakes, neither has there been any postponements. The purposes of God have moved on with infinite power and with amazing precision. The door (which is Christ and for all dispensations) into the kingdom of God, is open today for all who will to enter. In fact the only place for one to enter the kingdom of God is right here on this

earth, where Christ poured out His Life. The kingdom must come into us as well as for us to enter into the kingdom.

The church of Christ is the outward expression of the kingdom of God within. As there is but one kingdom of Christ and only "One Body," says Paul, for the church, what an anomaly to say, "the Roman, Greek or Anglican Catholic" Churches, as if there could be more than one Catholic Church. Then what shall we say of the hundreds of denominational names and bodies. "For there is none other name but Christ, whereby we must be saved." But God knows his own. 

Let us take Paul's confession of Faith viz. "If thou shalt confess with thy mouth Jesus as LORD” Ruler 

KING (Rom. 10:9), and see Him as our KING, al ready reigning within our hearts, then we will have the "kingdom of God within" and will be always ready to meet Christ with joy when He returns to fill the 

Earth with His Glorious Kingdom of Grace and Power.

BEAST REIGNning on EarTH 

"Why do the nations rage and the peoples meditate a vain thing? The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together against Jehovah and against his anointed saying, "Let us break their bonds asunder, and cast away their cords from us. He that sitteth in the heavens will laugh. The Lord will have them in derision" (Ps. 2:1-4).

This Psalm shows that God has never been mistaken about the destiny of man; but that He knows the end

from the beginning. No words can describe the present situation of the world better than the above Psalm. "Nations raging and the peoples meditating a vain thing" describe the nations of today in a graphic way. 

The failure of the greatest conference ever assembled, taking in all of the nations of the world; diplomats of the world going to and fro trying to meet the demands of the hour; the League of Nations feeling its own impotency to promote welfare and peace of the nations; the Disarmament Conference having failed, being discredited by Italy and Russia, and now Japan has abrogated the treaty of Washington, 1922, for The 

Limitation of Navies. These failures will lead the nations to distrust councils and conferences which will tend more and more to Dictatorship. If each nation has to take care of itself and carry on its work with other nations independently, it will need to have centralized power that can think and act quickly and not to be held back by cumbersome things like unwieldy parliaments and legislative assemblies. The time for calm deliberation, which was once their strength and safety, is giving away to quick action. It would not be surprising to see four to six more dictators in power before many months have passed. The ten horns, or 

Dictators, which will be given power to act "for one hour"-a short time may be already in the Offing (Rev. 17:12).

(II Thess. 2:7). When a nation or people breaks with God then anarchy prevails. But "God that sitteth in the heavens will laugh and have them in derision." 

Then in verse five we see the awful wrath being poured out (See Rev. 15:1, 7; 16-1) which culminates in the battle of Armageddon in the entire destruction of the Beast and the False Prophet and their entire 

ARMIES collected from the whole earth (Rev. 16:14; 

19:20, 21). But while their Governments and Armies are destroyed, the people will remain. One of God's greatest acts of Mercy and Love will be in His utter destruction of all ARMIES and ALL false govern ments and Religions that produce them.

GOD'S ETERNAL KINGDOM 

Then it is that God offers to the world anew this "Heaven's Kingdom" in the Millennial reign of His 

Son, "His anointed." "Be wise, O ye Kings; Be instructed ye judges of the earth; Serve Jehovah with fear and rejoice with trembling" (Ps. 2:10-11). They have learned their lesson and "rejoice with trembling"

(cf Rev. 21:24-26). But at the close of the Millennium, opposition begins to develop. Some people become tired of being good. God admonishes them "Kiss the Son (make peace) lest He be angry, and ye perish in the way, for his wrath will soon be kindled.” 

God turns Satan loose "for a little time" (Rev. 20:3), for God tempts no one, but His great purpose to have every one to be "in His likeness" must be accomplished. For God will not have any unwilling obedience in the hearts of his children for his Eternal Kingdom. No one will be admitted except he "be conformed to the Image of His Son" (Rom. 8:29).

When Satan gathers from the outer rim of the earth his unwashed throng and "compasses the camp of the saints about" ("unwalled villages" of a restored Israel -Ezek. 38:11), "and the Beloved City" (the Millennial City), then fire comes down out of heaven and devours them (Rev. 20:3, 7-9). This does not imply "a great Revolt" but it is the final sifting of the chaff from the wheat (Mat. 13:40-50). Then after the final Judgment and the second death (20:14), the Son 

Himself, as the Elder Brother, takes His place with this NUMBERLESS host of God's eternal children, "so that God may be All in All" (I Cor. 15:26–28). 

Then God says, "Blessed are all they that take refuge in Him"-God's Son-the Elder Brother (Ps. 2:12). 

Then there will be a "New Heaven and a New Earth," which is "Heaven's Kingdom on Earth."
