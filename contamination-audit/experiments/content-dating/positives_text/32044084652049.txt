FOREWORD. 

These messages were sent through inspiration and by the use of automatic writing from one in spirit, Herodius, by name. 

Spirit inspires and sends the thoughts through impressing the mind. This method of communication between the two worlds is a natural way, there is nothing strange or supernatural, and this mode of communication enables those in the Higher sphere to instruct and prepare those still on earth for their final entry into the Kingdom of God.

When once belief in the continued existence of man is established, then man is prepared to receive the thoughts and teachings which are sent. 

He learns to turn and ask for help in his daily life, he is continually aware of the influences for good and he learns how to live rightly. 

It is much the same, one goes to hear a fine sermon or talk, from those who have studied, and know how to impart knowledge, so with the communion of Saints, man turns his mind to receive the help from those who are prepared to teach him and so he benefits daily through the
