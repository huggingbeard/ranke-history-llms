This is your own spelling book. It will save much time for you because only the words that you need to study will be marked in it. 

If you wish to learn to spell accurately you must study the right way. A very good plan for studying is printed on the first page of the book. Be sure you know the six steps described on that page and are ready to use them before you try to learn to spell any of the words checked in your book.

At the beginning of each week your teacher will pronounce twenty words for you to write before you have studied them. 

The words you cannot spell will be marked in your book for . you to study during the week. At the middle of the week and again at the end of the week you will have tests to see how well you have learned the words you did not know.

Any words you miss in the test at the end of the week are to be written on page 20 or page 39.

HOW TO LEARN TO SPELL A NEW WORD TRANSFERRED TO HARVARD COLLEGE LIBRARY HALON 

1. Say the word.

Look at the word carefully. Ask your teacher or use the dictionary if you are not sure how to pronounce it. Say the word to yourself, pronouncing each syllable very carefully. 

2. Spell the word to yourself.

Look at the word. Spell it softly to yourself two or three times. 

Watch carefully for difficult syllables. 

3. See the word in syllables.

Close your eyes and try to see a picture of the word. If it is a long word, think of each syllable separately. Spell it letter by letter and syllable by syllable like this: (yesterday) y-e-s-t-e-r-d-a-y. 

Be sure you see each letter clearly. 

4. Check with the book.

Look in your book to see if you had the right picture of the word. If you did not, do steps one, two, and three again. Keep trying until you are sure you can say the letters correctly. 

5. Write the word. 

Write the word on paper without looking in your book. Say each letter to yourself as you write it. Look in the book to see if you have written it correctly. If it is not correct, do steps one, two, three, and four again. 

6. Write the word again. 

Write the word. Compare it with your book. If it is correct, write it once more. Look in your book again to see if it is correct. 

When you have gone through all the steps without making a mistake, you are ready to study the next word. 10 -1.
