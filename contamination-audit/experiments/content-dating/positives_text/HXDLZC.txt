Preface 

HIS book contains the lessons in human nature, essential knowledge and tact, which life has taught a practical man of the present day. 

Situations and problems of daily occurrence are the themes, and they are dealt with in a helpful manner that eschews hypocrisy and wordiness. Theory and superspirituality have no place in the work, and all counsel given is the fruit of experience.

Many valuable facts concerning woman are set forth, which were gained by the studied observation of child, lover and husband. Although man may never entirely understand his fair helpmate -on account of her many peculiarities-he ought to learn as much about her as he may, in order to better enjoy her companionship and make her happy.

Love is allotted much space in this work; and comprehensive advice is offered to men and women, trial of which will unquestionably contribute to the ecstasy of their loving. 

PAUL NAMI. 

San Francisco, Calif.
