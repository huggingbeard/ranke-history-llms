the Liberty Cecast There Must Be Revolution BY MORRISON I. SWIFT 

Man has at last reached the beginning of a great life. He has been saturated with religious superstitions and hallucinations which paralyzed and distorted his brain, and now he sees that all these have treacherously failed him. He never relied on himself and his brains alone, and that was his fatal blunder. All religion is stagnation and decay. The New Revolution we are now entering is the Epoch of Trust in Man's Intelligence alone. That intelligence has already vindicated itself in 

Science with surpassing brilliance after long black ages of repression by religion, and its work is only begun. 

Science and its inventions have been man's only really great achievements in the entire Christian era. 

From now on unlimited wealth must be expended in the scientific regeneration of man and the world, and his rescue from religion. Human relations must first be rectified by shutting off luxury and using the wealth shamefully squandered on it for developing all people to the topmost limit of their ability. The rich class and inequality must be liquidated. This is a Crusade which should be launched on the world by America, whose material resources and latent brains are so vast. 

Those mentally unable to rise to this standard of life should be eliminated by non-propagation. The great social crash was a most needed event for revealing the rottenness and ruin that religion and starved brains have brought on mankind. There is no reason for dismay. This greatest revolution must be executed by the masses of people in new forms of organization and action. No public officials can accomplish it. 

The pages of this booklet have been circulated separately at the times of their date. They are exhibits of phases of man's slavery to his folly, and their broad purpose is to show that man is the sole architect of his destiny and should take control.

The Curse of Capitalism 

Capitalism split America into rich and poor. It rotted the rich with luxury and rotted the poor with poverty. It robbed the poor of opportunities, degrading them and degrading the whole nation. The rich were excessively depraved by their greed and selfindulgence. Most of them were so dehumanized by their riches that they cared little what became of the country if they could gorge, dress, drink, strut, travel, play, squander and debauch. Of the colossal crimes of capitalists two were supremely deadly. Before capitalism got its pace this was an American country, fairly homogeneous in population. Capitalism wrecked that, first with the civil war with which capitalists crushed the south, killing hosts of genuine Americans of both sections; secondly, with the huge foreign populations which capitalists lured here with lies, to work slavishly for mean pay and capitalists' enrichment. This immense mass profoundly changed American character. The swindling capitalists, not the swindled immigrants were to blame.

The capitalists also debauched the American stock by putting money above manhood. To 'rise', young Americans had to toady to the soulless moneygrabbers. Capitalists owned the spineless college professors and the more spineless obsequious clergies, and used both to glorify the foul crimes of capitalism. Pursuit of intelligence was left solely to a few visionary lunatics called scientists, fellows without brains enough to get rich. Such was America until 

1929 when the whole vile system with its bandit bosses exploded. Machinery could not work because the capitalist owners would not let it without their Shylockian profits, which the bled-white people could not pay. So the American millions starved. 

If capitalists are not peacefully evicted from owning machinery, there will be a furious revolutionary eviction. Machinery and people must be united through machine ownership by the people, and the way is by eliminating the capitalist middlemen, who are useless parasites on industry and society, sucking up the whole cream of production and wasting it hideously. 

Living to live instead of to rob must be the new social law for all. Then no longer will the greatest hog be the people's god. Human equality is the goal, but it is absurd to talk of equality while the country reeks with rich and poor morons and defectives.

Legally stop the reproduction of both; prevent riches at the top and poverty anywhere; create a classless society with the motive of social creativeness; and standardize incomes to strangle greed and prevent personal accumulation. For all rich people are leeches living cruelly on others.

Our Senile Religions 

"The persistence of mathematicians in sticking to the Aristotelian laws of logic is an example of 'the unscratchable stupidity of the human race,' said a great scientist recently. As all our religions were invented by people beside whom, Aristotle was a god, religious persons are the very most unscratchably stupid people alive. All religion is senility, and the more ancient it is, the more senile. The mammoth stupidity and lazyness of civilized man is shown by his impotent 'gods'. Nature gave man a brain. It was evolved through animals, savages, and then ourselves. Think what man did with this intelligence which evolution gave him! The wretched fellow despised it as his enemy. Did he use it to think with? To search out the causes of his evils and conquer them? To elevate himself? 

No, he used it to pray with! A total fool could not have done worse. He invented ghosts, called them gods, and wasted his time begging them to get him out of the hole Nature and himself had put him in. He stayed in that hole, praying, and has grown steadily worse, for he has used his brain mainly to pray with and to sharpen his capacity to steal. The writer once heard an Indian doctor pounding a tin pan to drive the evil spirits out of a sick child. How dreadful? But all pious Americans who prattle prayers to their ghost-god are on the intelligence level of that benighted Siwash, no higher. And that is why the world is still a combined lunatic asylum, hospital and hell. Instead of using and evolving his great latent brain to conquer his hideous god-given evils and build a noble earth, civilized man has starved his brain while he whinnied to the baleful ghost he calls 'god'.

It is easy to hoot to 'heaven' in prayer and to hire priests to juggle the universe for your benefit, but these pious fooleries do no more good than a baby's crying to cure its colic. All man's gods are manmade idols. Abolish them. Brains are man's only god. Religion is crude savage idolatry. You only have to get on your knees and wiggle your tongue.

All religion is ignorance, imbecility, or profiteering. 

Toady to god and thou shalt be saved is the babble of babes. Impound or burn all bibles with their ghastly balderdash about ghosts and gods, and teach intelligence. Use men's vast united brain power to make a glorious earth and mankind. Then will the human race justify its existence, which now it does not. Used rationally man's brains could do more for human good in one year than all the prayers of all the world's people praying a billion years.

White Slaves of Rich Parasites 

Capitalism is white slavery and far more cruel than colored slavery. Black slaves were too costly to starve or kill; they had to be bought or raised by their owners, but white slaves feed and 'grow' themselves till the capitalist needs them, and when he doesn't he saves by turning them out to starve, die, or be fed by public doles that tax others. Capitalism is the highest peak of cowardly brutality. The noble capitalists evade risking their priceless persons defending themselves from the vengeance of their victims. Capitalist society hires soldiers and police to do that for them by risking their lives in clubbing and shooting the starved mobs of their brother workers who frienziedly fight for their children's bread, while the sportive capitalists gayly drink and joke about these merry events in their palaces and clubs. 

Yes, southern slavery was more humane than our black and bestial capitalism, and America is a ghastly nursery of brutes and dupes.

Machinery is a colossal new gift of nature to all men, and the combine of parasites that have seized and claim to own it, taxing the rest a starving price for its use, must be shorn of that criminal monopoly. 

Capitalists have no more right to rob the people of free and equal benefits from machinery than to tax them for breathing the air, as they surely would if they could cage it, or charge a fee for the sunlight if they could corral the sun. Just living on earth entitles all people to many rights which swinish capitalists have stolen from them, and which now must be restored if a volcanic revolution is not preferred. 

Capitalists will sandbag President Roosevelt on the first chance. But this is a people's war, and they must organize for it. Machinery belongs to all, not to rich gangsters and banks, and the State must own the machine system and install scientists to direct it. Unemployment and revolution cannot then happen. Profiteering will end and the shirking profiteers have to work or starve. No one will dole them.

This is white slave emancipation. There must be a super-combine, above parties, of all decent citizens to achieve this without a blasting revolution. The 

Capitalist Press, which is our actual Public School, is the main cause of our national crash. With noble exceptions it has always glorified greed and monopoly. 

To spike that poisonous press there should everywhere be an honest Community Press for adults, as part of the public school system. Free press is not free lying. Quacks cannot doctor bodies, and quack editorial Truth-Twisters should be barred from mutilating minds.

The American Sucker Class 

The American Constitution has become a bulwark of injustice, inequality, robbery, and the protection of rich scoundrels. It has succeeded in this far beyond the wildest hopes of its perverters and has brought the nation into the shadow of an avenging revolution. A new Constitution and Declaration of Independence are needed, declaring: The Right of everyone to life, liberty and happiness; equal benefits of machinery, land, education, recreation and means of development, with special riches and privileges for none. This will create a superior human race. The Federal Constitution was not as utterly asinine as the Courts have made it. It never gave the Supreme Court a right to mutilate and massacre it; calling that butchery 'Interpretation'. It never ordered Congress to pass 'no laws impairing the obligation of contracts', which made swindlers, crooks and thieves bosses of America. The Constitution forbade the States to do that, in order to leave the entire right to do it with Congress, which would have saved for honest people the countless billions crafty bandits stole from them by fraudulent contracts. 

Capitalist Sucker Crimes. Suckers are those who consume what others create, while producing no fair equivalent or none at all. Society, like fruit trees, grows suckers that have to be amputated. Capitalists are such suckers, partial or entire. Few of them produce more than a slight part of the wealth they seize. That class must go, or Revolution will come. Even human suckers will honestly work if otherwise they must starve. Numberless millions of workers have starved to fatten or debauch suckers. 

Eliminating suckers alone will save the nation. Prosperity requires brains: America produces mainly Robbers and Robbed. Raising prices and living costs will only starve the masses more. Equalizing incomes by national confiscation and operation of machinery and mills, evicting capitalists, is the real remedy. 

Religion also works for the suckers, keeping the starvers busy praising god from whom no blessings flow. Freeing American minds from religion is the first step toward a higher civilization. Churches are clubs for the pleasure of their members and corruption of their brains. They preach 'salvation' by ignorance and stupidity instead of by intelligence. 

What wonder the world is a swine-pen, and robbery seasoned with murder the chief end of man? All religions are mental poisons, killing intelligence. 

Unless our two Hebrew religions, Judaism and Jew Christianity, are discarded they will destroy civilization. Russia and Germany have wisely begun that work. It will spread. 

February, 1934.

Jerusalemizing America 

A strange thing is happening in America. Capitalist Jews are taking over most business lines with astounding speed. How do they do it? By brilliant ability? By no means; but by a curious business fluke. 

In fact, Americans are doing it for them. Americans buy most things of Jews, so starving out American business men, while building a swarm of Jewish multi-millionaires. The Jews are a relatively small, new group here, yet Americans are wiping themselves out by giving the country to these rather recent Jewish immigrants. 'Give him not only thy coat, but thy country also'.

There is a simple way to correct this pernicious process, wholly fair to both sides. It is for Americans to buy only of American-owned stores and industries, and employ only American services of all kinds, and for Jews likewise to patronize Jews exclusively for everything. This is not a boycott, it is equality. Each group will then support itself and its own people, whereas now Americans thoughtlessly boycott Americans, and both Americans and Jews support and enrich Jews through buying of Jews. 

But if present practices continue there will soon be few American stores left to trade with, the Jewish monopoly will be practically absolute, and Americans will be menials of the Jews, since owners of wealth are masters of the rest. American blindness is astounding, for this policy is American suicide, and fatal for mankind. America has the special function of pioneering world-liberation from slavery to antiquity, from which it must not be swerved by any of the ancient and absolute sacrednesses. But Jewish monopoly of wealth will speed up Judaizing. 

From historic causes America has the new modern mind, whereas the large Jewish majority are soaked with Asiatic antiquity and think themselves divinely ordained to drag the world back to that bigoted and benighted ancient level. Few enlightened Jews cut loose from and oppose the fossilized Jew mass. Reviving Hebrew antiquity in America would be a world-crime, canceling this nation's higher destiny. Most of the world's past is a curse to the present, and instead of trying to nurse it back to life as so many Jews do, it should be repudiated, blotted out and forgotten. The foremost duty of America is to defend and promote modernness against all decayed ancientness. When Americans see the gravity of this conflict between the old and new, they will firmly resist and prevent the Jerusalemizing of America in any degree, for that would bring atrophy and misery on all mankind. 

January, 1934.

American Property Hogs 

Nearly 300 Americans now have an income of a million or more dollars a year. We shall soon have a swarm of billionaires, then of multibillionaires. A swelling army of frivolous parasites already exists and insolently flaunts itself, gay idlers who never worked or earned anything, but simply loaf, sap, devour, squander and waste, robbing genius and intelligence of means to develop. Is not this a masterly preparation for the wildest revolution that ever rocked the world? Surely it is.

It is the American Property Hog System. America was founded on the principle of equal opportunity by men who fled from Europe to escape the curse of land hogs and kings who rated the masses as menials and slaves. These founders launched a nation of justice and equal opportunity. Look at America now! 

We have property hogs incomparably richer, grander, more numerous, arrogant, extravagant, wasteful and callous than the wealth hogs of Europe two centuries ago. Riches rule everything; the courts will not convict rich scoundrels; equal opportunity is as 

Idead as the dinosaur. These bloated acquisitors are legalized bandits, plundering the population, burning in criminal luxury wealth that would regenerate the nation.

The cause of this national collapse is our false creed of wealth. We allow riches to breed riches, then allow some individuals to "hog" the resulting wealth. That wealth is not theirs: the wealth-bred increase belongs to society, for use to promote health, education, intelligence, science, happiness and evolution. If a private person keeps it he steals it, robbing the people and society of all these precious values. Payne Whitney died recently leaving $182,028,279. Since then that fortune increased of itself, by "act of god," to $217,700,000. Did Whitney earn that $35,700,000 increase while he was dead?

No, it grew into his estate by the automatic action of the property hog system. By legitimate creating of value Whitney probably never earned a dollar, yet died "worth" nearly two hundred million. What he did not earn was not his. So of all the rich, Our hog system vastly rewards the non-producers who amass wealth by juggling it from others. It has made this juggling a science, and that is the essence of Big Business and Finance today. But these amassers are morally merely thieves, and a nation that tolerates them is on the toboggan slide to damnation. 1929.

Big Business Is Wholesale Stealing 

Big Business and Finance are Robbery on a mammoth scale. The Robbers, who are 13 per cent of the American people, own practically the whole of the nation's 400 billion dollars' worth of wealth. 

By what shrewd fraud did they steal this monster mass of riches from its creators, and get away with it? The trick was simple. American trade principles were framed when most business was small. 

Then a certain rate of profit for the owner on capital involved was fairly just. None could get rich enough to enslave others and shamefully rob society. 

The profits might still be considered personal earnings. But when the business greatly enlarged and the owner still exacted the same profit rate on the increased capital, the principle changed radically. 

By no stretch of theory could it then be said that he earned, deserved or had right to the same rate of profits. He commonly worked no harder and had no more brains to use than before. Yet he extorted a higher salary and far larger aggregate personal income, which now is enormous. Society thought it had to pay him this on the theory that he owned the 'works,' and so had the right to raise his own pay and profits in proportion to the wealth contained in those works or more. This principle was stretched to cover all financial uses of wealth. 

This spurious principle sanctioned the most colossal robbery of modern times. It has enabled 13 per cent of the people to pauperize 87 per cent in the fraudulent screen of law. The rich 13 per cent have largely degenerated into greedy squanderers of their stolen loot on vicious luxuries, while every decent cause starves. The cowering 87 per cent pauper class has lost all manhood and obsequiously crawls to the Royal Rich. From small retail traffic, Business and Finance are now risen to Wholesale Mass Banditry by octopian combines, and our most godlike and groveled to citizens are those who can steal most. They despise the 105 million Americans who cravenly create the wealth they steal, as weak. 

The Truckling Plutocrat Press calls this American Prosperity. It is American Brutal Savagery.

It shows our social fabric rotten to the core. The bloated wholesale thief is seen as white as snow and the humble courts kiss his holy toes. What must be done? Will the looters voluntarily disgorge and apply the wealth to producing a higher American race? Or must they be compelled to do so? 

The American Property Hogs have their choice. 1929.

It is now Riches against the People. The sword of the rich is the Confiscated Press. The people are a huge defenseless herd, milling impotently like a mass of cattle. For when you steal a nation's press, you steal its brains. It has no organ of expression. Plutocrat purchase of the Public Schools. By own ing these Plutocracy can mold the minds of children so that no seditious ideas of American rights can enter their heads. They will then cravenly create riches for the Czars of Wealth to squander on shameful luxury, profligate grandeur, dwarfed brain and character rot.

Americans are supremely gullible. The basis of this nation was equal opportunity. Then came a rich class smashing equal opportunity. These rich got their riches by confiscating the people's wealth. 

The Supreme Court forthwith legislated that only the rich may confiscate. If any rational American said, "Confiscate that stolen people's wealth back", he was branded a scoundrel. And there we are now. 

A man from Mars would call us all morons. We are. 1929.

Hosts of idle rich now loaf in swinish luxury on people-paid dividends. The huge salaries swiped by the rich for the work they do are merely bandits' loot. Inviting a French Revolution A Letter to the American Rich From Morrison I. Swift 

I question if you know the rising temper of the people, for if you did you would act differently. But you do know that many million citizens are in fiercest want. Most of you live as usual, with your habitual pleasures, luxuries, travels, superfluities and sports, all the best and costliest. Were the wealth you so waste contributed to the destitute, the far-flung cruel sufferings of your brother and sister Americans would cease. With this wealth as many workers would be employed producing necessaries for the wretched as now are engaged in creating luxuries and extravagances for you. Doubtless you have not been told of this fact.

But the American masses, white-collared and nocollared, are informed of it. Never did they know it so well. In previous periods of unemployment you could safely allow them to starve. It is different now. 

Then they were supposed to starve according to the will of God in retribution for their laziness and sins. They deserved the discipline of starvation. Torture (of the poor) improves their morals. Now they know that you, the rich, have absorbed so much national wealth that vast numbers of your countrymen can barely live from day to day, and must turn paupers when the lightning of your business incompetence and depressions strikes. There was less cause for the titanic

French Revolution than there now is for a repetition of it. We now know what justice and human rights are: then the rich were submerged in their castepride, sodden ignorance and imbecile orgies. We assumed that Americans had outgrown that vile stage. 

Yet we have the same staggering mass-destitution and dazzling Plutocratic dissipations and delights. 

Can you of the swollenly rich class read the burning warning on the sky? A Second American Revolution has begun. Whether a peaceful revolution of intelligence or an up-heaving explosion like those of France and Russia depends on you, the absorbers of American wealth. When passion and starvation swept 

France, human heads fell in showers. Do you want that? Are you entitled to shine in splendor while the multitudes rot in squalor? Is it not wiser to give billions of your accreted riches to cure the curse of destitution, and then remove the cause of it by a just redistribution of wealth? Is not this redistribution better than having everything torn from you by a raging human volcano? For the moment you can choose. Later, The Starved will.

The Cardinal, Boston Herald, and Rich 

Our good friend Cardinal O'Connell has come to the rescue of The Rich, and the Boston Herald says Amen. A Western priest has been "uttering demagogic stuff to the poor", and the Cardinal called him down with a thud. "The (Catholic) church does not take sides, rich or poor," he said. "When we are so near the crisis that anything may spill it, don't be whisked off your feet by.... spectacular talk, mostly froth but some with poison in it." "We do not like to hear almost hysterical addresses from ecclesiastics." "While we believe in free speech, you do not have to be told that the priest has his place and he bad better stay there." "If the speaker (priest) begins to talk nonsense or pure emotionalism or simply spectacular talk that is the end of him." The cardinal proved that American free speech by a priest is anathema. Priests must be puppets, not men. 

The Boston Herald came to the cardinal's support with glowing enthusiasm and dealt the Protestant clergy a smashing blow. It said: “Reading the cardinal's remarks, one is inclined to conclude that most of them may well be applied to the clergymen of every faith. . . . Unfortunately, many clergymen have taken it on themselves to advise the people indiscriminately in highly controversial, non-religious discussions." Meaning, the Protestant like the Catholic clergy should "shut up" on questions of earthly human welfare and preach only the sky, for religion is purely sky-stuff, quite safe to talk about. 

I agree with this view of religion wholly, and am glad to see all the clergies put where they belong by the cardinal and Herald. They know nothing about the after-death, and they can chatter about it to their heart's content without menace to earthly monopolizers of wealth while millions of workers who created that wealth slowly starve. 

I have often said that Religion is a scheme of the Rich for robbing the poor with the help of god, and for keeping the poor quiet while they rob them. 

I now repeat it, and add, with the help of clergies, cardinal, and rich men's newspapers. Post-mortem sky religion is mere clerical twitter, but without it the clergies would have to do useful work. Without it the ninety million poor and half-poor would eliminate the rich class and establish a just income for all who work and not a cent for rich loafers or poor ones. Rich people's monopoly and waste of wealth fundamentally caused the present crisis. Millions starve; Washington gibbers; Revolution creeps on.

Conscript wealth. Equalize wealth or Hell will break, and wreck the nation. The enemies of peace are those who oppose equality.

The Right To Steal 

Resolutions adopted by the Boston Unemployed on Boston Common, March 14th, 1914.

We, the Unemployed of Greater Boston, having learned that the rich are completely hard hearted toward us in our suffering from want of work, that they prefer to reduce us to the degradation of pauperism or let us starve rather than to open their full pocket-books to give us work;

Having learned from facts that they think more of their dogs and dog shows than they do of us, and that they go on flaunting their criminal wealth-wasting social functions in the face of our desperation while many of us are driven to eat garbage from the restaurant pails to keep life in our bodies, and to sleep on the 

Common or in alleys;

Having learned that the rich rob us of the wealth which we create, and play commercial poker among themselves to see who shall have the most of it, and call that earning it; 

Therefore, Resolved: That here on historic Boston

Common we enact a new morality. 

We declare that every one has an absolute and inalienable right to work; that if he is deprived of this he is robbed of the means of life and therefore of life itself; that this is not only robbery but the committal of constructive murder against him by society; that in thus casting him out from all social care and protection society excludes him from the operation of its laws and absolves him from all duty of obedience to them; that he is thenceforth authorized and obliged to preserve life by his own efforts; that he must therefore take food, clothing and shelter where he can, regardless of social edicts against his doing so. 

We consequently advise the unemployed everywhere to steal food and whatever else they need to maintain their health and welfare, and we affirm that it is stealing only in name and not in fact. Society forces it upon them and society is the primary thief and criminal by doing so, and by robbing them of all fundamental rights of man. A society which does this must either admit the inalienable right to steal, or must expect revolution and dissolution in chaos. The primal rights of man are supreme, above the artificial laws of a greed caste and the servile lawmakers who are commanded by them. 

That a statement of this new morality and advice to follow it be sent out to the unemployed of the United States.

These Resolutions were passed at a large meeting of the unemployed with but one dissenting vote. 

MORRISON I. SWIFT, Roxbury P. O. Sta., Boston, 1914.

The Pope's Blow At American Sovereignty 

The Pope's assault on the American school system is a revelation to Americans who imagined that the Catholic church does not invade state affairs. In his new encyclical Pope Pius attacks American sovereignty by appointing himself supreme authority everywhere in the most vital sphere of a nation's government and life-its Education. He is now Kingplus-Pope. Speaking as king, he violated national rights by breaking into our domestic affairs, an affront calling for reprimand from Washington. Speaking as pope, he arrogated political dictatorship. His words are: "We...confirm...the prescriptions of the sacred canons by which attendance at non-Catholic, neutral or mixed schools or of schools, that is to say, indifferently open to Catholics and non-Catholics without distinction, is forbidden to Catholic children..." "For a school to be acceptable it is necessary that the whole teaching and organization of the school, namely the teachers, the curriculum and the books, be governed by the Christian spirit under the maternal direction and vigilance of the Church".

This program aims to set Catholics against American institutions, thus destroying the highest essential of the nation's welfare, its unity of spirit and aim. 

It is a political act, sowing enmity and tending to wreck our political fabric. Calling this subversive policy 'spiritual' does not make it less carnal and earthly. The Pope's claim that he does not meddle in our political field is proved untrue by this encyclical; for by dictating the education of Catholic children he molds their minds, future thinking and acts on all questions, including those political. If the Pope can control the education of a majority of American children, when they are grown he can abolish our priceless inheritance of democracy, mental independence and a free press, debasing America to a petty province of the Vatican priests. Americans will then be mental puppets, wound up, herded and strangulated by Rome.

The Pope's parochial schools aim at this. They are a staggering misfortune to Catholics, for where students' minds are divided by priests between religion and education, religion overtops education making it inferior, and the students are handicapped for life. To remedy these evils all American youths should be required to attend public schools till their 16th year; and private and religious schools for children below that age should be prohibited. Private schools produce class clannishness and snobbery, while church schools slice a nation into enemy blocs and enclaves, a shameful reversion to barbarism. 1930 29

The Alien Pope and Birth Control 

There was a time when the Roman Catholic Church would not allow the earth to go round the sun. At the Massachusetts State House yesterday the Roman Church again showed itself the enemy of science and scientists in its solid opposition to a bill enabling reputable physicians to prescribe contraceptive measures to save women from pregnancy, where their life or health would be imperiled by having children. Representing 1290 doctors of good standing, Dr. George G. Smith said: "The problem of the physician is the protection of the patient from the injurious and perhaps fatal strain of pregnancy. Therefore, it is the life and health of the mother which are primarily being controled, not the birth of the child."

Restraining Japan- 

Boycott Urged 

A Letter in the Springfield Republican, Nov. 10, 1931

While other nations are surrendering their claims to control in China, Japan is insisting on her right to special privileges in the southern Manchurian section of that nation until the year 2014. Long before that time Manchuria could be thoroughly

Japanized. This is an extraterritorial demand conceded by China in 1915 when she was incapacitated to resist. Japan turned the screws and extorted the concession. It was an act of evident injustice, which, if not rectified now, will almost certainly lead to another world war, either very soon, or eventually when China is internally at peace. The right of other nations to intervene which Japan denies-is established by the certainty that such a war would wipe out half of the human race, Nations that are so blind and headstrong as to want war must be curbed for human salvation. 

Unhappily, Japan is making another mistake which brings her before the bar of mankind. Her population is extremely excessive and continues to grow with no effort for restraint from any source. 

Overpopulation may be considered an act of strategy if a nation desires an excuse for overflowing into and confiscating lands belonging to others. The argument seemed plausible formerly, when excessive fecundity was regarded as an act of God. But that is no longer the case. Where a country is already dangerously overpopulated it it an act of malice aforethought, and if it endangers war it is a consummate crime. The human race must everywhere control its reproductiveness or perish by war. No axiom is truer. National responsibility for the size of population has just been acknowledged by the governor of Rome, who offers prizes for families that have the most children, though Italy can scarcely bear the strain of the surplus people already there. More are needed to enhance Italian grandeur by dying.

The time will come when a nation that does not curb its superfecundity will be treated as a public enemy by the family of nations. A boycott rigorously applied by all other nations is probably the only method that will convince Japan of her obligations to the rest of the world. Our nation should not only cooperate in this action but should firmly advocate it if there is hesitancy on the part of others. In the last resort the boycott is the only potent preventive of another terrible world-catastrophe. A nation that causes war should be outlawed and implacably impounded. 32
