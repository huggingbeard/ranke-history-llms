RESEARCH ON THE MIDDLE EAST External Research List No. 6.21 

This is one of a series of research lists based on the catalog of social science research on foreign areas compiled by the External Research Staff from information furnished by private scholars throughout the United States. 

This compilation includes research projects reported as completed through October 1963; since the lists are not cumulative, this compilation supplements 

External Research List No. 6.19 of Fall 1962. Research in progress is reported in separate lists published annually in the spring. 

Copies of these research lists are sent to the libraries of universities that offer graduate degrees in social sciences, directors of language and area study programs, and faculty members who conduct relevant teaching or research. 

Because of cost, these lists are ordinarily not distributed more widely. 

The list reports on the status of research and is not a bibliography. is published to indicate completion, and therefore possible availability, of titles listed in the Staff's earlier compilations of research in progress. 11 It

Data for each entry are printed substantially as reported to the Staff and may be incomplete. Entries designated as "Research" or Project" by the authors may indicate continuing research interests or preliminary studies not in final form. Items preceded by an asterisk are in the External Research Staff Library and can be obtained on loan by GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS. Requests may be telephoned to Code 182, Ext. 2137. Requestors are reminded that authors' rights to their texts should be strictly honored. Non-governmental requestors and persons desiring items not preceded by the asterisk, should address their inquiries directly to the author, whose university affiliation or other address is given in parenthesis.

Annotations following individual items have been submitted by the author or prepared by the External Research Staff. The Department of State assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of the statements of fact, nor does it necessarily endorse the conclusions expressed.

The Staff welcomes letters from scholars and research organizations reporting new projects not already entered in the External Research Catalog. 

The Catalog is always available at the address below for use by students and private scholars as well as by government officials. 

Further information may be obtained from the External Research Staff, Room 8732, Bureau of Intelligence and Research, Department of State, Washington, D.C. 20520.
