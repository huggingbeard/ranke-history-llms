After the beautiful summer and fall weather, frost had come, and the great tumbling weeds had started their fantastic journey across the desert. A clear, bright blue lay against the horizon in the early morning, and the Meadow Larks were bursting their throats with their sweet whistle as though they knew the time was short for their singing; so their first winter began clear, sparkling cool days. Five seasons had now passed and David began to wonder whether the experiment was going to result in success or failure. He was still pursuing the elusive mirage that had beckoned him on ever since the vision of this new country had been portrayed to him back in Chicago.

When he looked over the ranch and saw the great improvements that had been made, the buildings that had been erected, the growth of the apple and shade trees, the miles of fencing, the fertile fields where once had been sagebrush, he felt as though the goal was very close.
