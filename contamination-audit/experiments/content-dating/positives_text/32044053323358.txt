RESEARCH ON THE MIDDLE EAST External Research List No. 6.19 

This is one of a series of research lists based on the catalog of social science research on foreign areas compiled by the External Research Staff from information furnished by private scholars throughout the United States. 

This compilation includes research projects reported as completed through October 1962; since the lists are not cumulative, this compilation supplements 

External Research List No. 6.17 of Fall 1961. Research in progress is reported in separate lists published annually in the spring. 

Copies of these research lists are sent to the libraries of universities that offer graduate degrees in social sciences, directors of language and area study programs, and faculty members who conduct relevant teaching or research. Because of cost, these lists are ordinarily not distributed more widely.

The list reports on the status of research and is not a bibliography. 

It is published to indicate completion, and therefore possible availability, of titles listed in the Staff's earlier compilations of research in progress.

Data for each entry are printed substantially as reported to the Staff and may be incomplete. Further information about particular entries should be sought from the author, whose university affiliation or other address is given in parenthesis. Entries designated as "Research" or "Project" by the authors may indicate continuing research interests or preliminary studies not in final form. 

Annotations following individual items have been supplied by the authors; a few minor editorial changes have been made in them. The Department of 

State assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of the statements of fact nor does it necessarily endorse the conclusions expressed. 

The Staff welcomes letters from scholars and research organizations reporting new projects not already entered in the External Research Catalog. 

The Catalog is always available at the address below for use by students and private scholars as well as by government officials. 

Further information may be obtained from the External Research Staff, Room 8733, Bureau of Intelligence and Research, Department of State, Washington 25, D.C.
