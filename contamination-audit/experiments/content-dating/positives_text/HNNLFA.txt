Stray Thoughts YOU CANNOT AFFORD TO GIVE UP 

THE TASK To-day it seems as if everything is working against me; my typewriter tries to misspell words and writes the wrong words in the wrong places. My thousands of thoughts which I have to write about have vanished from me and are nowhere to be found. My many known facts have left and gone out into the great unknown. My mind, what little I have, seems to be at its dullest point, and my weaker self says to me "You had better quit.

You had better lay your work aside and forget it for awhile. You are not feeling well enough to write. You are not in the right mood to write. Your brain is not capable of co-operating with you to-day. Lay your work aside until some other more convenient time. 

Lay your work aside until all your wandering thoughts have passed from your mind and

STRAY THOUGHTS 

it is again capable of producing practical thoughts for you."

Frankly speaking, this is one time when I would obey my weaker self and go off and forget all my work and forget everything, if it were not for the fact that I know that if I allow my weaker self to dictate to me and allow it to control me, I will never be able to accomplish much. I know that if I allow my weaker self to have its way to-day, to-morrow it will be back with me more prominent and more insistent and harder to overcome than ever before. I know that if I give over to the "take it easy" temptations of life I will soon become so weak that I will not be able to overcome them. So to-day there is a great argument within me between my weaker self and my better self. My weaker self says "you should stop and take it easy", but my better self says "you cannot afford to stop. You must go on and on and keep on going on".

The man who never gives up is the man who wins. Friends may oppose him. Foes may beset him, but if he has that "go on"

STRAY THOUGHTS 

9 spirit there is nothing but death itself that can hinder him from becoming successful in his undertakings whatever they may be.

The man who starts out to accomplish something and then lets a few discouraging words from the passerby stop him will never climb a very high ladder, but the man whose better self can overcome the weakness within him will always be on the very top. 

The quitter is never a winner. But the sticker is seldom a loser in the battles of life.

Open your eyes and look around you, for there you will find many golden opportunities to do something really worthwhile in life. THE WORLD STEPS ASIDE FOR THE MAN WHO KNOWS WHERE HE IS GOING 

You have seen people who get up in the morning and start the day without a plan of any kind. They just do whatever seems to strike them and if nothing strikes, they do nothing.

STRAY THOUGHTS 

You see people who start out in life with no particular aim. They wander from job to job and from place to place. To-day they are working at one trade and to-morrow they are working at another. They never stick to any one thing long enough to make good at it.

You can talk to them to-day and they are all interested in some particular thing but after a night's sleep they are more interested in some other thing. Their minds seems to wander with them wherever they go. They never accomplish anything worthwhile for themselves or anyone else. They seem to have no power to row up-stream. They are drifters and drift carelessly with the tide.

Suppose the farmer planted cotton in his land to-day and to-morrow he plowed up the cotton and planted corn and the next day he plowed up the corn and planted something else? What would the outcome be if he kept this up? It is the same thing in life.

The world needs men and women who can and will make some worthwhile definite plans in life and then stick to them regardless of

IF THE KID WANTS TO "TINKER” LET 

HIM DO IT If there is a kid in your home who wants to see the inside of his toys let him do it. To prevent him from doing so might be discouraging the world's coming inventor. Many a successful engineering genius to-day can look back to his boyhood days and tell you many stories of the seemingly foolish things which he did.

It is said that at the age of eight Thomas Edison sat on eggs trying to hatch them-at nine he fed the chore-boy Seidlitz powders, believing the gases from them would enable him to fly-assembled a chemical laboratory in the cellar-had little use for school-at twelve, was a newsboy on the Grand Trunk line-at sixteen, became a telegraph operator. 

To-day he has to his credit such inventions as the multiplex telegraph system, the Uni-

STRAY THOUGHTS 

versal stock ticker, paraffin paper, the carbon rheostat, the concrete house, the incandescent electric lamp, the motion picture camera, the alkali storage battery, the motion picture talkie and many others too numerous to mention here.

He had an ambition way back in the years that have passed into his memory that was worthwhile. He set out to accomplish something, and has done as much, if not more, for the good of mankind than any other living man in the world to-day.

But suppose someone had kept him from "tinkering" around when he was a lad? No one knows, of course, but he might have lost interest in what has made him one of the world's greatest inventors.

This "Evolution question" has come to be something that is being considered by a great many people. Some believe wholly in it. 

Some believe in certain forms of evolution and others do not believe in it at all. I do not profess to be a scientific evolutionist and therefore I will not try to discuss the topic in a scientific way. I do believe in certain forms of evolution and I do not doubt some of the scientific teachings we hear about. However, I do not believe that I came from a monkey. 

If you want to think you did that is entirely up to you. You have a right to your opinion,

STRAY THOUGHTS

I know a man who seems to turn everything he touches into money, but he has a son who cannot handle money at all. 

Now if my father leaves to me his singing knowledge and his well-trained voice when he leaves this old world, and my mother leaves her good cooking ability to my sisters when she goes, and the lady I mentioned leaves her musical talent to her daughter and the money making man leaves his valuable knowledge to his son, I will then believe wholly in evolution, but until all these things happen I will believe that God made man a man and woman a woman just as His word says and I will not believe that I came from a monkey tho, I be-

"AN OUNCE OF PREVENTION IS WORTH A POUND OF CURE" 

The originator of this saying did a good job, but he should have used the word "ton" in the place of his "pound", for truly an ounce of prevention is worth a ton of cure. 

You may not believe that, but always remember that your belief, or your unbelief, has nothing whatsoever to do with the truth of a matter. The trouble with many us tho, is, we will not take heed to what we really do believe. 

I like food well seasoned with salt, pepper, Worchestershire sauce, etc., and I have always eaten quite a bit of all, but now, I have recently learned, that Medical Science says these things are not good for my physical body. 

Shall I continue to eat them, as I have been doing, until some part of my body "gives out", or shall I discontinue them now while

STRAY THOUGHTS 

19 all parts of my body are still functioning properly?

I have always been a big eater of White bread, but now, I have learned that Medical 

Science says White bread, White sugar, and candies made from White sugar, are the teeths worst enemies. Shall I continue to eat them until my teeth are replaced with false ones, or shall I stop eating them now and preserve my teeth? Are not a few ounces of natural teeth worth many tons of false ones? Ask the man who owns the false ones.

I have sense enough to know that I cannot "stay out" all night, every night, and then be able to work the next day, as some people try to do, and still keep my health. Then shall I get a reasonable amount of rest and sleep each night, or shall I "stay out" every night until my health is gone, and then expect to "take care of it" and get along all right?

I have recently learned that practically all hair tonics contain a large amount of Alcohol, and are very injurious to the hair, so I have thrown away all of these which were in my

"Billy" Sunday is more active physically to-day, at the age when most men are either in their grave or holding on to their crutch to keep from sliding in, than many a man at twenty-five, because, years ago, he learned what to eat, and what not to eat. You may use any figures you wish, in whatever field you wish, and you will always get the same results, for truly an ounce of prevention is worth a ton of cure, but the thing to do is to apply the prevention whether we feel inclined to do so or not.

FEAR: MAN'S GREATEST ENEMY 

Yes FEAR is the greatest enemy of mankind. We fear to attempt to do this job because someone might criticise us. We fear to attempt that job because someone might ridicule us. We fear to attempt this undertaking because we might fall down on the job. We fear to start out in the face of trying circum-

STRAY THOUGHTS 

stances because we might not be able to finish the task. We live in a fearful mood. We live in a fearful world.

Some people are afraid to live, while others are afraid to die. The Suicide goes to a premature grave because he is afraid to live. He is afraid to face the world. He is afraid to face some great fact. He has committed some crime and is afraid it will become known. He is afraid of the future. He is afraid to meet his creditors. He is afraid he cannot make a success. He is afraid he will be laughed at. 

He is afraid he will be scoffed at. He is afraid to live longer.

Other people are afraid to die. They are afraid to go out into the great unknown. They are afraid to fly out into the great beyond.

They are afraid of the world to come. They are afraid of their record. They are afraid of the black sod under which they will be planted. They are afraid of the life beyond the grave. They fight for life because they are afraid to die.

Many a man would make a success in every-

STRAY THOUGHTS 

23 day undertakings if it were not for the fact that he fears he will not be able to make good, and therefore refuses to try. Many a man would accumulate money if it were not for the fact that he fears to invest what he has.

Many a man would live longer if it were not for the fact that he fears he is going to die, and therefore loses his will to live longer.

It is the fearless man who makes good. It is the fearless man who accomplishes great things in life. It is the fearless man who leaves a great record behind him when he passes out. It is the fearful man who falls down on the job. It is the fearful man who passes out into the great beyond without any great deeds to his credit. We need to fight that thing FEAR, for it is the greatest enemy of mankind.

WHY STAND YE IDLE? 

In the twentieth chapter of St. Matthew, we have the story of the householder who went out in the morning to find laborers to work in his vineyard. We find him going out the

STRAY THOUGHTS 

second time, and again the third time, and this is late in the day.

We see him each time as he comes to a group of people standing or sitting around doing nothing, and we hear him ask the question "Why stand ye idle?" and they answer "Because no man hath hired us". 

You know many people standing or sitting around this very day doing nothing. Some claim that they cannot find work to do. You have heard the story over and over again and again "I can't find a job".

It is true that we cannot always find just the kind of job or position that we want, but often the trouble is, we want an easy job with lots of pay. Too many of us want to work in a pie factory. We want a job in a place where we start at noon and get off about 1 P. M. with an hour for lunch.

Then there are some of us who do not want to work at all.

Just the other night, a crowd of unemployed were standing on the corner of one of the States Capital's Square "solving the un-

STRAY THOUGHTS 

25 employment problem". The speaker went on to tell how modern machinery had cut into the employment situation and thrown millions of people out of work. He said, "Now just suppose we set this equipment aside. For instance, the steam shovel; suppose we take it, which can dig a cellar for you in a half of a day, and sit it aside, and put a crew of men to work with picks and shovels?" At this point a man, whom had been standing back and listening very carefully, and who looked as if it had been many months since he had worked, spoke up and said, "But who in the H-- wants to work with a pick and shovel?"

He was bringing them back to the fact that they, or we, do not want to really work. We want an easy job.

Every person, regardless of his trade, should endeavor to get all the legitimate pay he can for his work, but there is no reason to be a bum just because we cannot get the particular job and pay we want.

I MUST STUDY IF I WOULD LEARN 

I was reared on a farm where there were so many of us kids that we neither had need nor room for a victrola, and I had never tried to operate one until one day soon after I had left the farm, in my teens. I was out with a "girl friend", and we were visiting one of her friends who had some other callers also.

I, being the dumbbell that I was, walked over and sat down by the side of the victrola, thinking it was probably a new model kitchen cabinet or something like that. About the time I got seated, the fellow who had been operating it, rose and walked out of the room and left me sitting next to it. As soon as he was out of the room, it stopped, and everyone looked straight at me. I knew that

I was supposed to do something, but was not sure of what or how. After raising a tremendously hot temperature, I finally got

STRAY THOUGHTS 

27 up and started turning the record over. First 

I took a good look to see where the needle came from so I would know where it belonged. 

I then turned the record over and put the needle back where I got it and started it to running again. I can truly say that I never felt more relieved in all my life than when it went to work. It was then that I reached over and started winding it up and at the same time, my temperature started back to its natural degree. Before I got cooled off tho, and before any music had been thrown out, it stopped. I started it again and it ran a few rounds and stopped again. I kept starting it and it kept stopping until finally one of the other fellows came over and told me that I "would have to put the needle on the outer edge of the record". Through this embarrassment, I learned to operate a victrola. I knew nothing about victrolas then, and know almost 'as little now.

When I was a kid, I had a small clock that would not run, so I decided that I would "fix" it. I began taking it apart, and when

STRAY THOUGHTS

I still know the same about repairing clocks.

There is no doubt in my mind tho, but what I could learn as much as any other man about victrolas, or about repairing clocks. But I would have to spend many long, and maybe weary, hours studying the mechanical functions of these, if I would master them. 

The same is true with you. If you would master some trade or profession you must spend much time studying that trade or profession. There is no getting around the fact.

WHAT ABOUT A SHORT-CIRCUIT? 

You have heard of automobiles being burned from fires which were started by "Short-Circuits". You have known of houses burning from the same cause. You have

STRAY THOUGHTS 

often heard of the "Short-Circuit", but did you ever fully understand what is meant by a "Short-Circuit?"

All wires carrying current for power or lights are insulated with a rubber covering, or wrapping of some kind. This wrapping is called "insulation". It covers the wire, to prevent the current from leaving its "track", should the wire come in contact with another wire, or piece of metal which might be connected with its source. 

Electricity follows the paths of least resist ance, and if this insulation wears off and leaves the wire bare in some place where it can come in contact with another wire or piece of metal connected with its source, it "cuts-across", and returns to its source instead of doing its work, and usually at such a high rate that it heats the contacting points and causes a fire should there be anything near by to burn. It "cuts-across" and returns because it is taking the path of least resistance. It is easier to go this way than to follow its regular circuit through some

STRAY THOUGHTS 

159 motor or other electrical device. It makes no difference to electricity if it does not do its work.

We often see "foot paths" through vacant lots, fields, etc., so that man may "cut-off" a part of his distance: so that man may follow the paths of least resistance in walking. 

Many people do not stop at making "cuts" across vacant lots, fields, etc., but they try to make "cuts" in every other way they possibly can, even when it might be to their advantage to follow the longest route. Many people follow the paths of least resistance in their work, in their business, in their everyday living.

Electricity that goes through a Short-Circuit never does any good, and usually does a great amount of harm. People who always follow the paths of least resistance, never do much good, but many times do a lot of harm. 

What good is electricity if it will not produce good results? What good is a man who will not do what his Creator expects him to do?

ORIGIN OF "BEAUTIFUL SNOW” 

During the early part of the war, one dark 

Saturday night in mid-winter, there died in a hospital in Cincinnati a young woman, over whose head only two and twenty summers had passed. She had once been possessed of an enviable share of beauty, and had been, as she herself said, "flattered and sought for the charms of her face", but alas, upon her fair brow was written that terrible word- "prostitute". Once the pride of respectable parents, her first false step was the small beginning of the "same old story over

STRAY THOUGHTS 

183 again", which has been the only life history of thousands. Highly educated and of accomplished manners, she might have shone in the best of society, but the evil hour that proved her ruin was at the door from childhood, and having spent a young life in disgrace and shame, the poor, friendless one died the melancholy death of a broken hearted outcast. 

Among her personal effects was found in manuscript, "The Beautiful Snow", which was immediately carried to Enos B. Reed, a gentleman of culture and literary tastes, who was at that time editor of the National Union. 

In the columns of that paper, on the the morning of the day following the girl's death, the poem appeared in print for the first time. 

When the paper containing the poem came out on Sunday morning the body of the victim had not yet received burial. The attention of Thomas Buchanan Reed, one of the first American poets, was so taken with their stirring pathos, that he immediately followed the corpse to its final resting place. MEASURING A MAN 

Not, "How did he die?" 

But, "How did he live?" 

Not, "What did he gain?" 

But, "What did he give?" 

These are the units 

To measure the worth 

Of a man as a man, Regardless of birth. 

Not, "What was his station?" 

But, "Had he a heart?" And- "How did he play 

His God-given part? 

Was he ever ready 

With a word of good cheer, To bring back a smile, To banish a tear?"

STRAY THOUGHTS 

191 

Not, "What was his church?” 

Nor, "What was his creed?" 

But, "Had he befriended 

Those really in need?" 

Not, "What did the sketch 

In the newspaper say?" 

But, "How many were sorry 

When he passed away?"

A WORTHWHILE QUESTION 

It is not so much WHERE you live

As HOW and WHY and WHEN you live 

That answers in the affirmative, Or maybe in the negative, The question, Are you fit to live? 

It is not so much WHERE you live 

As HOW you live and whether good 

Flows from you through your neighborhood. 

And WHY you live and whether you 

Aim and noblest ends pursue 

And keep life brimming full and true.

And WHEN you live and whether time 

Is at its nadir or at its prime 

And whether you descend or climb. 

It is not so much WHERE you live 

As whether while you live, you LIVE 

And to the world your highest give,

And thus make answers positive 

That you are truly fit to live.
