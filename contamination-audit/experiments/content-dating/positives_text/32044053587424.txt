THIS IS ES- 

PECIALLY TRUE OF TREATISES WHEREIN AT LEAST THREE OF THE COUNTY LAW LIBRARIES CONTAIN THE LISTED TREATISE IN THEIR COLLECTION BEFORE | HAVE LISTED IT. 

THE APPROXIMATE TOTAL VALUE OF THIS LIST IS AROUND $48,600. PRICES ARE INDICATED WHERE ASCERTAINABLE. 

INCREASE IN PRICES, DISCOUNTS, AND TAXATION MAKE ALL PRICES AN INDICA- TION ONLY AND A CHECK WITH THE PUBLISHER OR LAW BOOK DEALERS IS IMPERATIVE. CURRENT STATE PRINTING DIVISION MATERIALS WILL BE AVAILABLE WITHOUT CHARGE IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE 1961 AMENDMENTS TO THE DISTRIBUTION OF STATE PUBLICATIONS ACT (CHAPTER 818). 

SUCH OLDER ITEMS as RULING CASE LAW, FEDERAL CASES AND PRE-NATIONAL Reporter SYSTEM STATE LAW REPORTS ARE OMITTED SINCE THEIR UTILITY AS AGAINST SHELF SPACE OCCUPIED DOES NOT WARRANT THEIR RETENTION. THESE OLDER SETS ARE AVAILABLE, AS WELL AS OTHER LEGAL MATERIALS INCLUDING PERIODICALS INDEXED IN THE INDEX TO LEGAL PERIODICALS, FROM THE STATE LAW LIBRARY ON INTER-LIBRARY LOAN. ALSO THE STATE LIBRARY UPON REQUEST WILL PROVIDE CONSULTANT SERVICE TO ANY COUNTY LAW LIBRARY.
