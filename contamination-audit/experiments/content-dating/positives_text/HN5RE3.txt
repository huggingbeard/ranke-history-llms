PREFACE 

The author has tabulated the words in the English language (purposely excluding proper names, plurals that are formed by the addition of S or ES, verbs in the present tense, singular number, third person, words that would offend good taste, obsolete words, and unusual technical and scientific words and terms) in groups of words composed of four, five, six and seven letters.

These, again, have been arranged in definite groups whose first letters are identical.

WORD HUNT An Interesting Game for Young and Old 

In this volume there are 106 Word Hunts, which will enable you to test your knowledge of words. Being told that there are just a fixed number of words of the type to be "hunted," you cudgel your brain to make up the full list. 

If you are able to fill the list without reference to the dictionary, or to the SOLUTION which we supply, the "pride of accomplishment" is yours.

Should you be compelled to refer to the dictionary, or to the SOLUTION, we will have accomplished the educational purpose of this game; for we will have stimulated you to increase your vocabulary-your knowledge of words. Words are the things with which we express ideasthoughts, and the greater our knowledge of words the more readily can we express ourselves, and the more pleasing we become in conversation.
