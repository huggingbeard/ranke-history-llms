In 1916, struck with the huge loss to society from labor-capital strife, the author determined to see if he could not evolve some plan that would bring labor and capital together that they might divide a cake instead of fighting over crumbs.

Under this plan, labor had something to win for its cooperation with capital, and nothing to lose and, therefore, should gladly accept such a contract.

Under my plan each shared in proportion to his or her wage to the total payroll which in- Icluded those stockholders who were active in the business.

To cut down labor turnover, my plan paid the bonus for the first quarter at the beginning of the fourth quarter, and, anyone quitting in the interim forfeited such interim bonus to the net income.
