SHOW BOY 

THE leads in this story are a pair of lowly hoofers, a show girl and a chorus girl. But before the story is over three of them are stars in the mammoth show in whose brilliant lights and shadows the story takes its dazzling course. Here is realism, and here is romance. No novel of the theatre has ever given so realistic a presentation of theatre life, the ordeals of rehearsals, the heartbreak of stage fright, the drive of ambition, the exploitation of woman's beauty, the human jealousies, disappointments and defeats. 

But no novel of the theatre has shown so marvelously the romance that is the finest reality of all 

While the realism fascinates you, the romance will enthrall you. Two of the most heartfelt love stories written in years are in this one book.
