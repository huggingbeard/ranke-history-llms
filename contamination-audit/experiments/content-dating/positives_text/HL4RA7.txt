ENFORCEMENT LAW ASSISTANCE ADMINISTRATIO S. DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE Under the provisions of Section 406 of the Crime Control Act of 

1973 (Public Law 93-83), the institutions of higher education named in the following list are participating in the Law Enforce ment Education Program (LEEP). 

LEEP provides education grants and/or loans to public law enforcement and criminal justice personnel and students who are committed to careers in the criminal justice system. Awards may cover the costs of tuition, mandatory fees and books. Grants are available only to currently employed criminal justice personnel and may be up to $400 per semester or $250 per quarter. Loans are available only to full-time students (currently employed or preparing for criminal justice employment) and may not exceed $2,200 per academic year.

Actual awards of grants and/or loans to students are made by participating schools. Persons interested in applying should contact the Student Financial Aid Officer or Criminal Justice Program 

Director at one of the participating institutions. 

For further information, contact your appropriate regional office of the Law Enforcement Assistance Administration. Regional addresses are listed on the following pages. The abbreviations of criminal justice degree programs listed in this publication are also provided. us 0152
