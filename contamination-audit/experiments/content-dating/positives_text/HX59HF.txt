WHEN BEAUTY TRAVELS UNATTENDED, "ADVEN- TURE" MAY STEP UP ALONGSIDE. 

The night air was pleasantly cool, late in the month of June, while the railroad express flyer was devouring the distance, en route, between Chicago and New York City. 

Flickers of light, from the long string of sleepers, made the fast moving train appear like a shooting meteor that hugged the surface of the ground, streaking the darkness and, now and again, becoming distinctly audible, thundering on, by the roar of its powerful engine.

In the morning, once breakfast was over and all the berths made up, the passengers occupying the Pullman cars began to loll about in various ways, finding it difficult to pass away the time. 

However, seated at the forward end of the coach, was a young woman occupied reading over shorthand notes, inscribed in a fancy dictation pad, in her lap.

She was very attractive and her personality unusually distinctive. There was a total absence of cosmetic upon the clear soft skin of her face. The broad forehead, underneath a profusion of brown 11

hair, neatly parted, through which the sunlight, from the window, diffused itself at the side, enhanced the grace of her beautiful head. Wearing a costume well fitted, the color of which matched that of her hair, with a collar of delicate white lace, parted at the throat, with cuffs of similar material touching her hands, she looked exquisitely pretty.

She was about twenty years old, the glorious personification of a healthy, sweet American girl.

Presently, a pair of large, deep blue eyes were looking up at the conductor; her sweet smile, in accepting the receipt of her ticket, revealed two rows of even teeth, behind perfectly chiselled lips, and, right here, the young traveller presented a truly unconscious, in-artificial picture of rare loveliness.

In strong contrast to this refreshing picture, just across the aisle, a silver-haired woman nonchalantly reclined upon the white pillow provided by the porter. She was dressed very nicely, except that she wore an excess of jewelry; however, her "enbonpoint," to a certain degree in conjunction with the rich silver white of her hair, gave her the appearance of a kind and motherly woman. 

For the past half hour this elderly woman had appeared engrossed in the study of her vis-a-vis.

Three flights below, on the main floor, which was divided into several small parlors and one main one, everything was typical of a most exclusively high class house of ill-fame.

A great many persons are familiar with the strange disappearances of young American girls, of whom no one has or will ever hear from.
