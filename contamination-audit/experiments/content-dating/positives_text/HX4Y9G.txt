FOREWORD 

Words are seeds of thought. From them grow not only flowers of speech but all that humanity fashions. How vital then that the right seed be planted and tended. "In the beginning was the Word”—and man is using part of his divine prerogative when he uses words as they are used in this book. 

Values in literature are qualitative, not quantitative. Not how much? but how good? A single couplet which helps man live more kindly and more wisely the life more abundant is better than an epic which merely amuses. Not space on your library shelf but space in your heart! A single drop from the

Fountain of Eternal Spiritual Youth, which is ever within reach of all of us and which is the fabled well at the bottom of which lies Truth, nourishes the Tree of Knowledge. As a drop of rain can prism a rainbow, a couplet reflects the glow of Truth. 

The more concentrated an essence the stronger it is—and living needs strong healing these days. Poems and epigrams are the most condensed forms of literature, philosophy and religion of wisdom. Here all combine to help you in your daily living. These spirit-quickening couplets, easy to memorize as poetry is because of brevity and rhyme, should bring harmony of comfort and joy to hosts of readers. This is a book for your own happy use. Space is given in which you may write your own comments. And through text references all is linked to the 

Bible as all literature and living should be linked to the 

Universal Father.
