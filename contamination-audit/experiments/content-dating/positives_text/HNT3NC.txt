TOPIC I. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE WORKER Questions for Discussion 

1. Is it fair for a worker to limit his output in order to protect himself and his fellows from the danger of being laid off? 

2. Should workers attempt to prevent the introduction of new machines and processes that may reduce the number of workers employed in a given plant or industry? 

3. Is a worker under obligation to suggest to his employer methods by which wastes might be eliminated, efficiency increased, and costs cut? 

4. Is a worker under obligation to inform his employer concerning other workers who are lazy, wasteful, inefficient, or troublemakers? 

5. Should a worker take pride in his work? Is it always possible for him to do so? Is it ever possible for him to do so? 

6. Should a worker be loyal to his employer? Should he be loyal to his fellow-workers? Can he be loyal to both? If a conflict of interest does arise, where should his loyalty lie? 

7. Should a worker join a labor union and attempt to bargain collectively with his employer concerning wages, hours, and working conditions? 

8. Is it fair for workers to strike, i. e., stop working, in order to force an employer to accede to their demands? 

9. Is it fair for workers to take possession of an employer's plant in a sit-down strike in order to force him to accede to their demands? 

10. Should a worker accept employment as a strike-breaker in a plant where other workers are on strike? 

11. Should striking workers attempt to dissuade others from taking work as strike-breakers, either (a) by persuasion, or (b) by intimidation? 

12. Should workers who themselves have no grievances against their employers ever go on strike in sympathy for other workers. who are striking?

13. Is it fair for workers to refuse to buy the products of an employer whose workers are on strike? Is it fair for them to refuse to buy from dealers who handle his products? 

14. Is it fair for a union to demand that an employer hire no worker who does not belong to the union? 

15. Is it fair for a union to limit the size of its membership, by requiring a long period of apprenticeship, high initiation fees, etc.? 

16. Is it fair for one union to go on strike because it discovers that members of some other union are doing a kind of work which it believes should be done by its own members? 

17. Is there any limit to the amount of wages that unions should attempt to force employers to pay? 

18. Should unions undertake to obtain a reduction in the length of the working week to thirty hours? 

19. Are unions under any obligation to submit their disputes with employers to mediators or arbitrators for settlement? 

20. Should unions contribute to the campaign funds of political parties or otherwise attempt to influence elections, legislation, or the administration of law?

TOPIC II. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE BUSINESS MAN 1. TOWARD THE WORKER Questions for Discussion 

1. Is the business man under any obligation to see to it that the work-place is clean, light, well-ventilated, and safe? 

2. Should the business man undertake to put the right man in the right job, to train men for their jobs, or to develop a system which will promote the most efficient? 

3. Should the business man undertake to safeguard the health of his workers? Should he provide facilities for recreation? Should he provide other activities for the use of leisure time? 

4. Should the business man employ children under sixteen years of age in industrial or commercial occupations? 

5. Should the business man employ women to perform tasks that could equally well be performed by men? 

6. Is the business man under any obligation to pay workers anything more than the lowest wage for which they can be obtained? 

Is he under obligation to pay them enough to enable them to live in health and decency? 

7. Should the business man refuse to employ workers who are members of unions? Should he discharge workers who join unions? 

8. Should the business man organize and support a union in his own establishment and require his workers to join it? 

9. Should the business man refuse to meet with the representatives of his organized workers for the purpose of bargaining with them concerning wages, hours, and working conditions? 

10. In case of a strike, should the business man hire strike-breakers? 

Should he ask a court to issue an injunction against the activities of the workers who are on strike? Should he ask the city or state to give police or military protection to the strike-breakers? 

11. Should the business man accede to the workers' demand that he employ no workers who do not belong to the union?

12. In case of a strike, is the business man under any obligation to submit the dispute to mediation or arbitration? 

13. If his competitors employ unorganized workers at wages below the union rate, should the business man assist the union in its efforts to organize their workers? 

14. Should the business man join with his competitors in an organization to deal collectively with the representatives of organized labor? 

15. Is the business man under any obligation to disclose his accounts to organized workers who are demanding higher wages? 

16. Is it fair for the business man to discharge an honest and efficient workman without prior notice? Is the business man under obligation to pay a certain number of weeks of wages in advance upon discharge? 

17. Is the business man under obligation to attempt to stabilize production so that he may provide continuous employment? 

Should he undertake to guarantee the payment of a certain number of weeks of wages annually? 

18. Is the business man under any obligation to workers who reach the age of retirement after serving for some years in his employ? 

19. Should the business man attempt to enable workers to purchase stock in his business? Should he attempt to develop a method of sharing profits with his workers? 

20. Has the business man a right to attempt to prevent the enactment of legislation which is sought by workers in their interest?

TOPIC III. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE BUSINESS MAN 2. TOWARD THE CONSUMER Questions for Discussion 

1. Is the business man under obligation to give honest weight and full measure? Is it fair for him to adulterate his product? Is it fair for him to misrepresent the quality of the product? 

2. Is the business man under obligation to inform the consumer concerning the exact composition of his product? Is it fair for him to identify his product by a brand name? 

3. Is it fair for the business man to make extravagant claims for his product? Is it fair for him, in advertising his product, to appeal to fear, ambition, pride, and envy? 

4. Is it fair for the business man to urge consumers to buy goods on the instalment plan? Is it fair for him to express the price of instalment credit in dollars per month for the purchase instead of expressing it in the per cent of interest he collects on his loan? Since each instalment paid reduces the amount of the loan, is it fair for him to express his interest charges as a per cent of the original purchase price? 

5. Is it fair for the business man to sell at the same price to customers who buy on credit and have goods delivered and to those who pay cash and carry their purchases home? 

6. Is it fair for the business man to sell his product at a higher price to the buyer of a small quantity and a lower price to the buyer of a large quantity? Is it fair for him to sell to two buyers of the same quantity and quality at different prices? 

7. Is the business man under any obligation to fix a price that will enable persons with small incomes to purchase his product? 

8. When demand falls off, is the business man under any obligation to reduce prices so that the volume of production will be maintained? 

9. Is it fair for the business man to ask the state to pass a law that would prevent his competitors from selling products below a certain price, say cost plus ten per cent?

10. Is it fair for the business man to forbid other business men who handle his product to resell it at less than a certain price? 

11. Is it fair for the business man to ask the state to impose a tax on his competitors that would put them out of business? 

12. Is it fair for the business man to ask the government to impose a tariff on imports that will prevent consumers from obtaining products made abroad? 

13. Is it fair for the business man to ask the government to give his industry a subsidy out of funds raised by taxation? 

14. Is it fair for the business man to reach an agreement with his competitors that none of them will sell below a certain price? 

15. Is it fair for the business man to obtain a patent on an invention that will prevent other producers from making the newly invented product? Is a business man under any obligation to produce and sell a product on which he holds a patent? 

16. Is it fair for the business man to maintain secrecy concerning the processes by which he makes his product? 

17. Is it fair for the business man who makes and ships a heavy product to deliver it to all purchasers, wherever located, at the same price? 

18. Is it fair for the business man to obtain possession of the entire supply of a scarce natural resource? Is it fair for the business man wastefully to exploit some scarce natural resource? 

19. Is the business man under any obligation once he has engaged in a business activity, to continue in it? 

20. Is it fair for a business man to make and sell narcotics or other products that people want but that they would be better off without?

TOPIC IV. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE BUSINESS MAN 3. TOWARD COMPETITORS, INVESTORS, AND THE STATE Questions for Discussion 

1. Is it fair for a business man to disparage the goods sold or the service given by his competitor? Is it fair for him to claim that his goods are better than those sold by his competitor?. 

2. If his competitor originates a popular design of some common product, is it fair for the business man to copy it? Is it fair for him to imitate his competitor's brand name or trade mark? 

3. Is it fair for a business man to pay money or make gifts to the purchasing agents of customers to whom he sells? Is it fair for him to hire his competitor's employees to give him information about his competitor's business? Is it fair for him to employ shoppers to discover the prices at which his competitor is selling? 

4. Is it fair for a business man to induce his competitor's customers to break their contracts by offering lower prices on such contracts? Is it fair for him to attract customers away from his competitor by publicly selling at lower prices? 

5. Is it fair for a business man to attract customers away from his competitor by selling certain conspicuous goods at less than cost? 

6. Is it fair for a business man to attract customers away from a competitor in one locality by selling for low prices there while he charges higher prices elsewhere? 

7. Is it fair for a business man to require that a dealer who carries his product shall not carry his competitor's product? 

8. Is it fair for a business man to require that any dealer who carries one of his products shall carry all of his products? 

9. Is it fair for a business man to enter into arrangements with suppliers of materials and machinery which require that they shall sell to him and not to his competitor? 

10. Is a business man under any obligation to join any association set up by the competitors in his line of business? 

11. Are the directors or officers of a corporation which is offering securities for sale under any obligation to make a full disclosure of the financial condition of the corporation to prospective purchasers of such securities? 10

12. Is it proper for the directors or officers of a corporation to concentrate the control of the corporation in their own hands by selling to the public only securities that have no voting power? 

13. Are the directors or officers of a corporation under obligation to make complete, accurate periodic reports to persons who have invested in it? 

14. Is it fair for the officers of a corporation to pay themselves large salaries or bonuses without informing the stockholders of this fact? Is there any limit to the size of the salary and bonus which a man can fairly collect from a corporation of which he is an officer? 

15. Is it proper for the directors or officers of a corporation to have an interest in another business which sells to or buys from the corporation? 

16. Is it proper for the directors or officers of a corporation to borrow money from the corporation? 

17. Is it proper for the directors or officers of a corporation to make a profit by buying and selling the stock of that corporation on the market? 

18. Is it proper for the directors of a corporation to keep its earnings in the business instead of declaring them as dividends? Is it fair for them so to present the corporation's accounts as to conceal from stockholders the fact that such accumulations exist? 

19. Is the business man under any obligation to obey a law which he believes to be unjust? Is he under obligation to obey a law which he believes to be unconstitutional before its constitutionality has been determined by the courts? 

20. Does the business man fulfill his obligation to the state by passively acquiescing in the administration of its laws or is he under obligation actively to participate in procuring their effective administration?

TOPIC V. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE FARMER Questions for Discussion 

1. Is the farmer under any obligation to maintain the fertility of the soil? Is it fair for him to employ methods of cultivation that may lead to erosion? 

2. Is it fair for the farmer to employ as full-time laborers children under sixteen years of age? 

3. Is the farmer under any obligation to provide for the health, safety, and comfort of his employees? If so, what is the character of the provision which he should make? 

4. Is the farmer under any obligation to pay his employees a living wage or is it fair for him to hire them for the lowest wage for which they are willing to work? 

5. Is the farmer under any obligation to provide his employees with steady work or is it fair for him to take them on and lay them off as he needs or does not need them? Should he pay them a certain number of weeks of wages in advance when he dismisses them? 

6. Should the farmer contribute to a system of unemployment insurance which would pay benefits to farm laborers during unemployment and old age? 

7. If the farmer's employees join a labor union, is he under any obligation to negotiate with the representatives of the union? 

8. If the workers in a factory to which he sells his crop should go on strike, should the farmer join with other farmers to help the owner of the factory to break the strike? 

9. Should the farmer work and vote against legislation which would strengthen the organization of industrial workers or raise their wages? 

10. Is the farmer under obligation to give the purchasers of his product full weight and measure? Is he under obligation to guarantee the quality of his product? 

11. Is it fair for the farmer to refuse to harvest a part of his crop when the price is low? Is it fair for the milk producer to go on strike? Is it fair for him to attempt to prevent other producers from delivering their milk to the market? 12

12. Is it fair for the farmer to ask the government to impose a tariff that will make it impossible for consumers to purchase food that is grown abroad? 

13. Is it fair for the farmer to ask the government to impose a heavy tax on the production of oleo margarine? 

14. Is it fair for dairymen to join an association for the purpose of bargaining collectively with milk distributors concerning the price of milk? Is it fair for such an association to agree with the distributors to raise the price of fluid milk in urban markets? 

15. Is it fair for the farmer to ask the government to pay him a subsidy from the public treasury? Is it fair for him to accept a subsidy from funds which the government has obtained by imposing a tax on the consumers of food? 

16. Is it fair for the farmer to sign a contract with the government under the terms of which he agrees to curtail the output of food? 

17. Is any one farmer under obligation to participate in a plan which is designed to curtail the output of food if other farmers are doing so? 

18. Is it fair for the farmer to ask the government to lend him more money on his crop than it is likely to be worth? Is it fair for him to default on the loan and turn the crop over to the government if its price turns out to be less than the amount of the loan? 

19. Is it fair for the farmer to ask the government to buy his crop at a high price and sell it abroad at a low price, meeting its loss by taxation? 

20. Is the farmer under any obligation to join with other farmers in co-operative associations for the marketing of crops or the purchase of supplies?

References Saving Our Soil, PUBLIC AFFAIRS PAMPHLETS, No. 14.

TOPIC VI. 

THE OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROFESSIONAL MAN Questions for Discussion 

1. Is the professional man under any obligation to join an association of persons engaged in the profession of which he is a member? 

2. Is it fair for an association of professional men to restrict the number of persons practicing the profession by setting up high standards of training and competence as a condition of admission? 

3. Is it fair for an association of professional men to expel from membership persons who violate the code of ethics set up by the association? 

4. Should professional men advertise their services or solicit clients? 

5. Should a lawyer undertake the defense of a person whom he knows to be guilty? Should he accept a case which he knows to be unjust? 

6. Is a lawyer under any obligation to defend a person who is financially unable to pay him a fee? 

7. Is it fair for a lawyer to suppress evidence, to secrete witnesses, to deceive the court or the jury, or to avail himself of legal technicalities in trying a case? 

8. Is it fair for a lawyer to accept fees from more than one of the parties to a suit? Is a lawyer under obligation to keep secret the things that his client has told him in confidence? 

9. Is the lawyer under any obligation to expose another lawyer or a judge whom he knows to be dishonest? 

10. Is a doctor under any obligation to respond when he is called to take a case? 

11. Is a doctor under any obligation to care for persons who are financially unable to pay him for his services? Is it fair for a doctor to charge one person a higher fee than he charges another for the same service? 

12. Should a doctor accept money for publicly endorsing any food, medicine, or medical appliance? Should a doctor who discovers a new medicine or invents a medical appliance obtain a patent on it so that he may profit by its sale? 

13. Is it fair for a doctor to criticize the diagnosis made or the service given by another doctor? Is it fair for him to accept a case on which another doctor has been working? When a general practitioner recommends a specialist to his patient, is it fair for him to accept from the specialist a part of the fee which the specialist charges the patient? 14

14. Under what circumstances is the doctor under obligation to maintain secrecy and under what circumstances is he under obligation to make public his knowledge of the disease or defect from which a patient may suffer? 

15. Is it fair for a medical association to expel from its membership a doctor who has entered into an arrangement with a group of consumers under which he supplies medical care on some other basis than a separate fee for each service? 

16. Is it fair for doctors to attempt to prevent the government from engaging in research on the causes of disease, from giving public assistance in cases of maternity and infancy, from opening free public clinics? 

17. Is a doctor under any obligation to acquiesce in a plan for the organization or financing of medical care which may be in the public interest if he honestly believes it to be opposed to his own interest? Is it fair for doctors to attempt to prevent the initiation of compulsory public health insurance? 

18. Is an engineer under any obligation publicly to denounce a design for a structure prepared by another engineer if he believes it to be unsafe? Is it fair for an engineer to include in the specifications he prepares for his client a material sold by a concern in which he has some financial interest? 

19. Is an editorial writer under obligation to express his own opinions or is it proper for him to express the opinions of the owner of the paper when those opinions differ from his own? Is it proper for the editorial and news writers on a newspaper to join a trade union? 

20. Is the teacher under obligation to teach his pupils to be loyal to existing institutions? Is he under obligation to tell his pupils what he believes to be true? Is he under obligation to train his pupils to think for themselves? If these obligations should ever be in conflict, which should be paramount? Is it proper for teachers to join a trade union?

TOPIC VII. 

THE OBLIGATIONS ATTACHED TO INCOME FROM PROPERTY 

Questions for Discussion 1. Is the individual under obligation to inform himself concerning the practices of a corporation in which he owns securities and from which he draws income? 

2. Is it fair for a person to draw income from a corporation which does not deal fairly with its workers? 

3. Is it fair for a person to draw income from a corporation which does not deal fairly with the consumers of its product? 

4. Is it fair for a person to draw income from a corporation which is producing a product which is socially harmful? 

5. Is it fair for a person to draw income from a corporation which does not deal fairly with its competitors? 

6. Is a person who owns securities in a corporation under any obligation to attempt to influence its policies? 

7. Is a person who has deposited his savings in a bank or purchased a life insurance policy under any obligation to discover how his savings are invested by these agencies? Is he under any obligation to attempt to influence their investment policies? 

8. Is a person who has invested money in a finance company or a small loans business under obligation to see to it that these agencies do not cheat or oppress their clients? 

9. Is it possible to justify the receipt of an income from the ownership of dwellings that are insanitary, overcrowded, or unsafe? 

10. Is it legitimate for the owner of a mortgage to foreclose it against tenants where misfortune makes it impossible for them to make their interest payments? 

11. Is it legitimate for the owner of a building to evict tenants where misfortune makes it impossible for them to pay their rent? 

12. Is it fair for the owner of a piece of land beneath which oil is discovered to collect a royalty on each gallon of oil that is removed from the ground? What service does he render the community which justifies this income? 16

13. Is it fair for the owner of a piece of land to withhold it from use while waiting for its price to increase? 

14. Is it fair for a person who has bought land at a low price to sell it at a high price and keep the profit? What service has he rendered the community that justifies this income? 

15. Is it fair for the owners of a franchise which gives them the exclusive right to operate street cars on a city's streets to derive an income, not by operating cars, but by renting the right to use the streets to another company which does operate cars? 

16. Is an individual under any obligation to work for a living, or may he legitimately live on the interest on property that he has earned and saved? 

17. Is it fair for a person to live on the income from property that he has inherited? What service has he rendered the community that justifies this income? 

18. Is it fair for a person to live on the income from property that he has acquired by gift or marriage? What service has he rendered the community that justifies this income? 

19. If an individual makes a lucky investment which yields him a large and unanticipated profit, is he under obligation to give this profit away or is he justified in keeping. it? 

20. If an individual acquires money through inheritance, gift, or marriage, from the chance discovery of natural wealth, or through an increase in the value of land, is he under obligation to give it away, or is he justified in keeping it?

TOPIC VIII. 

THE OBLIGATIONS ATTACHED TO SPENDING, TAX-PAYING, GIVING, AND CITIZENSHIP Questions for Discussion 

1. Is the individual under any obligation to make adequate provision for his own health, comfort, and education and for that of his family? Is the individual under any obligation to live simply? Where is the line to be drawn between those expenditures that constitute adequate provision and those that constitute extravagance? 

2. Is the individual under any obligation to save a portion of his income in order to secure himself and his family against the risks of the future? Is he under obligation to make provision for his dependents in the event of his death? Should he undertake to accumulate a large amount of property for the benefit of his heirs? 

3. Is the individual under any obligation to get his money's worth when he buys goods and services? Should he attempt to inform himself concerning the quality of the goods which he buys? Should he refuse to buy from sellers who misrepresent their goods? 

4. Is the individual under any obligation to inform himself concerning the conditions under which the goods that he buys were made? Is it fair for him to refuse to buy goods that were made by workers who were inadequately paid or by workers who were denied the right to bargain collectively with their employer? 

5. Is the individual under any obligation to refuse to buy goods that were made in a country whose government is oppressing a minority group or in one whose government is waging war against another people? 

6. Is it fair for the consumer to ask the government to pass laws in his interest or to repeal laws that are opposed to his interest? Should consumers ask the government to strengthen the anti-trust laws or to reduce the tariff? 

7. Is it fair for consumers to form co-operative associations for the purpose of engaging in the distribution of goods? Is the individual consumer under any obligation to join such an association? 18

8. Is the taxpayer under any obligation to oppose public expenditures that he believes to be unwise? Is he under any obligation to oppose forms of taxation that he believes to be unjust? 

9. Is it fair for the taxpayer to avoid the obligation to pay a tax by failing to participate in the taxable activity? Is it fair for him to reduce his obligation to pay taxes in every manner permitted by the law? Is it fair for him to report for taxation any amount of property or income that is less than the amount that he owns or has received? Is it fair for him to claim deductions from his taxable income that are larger than those allowed by law? 

10. Is it fair for the taxpayer to ask the government to pass a law that will exempt him from the obligation to pay taxes? Is it fair for the taxpayer to purchase tax-exempt government bonds? Should religious, educational, and charitable institutions ask the government to exempt their property from taxation? 

11. Is it fair to exempt a person with a very small income from the obligation to pay a tax? Is it fair to ask a person with a very large income to pay a much higher rate of taxes than is paid by a person with a moderate income? 

12. Is the individual under any obligation to inform himself concerning the condition of the poor and the needy? Is he under any obligation to attempt to understand their needs and sympathize with their desires? 

13. Is the individual under any obligation to give a portion of his income to charity? If so, how large a portion should he give? 

Should each individual give the same portion or should one give more than another? 

14. Is the giver under any obligation to see to it that the funds that he gives to charity are efficiently spent? Is he under obligation to see to it that the money he gives to charity does not harm its recipients instead of helping them? 

15. Is the individual under any obligation to contribute to the support of religious bodies, educational activities, cultural institutions, and organizations interested in bringing about social reforms? 

16. Is the individual under any obligation to inform himself on public issues? 19

17. Is the individual under any obligation to vote? Is he under obligation to inform himself concerning the character and qualifications of candidates for public office? 

18. Is the individual under any obligation to work for the defeat of candidates for public office who are known to be corrupt?

Should he vote for a dishonest candidate if he believes that this candidate, if elected, will carry out policies that are in the public interest? 

19. Is the individual under any obligation to join a political party? 

Is he under any obligation to run for public office? Is he under any obligation to devote a part of his time to the performance of public duties without pay? 1 20. Is the individual under any obligation to participate in the work of organizations which are working to preserve peace, to maintain civil liberties, to improve race relations, or to bring about other social or economic reforms?
