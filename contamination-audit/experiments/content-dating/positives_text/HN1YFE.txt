PUBLISHERS' NOTE 

The carefully selected and edited texts, the musical arrangement of the hymns in such a manner as to bring them within the range of the average child voice, and the high order of the harmonization, combine to make this collection a valuable as well as notable addition to existing hymnbooks. 

The inclusion of complete musical services for the Sabbath and all the Holydays and Festivals of the Jewish year, drawn from classical Jewish composers, forms a unique and invaluable feature. 

The compositions are printed in the order in which they are to be used, and are marked by simplicity and beauty. It is one of the most complete collections of the kind published and will prove an indispensable aid to Religious Schools and 

Junior Congregations.

The Children's Services, by the late Rabbi Rudolph Grossman of New York, are an added feature to this edition.

FOREWORD 

This little book of Services may be used either at the beginning or at the conclusion of the Religious School session, or for children's services held on the Sabbath day or in the Home. It contains a special service for each 

Sunday or Saturday of the month, and one for the Holidays and for patriotic occasions. The Holiday Service will be found appropriate for Succoth, Shevuoth, Chanukah and Purim. Each service is based upon a particular idea, and the Responsive Reading has been selected with the view of emphasizing that idea. These Responsive Readings may be used as texts for a Sermonette, and may be memorized by the children, as they contain many of the gems of the Bible. The music for the

CHILDREN'S SERVICES FIRST SERVICE  

MINISTER: 

O Lord our God! We thank Thee for Thy manifold blessings. Thy love watches over us by day and night.

We thank Thee for our parents, for the peace and joy of home, for the opportunities of education and for all the mercies Thou dost daily bestow upon us. Make us worthy to be called Thy children. May we prove our gratitude to Thee by our obedience to Thy commandments and by our devotion to Thee and to our fellow-men. In humble acknowledgment of Thy greatness and Thy love we extol and praise Thee. 

SCHOOL: 

May the day soon come, O Lord our God, when all men shall recognize Thee as the universal Lord and Master, when all unbelief and superstition shall disappear, when falsehood and error shall be no more, and all mankind shall proclaim, even as do we: 

Children sing: Shema, Mi Komocho and Yimloch. 

MINISTER: 

Our Heavenly Father! May Thy blessing of peace ever rest upon us. Teach us to obey the voice of conscience, and to follow the guidance of parents and elders. 

May we be ever kind and gentle to one another, and always remember Thy commandment, 'Love thy neighbor

Help us to do our duty and to avoid temptation and sin. May we be always true to our sacred religion. Bless our parents, our teachers, and all who labor for the good of mankind. Amen.

SECOND SERVICE 

MINISTER: 

Through Thy great mercy, O our God, we are permitted to assemble here in health and in strength. We thank Thee that Thou hast guarded us during the past week, and hast shielded our homes and our beloved from sickness and sorrow. We thank Thee that Thou hast enabled us to study Thy will, as it is revealed in the Bible, Israel's sacred possession, and in the history of our fathers. Help us, O God, to carry into practice the truths that here are taught us. May we ever so live, that our daily conduct shall be the best proof of our reverence for 

Thee, and of our obedience to Thy commandments.

THIRD SERVICE 

MINISTER: 

Gathered in this solemn hour, we praise and thank Thee, O our Father, for the many blessings that we daily enjoy. All that we are, and all that we have, are the gifts of Thy loving Hand. Thou hast filled the world with beauty and glory, and established it on eternal laws that work for our good. Thou hast given us devoted parents, faithful friends and helpful teachers who, by word and deed, show us the way that leads to right-living.

Thou hast endowed our minds with reason, our hearts with affection, our souls with love for truth, that we might live good and useful lives. How great is Thy kindness, O God, how numerous the tokens of Thy love! O give thanks unto the Lord, for His mercy endureth forever.

Master, when all unbelief and superstition shall disappear, when falsehood and error shall be no more, and all mankind shall proclaim, even as do we:— 

Children sing: Shema, Mi Komocho and Yimloch. 

MINISTER: 

O Lord our God! Whose children are all mankind, we pray for a better understanding of our duties unto our fellow-men. Help us, O Father, to see in all human beings our brothers, and to do unto them even as we wish them to do unto us. Blot out from our hearts all selfishness and vanity and pride. May we be modest in our words and deeds, just and kind in all the actions of our life. Inspire our souls with sympathy for the poor and the distressed. Thankful to Thee for our happiness,

may we be messengers of happiness unto others. May we prove how grateful we are to Thee, by sharing the abundance with which Thou hast blessed us, with those who are in want and in sorrow. O hasten the coming of that day, O Lord, when all mankind shall be one, one in love and in fellowship, one in the pursuit of justice and mercy, even as Thou art One. Amen!

Let justice flow as a river and righteousness as a mighty stream.

Cease to do evil; learn to do well; relieve the oppressed; do justice to the fatherless; plead for the widow. 

Blessed is he that considereth the poor; the Lord will deliver him in time of trouble.

The rich and poor are alike, for God is the Maker of them both.

He that despiseth his neighbor sinneth, but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he. 

Thou shalt open wide thy hand unto thy brother, to the poor and to the needy in thy land. 

Treasures of wickedness profit nothing; but righteousness delivereth from death. 

He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, honor and peace.

FOURTH SERVICE 

MINISTER: 

"How wondrous are Thy works, O Lord! in wisdom hast Thou made them all; the whole earth is full of Thy glory!" All creation proclaims Thy greatness, and all Thy handiwork sings Thy praises. Thou are the source of all life and light. From Thee alone comes strength and success. We thank Thee, Eternal Ruler, for the days of the past week, and for the work that was ours to do. We thank Thee for our health and happiness, for the peace of home, for the love of parents and dear ones, for the better knowledge of Thee and of Thy commandments that the past week has brought us. Unto Thee, Giver of all good, be our praise and thanksgiving!

What is man, O God, that Thou art mindful of him, what is the child of earth that Thou considerest him! 

Yet hast Thou crowned him with honor and with glory, and hast given him dominion over all the works of Thy hand, for man alone hast Thou endowed with intelligence and ambition, that by work and industry, he might be ruler of earth. Of all the blessings Thou hast bestowed upon man, highest and most precious is the blessing of work, that wondrous power whereby man builds a para-

FIFTH SERVICE 

MINISTER: 

Before we return to our homes, we render thanks unto Thee, Almighty God, for the wise and useful knowledge that here has been taught us. We thank 

Thee for the happy hours spent in this school, for our kind teachers and friends, for those noble men and women who devote themselves so earnestly to the education and the care of the young. May their words of truth have not been spoken in vain. May the lessons they have striven to impart to-day, sink deeply into our hearts, and remain in our memories, that our lives may be devoted to that which is pure and holy, and deserve the blessing of Him who is our Judge and Father.

High above all other creatures has man been placed. 

Him alone has God created in His image; upon him alone has He bestowed the almost divine power of speech, that he may proclaim His glory, and make known His law unto all the world. But blessed as is speech, it is often also a source of evil. Life and death are in the power of the tongue, and words may cheer and uplift, and also wound and pain. 11

SERVICE FOR HOLIDAYS 

Read the Service for the particular week, then:

MINISTER: 

O God of Israel, Father of all mankind! We recall to-day the wondrous protection Thou didst always grant unto our ancestors. In all times of danger, when the enemy threatened the very life of Israel, Thou hast been our strong Defense, our Shield and Savior. May we, living in this happier age and in this land of liberty, be faithful to Thee, even as were our fathers in the dark days of the past. May we be ever proud of our glorious history and true to our Jewish duty. May prejudice, hatred and oppression cease, and love and truth unite all mankind in the brotherhood of man, under the Fatherhood of 

God. Amen!

SERVICE FOR PATRIOTIC OCCASIONS 

Read the Service for the particular week, then:

MINISTER: 

Our Father and Master! With hearts full of gratitude have we assembled before Thee, to thank Thee for Thy mercies which never fail, and for Thy loving-kindness which never ceases. We praise Thee for the blessings that are ours in this our beloved country. Guard our nation, O God, from all danger and evil. May peace and prosperity ever rule in our land, and justice and righteousness be the strong supports of our Republic. 

May we faithfully guard the priceless treasure of liberty, and be ever loyal to our flag, and to the great principles for which it stands, freedom, justice, peace. Hasten the coming of that day, Ruler of the universe, when over all the earth falsehood and oppression shall cease, and all mankind be united in brotherly love. Amen!

RESPONSIVE READING

By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted, but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked.

When the righteous rule, the people rejoice; but when the wicked hold sway, the people mourn. 

Thus saith the Lord: Keep ye judgment and do justice, for in these do I delight. 

These are the things ye shall do: speak ye, every man, the truth unto his neighbor; execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates. 15
