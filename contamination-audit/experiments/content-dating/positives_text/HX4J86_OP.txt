Foreword 

This narrative was written to be incorporated into a Pageant of Nations prepared under the leadership of the W.P.A. Adult Education Project and presented at the South High School, Salt Lake City, June 1st, 1939. The occasion was New Citizens' Day, designed to honor the new citizens of the year 193839, the majority of whom had attended W.P.A. Citizenship classes in Salt Lake County. These lines were intoned by a narrator against a background of appropriate national music, and were accompanied by a simple pantomime symbolical of the union of foreign nationalities and foreign traits with the ideals and commonwealth of America. The directors and teachers of these classes felt that it was well for the new citizen, and indeed, for the old one, to remember that the virtues of America are a careful selection of the virtues of Europe and Asia. Accordingly, the context of the narrative is concerned with the gifts brought, knowingly or unknowingly, by those who seek citizenship in the United States of America. R.C.T.
