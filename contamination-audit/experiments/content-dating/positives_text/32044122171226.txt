INTRODUCTION 

In all departments of Science there continues to be study, experiment, experience, and through these three, PROGRESS. The influence of the New Age tends toward specialized effort. 

The Author of this book has made a special study of the Unfavorable Aspects of Astrology, and has, through experiment and experience, succeeded in discovering a method of procedure which has enabled him to overcome or lessen the effects of unfortunate influences. 

Every Horoscope has both favorable and unfavorable aspects. Adverse Aspects indicate circumstances to be overcome, lessons to be learned. 

It is equally important, in life's experience, to overcome or reduce the effects of unfavorable influences and to take advantage of those that are favorable. But there seems to be more satisfaction in gaining a safe landing through "bucking the tide" than through drifting with it.

Real Progress is attained through Effort.

PART ONE CHAPTER I Judging An Aspect 

The particular Department of Life in which the influence of an aspect tends to take effect has been a puzzle to many students. In forming a judgment in this respect the relative strength of the two aspecting planets should first be considered. Notice which of the two is the stronger through elevation, house position, essential dignity. EXAMPLE; Mars in Aries in opposition to the Sun in Libra; Mars above the horizon, the 

Sun below it. Mars, in this case, is elevated above the 

Sun, consequently Mars would be considered the stronger of the two. Mars is strong in Aries, the Sun is weak in Libra, so in respect to sign position also, Mars is the stronger planet.

Under these conditions the department of life indicated by the House occupied by the Sun, together with affairs relative to the nature of the Sign Libra, would be subject to strongly adverse influence, while the department of life indicated by the house position of Mars would be subject to petty annoyances. 

Judgment is more difficult in cases in which the strength of aspecting planets is nearly equal. The planets should be considered point by point before judgment is rendered. The nature of the transits should be taken into account. The sex of the Native modifies the situation to some degree.

The position of either planet in a so-called "double bodied" sign may bring more than one experience of an unpleasant nature in cases of unfavorable aspect. 

Two or more aspects in effect at the same time may act simultaneously, especially if one or more of the aspecting planets is posited in a double bodied sign, or if the Native was "born under" a plural or double

bodied sign. Signs having a plural or double nature are: Gemini, Pisces, the first half of Sagittarius, and also, to some extent, the sign Libra.

It has been observed that the Moon, if situated in a double bodied sign at birth, will produce a more or less plural effect when passing through its own sign Cancer. The same applies to all the planets, to some degree. (The Sun and Moon are not planets, strictly speaking, but are classed as planets for the sake of convenience).

EXAMPLE: Native (having Sun in Pisces at birth) 

lost his house by fire and received an inheritance upon the same day. One aspect was good, the other adverse, both in operation at the same time. Two independent effects were produced, each influence being independent of the other. The favorable aspect being the stronger of the two, the gain through inheritance was greater than the loss through fire. The benefic influence, producing its effect through a fixed sign, brought to the Native an income for life. 

An aspect producing effects through a plural sign may manifest influence through two or more unrelated events of the natures relative to the Houses in which the two planets have dominion by Sign, especially if no planets are posited in such Signs or Houses.

Proper interpretation of influences requires natural ability as well as experience in delineation. Natural ability for the interpretation of Horoscopes is indicated by favorable aspects to Mercury from the Moon, Saturn or Uranus; also by either a favorable or an unfavorable aspect to Uranus from any other planet. 

CHAPTER II How To Overcome Adverse Influences 

Adverse influences may be overcome in two different ways. They may be overcome either by design or by accident. In order to overcome an unfavorable

influence by design consult the horoscope for indications regarding the best thing to be done under the circumstances. Accidental overcoming of adverse influences is a possibility-it does happen occasionally. 

If a man aims a blow at you, you do not have to allow it to land. You can side step or "duck" and let it pass by. If it comes too swiftly, and with sufficient force, escape is difficult, but avoidance of its full effect should be attempted.

First, let it be borne in mind that there are some strong influences that cannot be fully overcome, notwithstanding the claims of those who say that one can rise above the influence of any adverse aspect. Of all such we ask: "Is the building up of a strong, husky constitution possible if one had the Sun and Saturn in opposition at birth?" We know that there are remedies which tend to prolong life in spite of strong handicaps. We suggest the avoidance of matters coming under the effect of strongly adverse influences, but we consider that it is practically impossible to completely eliminate the effects of such influences. The attention may be turned to other matters which are pointed out by the indications of the horoscope as being beneficial.

Unfavorable influences often do work out for good by forcing one to greater efforts. Continuous effort produces strength and power. "The toughest wood is found where the strong winds blow." The toughest wood does not come from trees growing in shaded glades or in quiet valleys but from trees growing on exposed hillsides.

The Horoscope having the most number of adverse aspects we have ever seen, was that of a street car conductor. His chart showed that if he had exerted himself sufficiently, he could have been at the head of a business organization of considerable magnitude, with a salary in proportion. But he did not have enough adverse aspects in his chart to bring out a

fighting spirit. He lacked the energy and ambition which would have enabled him to accomplish something worth while.

No doubt he drifted into the first thing that came to him, and allowed circumstances of the moment to rule. A famous General once exclaimed, "Circumstances? I make circumstances!" This street car conductor took the foregoing interpretation of his chart as a case of flattery on the part of the Astrologer. He had drifted with the tide too long to realize the power within him. Few worries plus a steady job appeared to satisfy him. 

We call aspects "bad" or "good" in order to distinguish them, but bad aspects often bring good results. With favorable aspects or influences the good is apparent, but the good that is back of adverse aspects is usually hidden from us. One thing especially should be noted whenever possible in studying any Horoscope. IF the charts of the parents are available, notice whether the chart of either parent shows aspects similar to those of the native, for in that event the influence of those aspects in the native's chart is greatly strengthened.

EXAMPLE: In the author's own chart, Mars was in opposition to the Sun; in the chart of the author's wife, Mars and the Sun were in square. The oldest daughter had Mars square to the Sun in Aries. The Moon in her chart was weak. She passed away under the influence of this aspect, yet the fact of her death seems to surprise an Astrologer who has studied her chart purely from a health point of view. The author himself was too ill at the time to render any assistance (double bodied sign effect as previously mentioned); the author's mother also passed away during the same period. 

One might ask "Why were not these adverse aspects overcome?" The answer is that the author had not discovered at that time his present method of sidestepping the results of unfortunate influences. The theory that 10

a similar aspect in a parent's chart intensifies the influence of an aspect in the native's chart was not taken under consideration until later. It is probable that we were the first to notice this condition. Since then, others have noted this intensification. In studying any aspect one should always consider the chart as a whole. 

We believe that the influence of similar adverse aspects in a child's and a parent's chart is still further intensified if the aspects occur in the same house, and to a less extent if they occur in the same sign. The same would be true of similiarly located favorable aspects. 

Many factors enter into the strength of an aspect; the location in signs, in houses, in angles, the retrograde or direct motion of the planets, the applying or separating condition of the aspect; as well as the closeness or the importance of the relationship. We believe that the total number of aspects in a chart has a strong bearing upon the subject. In the case of many simultaneous aspects, bad aspects will act more quickly and sharply than in cases in which the total number is below the average. 

The sensitiveness of an individual is increased or diminished according to the number of aspects in operation at the time of birth.

The smallest total number of aspects (semi-sextile, semi-square and stronger) we have ever seen in a birth chart was 8; the largest number 33. Many charts have no parallels. We have seen charts with as many as twelve parallels, five parallels to one planet alone, all being within one degree of perfect; four more within a degree and a half of perfect. The large number of one degree parallels may have been the cause of bringing the other four into effect, although ordinarily only parallels within one degree of perfect are considered. 

One Astrologer we know always marks all parallels as conjunctions or oppositions, claiming that they are either one or the other in effect. Two planets in conjunction are often also in parallel; the same holds true with 11

planets in opposition. The strength of an aspect is intensified by a parallel.

Another point often neglected is the latitudes of planets in aspect. An aspect which is not close, having the planets widely separated in latitude, will not be very effective. This applies more strongly to progressed aspects than to aspects in the natal chart. 

The various periods of life also have their effect on aspects in so far as power is concerned. These periods run from about five years for the Moon in early childhood, to about twenty years in later life, for a slower moving planet. No hard and fast rule can be laid down as the boundary of these periods may, and probably do, vary with different individuals. These periods also may overlap considerably. A planet strong in a chart will have a far longer period than the same planet would if it were weak in the chart. The life periods run in this order: Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus. The last appears to be concerned with the grandparent period. Neptune is too elusive to be properly classified, but may indicate the life period of the centenarian.

An aspect between planets is either strong or weak according to the degree of natural harmony existing between the planets. For instance the Moon and Saturn affiliate, so do the Sun and Mars; Venus and Neptune; 

Mercury and Venus; Mercury and Uranus; and also 

Mercury and Neptune to a considerable degree. Mercury and Saturn do not get along well. One represents youth, the other, old age. The greatest enemies are 

Mars and Jupiter, one common, the other aristocratic; the former indifferent to wealth, the latter ruling money affairs; Mars, dry; Jupiter, moist; Jupiter showing the avenue of gain; Mars showing the circumstances surrounding the greatest loss or expense. Mars, as the God of War, brings loss or expense to the world. Mars likes to pioneer; Jupiter abhors anything that is contrary to established customs; Mars is executive; Jupiter is judi- 12

cial; Mars is somewhat original; Jupiter says: "Let well enough alone." 

Regarding both planets and aspects, the terms "good" and "bad" are relative only. Venus is called good, but if afflicted, she may assume an influence which tends to laziness or to running after pleasure. Jupiter, the greater benefic, if afflicted can bring much harm from much excess in pride or wealth, or from an exaggerated idea of one's own importance. Jupiter, when afflicted, may produce excess in various directions. 

The malefic planet Mars, on the other hand, gives energy and activity as well as strength of body and courage. 

Saturn gives a sense of preservation, patience, orderliness and system, as well as tenacity of purpose, yet we call Saturn "the greater malefic." Saturn usually produces a lasting influence even when posited in a "Common" sign. An aspect coming from Saturn through a Fixed sign often lasts for a life time. 

CHAPTER III Nature of Influence 

The opposition is destructive, a rending or tearing apart; a conflict,-in some cases explosive; a clash of wills or opinions. Losses are indicated through the things or persons which the planets represent through their house and sign affiliations. The planet in elevation usually gains the mastery or brings about the action; in other cases this is done by the planet which is the stronger through sign or house position. This applies to all aspects, either favorable or unfavorable.

Always try to circumvent the influence of an adverse aspect through whatever favorable aspect either planet may form with another. If a favorable planet aspects both participants of an adverse aspect-that benefic influence may be used to offset the unfortunate influence. 

For instance: Often we find an opposition between two 13

planets with a third planet in trine to one participant and sextile to the other. The third planet represents a third person who may be able to settle things in a manner satisfactory to both parties represented in the opposition. An unfavorable conjunction can sometimes be handled in the same manner, but the third party may have more difficulty in bringing harmony out of the situation, since in this case he is liable to take sides or be more interested in one side than the other. 

In case of an adverse aspect with no planet favorable to either participant, the difficulty is more noticeable. 

In such cases, find a point by longitude on the chart where a transiting planet may be in harmony. Find several such points and try to locate a time when more than one point is occupied, and work as the natures of the planets transiting these points indicate. Also try to find a lunation that is favorable by sign and degree to the adverse aspect, then note the time when the influence will be most effective. The foregoing refers to the natal chart only.

An adverse parallel usually can be read as an opposition, but not always. The longitudes of planets in parallel may be such that they may be in semi-sextile or sextile aspect. In that event the parallel acts as a brake on the good indicated by the favorable aspect. However, this situation is rare. In some instances, an adverse parallel may act as if it were favorable, or it may intensify the good indicated by the favorable aspect. Here we are referring to the parallel between two malefic planets only. The ability to side-step adverse influence depends upon the attitude of the individual; his mental poise; his diplomatic ability in handling difficult situations.

Let us suppose Mars and Uranus are in sextile aspect and also in parallel. This parallel would not be liable to act in an adverse manner, yet might modify the influence of the sextile; more misfortune will be threatened than will actually occur. Apprehension might be 14

created if either planet has dominion or influence in the third house. To a less extent this is true with the other houses except such houses as are ruled by Mars or Uranus. However, this is a rare situation. Planets in parallel are seldom also in other aspects than the conjunction or opposition. The parallel when acting as a conjunction is favorable with good planets and unfavorable with adverse ones. When both a favorable and an unfavorable planet are involved in a parallel and conjunction, or in a parallel alone, note which planet is the stronger by sign, house, etc. The position of this planet will indicate an annoying rather than a frustrating influence.

An adverse conjunction is a difficult aspect to handle as it is often tricky. Its influence is often somewhat hidden, or of a surprising nature; conjunctions to Uranus are especially surprising in nature. Much depends upon which planet is the stronger by position and upon the degree of harmony existing between their essential natures.

Take the conjunction of the Moon and Saturn; this may not be very adverse as their natures tend to affiliate.

Again we must consider whether a male or a female chart is being interpreted. The influence of the conjunction of the Moon and Saturn is not so unfavorable in a male chart as it is in a female chart, all other things being equal, including strength of sign and house positions. In a male chart this conjunction tends to delays in business affairs but the results of these delays may prove to be beneficial through emphasizing continuance and stability and preventing too many changes. This conjunction tends to permanence in business, yet it may lead to sticking too long to a losing venture. With a woman, it tends to ill health more than to anything else. The square aspect is an upsetting one. Things are at cross purposes. The influence of the square is not so destructive as that of the opposition, but the results 15

of the influence can be very annoying. This aspect brings disappointments and hindrances. These, however, can be overcome to a great extent thru proper efforts at proper times. (Note individual cases in later chapters). 

The semi-square produces about the same result as the square but in a lesser degree. 

We have found that the 22½° aspect works out adversely in cases in which both planets are weak by sign or position. In one such case, the influence from this aspect appears to have been the primary cause of death. 

The whole chart was weak and there were indications that the physical body had been neglected. This neglect appears to have been the main cause of death. The native had a poor start in life, an unfortunate childhood because of the early death of his mother. Military training did wonders for this man. However, there was little to build on, so this good effect was nullified when Mars and other planets progressed to less fortunate positions and relationships. With proper attention to his chart, by avoiding the use of tobacco, using a proper diet and observing the ordinary rules of health, this native might have lived many years but he died at the age of twenty-five.

The influences of the thirty-five degree aspect and the one hundred fifty degree aspect seem to be indecisive. 

Through either, one is apt to have a loss, which may come about thru the neglect, mistake or indifference of others. Other aspects frequently work in the same way. 

Every mention of aspects, in this work, refers to natal aspects unless otherwise stated. The influence of aspects between progressed planets may or may not be noticeable, depending upon the degree of excitement occurring at the times of their culmination, and also depending upon the degree of difference in latitude between the aspecting planets. Wide difference in latitude is probably usually the reason for progressed aspects not coming up to expectation. The results from influences of aspects between progressed planets prove to 16

be stronger with some people than with others. In some cases we have seen these aspects work out more powerfully than natal aspects do with certain other people. 

Some Astrologers ignore progressed aspects entirely. 

We have seen too many cases in which they produced results, so we cannot disregard them. 

Any progressed aspect will be of little consequence if there are no similar aspects in the natal chart or if the aspecting planets were without any kind of aspect at the time of birth. Aspects between transiting planets will have little or no apparent effect unless they occur very close to important points in the natal chart. This does not necessarily mean the point of a conjunction with a natal planet, but may be near the point of some other important aspect to a natal planet. 

The influence of a new Moon is important if the Moon is in conjunction, opposition, or square to a planet in the natal chart, but the influence of the square is not very strong. The influence of lunation (new Moon) seems to be more liable to manifest during the third week than at any other time, yet effects may become apparent at any time during the twenty-eight days following the lunation. Just why the influence of a lunation should be more likely to manifest during the waning of the Moon is not clear.

A case in point: A few years ago three U. S. submarines ran aground near Monterey, California, with a loss of twenty-two lives. At the lunation (nearly a month previous) Mars and Neptune were in conjunction as were also the Sun and Moon. The primary cause of the disaster was a mystery which was never fully cleared. We predicted at the time that Saturn, casting his rays to the lunation, would give permanence to the mystery. A naval officer told the author that he believed that the head navigator of the submarine fleet was intoxicated at the time and that the other submarines naturally followed his lead blindly-probably a Neptune influence. 17

In contrast to all this, a lunation which was also an eclipse which came close to a birthdate appeared to be effective nearly ten days before the lunation. The previous lunation had been of little importance to the individual, and in the previous year the lunation following the birthdate was also of little account. This lunation was also an eclipse and occurred in the individual's birth sign, but considerably beyond the Sun's place. 

In the ensuing chapters the author has taken up the various aspects in a more specific manner with the purpose of contributing to the public the results of his experience as to the best manner in which to overcome or reduce the effects of unfavorable influences. 

In some instances the effects of the influences are the only things mentioned. In these particular cases the proper methods of procedure in overcoming the conditions seem so obvious that mention of these methods appears to be unnecessary.

CHAPTER IV 

Adverse Aspects of the Sun 

Overcoming Adverse Aspects Between Sun or Moon 

An unfavorable conjunction of the Sun usually can be handled to good advantage. Other cases are difficult to control; natives give up before any good can be accomplished. A native should see if there is some favorable influence which could be used to modify the adverse conjunction. Above all, behavior which would tend to increase the power of the influence, should be avoided.

The Sun and Uranus in conjunction in a male chart can be overcome to a great extent if the native avoids sudden changes in occupation or business and is especially careful regarding sudden opportunities or offers. This influence is not so noticeable if the native is a government employee, in fact this aspect often indicates government employment. The native might be inclined to establish innovations or to make radical changes in his business which would be to his detriment. 18

Under this aspect he should not make changes except after due consideration of all the factors involved, including the sign and house positions of the aspecting planets.

Eccentric ideas should not be allowed to develop. 

Credit and standing should be carefully guarded at these times, for with this aspect in operation, the native, feeling himself secure, may suddenly find that his credit is destroyed. The native should also be careful about handling electric wires or electric appliances. 

The above applies also to the opposition which appears to be even more adverse.

With a woman an unfavorable influence of the Sun and Uranus is apt to cause her to leave her husband or to elope with another man, perhaps even a married one. 

This influence has a strong tendency to bring about separation or divorce. Under this influence divorced persons may prove to be detrimental, especially those of the opposite sex. To a woman of high moral tendencies and training, the effect of this influence might be in the nature of a passing temptation, especially if the fifth or seventh houses were not involved. This influence may bring about separation because of business conditions.

Sun Conjunction Saturn 

This influence has a tendency to weaken the constitution, usually through the part of the body represented by either or both of the signs which are occupied by the aspecting planets. This applies to both men and women. Sun conjunction Saturn sometimes indicates a weakness inherited from the father. In a female chart it is more apt to indicate the early passing of the father than in a male chart.

A native having this aspect in operation at birth should avoid dealing with elderly persons, and with those having Saturn occupations. Chronic troubles in the body should not be allowed to get the upper hand, 19

nor to go too long without proper attention. The native should avoid strain from heavy physical labor especially in handling building materials. Colds are apt to settle in the parts of the body represented by the signs which the aspecting planets occupy; the circulation in these parts is liable to be poor. In this respect the conjunction of the Sun and Saturn is not so bad as the opposition, because the heat of the Sun helps to counteract the cold of Saturn. With the opposition in operation, apply heat to that part of the body represented by the sign in which Saturn is located. This application of heat should be used with moderation.

The movements or speech of a person having this aspect at birth are apt to be exasperatingly slow. Some effort should be made to quicken the speech and physical activity. Good opportunities may be lost through procrastination or through too careful deliberation. This is especially true if these aspects occur in the third house. Some organ or part of the body which is represented by the sign occupied by either the Sun or Saturn, may be underdeveloped. This part or organ should never be overworked. This is more liable to be the case with the opposition than with the conjunction. 

A native having the Sun in opposition to Saturn would not be likely to succeed in any Saturn business, while with the conjunction, he might do well if his natal planets were otherwise well aspected; in fact he might prove to be exceptionally successful, but hard work or long application would be necessary. This is especially true if the aspecting planets are in fixed signs, particularly Leo or Taurus. 

Unless Jupiter is favorable these natives should avoid large animals. In their diet they should avoid foods requiring slow digestion and also iced drinks, especially if either of the aspecting planets is in the sign Cancer. 

Heavy labor should be avoided. Perspiration should be free but never excessive. 20

Under this aspect promotion can seldom be hurried, but steady conscientious efforts should bring their just reward in the end. Impatience should be avoided in all 

The teeth should be given good care. While this is important with everyone, it is doubly so with natives having the Sun and Saturn in adverse aspect. Extremes of heat and cold should be avoided, since these may lead to chronic conditions and disabilities. Unfavorable influences from Saturn incline to chronic conditions. 

If either the Sun or Saturn has dominion in the second house, care should be exercised in making loans to older persons. Death of the borrower is liable to occur before the loans are paid.

Jupiter And The Sun In Adverse Aspect 

Extreme care should be exercised in dealing with those in authority. Promotion is difficult, sometimes due to mistaken ideas on the part of an employer in regard to the native's ability. At other times difficulties are due to the native's exalted opinion of his own powers or, as often expressed, "an exalted ego." The native should beware of over expansion in his business. In a suit of law the presiding judge may have sympathy for the opponent. The reputation may suffer without any real cause. The native may be much annoyed by false rumors. This can be overcome to a great extent by earnestly trying to maintain a reputation for honesty and fair dealing, then when such rumors start they will not be believed. Those having the Sun adverse to Jupiter are more subject to false rumors than are others.

With women, this aspect often leads to difficulties in the marriage state or to error in chosing a mate. Both men and women having this aspect at birth should avoid people of the decided Jupiter type as much as is practicable. Misunderstandings with this type of people are liable to occur. 21

Sun In Conjunction With Mars 

This is not always an unfavorable influence if care is taken in handling it. This is one of the easiest adverse aspects to turn into good account since the Sun and Mars affiliate easily. There is a tendency to overdo through misjudging the personal powers or the physical strength. 

This aspect tends to produce much impatience, which should be curbed. This influence tends to diseases of a feverish nature, rushes of blood to the head, etc.; contagious diseases are apt to be severe. Much can be accomplished under this aspect when the amount of physical energy is not misjudged. With the Sun in opposition to Mars the conditions are somewhat the same, but there is less stamina to the constitution, and the physical energy must be conserved. Men of the Mars or military type should be avoided. No man having this aspect should enter military service. Women should avoid men wearing uniforms. With both sexes this influence tends to quick temper and consequently to destructive tendencies. Under this aspect personal poise is very essential. A native born under this aspect should avoid entering arguments as he will accomplish nothing even when sure that he is in the right. 

A native under this aspect should avoid haste since through hurry accidents may occur, especially cuts or burns. He should be cautious in handling sharp edged tools and in approaching fire. If the Sun conjoins Mars in a fire sign there is a greater tendency for the unfortunate side of the aspect to manifest through impatience and haste. There is a strong desire for leadership, resentment if leadership is not attained. If the aspect occurs in a cardinal sign the ambitions may have to be curbed. "Make haste slowly." Contention should be avoided as much as possible. It would be better to exercise diplomacy. The department in which the above affairs are liable to appear will be indicated by the houses in which the Sun and Mars are situated. 22

Since the Sun and Venus do not form a stronger adverse aspect than the semi-square in the natal chart, little bad effect may be expected except when Venus progresses to the square of the Sun's natal place. However in this work we are not so much concerned with progressions as with the natal aspects, which are more important. Under the aspect of Venus progressed to the square of the natal Sun, there should be caution in all dealings with the opposite sex. The native, if a woman, may have difficulties in selecting suitable apparel. This also applies if Venus and the Sun are in conjunction and an unfortunate planet is in an unfavorable aspect with both, especially if the planet is Saturn. The following matter may be of interest to the female sex. Every woman should know that she will be becomingly dressed if her dress matches the color of her eyes. The next best thing for her to do is to match the color of her hair. 

There are other factors of course, but these two are of greatest importance.

The conjunction of the Sun and Mercury is often called adverse, as a close conjunction to the Sun was called "combust" by the older Astrologers. We have not found the influence of this aspect to be detrimental if care is taken to keep some of its tendencies under control. There is a liability to mistakes especially in writing or speech. More or less trouble is encountered in "getting things straight," in understanding information and explanations. All this does not mean that the person is lacking in brain power but that the brain may act a little too quickly for accuracy.

The Sun conjoined with Neptune may act adversely in some cases. This aspect tends to a lofty spirituality with the native living in such a high realm that he becomes uneasy when he has to leave his dream world and come into contact with the ordinary affairs of this worka-day world. He should try to pay more attention to things of a practical nature. A native born while this aspect is operating may be really prophetic, or at least 23

have powerful hunches which it is best for him to heed. 

With this conjunction and also the opposition, a native may be subjected to attempted fraud. This may not be so much in evidence with the conjunction as with the opposition. Here, the houses which the Sun and Neptune occupy have much to do with the effect. If either planet is in the eighth, death by drowning may occur. These natives should be careful of overdoses of medicine. This is also true if one of these planets is in the sixth. These individuals are unusually sensitive to narcotics and anesthetics. They should be very cautious in the use of sea foods. Self-deception is indicated in matters of the nature of the signs and houses. Strange, unexplainable experiences are liable to occur all through the life, and these may be favorable or adverse according to the transiting aspects. They may pertain to the native or to others in his surroundings according to the houses involved. The list of things the Sun and Neptune cover can be extended almost indefinitely. People born with this aspect in operation will meet many things in life that are not what they appear. All aspects between the 

Sun and other planets tend to work out in a more strictly personal way then do other aspects.

Aspects between the Sun and Moon show more difference in the trends of their influence in male and female charts than do any of the other aspects. The Moon with a man, represents business conditions. With a woman, the Moon has mainly to do with health, unless this luminary is posited in the fourth house. 

A man with the Sun and Moon in adverse aspect, especially the opposition, should be careful not to make frequent changes in business. He should be careful in dealing with superiors or employers, especially if either the Sun or Moon has dominion in the tenth house. He may have good and sufficient reasons for desiring a change but under this adverse aspect he might be a long time out of employment. The native's efforts pertaining to his business may not be fully appreciated; he 24

may be blamed for the errors of others or he may have a host of other annoyances. If he should be in business for himself, he may be inclined to change his location too often or to make changes within his business before certain conditions have time to be properly adjusted. 

A native born under this aspect should avoid dealing with the general public unless the Moon is favorably aspected by another planet. Dealings with public servants would probably be unsatisfactory or annoying; complaints would be useless. If either the Sun or Moon at birth was well aspected by a third planet, such situations could be successfully handled by a disinterested person. This would be especially true if such favorable planet had dominion in the eleventh house. With a man, the conjunction of the Sun and Moon is not nearly so adverse as it is with a woman. In fact this influence may benefit him. With a woman, the conjunction tends to poor health, through over abundance of the fluids in the body. A woman should protect herself against changes in weather conditions and against over exertion at the time of her monthly periods. In the fire signs this conjunction is not so bad. If the opposition comes from a water sign it is very necessary to guard against effects.

The Author believes that the prenatal epoch is not so important as many Astrologers consider it. He has seen two widely different times given for the prenatal epoch of former President Wilson. These presumably were figured out from the same birth data. One prenatalist remarked that he considers the natal chart to be only about half of a horoscope. He points out a particular trait of Wilson which he claims does not show up in the natal chart, but is plainly apparent in the prenatal. 

To the Author the natal chart seems to show sufficient evidence of this trait, but it is true that the indication in the prenatal chart is stronger. 59

Raphael gives an example of the impossibility of finding the right moment of conception.

In the Author's family, one daughter is supposed to have been born two weeks too soon according to the belief of the attending physician. There was no outward indication of a short period, but the Doctor thought that the heart valves were not fully developed. At the time of this daughter's birth there were no adverse aspects from Leo (heart) nor from Aquarius, the opposite sign to Leo, nor were there any adverse aspects to the Sun, with the exception of a square aspect from the Moon. 

Some claim that the time of coitus, if known, could be used for the time of the prenatal epoch. Well informed physicians say that conception often does not take place immediately; some say it occurs within twenty four hours, that this is the longest period of life for the fertilizing cells. Others say conception may take place any time within three days; still others extend the time to a week.

It is our belief that the time of conception determines the moment of birth and the duration of the prenatal period. A physician who has practised for twenty years says that he believes no woman carries a foetus longer than the usually allotted time. Some Astrologers claim that the period varies as much as fifteen days either way. We are inclined to accept this view. 

Other cases have come to our attention in which writers on astrological topics, in advancing their pet theories, overlooked strong evidences to the contrary.

Take the matter of which house represents which parent. 

Some claim that in all charts the tenth house represents the mother and the fourth the father. Others hold that the tenth represents the mother and the fourth the father in male charts, while in female charts the tenth is taken for the father and the fourth for the mother. One writer whose opinions are greatly respected claims that the tenth is the father house in all charts. He gives a 

Horoscope to prove his contention, but we proved, to 60

our own satisfaction, from the same chart that the opposite is true.

Horoscopes of both parents as almost indispensable to the proper reading of a chart. In regard to this much depends upon the degree of natural independence manifested in the child's chart. This is indicated by the distance of Mercury from the Sun. Mercury's position in a sign other than that of the Sun also indicates independence.

Ocean Tides and Human Beings

The ebb and flow of the tides have much influence upon human lives; the same forces that move the water 76

also affect people. It seems strange that scientific men, who readily admit that the Sun and Moon influence the tides, refuse to believe that that influence or power is concerned in any way with the super-sensitive nature of Man.

We have not been able to verify these statements but we are in sympathy with these ideas, which must seem reasonable to those who have studied the heavenly bodies. We personally have observed that storms along the sea-coast abate with the outgoing tide. 

In the few cases in which this does not occur, the storm continues for another twelve hours and abates at the next ebb of the tide. This matter is common knowledge among people living near the sea. They speak of a "tempest going out with the tide." We have seen storms begin to abate at the very moments of the receding turns of the tide.

This leads to a question as to whether or not there are similar slow pulsations on land. This is difficult to determine but we believe that eventually there will be scientific instruments to determine this and no doubt whatever pulsations are determined will coincide with planetary movements.

There is a great difference between the tides on the Atlantic and those on the Pacific Ocean. While there is a similarity in the daily variation as to full tides, which closely follows the variation in the rising time of the Moon, there is a great difference in the way these tides act. For instance: On the Atlantic, if the tide goes out four feet one morning the water will go out four feet at the next turn. On the Pacific Ocean, there is what is called a "short tide" and a "long tide." On the Pacific, if the tide goes out four feet one time, the next going-out will be for a much shorter distance. At alternate tides the going-out distance is the same. On both coasts of the 77

United States the tides rise higher and higher as one goes north. We have been wondering if they rise higher as one goes south below the equator. 

At the San Juan Islands in Puget Sound the tide rises about eleven feet. The long tide goes out only about a foot, then rises to its full height, going out the entire eleven feet again. We understand that there are other variations in the Pacific tides. The highest tides on the eastern coast occur in the spring and early fall, while on the Pacific the greatest rises occur in June and 

December. 

We asked an old navigator, who had some original theories about tides, the cause of the difference between the tides on the eastern and western coasts. He replied that this would take too long to explain because it is a subject in itself.

There are people who claim that there is a perceptible tide of a few inches on the shores of the Great Lakes of the United States. We can neither verify this nor deny it. 

No doubt there is a variation in natural gravity or land tides all over the country due to the differences in topography and in mineral content of the Earth's crust. Applying the Rules 

The rules found in the various text books of Astrology should be applied with discretion and understanding.

These rules cannot be applied literally in all cases. Some rules are subject to variation or modification according to the relative factors of the individual Horoscope.

Take, for instance, the rule for the description of the husband or wife. The Astrologer must judge whether or not the native is liable to marry for another reason than pure affection. Women, probably more often than men, marry for social position, home, money or for various other reasons. The male, being the aggressor, pursues the female in practically all animal life. His love is positive, possessive, compelling; while that of the female is more passive or yielding. We are not going to express an opinion as to whether men or women are the more changeable in affections. The stability or variability of the affections depends upon birth chart influences and there is a great difference in this matter between different members of the same sex. 

Let us now consider the rule that, in a female chart, the first planet to which the Sun applies by aspect, describes the husband; in a male chart, the first planet to which the Moon applies, describes the wife. 

This rule holds good in the majority of cases, but we have seen a number of cases in which the rule does not apply, even cases in which affection was the marriage motive. We have noticed that a variation from this rule occurs more often with female charts than with male. 

The reason for this difference may be the fact that custom denies the female the right to pick and choose, giving this privilege to the male. 86

For instance: A Libra woman had the Sun applying to a conjunction with the planet Venus. This is a powerful aspect for any woman but in this case Venus did not describe her husband, except that he was fairly good looking. He was strongly of a Saturn type, yet Saturn was the third planet to which the woman's natal Sun made application. This application was to a trine. The husband, in his birth chart, had Saturn in Capricorn and near the Ascendant. This man's offer of marriage was not the first which the woman had received. Venus did describe the business of the man who made the first offer, but his appearance tended toward a Uranus- 

Mercury type.

At the time of their marriage the woman's husband had his progressed Sun in conjunction with his natal 

Venus. The planets were, at the time, in opposition to the woman's natal Venus, but apparently this opposition had no effect. Aspects to the Ascendant are of greater importance in such matters. In this case there were, in both charts, aspects to the Ascendant from one chart to the other. In the cases of her former suitors, the aspects between the factors of her chart and the factors of theirs may not have been harmonious, so that the influences were not sufficiently attractive to bring about the close relationship of marriage.

The man which this native married had the Moon applying to Saturn, and no other aspect to the Moon. The native herself did not appear to be of the Saturn type, yet Saturn was in elevation in her chart, while his Saturn also was in a powerful position, and these strong Saturn influences may account for the attraction between them. 

This woman's appearance was not indicative of her birth sign, nor was her husband's appearance of a type which would indicate his sign of birth. 

The native (woman) had no planets in the fifth and seventh houses. These two houses, the planets situated within them, and the rulers of the fifth and seventh cusps, must be considered in questions of marriage. The 87

native had Scorpio on the seventh cusp. If we use Mars as the ruler of Scorpio, we find this planet in elevation in the husband's chart, in the center of her own fifth house, and this planet in an intercepted sign in both charts. If we take Pluto as ruler of her seventh cusp, we find that this planet was in her first house and in his fourth. 

Another case:

A woman, at birth, had the Sun applying to a square aspect of Jupiter. After having refused two proposals of marriage, she became strongly attracted to a man of the Saturn type. Notice this: Saturn ruled her fifth house and was the second planet to which the Sun was in aspect, but the aspect was separating by more than two degrees. Again note: the man to whom she was attracted had his Jupiter in conjunction with her Sun. 

Their ascendants were in trine. His Ascendant was in almost exact conjunction with the cusp of her fifth house. However, there was no marriage between them, due to a misunderstanding indicated by his progressed Sun being in aspect with his Uranus at the same time that her progressed Venus was in opposition to the ruler of her fifth. His seventh house also was involved, a transit of Neptune helping to complicate matters. The man this woman finally married was unquestionably of a 

Mars-Uranus appearance and an Aquarian. 

Another case: A man at birth had the Moon applying to an aspect of Saturn. This was the Moon's only aspect. 

The bi-corporeal sign Gemini on the fifth cusp, and Jupiter situated in the fifth house, gave indications of many attractions to the opposite sex. However, this native did not propose marriage until he met a woman who had her Sun in his fifth house. Her Moon and his 

Venus were nearly in conjunction.

We could cite many other cases but this is unnecessary as, from the foregoing, it can readily be seen that successful mating is dependent upon meeting the right person and taking the right time for the union. The great amount of marital trouble in the world is probably 88

accounted for by the fact that few couples select the right time for marriage. We have never seen a case in which a woman did not later regret her marriage if Neptune was, at the time of marriage, transiting her natal Sun. Several of these women have been heard to say, "I don't know just why I did it." Another remarkable fact: We have never seen a Horoscope of male who married at the time of a transit of Neptune over the position of his natal Moon. 

The transit of Neptune over any natal planet seems to produce some sort of hypnotic influence concerning the things represented by the natal planet in the individual Horoscope.

The Astrologer should try to point out the rough spots indicated by making comparison charts of husband and wife, and should try to show how these spots may be smoothed.

If each individual would try to correct his own faults and incompatabilities, what a happy world this would be! We, who understand Astrology, can try to overcome our own shortcomings. If I "cast the beam out of my own eye, the moat in my brother's will take care of itself."

But to get back to our subject. None of the rules of 

Astrology should be too rigidly applied.

The fact that an individual may be desperately in love without the feeling being reciprocated, is acknowledged. 

We are puzzled over the Astrological indications in such situations and would like to have this subject discussed. 

Necessity of Good and Bad

For centuries people have asked "Why is there evil in the world? Why was not everything created perfect?"

Orthodox preachers have replied that God cannot create a being equal to Himself. If a man was perfect he also would be a God.

Whether or not we agree with this reasoning, we must admit that what is true in a spiritual sense, is true 89

also in material things and in the lives of men. We grow and gain in knowledge by the obstacles we overcome. 

All good would defeat its own purpose. We would become weaklings if there were no difficulties to overcome. 

If there were no physical adversities we would soon wither away and cease to exist. If there was no resistance to the attainment of our desires, we would soon become satiated with success and all effort would cease. We might cry out for "more worlds to conquer." We would not be able to appreciate good, for there would be no contrast. We would become passive, and languish from our own inactivity because there would be no motive or ambition to bring about activity. All progress would cease.

We human beings are prone to look upon successful persons with jealousy. We are liable to think that the lives of successful people must have been free from obstacles. We do not know the troubles and secret sorrows these people may have. Nothing gives greater satisfaction than achievement through overcoming adverse circumstances. This satisfaction does much to offset the effects of the hard blows which fate distributes to most of us, at one time or another. 

Good fortune and adversity are as necessary in this world as a positive and a negative pole in an electric battery, a generator in a power house or the two sexes in animal life. This does not mean that one sex has all the good and the other all the bad qualities, though some one-sided individuals would like to have us believe this. Good fortune and adversity are as necessary as are land and water, and fire needs air for its very existence. 

Control of adverse influences is the thing to be attempted and attained.

Man must not live for himself alone. A man's life is a failure if he has made no one better or happier. All true Astrologers try to aid humanity. 

Hints to Teachers 

Teachers are often asked the meanings of certain as- 90

pects. The effect of an aspect depends upon the natures of the aspecting planets, and their positions as to elevation, sign and house; also upon the general influences of the chart taken as a whole, and the state of evolution of the individual for whom the indications are being determined. We believe that there is no birth chart in which the indications do not need modification from the generally accepted meaning of some position or aspect. The fact that there are exceptions to every rule applies to all departments of astrological interpretation. 

Let us take Mars conjunction Saturn, the one transiting over the natal position of the other. If no other adverse aspects are in operation at the same time, or if there were no other adverse aspects in the natal chart, and if either, or both Mars and Saturn, were well placed at birth, the adverse nature of the aspect brings little or no result. But if another planet is in opposition to 

Mars or Saturn and still another in square aspect, then unfortunate results may be expected, which would be difficult to avoid. Note what has previously been said about these adverse aspects of Mars and Saturn. One can readily see that no simple rule can be stated which would cover all these variations. 

We have found that students are inclined to attribute more power to transits than is actually found in practice. 

A transit which is not in conjunction with a sensitive point in the natal chart has little power. 

Any real student of Astrology who has pursued his investigation for some years will have found out things that are not seen in any text-book. What he has discovered may be nothing really new, but may be new to him, and may benefit others as well as himself. 

We advise enthusiastic students not to rush into predictive astrology. Public predictions which fail are usually the work of students who have not had enough experience to know how necessary it is to take all of the facts into consideration. Minor indications are often neglected or considered to be of no account. 91

Those who oppose Astrology call the confirmation of a prediction "a coincidence." We ask such know-it-alls: "How many coincidences does it take to establish a fact?"

A student is apt to color his answer to a public question by his own desires or his own private opinion. This attitude should be avoided.

Astrology's Critics 

Those who oppose the teachings of Astrology say that predictions must be certain in order to be of value to human beings. These opponents also claim that we would have no free will if it were true that the planets control our lives.

We reply: "If there is a mud-puddle in your path do you need to walk through it?" If the planets place a golden opportunity in the way that opportunity can be embraced or ignored. Our opportunities are not placed before us until we are prepared for them. Our immortal 

Lincoln said: "If I prepare myself, perhaps my chance will come." This quotation may not be exact, but is true as to substance.

The stars point to a certain way but we are free to go that way or to take a by-path. The stars may indicate some menace, and through warning from the stars the divine Creator of the heavens may permit us to avoid the danger.

The individual who invented the expression "take time by the forelock" probably knew something of Astrology. The forelock is that part of a horse's mane which falls between the eyes and ears. A gentle horse may be led by taking his forelock in the hand. Astrologers have a duty to perform in directing people how and when to "grasp time by the forelock."

Astrology cannot be of great use to the general public when unqualified individuals are allowed to claim astrological ability. The ability of an Astrologer should be 92

attested by a certificate given by a legal examining board of the state. A few fakirs can do, and have done, more to discredit Astrology than all the newspaper columnists put together.

Many years ago, when we first began to investigate Astrology, there seemed to be more charlatans than real Astrologers. Today this situation appears to be reversed, yet we still have bogus or ballyhoo artists among us. 

Solar Houses 

We have not much sympathy with this phase of Astrology. Since Solar or Sun Sign readings have been exposed as not being reliable, certain individuals have resorted to giving readings based upon the so-called solar houses. This is done in a manner that induces a client to believe that the interpretation pertains to the natal Horoscope houses. Making prognostications from solar houses as if they were natal houses confuses the client and is liable to create animosity toward the science of Astrology.

We have found that indications according to the solar houses have little or no truth as to effect. To us it seems the height of absurdity to call Gemini the second house because one was born when the Sun was in Taurus, unless Gemini was actually on the cusp of the second house in a Horoscope erected for the moment of birth.

When Sun-sign readings were exposed as not being actual Horoscope work, many would-be Astrologers took up solar house readings, hoping to make them appear authentic and scientific to uninformed clients. It is a shame to see how some of our otherwise worthy astrological publications fall for this solar stuff. We believe that the sooner writers on astrological topics drop the solar house idea the better it will be for Astrologers in general and clients in particular. 

Astrology A Divine Science 

Some worthy Astrologers think that, since Astrology is a divine science, all astrological work should be done 93

without remuneration. We wonder whether these individuals refuse to drop something in the contribution plate at church, or think that preachers should be paid nothing for their services. 

The Word of God tells us that the laborer is worthy of his hire. If the calculation, erection and interpretation of a Horoscope is not hard work, what is? Astrological work is not, of course, of a physical nature, but requires much time and mental effort when conscientiously done. 

We notice that many who advocate no remuneration for astrological work charge well for their own services or for productions which are of no more importance to human beings.

The "padding" of a delineation with a lot of high moralizing which has little relation to the Horoscope itself, is another thing that is sometimes done. 

We admit that Astrology is often over-commercialized. 

Many people would regard astrological readings to be of small value if they were furnished gratis, therefore they would be inclined to pay little attention to favorable and unfavorable indications and advice. 

In the early days of personal study, the writer refused to avail himself of class work and a Horoscope reading for the reason that no charges were made for either class or Horoscope work. He felt that he had no moral right to receive without remuneration that which was of value.

Let us study and become familiar with the truths of Astrology in order that, through knowledge of this divine science, we may learn to make our lives real and complete. 94
