Decline in Morals

There is a book, The Bible, which like other books, for instance my own Treatise, Investment For Appreciation, can be interpreted in different ways by different people. 

Parts of the text can be made to fit into almost any situation. But the Bible is an inspired book, the world's bestseller: and in the second-half lies a code of morals unmatched in other philosophies.

In the first-half there are numerous prophecies, particularly in Isaiah, Ezekiel and Daniel. There are also some prophecies in the Revelation, and in the 24th Chapter of St. Matthew.

Just as men turn to the Deity on their deathbed, so have the nations throughout the centuries returned to God in times of tribulation. [But when all looks rosy and all looks bright, God is forgotten;-men perk up and have confidence in themselves]. 

The Victorian Era with all its faults fostered the principles of duty and self-sacrifice, though admittedly the element of kindness was often lacking among those with the highest ascetic morals. But in our own generation there has been a decline in probity, and in ethics, both physical, material and international. Yet the Credit System, on which the 

Capitalistic System of promises is based, requires a high standard of probity and ethics. "Swear unto your neighbor and disappoint him not, even though it were to your own hindrance." Internationally this is all forgotten. And even in domestic business, trickery is often considered smart.

Although in my own view Capitalism has never yet been given a fair chance, (i.e. with decently managed money), in the opinion of many, Capitalism has already failed. Hence the growth of a Socialist Party in every nation, which advocates State management and production for use (i.e., for the benefit of the public... one's neighbor) rather than for profit for oneself.

Many Capitalists however argue that so long as human nature remains (selfish) as it is, a Socialist System will not work, unless it is inspired by Christian sentiment as distinct from greed and graft. Indeed the prerequisite of State Socialism is (in my view also) a wider Christianity, which does not yet exist.

Perhaps it is all just around the corner. But a change of heart must take place first. 

And here comes in the question of The Bible. 

Biblical Interpretation-The Jews 

There is an ever-growing group of Bible students (and yet if you go into a shop and ask for a Bible some of the girls will giggle in rather a superior way, indicating that "they" are not subject to superstitions) who believe that there are various Dispensations, or periods of Biblical Time. They believe that the Jews were the first chosen race: that they were given the chance to behave themselves decently under the Law of Moses: that they failed to do so: that they suffered numerous scourges: that they were sent back to Jerusalem and given one more chance . . . the Christus.

These biblicists further believe that since the Jews rejected the Christus they have been punished for two thousand years by the contempt of all other races, who frequently persecute them-the Jews arguing that the persecutors are merely vicious and jealous, while the persecutors argue that the question is not one of either religion or race, but 2

merely business ethics: that the Jews are too slick in their business dealings: irritating in their obsequious flattery of superiors (when there is something to be gained) and yet bullies towards the Christian (sic) underdog: and that since they (the Jews) prefer "smart" methods to straightforward dealing, they deserve all they get and still more. It is not the religious code of the Jews that the so-called Christians object to, but their business and drawing-room behavior (c.f., Shakespeare's Merchant of Venice, written centuries ago.) 

This has been the Nazi argument. Although the Jews insist that they are as fair and honest as anyone else, and that their "persecution" is religious and racial. 

Hitler meanwhile, whose gospel is founded on hate and greed, finds it (or found it) useful to attack the Jews, partly because he was able to say that it was the foreign influence (the international Jew) who was responsible for German unemployment; and partly because he could build up the ego of the oft-dumb German peasant by making the German feel himself superior to someone else within the same national borders... the Jew. 

The Jews, in other words, were convenient in Nazi-Germany as scapegoats, as something to hate, and as something over which to feel superior (and as something to despoil). 

The Jewish question will (I think) remain important. It is something which Fifth 

Columns can exploit.

Hitler and his followers (by the way) live and thrive on successive Hates . . . first the 

German democrats, then the communists, then Versailles, then the Jews, then the Poles, then the French, then the English. (Americans next?)

Yet according to certain interpretations of the Bible, Hitler was merely fulfilling his part of destiny in tormenting the Jews. . . . And as the Jewish Rabbis in Germany often said, "You should not have inter-married with and curried favor with the Germans. As soon as you deserted your religion, disaster overcame you." That, I believe, was the talk in the concentration camps. 

The "New" Israelites

But to go on with the Biblical interpretation. The Jews, having failed to accept the 

Christus with all his sane and rational and gentle philosophy, the chance was given to the "New" Israelites.

The group of biblical interpreters to whom I refer regard the "new" Israelites as the Anglo-Saxon-Celts, as distinct from the "old" Israelites, the Jews (Judah). And in some of their writings they make out a remarkably convincing case, based on ethnical migrations, to show that the British and the Americans are the "new" Israelites referred to in The Prophets. (The Germans are ethnically the Assyrians). 

This same school of interpreters goes on to argue that these modern Israelites or Christians (so-called), although knowing the teachings of Christ, reject them in practice, preferring mammon and idolatry. Or, as the Prophets called it, Babylon; i.e. the Profit 

System, greed, vice, selfishness. . . all the reverse of Charity. It was (is) only the minority who are charitable.

But the word of God has to be fulfilled, and some day not only the Christians but also the Jews, will revert, in practice, to the teachings of Christ... the teachings of The Sermon on the Mount, trying to apply them not only in high politics, but also in their private lives. 

It is however prophesied that a period will first occur during which the Bible will be ridiculed. And when referred to, it will be rather giggled at (mocked). And that period seems to be today: It is "sissy" to be good: effeminate to be religious, and "soft" to talk about Christ. Most parents are afraid to do so to their children. The Bible has been virtually banned from most schools.

It was also prophesied that the Israelites would forget of what race they really were. 

And certainly if the modern Anglo-Saxon-Celts are in reality the "new" Israelites, they have forgotten!

And the result is that the moral tone of our and the rising generation is not what it was, nor what it ought to be. (And yet if world-wide Socialism is to come a higher moral tone of unselfish social service is essential.)

Religious "Dispensations" 

Now the Biblical forecasters to whom I refer believe that after a period of about two thousand years, the Christus will come again . . . this time not humbly, but with Power. 

But the time is not just yet. Certain other prophecies must first be fulfilled. 

And these various other prophecies amount to roughly this: that the world will be filled with tribulation: that nation will rise against nation: that there will be famine and tribulation, wars and rumors of wars; gnashing of teeth, an abomination of desolation, an apocalypse, a holocaust-i.e., Armageddon, and the Battle of the Ages. 

It is already here, [or rather, it is just beginning (they fear)]. 

Despair will recurrently fill the world until at last, like evil men on their deathbeds, there will be a sudden religious revival and turning (returning) to God. The soul will be thought more important than the body: than pride, or place, or the pocketbook. 

But before that "three dictators" will arise (who appear to be Hitler, Mussolini and Stalin), who will scourge the earth. Armageddon will occur, though no one will win. 

There will just be desolation everywhere. Until at last (from America) someone arises who will be able, owing to the disgust of all men, in all the various opposing groups of nations, to organize world-wide disarmament (swords into ploughshares): set in motion a new religious philosophy: and prepare the world for Christ's Second Coming ("around 

1953"). And then, the reign of Peace. (Not Pax Germanica but Pax Christiana).

But first Hitler is to sweep over Europe. Russia is to sweep down south through Turkey towards India. Italy and Germany, sweeping through the north of Africa, over a dried-up delta of the Nile (due to the diversion of its waters further north), will move up into Palestine from the south, meeting the conqueror from the north, Moscow, and trapping a New Isrealite army in Palestine. 

And then eventually an earthquake, very definitely and repeatedly defined as an earthquake, will occur to destroy the dictatorial armies in the East. But it will not be the Allies, i.e. the Anglo-Saxon-Celts, who will destroy the dictators. It will be a miracle: an act of 

God, an earthquake and a storm.

And this tribulation will last about seven years. During which, religious revival. Then the disarmament. Then the Reign of Peace. 

This I believe is the interpretation of the Biblical students to whom I refer. Everyone is going to get chastised and chastened-to force them, through despair, to turn over a new leaf.

Take it or leave it. (Though perhaps you ought to take it.) And if you want to read more of it, subscribe to a magazine called "Destiny." $3 a year from... Destiny, 22 Summit Avenue, Haverhill, Massachusetts. Read particularly the issue of June, 1940. You will then see if I have got the story right.

It may be all nonsense-but perhaps it is not. Indeed to giggle at the scriptures may be unwisdom. The fool hath said in his heart "There is no God," etc., etc. Be wise.

Our Duty 

Although destiny may have ordained that Hitler should sweep over Europe, and possibly America, and although Hitler may be the tool of destiny; and although it is desirable that we Moderns should turn over a new leaf in our private and public lives; I am not advocating in any way that the forces that inveigh against Hitler should not do their best to stop him from a military viewpoint. A man should fight for his principles, and against all evil. He should not cower and give himself up... the test of his character is the way he fights Evil.

CHAPTER II HITLER-THE MAN 

Keeping the foregoing (superstitious, if you like) analysis in the back of your mind, now look at the European outlook. 

The dominant figure in Europe, at the moment, is Adolf Hitler, real name Schickelgrüber. 

I was once invited to fly to Berlin to meet him but refused. I therefore cannot describe the man's character except by hearsay. But the following notes are perhaps acceptable. 

For his political activities Hitler was imprisoned for three years in the fortress of Landsberg, where, in 1923, he wrote "Mein Kampf." And it is interesting to note that in the Eleventh Chapter of Daniel reference is made to a man (the King from the North [and the word "king" means leader]), who should personally forecast his own future actions!

In practice Hitler seems to be a sort of Satan-inspired man, just as Christ was a Godinspired man. His philosophy seems to be one of hate, not love. Of ruthlessness, not gentleness... the epitome of the reverse of the Sermon on the Mount. And he puts his own philosophy in writing in Mein Kampf ... maybe to convince unbelievers (ultimately).

Hitler's Character 

The book written by Hitler at a time of personal humiliation affords much insight into the mainsprings of his character. For a time he was employed as a political spy. A failure in the social environment in which he had spent his adolescence, Hitler was vengefully attracted to any movement which would destroy the environment. By his violent and abnormal reaction to personal failure; by his fanatical belief in the destiny of German nationalism, Hitler developed into the ideal instrument of German "Macht Politik." 

From the moment of his emergence into public life, his one supreme aim has been the acquisition of power, over his party, over the German masses (for whom he has an undisguised contempt). Hitler has no human pity, no mercy, no conscience. As Rauschning points out: ". . . acts of generosity are usual on the part even of dictators and tyrants in the hour of success; this German dictator has shown no signs of generosity even toward the noblest of his opponents"-such as Dollfuss and Schuschnigg. "Germany's Führer is not only vain but also as sensitive as a mimosa: he is brutal and vindictive. Hatred is like wine to him, it intoxicates him. One must have heard his tirades of denunciation to realize how he can revel in hate and revenge. He is entirely without generosity. He lives in a world of insincerity, deceiving and self deceiving."

According to Rauschning, Hitler's laugh is hardly more than an expression of scorn and contempt. There is no relaxation about it. His pleasures have no repose. 

Some idea of Hitler's philosophy may be grasped from the following excerpts from "Mein Kampf," "Accustomed to telling insignificant lies themselves," he writes, "the masses can detect them. They are more easily misled by a great lie than a tiny lie. But never having dreamed of the vast possibilities of lies, they generally fail to detect a truly gigantic distortion." "Even when in the process of being enlightened as to the actual truth... after a great lie has once been told (the masses), will for a long time have their doubts, completely unable to believe that some truth was not contained. . . . This is a fact which all the great falsifiers know so well." "Every attempt made for the sake of a world fails unless the struggle takes the form of a violent attack. The weapon of ruthless, brute force is of greatest value in war between two diverse views of the world." "Nations fighting for existence on this planet should not concern themselves with particulars of humaneness and aesthetics. Nature does not know these driveling thingsquestions of destiny have no obligation to beauty." 55

"The moment one's own propaganda grants even a glimmer of justice to the other side, seeds are sown for the doubting of one's own cause. The masses are incapable of deciding where the enemy's sins end and their own begin." "The important point is that such enthusiasm as war enthusiasm, once broken, cannot easily be awakened again. It is intoxication, and it should be kept." "Every idea designed to change the world has not only the right but the duty to adopt any means which make possible the achievement of its aims." "The value of physical terror, against the individual and against the masses, now was revealed to me."

Some illuminating dicta attributed to him by Rauschning, ("VOICE OF DESTRUC- TION"): 

"Conscience is a Jewish invention. It is a blemish, like circumcision." "There is no such thing as truth, either in the moral or in the scientific sense." 

Hitler is the logical extension of the complete amoral pantheist placed in a position of terrifying power. Yet he is a mystic who loves to indulge in communion with nature; the supreme anti-Christ who believes that he is the prophet of a new order. "But for our people (the Germans) it is decisive whether they acknowledge the Jewish Christ creed with its effeminate pity-ethics or a strong, heroic belief in God in Nature, God in our own people, in our destiny, in our blood." 

Hitler believes that there is a perpetual struggle among nations for power; that his people are the "Chosen People"; that to achieve their destiny all means, fair or foul, are to be employed; that no quarter must be given to any opponent however insignificant; that once dominion has been achieved it must be maintained by ruthless violence. He believes that force must prevail until it is overcome by a superior force: therefore German power must be perpetually increased. 

According to Rauschning, Hitler looks upon himself as a great strategist of a new kind, a future war-lord in a sense and to a degree hitherto unknown. "I shall never start a war without the certainty that a demoralized enemy will succumb to the first stroke of a single gigantic attack.” "When the enemy is demoralized from within, when he stands on the brink of revolution, when social unrest threatens-that is the right moment. A single blow must destroy him. Aerial attacks, stupendous in their mass effect, surprise, terror, sabotage, assassination from within, the murder of leading men, overwhelming attacks on all weak points in the enemy's defense, sudden attacks, all in the same second, without regard for reserves or losses: that is the war of the future. A gigantic, all-destroying blow. I do not consider consequences; I think only of this one thing." "I shall shrink from nothing. No so-called international law, no agreements will prevent me from making use of any advantage that offers." "One is either a German, or a Christian. You cannot be both." "I will do anything to facilitate the success of my policy."

Hitler's World Plan 

Hitler's opportunity in Germany came with the destruction of all faith by the German people in every social system they had tried; neither industrial nor finance capitalism, neither socialism nor social democracy had given economic satisfaction to the masses. 

National socialism was a rebellion against everything that had gone before but its creation of a military hierarchy impregnated with a will to power, presented the German philosophers of "might is right" with the instrument they had been waiting for. 

Hitler, the man of destiny, has made realizable the old German dreams of world dominion. Once it is grasped that the inner dynamic of National Socialism consists in perpetual revolution, it is no longer fantastic to conceive the eventual aims of Hitler. 

As Rauschning has put it ("Revolution of Nihilism" pp 226) "But yet another question arises: can the dynamic revolution stop at a sharing of the world? Must not the struggle for existence in foreign politics continue until the final world domination of a single nation? The triumvirate sharing the world in the age of Cæsarism is always no more than a preliminary to the decisive final struggle for exclusive rule. Thus at the back of Germany's continental empire stands the will to absolute dominion in the world, the technical means of which are no longer lacking as hitherto. England, crippled as a European Power by the occupation or Anschluss of Holland and Belgium, will not escape the dismemberment of her empire even by concluding a pact with the Third Reich. Italy, even as a vast Mediterranean empire, will not be equal to a crossing of swords with the Germanic union. And America is already at the outset of internal convulsions produced by a war of ideologies."

Again on pp 263: "No order, no balance, no neighborliness between nations is aimed at, but fundamental disharmony. No new equilibrium can be attained, no matter what concessions are made to National Socialism by other nations or what successes it achieves. The essential aim is preparedness, with the determination to push onwards and emerge from central European, continental confinement: an unscrupulous, doctrineless determination to seize anything and to be ready to do anything that serves the increase of power and dominion. 

It is a conquistador policy. With the revolutionary breaking-up of all elements of order in the world, the chances increase of succeeding, if not in every enterprise, at all events in some. National Socialists in high places declare that this is one of those periods that come only at intervals of centuries, a period of revolutionary change in the world, in which, amid the general insecurity, every resolute stroke has good prospects of success. It is the period of a modern type of buccaneer and filibuster, of semi-legalized elements of disturbance who are followed by the flag if they succeed." 

Again on pp 289-290: "It is only necessary to glance at the latest literature, maps and instructions, the publications of the Hitler Youth in the last six months, or the latest articles of General Haushofer: the aim is the hierarchical organization of the nations of Europe around Germany, whether past members of the Holy Roman Empire or remnants of the "sub-Teuton zone of fragments" in the east, with protectorates and alliances of various types; the weakening of Great Britain and destruction of her empire, the setting up of a colonial empire in Africa, and thrusts against South America and the Pacific; union with Russia or annexation of Russian territory; and, in the end, world dominion." 

Hitler's Word 

It is clear from the analysis of Hitler's character, that his word is distinctly not his bond. His views on treaties have been admirably described by Rauschning (Revolution of 

Nihilism, p 237, Putnam): "He was ready, he said, to sign anything. He was ready to guarantee any frontier and to conclude a non-aggression pact with anyone. It was a simpleton's idea not to avail oneself of expedients of this sort because the day might come when some formal agreement would have to be broken. Every pact sworn to was broken or became out of date sooner or later. Anyone who was so fussy that he had to consult his conscience about whether he could keep to a pact, whatever the pact and whatever the situation, was a fool. Why not please other people and ease one's own position by signing pacts, if the other people thought that got them anywhere or settled anything? He could conclude any treaty in good faith, and yet be ready to break it in cold blood the next day, if that was in the interest of the future of Germany."

In the years preceding the outbreak of war, Hitler broke every treaty that he had signed. His record in this respect was reviewed by N. Y. Times in an editorial published 

26th of Sept. 1939:- 

This is an anniversary, an anniversary of a speech made at the Sportspalast in Berlin just one year ago today, a speech made by Adolf 

Hitler during that tense week when the "September crisis" over the Sudeten area of Czecho Slovakia was heading to its climax. In the light of what has happened in twelve months it is instructive to recall certain passages in that famous declaration. 

Of the Sudeten area itself, Hitler said:

We confront the last problem that must and shall be solved. This is the last territorial demand I have to make in Europe. 

Of the idea that he would ever use the Sudeten area as a stepping-stone to the conquest of the rest of Czecho-Slovakia, he said: 

The moment that Czecho-Slovakia has solved her other problems, that is, when the Czechs have reconciled themselves with their other minorities, the Czech State no longer interests me; if you please, I give the guarantee: We do not want any Czechs. 

Of Germany's relations with Poland-that same Poland which was later to be accused of systematic and brutal terrorization of German minorities over a period of more than three years, Hitler said, just one year ago today: .8

We succeeded in arriving at an agreement which for the duration of ten years basically removes the danger of any clash.

We are all determined, and also convinced, that this agreement will bring about lasting and continuous pacification, because problems in eight years are no different from those of today. We do not have to expect anything from each other. We recognize this. We are two peoples. They shall live. One cannot annihilate the other. I recognize this and we must see it: a state of 33,000,000 people will always strive for an outlet to the sea.

Of the prospect for continued peace with 

Poland: 

Here the road to understanding had to be found; and it was found. And it is being widened and expanded. * * * The decisive thing is this: the two administrations and all sensible and reasonable people in both countries have a firm will and a firm resolve continually to improve relations. 

Assurances for Poland, because Poland was not the next immediate victim in his intended line of conquest; assurances for the rest of the world that the business immediately in hand represented fulfillment of his “last demand"-the Sportspalast speech was a perfect example of the Hitler technique. The man who made it is the same man who, having crushed 

Poland and seized what remained of Czecho Slovakia, now announces that partition of Poland is his last demand and asks Britain and France why the war should be continued. 

Far from being ashamed of the above philosophies, which have already proved highly "practical" in practice, Hitler seems to revel in his ruthlessness, in his sly double-dealing, and in his policy of appeal to all the baser instincts of humanity. (Maybe he is meant to epitomise modern crime... so as to set in motion ultimately a wave of revulsion). 

And yet in person Hitler seems something of a saint: frugal, a vegetarian, a teetotaller, a bachelor. He is the puzzle of a man, like Rasputin. And he also has something mesmeric about him, particularly about his eyes, like Rasputin. The strongest characters in Germany quail before him, or sweat with embarrassment, even though at times he behaves openly like a child: throws himself on the floor: kicks the furniture: and screams.

It is even said (by Hermann Rauschning) that he suffers from fits, and bursts into paranoic tempers, during which he imagines somebody who is not there to be crouching in a far corner of the room. At nights he wakes up in a sweat, sits on the side of his bed: trembling and uttering complete gibberish. 

And yet the man is the greatest political and military strategist of all time. Is he normal? Is he mad? Or is he just a genius (perverted)? Or is he a tool to fulfill God's will... and to test men's characters?

We do not know. Or at all events, I don't know. But as a sometime student of military technique I am overwhelmed with wonder at the way in which his general staff (Hitler himself) has moved his armies through Belgium and France, [when the official analysis of the first World War (called Great) stated that the attack should numerically be five times stronger than the defense]. The losses of France were apparently ten times greater than 

Germany's: 1,500,000 against 162,000.

And somehow or other, the infantry, airmen and tank corps which he leads seem equally efficient in carrying out his Will. 

Chased Out of Flanders-The Lessons 

And at this point I want to go on record, as a soldier, in rather disputing the belief that the Allied retreat through Belgium was just a glorious rear guard action. I think it must have been, in parts, a demoralized runaway, although undoubtedly great valor, in parts, was shown in its course. And note this, ye leaders who train boys at West Point. 

A tank may pass your post as an infantryman or as a machine gunner, but that is not necessarily any reason to run away. But I fear that trying to go back as fast as the enemy tanks went forward was what in practice largely happened in Flanders, and that this will eventually come out in the future history of the war. 

And maybe the artillery, who were brought up to think it is an unforgivable dishonor to lose their guns, felt it their duty to save their guns, as soon as they found no infantry in front of them,-instead of keeping their guns in the front line and shooting at the tanks.

In actual practice I have seen the tendency of infantry to find excuses for withdrawing, either because somebody else has withdrawn, or if they cannot see other troops "in line." They feel lonely out in the front by themselves, and feel that they are not conforming to the general plan of campaign in their own small sector of the line. And back they often go! And I think this has happened, although I will be most unpopular for saying so. 

Bad Training-Crumbling of Fighting Spirit 

But if the American army is eventually to be involved in this world war, as I believe it will be, troops, and above all, field gunners, must be trained to the view that they must expect to suffer the misery of "loneliness in battle"; and that it is not their duty "to keep in line", if the line is behind them (but only if it is in front of them); and that it is more honorable for an artilleryman to lose his guns than to save them by retreat. And that there is no objection to the artillery not having infantry in front of them, when America is at stake. 

Maybe I am wrong in my military conclusions. The outlook for England is, on paper, certainly not so good-unless the Americans send all their 3,000 airplanes and 1,700 tanks at once; and their fleet; and unless the Russians join in against the Germans immediately, aiding England with aircraft as well as an attack on Germany's eastern frontiers.

But these things seem, at the moment (owing the problem not being understood), politically rather impossible for America and Russia, which leaves the outlook for England, on paper, far from good. Perhaps however there is something left in the English character which may produce surprises; and the following letter written by a young airman to his mother (before he was shot down and killed in the present campaign) does perhaps, quite accurately represent the attitude towards England of most of her young men and women. It appeared in the Times of June 22, 1940. I repeat it (without permission). 

Felt No Premonition 

Dearest Mother:

Though I feel no premonition at all, events are moving rapidly and I have instructed that this letter be forwarded to you should I fail to return from one of the raids which I shall shortly be called upon to undertake. You must hope on for a month, but at the end of that time you must accept the fact that I have handed over my task to extremely capable hands -my comrades of the Royal Air Force, as so many splendid fellows have already done. 

At first it will comfort you to know that my role in this war has been of the greatest importance. Our patrols far out over the North 

Sea have helped to keep the trade routes clear for our convoys and supply ships, and on one occasion our information was instrumental in saving the lives of men in crippled lighthouse relief ships. Though it will be difficult for you, you will disappoint me if you do not at least try to accept the facts dispassionately, for I shall have done my duty to the utmost of my ability. No man can do more, and no one calling himself a man could do less.

I have always admired your amazing courage in the face of continual setbacks, in the way you have given me as good an education and background as any one in the country and always kept up appearances without ever losing faith in the future. My death would not mean that your struggle has been in vain. Far from it. It means that your sacrifice is as great as mine. Those who serve England must expect nothing from her; we debase ourselves if we regard our country as merely a place in which to eat and sleep.

History resounds with the illustrious names of those who have given all; yet their sacrifices resulted in the British Empire, where there is a measure of peace and justice and freedom for all and where a higher standard of civilization was evolved and is still evolving than anywhere else. 

Challenge to Christianity

But this is not only concerning our own land. 

Such soldiers may need some defeating. 

Today we are faced with the greatest organized challenge to Christianity and to civilization the world has ever seen, and I count myself lucky and honored to be of the right age and fully trained to throw my full weight into the scale. For this I have to thank you. Yet there is more work for you to do. The home front will still have to stand united for years after the war is won.

For all that can be said to the contrary, I still maintain that this war is a very good thing; every individual is having a chance to give and to dare all for his principle, like the martyrs of old. However long the time may be, one thing can never be altered-I shall have lived and died an Englishman. Nothing else matters one jot, nor can anything ever change it. 

You must not grieve for me, for if you really believe in religion and all that it entails, that would be hypocrisy. I have no fear of death, only a queer elation. I would have it no other way. The universe is so vast, so ageless, that the life of one man can only be justified by the measure of his sacrifice. We are sent to this world to acquire the personality and character to take with us. Those who just eat, sleep, prosper and procreate are no better than animals if all their lives they are at peace. gre I firmly and absolutely believe that evil things sent into the world to try us; they are sent deliberately by our Creator to test our mettle, because He knows what is good for us. The 

Bible is full of cases where the easy way out has been discarded for moral principles. 

I count myself fortunate that I have seen the whole country and know men of every calling. 

But with the final test of war I consider my character fully developed. Thus at my early age, with my earthly mission already fulfilled, I am prepared to die with just one regret, and one only-that I could not devote myself to making your declining years more happy by being with you; but you will live in peace and freedom, and I shall have directly contributed to that, so here again my life will not have been in vain. 

Your loving son. "Not once nor twice in our rough island story, "The path of duty was the way to glory'." 

THE END 

Major L. L. B. Angas, Investment Consultant, 570 Lexington Avenue, New York
