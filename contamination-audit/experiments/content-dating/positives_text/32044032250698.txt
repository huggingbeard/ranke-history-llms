Briefs and Arguments in the Full Court 

All briefs for all parties in cases to be argued orally at any sitting of the Full Court must be filed at least two days before the first day of the sitting. Twenty printed copies must be filed (Full Court Rule 2, as amended 1939).

In computation of the time, any Sunday or holiday is excluded. For sittings at Boston, the last day for filing is ordinarily the 

Friday preceding each sitting.

The rules provide that the minimum time between entry and argument shall be three weeks (Full Court Rule 1, as amended 1939). This time should be utilized in preparing the briefs and getting them printed without delay. The printers are apt to be overburdened with work during the last week before the sitting.

Arguments are limited to one half hour on each side, unless before the commencement of the argument, for good cause shown, the court allows further time; and when more than one counsel are to be heard on the same side, the time may be divided between them as they elect (Full Court Rule 5). 

The Full Court at any time will receive briefs in cases submitted by the parties without oral argument. If at the time of such submission the Full Court is not sitting in the county where the case is pending, the clerk transmits the record and the briefs to the clerk of the Supreme Judicial 

Court for the Commonwealth at Boston (Full Court Rule 3, as amended 1939).
