LOVE COMES LATE By GLADYS MALVERN 

She was "Madame Margo" in her successful hat shop. At thirty-nine she had lost a husband but accumulated money. With that and son who was already graduating from college, she felt satisfied. Her life was planned to revolve around her son, but his sudden marriage left Margo alone and discouraged. Then she opened her heart again and let romance in. A woman of thirty-nine is still a woman, and Margo discovered that the love that comes late may be as thrilling as young romance.
