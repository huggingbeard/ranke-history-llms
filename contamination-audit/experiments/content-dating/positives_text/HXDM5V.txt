This book is dedicated to all women who have no husbands to keep them from folly and sin. 

Beware of the scoundrel, the rogue and the rake, who lustful and debauched, hoe in heavily on ageing ladies' stocks (not the garden variety nor the pillory) and bonds (not the matrimonial ties). Never take a man having a prison record into conjugal felicity without expecting suffering and death.

That is, unless you have too. How be it, if preyed on by some wanton wastrel unawares this is what will happen.

PREFACE 

To begin with I would like to explain this was never meant to be anything but a funny story taken in light doses according to the patient's desire. Of course, not the kind of "funny story" the garage mechanic whispers in audible undertones to a good friend after carefully curving his hand around his mouth and eyeing everyone surreptitiously so they can see how snappy it is, the while he is putting air in your tires or gas in your tank. No, not at all. Just a relaxitive to amuse more serious people or these same at home, who, like myself, are tired of reading modern tear-agic death notices, like a bridge breaks precipitating all the postdeluvian immortals thereon into unknown depths below, or poor old Hiram Williams, the hero of Bull Run, a Civil War veteran, decorated by Abraham Lincoln himself, is celebrating his golden wedding anniversary at Coney Isand, while enjoying a bag of pop-corn just as mother used to make it, a bridge breaks-Oh, yes! he swallowed it, gold plate and all, so, in spite of the ambulance knocking down two women, a horse and tipping over a cartload of oranges in rushing him to the hospital, the dear old soul is aged and infirm, with the sad ending you can easily imagine. Well! World serials and home cooked cereals-if you prefer melodrama don't read this, but my next-"Coffins and Kisses" it's the grapenuts. Almost ripe and will be rotten. Children are crying for it. Now it only remains to add if the longevity of literature were gauged by that of animals this piece would have the life of an elephant, so many years having elapsed from its conception to its birth, and I also wish to invite you all to a good laugh. It won't cost you a cent and is about the only thing I ever gave away. 

P. P.
