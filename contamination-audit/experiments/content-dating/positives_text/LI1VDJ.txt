1951 Trials of Annual Flowers At The Pennsylvania State College® R. P. MEAHL, L. D. LITTLE, JR., AND SAM ATMORE† 

During 1951, 844 samples of flower seeds were grown and tested in the Flower Test Garden of The Pennsylvania State College. 

These included 40 samples of Aster, 60 Marigold, 36 Pansy, 169 

Petunia, 57 Snapdragon, 75 Verbena, 49 Zinnia, together with 320 samples of other annuals, 32 All-America Selections for 1951, and 6 repeated from the All-America Selections for 1950. 

Sufficient seed was sown in the greenhouse to assure 35 to 50 plants of each sample, except larkspur, nasturtium, and poppy which were seeded directly in the beds. In some cases poor germination reduced the number of plants available. Seedlings, germinating in the greenhouse, were transplanted into flats and kept inside or in coldframes until June 1 to 10 when they were set in the garden. 

Planting distance varied from 6 inches for very small types to 15 inches for large types; however, the majority were planted at spacings of 12-by-12 inches. The plants were watered immediately upon planting and thereafter as often as necessary to maintain good moisture conditions. This was usually at about 10-day intervals. 

Watering was accomplished by an overhead irrigating system. 

Records were taken of the height of plants, flower size, color of flowers, uniformity of habit and color, flower production, and general garden value. Results for the samples of Aster are given in Table 1, Marigold in Table 2, Pansy in Table 3, Petunia in Table 4, Snap dragon in Table 5, Verbena in Table 6, Zinnia in Table 7, miscellaneous annuals in Table 8, 1950 All-America Selections in Table 9, and 1951 All-America Selections in Table 10. 

Variety names used are the same as appeared on the seed packets. •Authorized for publication March 3, 1953. +Professor of Ornamental Horticulture, Instructor in Ornamental Horticulture, and Foreman, Ornamental Horticulture, respectively.

COMMENTS 

1. The planting distance for large flowered types was 15 by 15 inches, and for small flowered types 12 by 12 inches. 

2. Canary Bird had clear deep yellow color and was good for cutting. 

3. Will Rogers and Crimson Monarch were best red samples of Dahlia flowered type.
