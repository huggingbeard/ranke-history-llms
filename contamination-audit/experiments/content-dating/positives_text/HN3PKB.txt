TO THE PUPIL 

As you read the stories in this book, you will meet 

Dick and Janet Allen and their good friends, John and Bob Davis. They are about your own age and go to school just as you do. In the summer they spend their time playing games and going on trips. 

They are the kind of playmates you like.

Do you like to take long trips of various kinds, visit strange places, and have picnic lunches? As you read about the happy travels of Dick and Janet and their friends, you will almost feel that you are with them. First they take a long automobile trip across the United States. Then they travel in a great ship across the ocean and see how people live in faraway places. You will enjoy reading about their travels and about the interesting people they visit. 

Have you wondered whether children in other lands go to school as you do, play the same games, and eat the same foods? Some of these questions will be answered as Dick tells his classmates at school about his trips. You will enjoy reading what he has to say. Perhaps you can imagine you are sitting in the classroom listening to him.

Of course boys and girls in other lands do not always dress or speak exactly as you do. They do not always play the same games, eat the same

Dick and Janet find that they play and work to get strength, eat the right kinds of food, get plenty of rest and sleep, take care of their clothes, and try to avoid accidents. You will learn, too, what these children do as you read this book.

The book is divided into five large parts, or units. 

Each unit tells a separate story about Dick and his sister Janet. At the beginning of each story you will be told what they are about to do. Notice the questions that are asked. Talk about these questions before you begin to read, for they will help you enjoy the story.

After you read each story, talk about it in class. 

Every few pages you will find questions to help you think about what you have read. At the end of each story you will find an exercise called Something
