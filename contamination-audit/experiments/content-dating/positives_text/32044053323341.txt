RESEARCH ON THE MIDDLE EAST External Research List No. 6.18 

This is one of a series of research lists based on the catalog of social science research on foreign areas and international affairs compiled by the External Research Division from information furnished by private scholars throughout the United States. 

This compilation is a revision of External Research List No. 6.16 of 

April 1961. It includes only research projects reported as in progress by scholars. Completed research is reported in separate lists published annually in the fall.

Copies of these research lists are sent to the libraries of universities that offer graduate degrees in social sciences, directors of area study programs, and faculty members who conduct relevant teaching or research. Because of cost, these lists are ordinarily not distributed more widely.

Data for each item is as complete as the Division's file permits. 

To facilitate exchange of information, a private address or university affiliation is shown in parentheses following the name of the author. The initials ECD stand for the estimated completion date as established by the author. Uncertainty about completion date is shown by "indef.", while an 

ECD prior to 1961 indicates lack of recent information from the author. 

Titles of studies reported in progress in earlier lists about which no information has been received since January 1, 1961, have been deleted. 

Annotations following individual items have been supplied in all cases by the authors; a few minor editorial changes have been made in them. 

The Department of State assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of the statements of fact nor does it necessarily endorse the conclusions expressed.

Scholars are invited to send the Division annotations for their research topics entered in these lists. The Division also welcomes letters from scholars and research organizations reporting on the status of projects listed, as well as information on new projects not already entered in the External Research Catalog. The Catalog is available at the address below for use by students and private scholars as well as by government officials.
