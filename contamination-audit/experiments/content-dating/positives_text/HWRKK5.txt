TO THE CHURCH In Whose Service I Came to Know Him.

A FOREWORD. 

A simple experience goes before this Book-and yet the most magnificent one that can happen to a human being. Let me see if I can recount it.

God works, let me preface, by a Holy Will which urges on the ages. And He has but one secret of action: the utmost mercy and providence for the individual soul. Now some things that seem strange by their very bareness will be clear. I must be brief for this article. "As after dinner, I was going down to get my car after its being greased-I had to stop and note an unbidden but definite report of experience-a feeling I had of abiding gift of the living grace of God that would make me equal to all things. I gave my life over to the guidance of the Holy Spirit and was happy.- 

Everything else follows. I become invincible." (From -Diary). 

One more extract: "Like a new conversion. Yes, like a new and joyous conversion.-Jubilation-Nothing but jubilation." 

Then came ecstasy-quiet. Then came joy unspeakable. If my experience has tinged my expressional power under the Spirit, it is even so as it is. 

It's a real confession; and perhaps it has that value. 

The heart's own aching unravelled on the sleeve.

FOREWORD 

You'll think this hilariously, drunkenly intoxicated with the Spirit's mood, but so thought they of the 

Spirit's power on Pentecost's converts.

The responsibility for the high positions taken in these pages is mine and His who is with me. 

The only liberties I have taken are some days being longer or shorter, I have added to or divided up materials which properly as they came belonged to one or less than one day.

A Diary in sense of a daily record. 

Also in time of beginning the record, I have taken freedom. Assuming it began with the New Year which we understand from the experience was not the true date. But for convenience of use let it be on this plan. 

Having had an experience which was an awakening by the gliding into my life of the Holy Spirit, I am led sometimes to pass upon the wonders of God's dealing with me. And what I was without this happening and what others are similarly without His touch of joy and perfectness on the Soul. 

Intemperate it seems but is not. Intemperate it seems but is not. Only the God-annointed speaks.
