LOUISIANA HAS AN UNUSUAL BRACKET-SYSTEM FOR ITS SALES TAX...THE 2% SALES TAX IS COLLECTED FROM CONSUMERS ONLY ON SALES TOTALING 25 CENTS OR MORE. THIS SAVES THE BUYER OF SMALL ITEMS FROM OVERPAYING AND PREVENTS SOME MERCHANTS FROM "MAKING MONEY" ON THE TAX OUR WELFARE PROGRAM IS AIMED TOWARD REHABILITATION.
