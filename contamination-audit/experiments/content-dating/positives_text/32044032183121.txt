Problem: To introduce into evidence a public document. 

Secs. 19, 617, and 647.

Action to foreclose a chattel mortgage. In response to a subpoena duces tecum served by you on the County Clerk of Kings County, a messenger employed by the County Clerk appears in Court with the mortgage, but upon looking at it you find that there is no filing mark, seal, or signature of the County Clerk of which the Court should take judicial notice. 

The messenger having been duly sworn as a witness, the following questions are propounded to him: 

Q. Are you employed by the County Clerk of Kings County? 

Q. In what capacity? 

Q. How long have you been so employed?

Q. As such employee, have you gained familiarity with the methods of your office relating to the keeping, filing, and permitting inspection of chattel mortgages?

County Clerk of Kings County a (certain paper)? 

Counsel: I request that the paper now produced be deemed marked "Plaintiff's Exhibit A for Identification," since it appears to be a paper produced from a public office and, therefore, should not receive a physical mark. 

Marked "Plaintiff's Exhibit A for Identification."

Q. Are chattel mortgages ordinarily kept there?
