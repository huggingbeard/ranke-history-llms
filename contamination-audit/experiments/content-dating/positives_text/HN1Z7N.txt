From the time she was a high school girl, Scoot Frazier dominated every man she knew. Endowed with a very pretty face, a tiny, cute figure and a great wealth of sex appeal, she attracted and bedeviled these men without fear or failure. She broke up homes and hearts and made all women her enemies. 

But being a hell cat, without any moral sense, and endowed also with a vicious and violent temper, she never held a man. Her every love affair proved a boomerang. In the end, she lost out-badly beaten. 

Here is a book that moves like a cyclone and is packed with racy dialogue, amorous adventure and an excitement that will make your hair stand on end.
