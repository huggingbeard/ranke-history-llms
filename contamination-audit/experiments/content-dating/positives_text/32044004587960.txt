Remark 

"Now thou art destroyed by the sea, thy riches are in the bottom of the waters, and all the multitude that was in the midst of thee is fallen." 

(Ezech. 27:34) 

In the early part of the year 1908 it was announced in England that a ship would soon be given to the world eclipsing in size and capacity all other ships ever built. 

On May 31, 1911, this ship was launched and given the name Titanic; in February, 1912, it was completed. Its length__was 888 feet, or about four city blocks. On board were all kinds of comforts, among them a gymnasium, a swimming-pool, a grill, and even a palm garden. 

Elaborate suites of rooms were provided, one of which, it is said, was priced at over four thousand dollars for a single trip. Such excessive figures will be the better understood when we read that, among many other very wealthy passengers, there were ten persons whose fortunes ran over the ten-million mark. Because of the fact that this ship was fitted with as high as fifteen watertight compartments, it was held to be practically unsinkable.

To open show: 

Go, hide thee, e'en as Cain, Fleeing from Heaven's eye; 

For thou'rt despised,— 

The deed is done, The victim's sacrificed!"

The End
